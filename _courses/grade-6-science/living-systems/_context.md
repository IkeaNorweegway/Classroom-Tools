# Unit Context — Living Systems
**Course:** Grade 6 Science | Alberta 2023 | **Tier:** 1 | **Weeks:** 5–6
**Guiding question:** In what ways are ecosystems complex?

---

## Learning Outcomes

Students investigate the characteristics and components of and interactions within ecosystems.

| Cluster | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Biotic and abiotic components — ecosystem types; characteristics; role in diversity |
| 2 | Photosynthesis — light + water + CO₂ → glucose + oxygen; chlorophyll; starch as stored glucose; three controlled experiments |
| 3 | Plant-animal relationships — interdependence; human reliance on plants; sacred plants (FNMI — sage, sweetgrass, cedar, tobacco) |

---

## Scope boundary — Photosynthesis (Grade 6: process level only)

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| CO₂ from air, water from roots, light from sun → glucose + oxygen | Chloroplasts, stomata, cell types |
| Where photosynthesis happens: green parts / leaves | Wavelengths absorbed by chlorophyll |
| Chlorophyll as light-capturing substance (why leaves are green) | ATP, electron transport, biochemical steps |
| Glucose = food/energy; stored as starch | Molecular structure of glucose or starch |
| Three experiments: light required, oxygen produced, green parts required | Lab procedures beyond experimental logic |

The three experiments are taught at the *logic* level — what question is asked, what setup tests it, what the result tells us.

---

## Misconceptions to address

- Plants get food from soil (most persistent misconception in biology)
- Photosynthesis and respiration are opposites that cancel out
- Animals are independent of plants beyond food (miss the oxygen/CO₂ cycle)
- Decomposers are not part of the ecosystem

---

## CS Embedding

Scratch simulation of a food web or photosynthesis process. Ecosystem logic maps directly onto conditionals (sequences, loops, conditionals + block coding activity).

---

## Inquiry Project Options

1. Photosynthesis experiment series — three controlled experiments from the curriculum: light importance, oxygen release, starch presence
2. Local ecosystem study — map biotic and abiotic components of one local ecosystem, compare to a contrasting ecosystem type
3. Sacred plants and ecosystem roles — research FNMI sacred plants in Alberta; connect cultural significance to ecological role in local ecosystems
