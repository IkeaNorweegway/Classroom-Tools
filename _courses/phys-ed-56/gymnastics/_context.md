# Unit Context — Gymnastics and Body Management, Grades 5–6
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 5–6

---

## What This Is

Gymnastics at this level is about body management — using body weight safely and skillfully. The focus is on stability, balance, controlled movement, and creative sequencing. It is not competitive gymnastics.

**Core learning problems:**
- How do we control our body weight in different positions and transitions?
- How do we link individual skills into a smooth sequence?
- How do we land safely and spot a partner?

---

## Skill Categories

- **Balances:** on different bases of support (hands, feet, knees, combinations), partner balances (Gr 6)
- **Locomotion:** rolling (log, egg, forward roll), sliding, crawling, jumping
- **Inversions:** tripod, headstand prep, cartwheel (Gr 6)
- **Flight:** jumping with body shape, controlled landing mechanics
- **Sequences:** linking 4–6 skills with smooth transitions

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 5–6)

**Movement Skills**
- Maintain balance on different body parts and in different body positions
- Perform forward roll with tuck and straight body alignment
- Land from jumps with bent knees, controlled weight transfer
- Link 4 skills into a sequence with clear start and end positions

**Understanding**
- Centre of gravity and base of support as balance principles
- Weight distribution in partner and group work

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Spotting: physical support and responsibility for a partner's safety
- Encourage — gymnastics feels exposing; comments about body or skill level are not appropriate

**Safety**
- Mats: always use mats for rolling and inversion activities
- Clear zones — no crossing through another student's sequence space
- Spotting technique before any partner work
- No inversions without teacher demonstration first

---

## Misconceptions to Address

- You have to be flexible to do gymnastics (strength and body control are the primary requirements at this level)
- Gymnastics is for girls (body management is a universal movement literacy — all students develop it)
- Falling is failing (controlled falling — rolling, landing — is a skill and a safety competency)

---

## Teaching Approach

Station-based. Students rotate through balance, roll, jump/land, and sequence stations. Introduce spotting formally before any partner work. Emphasize quality of movement over quantity of skills.

**Sequence assessment:** Students design and perform their own 4-skill sequence. Assess on: clear start, smooth transitions, controlled landing, appropriate challenge level.
