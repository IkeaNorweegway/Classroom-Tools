# Course Context — Physical Education and Wellness, Grades 5–6
**Subject:** Physical Education and Wellness (PEW) | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 5–6
**Implemented:** September 2022 (K–6 full implementation)

---

## Curriculum Framework

Alberta PEW is organized around **eight organizing ideas:**

| Organizing Idea | Core Focus |
|---|---|
| Active Living | Physical activity choices, personal fitness goals, benefits of movement |
| Movement Skill Development | Skill competence, movement concepts (body, space, effort, relationship) |
| Social and Personal Responsibility | Cooperation, fair play, leadership, inclusive participation |
| Safety | Risk assessment, injury prevention, safe participation |
| Healthy Eating | Food literacy, fueling for activity |
| Healthy Relationships | Consent, communication, respect, conflict resolution |
| Growth and Development | Body changes, decision-making (puberty introduced Gr 6) |
| Daily Physical Activity (DPA) | 30 min moderate-to-vigorous activity daily |

**Primary organizers for PhysEd class materials:** Active Living, Movement Skill Development, Social and Personal Responsibility. Safety is embedded across all units.

---

## Learning Framework — KUSP

All outcomes are written using KUSP:
- **Knowledge** — what students know (concepts, vocabulary, rules)
- **Understanding** — why it matters (principles, connections)
- **Skills and Procedures** — what students can do (technique, tactics)
- **Performance** — demonstrating competence in context

---

## Movement Concepts (embed in all units)

| Concept | Components |
|---|---|
| **Body awareness** | What body parts move, body shapes, balance points |
| **Space awareness** | Self-space vs. general space, direction, level, pathways |
| **Effort** | Speed (fast/slow), force (strong/light), flow (bound/free) |
| **Relationship** | With objects (equipment), with others (partner, group, opponent) |

## Grade-Level Emphases

### Grade 5
- Combine movement skills into movement patterns
- Lead-up versions of invasion and net/wall games (small-sided, modified rules)
- Basic tactics: create/deny space, support teammates, communication
- Begin tracking personal fitness data
- Cooperative skills in small groups and whole class

### Grade 6
- Consistent application of manipulative skills (catch, throw, kick, strike, volley)
- Apply strategies across game categories — recognize when tactics transfer
- Growth and development: puberty, decision-making
- Self-directed goal setting for Active Living
- Leadership opportunities in group activities

---

## Misconceptions to Address

- Fitness = athletic performance (fitness is personal and multi-dimensional — cardio, strength, flexibility, balance)
- Winning is the goal of games (games teach tactics, cooperation, and physical literacy — outcome is secondary)
- Some people are "not athletic" (physical literacy is for everyone; skill develops with practice)
- Modified rules aren't real games (modifications are pedagogically intentional — same underlying tactics)
- Stretching prevents soreness (not supported; dynamic warm-up prepares for activity, cool-down aids recovery)

---

## Assessment Notes

- Assessment is competency-based — movement quality, tactical decision-making, participation, goal progress
- Avoid grading on athletic ability or winning; assess on learning targets (skill application, cooperation, effort)
- Peer observation checklists and self-assessment are appropriate and efficient
- Exit slips work well at end of class for tactical understanding checks
- Fitness data is personal — students track their own progress, not compared to classmates
