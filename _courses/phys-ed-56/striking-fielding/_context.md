# Unit Context — Striking/Fielding Games, Grades 5–6
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 5–6

---

## What This Is

Striking/fielding games involve one team striking an object and running bases while the other team fields and attempts to get runners out. The underlying tactical structure is shared — strike to open space, field efficiently, back up plays.

**Core tactical problems:**
- Strikers: how do we hit to a space no fielder covers? How do we decide when to run?
- Fielders: how do we cover the field efficiently? Who backs up? Where does the ball go next?

---

## Sports in This Category

Baseball, softball, kickball, cricket, rounders, Danish longball, T-ball (Gr 5 entry)

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 5–6)

**Movement Skills**
- Perform striking skills: batting, kicking, throwing, catching
- Run bases with decision-making (when to advance, when to hold)
- Fielding positions and ready stance

**Tactical Understanding**
- Strikers: aim away from fielders; read fielder positions
- Fielders: cover bases, communicate, back up throws
- Decision-making in the moment: advance or stay?

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Patience — wait your turn in batting lineup
- Collective responsibility in fielding — every position matters
- Call plays fairly and honestly

**Safety**
- Batting — only one student with a bat at a time
- Clear base paths — no crowding between bases
- Soft equipment (foam bats, softer balls) preferred at this level

---

## Teaching Approach: TGfU

Start with a simplified game (fewer fielders, larger targets, shorter distances) → identify the problem ("why can we always catch the ball?") → teach the striking skill with directional intent → return to game.

---

## Misconceptions to Address

- Hit as hard as possible (placement is more effective than power at this level)
- Fielding is passive waiting (active anticipation and backing up are the real fielding skills)
- Only the batter matters (off-turn baserunning decisions are as important as the hit)

---

## Modifications for Grade 5–6

- T-ball or coach pitch before self-pitch
- Kickball as entry — easier striking skill
- Fewer fielders (4–5) to open more space
- No strikeouts — everyone gets a fair turn
- Bases closer together
- Continuous batting (no three-outs — everyone bats each inning)
