# Unit Context — Invasion/Territorial Games, Grades 7–9
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 7–9

---

## What This Is

Invasion games at Grades 7–9 move toward modified full-game formats with increasing tactical complexity. Students apply the same underlying principles from Grades 5–6 but with faster play, more players, and more sophisticated decision-making demands.

**Core tactical problems:**
- How do we exploit space and mismatches against a real defense?
- How do we organize our team on transition (attack → defense, defense → attack)?
- How do we adjust tactics mid-game when the initial plan isn't working?

---

## Sports in This Category

Soccer, basketball, hockey, floor hockey, ultimate frisbee, flag football, team handball, lacrosse, water polo, netball, rugby (touch)

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 7–9)

**Movement Skills**
- Execute manipulative skills (pass, receive, dribble, shoot) at game speed under defensive pressure
- Defensive positioning: between opponent and goal, deny passing lanes
- Transition: immediate role-switch on possession change

**Tactical Understanding**
- Recognize and exploit outnumbered situations (2v1, 3v2)
- Shift from direct to indirect play as defensive pressure increases
- Positional systems: understand roles within a team structure

**Skills and Procedures**
- Apply game category principles: recognize when a soccer tactic works in ultimate or floor hockey
- Peer coach: observe a teammate's decision-making and give specific feedback

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Leadership in team contexts — organize, encourage, adapt
- Manage competitive pressure without compromising fair play
- Include all teammates in tactical decision-making

---

## Grade Progression

### Grade 7
- Modified games (5v5–6v6), transition focus, 2v1 advantage situations

### Grade 8
- Larger teams, overloaded situations (defending outnumbered), organized positional roles

### Grade 9
- Near-full-game formats, peer coaching roles, student-led tactical adjustments

---

## Misconceptions to Address

- The fastest player should always be the primary scorer (positional understanding and off-ball movement beat athleticism at this level)
- Defense is reactive only (proactive positioning — anticipating the next pass — is the advanced defensive skill)
- Tactics are the coach's job (students at Gr 7–9 make tactical adjustments during play — build this as a skill)
