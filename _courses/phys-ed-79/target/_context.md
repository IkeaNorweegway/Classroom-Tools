# Unit Context — Target Games, Grades 7–9
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 7–9

---

## What This Is

Target games at Grades 7–9 add competitive strategy beyond personal accuracy — students make decisions about blocking, placement of their object relative to opponents, and risk/reward trade-offs.

**Core tactical problems:**
- When is accuracy the priority versus when is blocking or disrupting the opponent's target worth attempting?
- How do we manage focus and routine under competitive pressure?
- How do we adjust our approach based on the game state (score, opponent's position)?

---

## Sports in This Category

Curling (modified/full), disc golf, archery, bocce ball, golf (putting, chipping), billiards/pool, horseshoes, lawn bowling

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 7–9)

**Movement Skills**
- Consistent pre-shot routine across multiple attempts under pressure
- Fine force control — differentiate force output for short vs. long targets
- Adjust release/swing mechanics based on feedback from previous attempts

**Tactical Understanding**
- Guard/block: place object to protect your scoring position
- Risk assessment: high-risk shot vs. safe shot relative to game state
- Read the game: what does the opponent need? How do you deny it?

**Skills and Procedures**
- Track own performance data: identify pattern of misses and adjust
- Develop and articulate a personal pre-shot routine
- Peer coach: observe technique and give specific, corrective feedback

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Patient, focused atmosphere — do not distract during others' turns
- Honest scorekeeping in non-supervised competitive play
- Sportsmanship in close outcomes

---

## Grade Progression

### Grade 7
- Accuracy focus with simple competitive formats (bocce, curling basics)

### Grade 8
- Tactical decision-making: guard/block, risk assessment, shot selection

### Grade 9
- Full game formats (curling ends, disc golf rounds), self-regulated pre-shot routine, peer coaching
