# Unit Context — Striking/Fielding Games, Grades 7–9
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 7–9

---

## What This Is

Striking/fielding games at Grades 7–9 move toward more standard formats with strategic depth — fielding positions, batting order decisions, when to advance vs. hold.

**Core tactical problems:**
- Strikers: where do we hit to exploit the fielding configuration? When do we run?
- Fielders: how do we cover efficiently? Who covers which base? Where does the ball go next?
- Baserunners: how do we read the situation and make real-time decisions?

---

## Sports in This Category

Softball, baseball (modified), cricket (modified), rounders, Danish longball

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 7–9)

**Movement Skills**
- Batting: consistent swing mechanics, directional hitting intent
- Pitching/bowling: controlled delivery appropriate to the game format
- Fielding: glove work, throwing mechanics, relay plays
- Baserunning: read the fielder, not just the ball

**Tactical Understanding**
- Hitting to gaps — read where fielders are positioned
- Fielding alignment: shift based on batter tendencies
- Relay and cutoff plays: nobody covers alone
- Baserunning decisions: safe/advance reads based on fielder position and ball contact type

**Skills and Procedures**
- Play without constant teacher direction — teams make tactical decisions together
- Peer coach batting technique and fielding positioning

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Batting lineup management — fair rotation, support for all skill levels
- Call your own plays fairly
- Slow-paced turns require patience from teammates

---

## Grade Progression

### Grade 7
- Modified softball rules, focused on fielding communication and baserunning reads

### Grade 8
- Positional fielding (who covers which base), team defensive alignment, directional hitting

### Grade 9
- Near-standard rules, student-managed games, tactical self-adjustment
