# Unit Context — Net/Wall Games, Grades 7–9
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 7–9

---

## What This Is

Net/wall games at Grades 7–9 develop tactical sophistication beyond consistency — students read opponent positioning, manufacture opportunities, and construct points rather than waiting for errors.

**Core tactical problems:**
- How do we construct a point systematically (not just wait for the opponent to miss)?
- How do we read and anticipate opponent positioning to choose the right shot?
- How do we manage the pressure of serving and receiving under score?

---

## Sports in This Category

Volleyball, badminton, pickleball, tennis, table tennis, racquetball, squash, handball

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 7–9)

**Movement Skills**
- Execute send skills with directional intent at game speed (placement, not just clearance)
- Ready position and court coverage: shadow footwork, split-step timing
- Overhead clear, drop shot, drive — appropriate selection based on opponent position

**Tactical Understanding**
- Shot selection: attack from the back, defend at the net
- Force opponent out of position before going for the winner
- Serve as a tactical weapon — vary placement, pace, spin where appropriate

**Skills and Procedures**
- Select shot type based on own court position + opponent position
- Peer assessment: observe rally patterns and identify tactical strengths/weaknesses

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Honourable line calls at game speed
- Control frustration and maintain positive partnership communication

---

## Grade Progression

### Grade 7
- Consistent rally with directional intent; serve placement; basic shot selection

### Grade 8
- Positional play: force movement then attack; deceptive placement

### Grade 9
- Full game formats with tactical self-correction during play; serve-return tactics

---

## Misconceptions to Address

- Power always beats placement (deceptive placement against an opponent in position wins more often)
- The winner is hit from anywhere on the court (winning shots are set up — position first, then execute)
- Line calls are judgment calls (accurate line calling is a character and integrity skill, not a grey area)
