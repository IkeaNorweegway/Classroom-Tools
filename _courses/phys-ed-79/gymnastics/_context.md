# Unit Context — Gymnastics and Body Management, Grades 7–9
**Course:** Physical Education and Wellness | Alberta 2022 | **Grades:** 7–9

---

## What This Is

Gymnastics at Grades 7–9 extends body management into more complex sequences, partner/group work, and apparatus use where available. The emphasis remains on movement quality and safety — not competition.

**Core learning problems:**
- How do we link complex movements into sequences with smooth, controlled transitions?
- How do we safely support a partner through a movement they're uncertain about?
- How do we design a sequence that challenges our current ability without exceeding it?

---

## Skill Categories

- **Balances:** partner balances, counter-balance, group pyramids (with safe spotting)
- **Locomotion:** complex rolling sequences, cartwheels, round-offs (Gr 8–9)
- **Inversions:** headstand, handstand prep, cartwheels (Gr 7–8), round-off (Gr 9)
- **Flight:** jump combinations, controlled landing under fatigue
- **Sequences:** 6–10 skills with defined transitions, group synchronization

---

## Key Learning Outcomes (Grades 7–9)

**Movement Skills**
- Perform and land inversions with appropriate body alignment and spotter present
- Execute a 6+ skill sequence with smooth transitions and defined beginning/end
- Partner balance: shared weight with clear communication and mutual control

**Understanding**
- Biomechanical principles: centre of gravity, base of support, lever arms
- Trust and communication as physical skills in partner work

**Skills and Procedures**
- Design a sequence with deliberate movement element choices — not just what they can do
- Spot a partner through a new skill: support placement, when to release
- Peer assess a sequence using teacher-provided criteria

**Social and Personal Responsibility**
- Spotting is a responsibility — take it seriously
- Encourage without pressure — respect when a classmate says "I'm not ready for that skill"
- Feedback is specific and movement-based

---

## Safety — Non-Negotiable Rules

- Mats always in place for rolling, inversion, and jumping activities
- Spotting technique must be taught before any partner inversion or partner balance
- Inversions only after teacher demonstration and individual readiness check
- No pyramids above 2 levels without teacher supervision and clear role assignment

---

## Grade Progression

### Grade 7
- 4–6 skill sequences with partner balances; spotting introduced formally

### Grade 8
- 6–8 skill sequences; inversions with spotting; peer assessment using criteria

### Grade 9
- 8–10 skill sequences; student-designed with deliberate element choices; group synchronization option
