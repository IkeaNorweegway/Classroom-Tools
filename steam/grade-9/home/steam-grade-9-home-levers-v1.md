::: tip
**For the adult:** A lever has a pivot (fulcrum), an effort and a load. Moving the pivot changes how much force and distance you get.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A seesaw balances a 300 N child 1 m from the pivot. Where must a 150 N child sit to balance?**

1. To balance: force × distance on one side = force × distance on the other.
2. 300 N × 1 m = 300.
3. 150 N × distance = 300, so the distance is 2 m.
4. The lighter child sits twice as far from the pivot.
:::
