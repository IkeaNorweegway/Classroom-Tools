::: tip
**For the adult:** Triangles keep their shape when pushed. Squares fold into diamonds. That is why bridges and towers use triangles.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Push on a corner of a straw square and a straw triangle. What happens?**

1. The square folds into a diamond: it isn’t rigid.
2. The triangle keeps its shape: its sides can’t move without stretching or breaking.
3. So engineers build bridges and towers from triangles.
:::
