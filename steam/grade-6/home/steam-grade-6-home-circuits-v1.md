::: tip
**For the adult:** A circuit is a complete loop. If there is a gap anywhere, nothing works.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Why does a light only turn on when its switch is closed?**

1. The circuit is a loop: battery → switch → light → back to the battery.
2. A closed switch completes the loop, so electricity flows and the light turns on.
3. An open switch makes a gap, so nothing flows and the light is off.
:::
