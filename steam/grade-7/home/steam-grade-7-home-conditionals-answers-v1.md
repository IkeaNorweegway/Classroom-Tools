**For the adult:** For question 4, check the sign carefully: 5 is not greater than 5.
:::

**1.** the **if** part is **not true**

**2.** then **stop and turn** · else **keep moving forward**

**3.** If it is **dark**, then turn the **light on**; else turn it **off**.

**4.** **"Great!"** When score = 5, **"Try again"**, because 5 is not greater than 5.

**5.** A start box, a **diamond decision** ("Left door or right door?") and **two arrows**, each to a different scene.

**6.** Examples: **thermostat**, **automatic door**, **fridge light** (if door open, light on), **phone screen dimming**.
