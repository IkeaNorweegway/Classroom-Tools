**1.** **things keep moving (or stay still) unless a force acts** · **the same force moves a lighter object more** · **every push has an equal push back**

**2.** **air pushing against a moving object**, slowing it down

**3.** The **50 g** dragster. With the **same push, less mass speeds up more** (second law).

**4.** Examples: a **pointed, smooth shape**, **sanding and painting** smooth, **fewer bumps** sticking out.

**5.** (a) **third** (b) **first** (c) **second**

**6.** Your body **keeps moving forward** when the bus stops (**first law**), until something like a seatbelt or your feet stops you.
