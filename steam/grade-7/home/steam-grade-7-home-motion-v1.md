::: tip
**For the adult:** First law: things keep doing what they’re doing unless a force acts. Second: the same push moves a lighter object more. Third: every push has an equal push back.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Explain how a CO2 dragster moves using Newton’s laws.**

1. Third law: the cartridge pushes gas backward, and the gas pushes the car forward.
2. Second law: the same push speeds up a lighter car more.
3. First law: the car keeps rolling until friction and air drag slow it down.
:::
