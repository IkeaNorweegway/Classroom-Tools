::: tip
**For the adult:** "If ___ then ___ else ___" is how programs make decisions. The "else" part says what to do when the "if" isn’t true.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A thermostat keeps a room at 20 °C. Write its rule.**

1. The sensor measures the room temperature.
2. If the temperature is below 20 °C, then turn the heat on.
3. Else (it’s 20 °C or warmer), turn the heat off.
:::
