::: tip
**For the adult:** Question 6 is about the method: check one thing at a time, like batteries first, then the next thing.
:::

**1.** **a mistake that makes a program work wrongly** · **finding and fixing bugs**

**2.** **one**

**3.** The counter probably **starts at 0 instead of 1** (and stops one too early).

**4.** **make the bug happen again**, **find where it goes wrong**, **make one change**, **test the fix**

**5.** Saying each step out loud makes you **slow down and check** what the code really does, not what you meant it to do.

**6.** Example: check the **batteries**, then whether it’s **pointed at the TV**, then **restart** the TV, testing after each step.
