**1.** **lever, wheel and axle, pulley, inclined plane (ramp), wedge, screw**

**2.** **output force** ÷ **input force**

**3.** ramp **inclined plane** · scissors **lever** · flagpole rope **pulley** · doorknob **wheel and axle** · axe blade **wedge** · jar lid **screw**

**4.** 200 ÷ 50 = **4**
