::: tip
**For the adult:** Machines don’t make energy. They let you use a smaller force by moving it a longer distance.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A lever lets you lift a 60 N weight by pushing with 20 N. What is the mechanical advantage?**

1. Mechanical advantage = output force ÷ input force.
2. 60 ÷ 20 = 3.
3. The trade-off: you push your end about 3 times as far as the weight moves.
:::
