Guiding question

How can urbanization shift and shape a society?

Urbanization

More people live in cities, and the change reaches every part of society

Population

More diverse people, a pluralistic society

Economy

Growth in oil and gas, hydro, and manufacturing

Environment

Pollution, land division, and disrupted wildlife migration

Notebook key words

Pluralistic society

A more diverse population, with many cultures and backgrounds living together

Ask for three quick answers from the room. Write them on the board and leave them up all unit. Students will test them against the three rows of the diagram at the end. Have students copy the key word into their notebooks.
