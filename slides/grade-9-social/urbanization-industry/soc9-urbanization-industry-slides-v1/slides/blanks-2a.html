Fill in the blanks

Urbanization is the process by which a growing share of a population comes to live in (1) __________ rather than rural areas.

After the Second World War, Canada experienced a (2) __________ boom that, with returning soldiers and new immigrants, sharply increased demand for development.

Oil and gas exploration: the growth of the (3) __________ industry, especially in Alberta.

Answers

1. cities (or urban centres)

2. population

3. petroleum
