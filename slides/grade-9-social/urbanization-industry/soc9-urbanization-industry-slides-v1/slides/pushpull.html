Notebook key words

Push factor

A condition at home that drives someone to leave, such as war or persecution

Pull factor

A condition at the destination that draws someone there, such as safety or jobs

Displaced person

A refugee left homeless or unable to safely return home

Ask: for a Holocaust survivor arriving in 1948, what was the push, and what was the pull? Most people experience both at once. Students copy the three key words into their notebooks.
