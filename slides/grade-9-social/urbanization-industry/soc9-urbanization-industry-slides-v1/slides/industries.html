What postwar urbanization built

Public infrastructure

Roads, sewers, schools, and utilities for growing suburbs

Oil and gas

Exploration and refining, especially in Alberta

Hydroelectricity

Dams and generating stations for new homes and factories

Automobiles and paper

Vehicles for a car-dependent population, and paper for a consumer society

Notebook key words

Public infrastructure

Roads, sewers, schools, and utilities built to support growing suburbs and populations

Petroleum industry

Drilling for and refining oil and gas, which grew strongly in Alberta

Have students match each industry to the reason it grew. Ask which of these would have grown even without cities. Most will say infrastructure only. Students copy the two key words into their notebooks.
