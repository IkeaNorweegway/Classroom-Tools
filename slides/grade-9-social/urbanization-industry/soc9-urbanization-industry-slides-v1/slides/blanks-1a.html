Notes · Section 1: Push and pull factors

Fill in the blanks

A push factor is a condition in a person’s home country that makes them want to (1) __________.

A pull factor is a condition in the destination country that makes it (2) __________.

After the Second World War (1945–51), displaced persons arrived in Canada: refugees, including (3) __________, who had lost their homes, families, and countries during the war.

Answers

1. leave

2. attractive (or appealing)

3. Holocaust survivors

Give students a minute to fill the three blanks from memory, then click to show the answers. For blank 3, accept other refugees who could not return home, but name Holocaust survivors.
