Common misconception

The environmental costs came later, with modern industry. In fact, pollution, land division, and habitat loss were already visible in the 1950s and 60s.

Notebook key word

Urbanization

The process by which more of a population comes to live in cities rather than rural areas

Ask students which of the four impacts they can see near them today. Then ask whether the misconception held because the costs were less discussed at the time. Students copy the key word into their notebooks.
