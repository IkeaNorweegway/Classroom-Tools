## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admission requirements** | Courses and grades you need to get into a program | Math 30-1 and Chemistry 30 | A school's colours |
| **Competitive admission** | More people apply than there are seats | Only the top averages get in | Everyone who applies gets in |
| **Credit** | A unit you earn by passing a high school course | Math 10C = 5 credits | A grade on a test |
| **Prerequisite** | A course you must pass before taking another | Math 20-1 before Math 30-1 | An elective you pick for fun |
| **Scholarship** | Money for marks, talent or leadership; not paid back | Rutherford Scholarship | A loan |
| **Bursary** | Money for students who need it; not paid back | A community bursary | A paycheque |
| **Grant** | Money, usually from government; not paid back | A student grant | A credit card |
| **Networking** | Building relationships to share information and support | Talking to your coach's brother, an electrician | Following a celebrity online |
