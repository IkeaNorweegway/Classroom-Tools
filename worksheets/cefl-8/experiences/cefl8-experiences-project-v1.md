## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Representation** | Who is in a career compared with the population | Women are 4.8% of electrical apprentices | How many jobs exist |
| **Under-represented** | A group with a much smaller share than its share of the population | Men in nursing | A group that's equally present |
| **Equity** | Fair access so everyone has a real chance | Welcoming workplaces for all | Giving everyone the same shoe size |
| **Role model** | Someone whose path shows what's possible | A woman electrician visiting class | A cartoon character |
| **Growth mindset** | Believing skills grow with practice | "I'm not good at this *yet*" | "I'm just bad at this" |
| **Employability skills** | Skills that help in any job | Communication, teamwork | Knowing one machine |
| **Risk management** | Planning to lower the chance or cost of things going wrong | Ordering 15 shirts, not 50 | Hoping for the best |
