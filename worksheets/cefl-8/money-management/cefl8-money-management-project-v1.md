## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed cost** | A cost that stays the same each month | $35 phone plan | Food out |
| **Variable cost** | A cost that changes with your choices | Snacks, clothes | Rent |
| **Principal** | The money you start with (or borrow) | $500 deposited | The interest earned |
| **Interest rate** | The percent earned or charged per year | 3% a year | A dollar amount |
| **Simple interest** | Interest on the principal only | 500 × 0.03 × 4 = $60 | Interest on interest |
| **Compound interest** | Interest on the principal and past interest | $500 → $562.75 in 4 years at 3% | The same amount every year |
| **Investing** | Putting money into something that may grow (or lose) | An investment fund | A piggy bank |
| **Risk** | The chance an investment loses value | A fund drops 10% in a year | A locked GIC |
