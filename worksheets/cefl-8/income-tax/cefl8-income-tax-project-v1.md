## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tax bracket** | A slice of income taxed at one rate | First $58,523 at 14% (federal, 2026) | Your whole income at one rate |
| **Basic personal amount** | Income everyone can earn tax-free | $16,452 (federal, 2026) | A bonus |
| **Progressive tax** | Higher incomes pay a bigger share | Canada's income tax | GST |
| **Flat tax** | Everyone pays the same rate | 15% for all | Brackets |
| **Regressive tax** | Takes a bigger share of low incomes | GST, as a share of income | Income tax brackets |
| **Average tax rate** | Total tax ÷ income | 17.4% on $70,000 | The top bracket rate |
| **Total compensation** | Salary plus benefits, bonuses and other extras | $48,000 + benefits + bonus | Salary only |
| **Social contract** | People pay taxes; governments provide shared services | Taxes fund hospitals | A phone contract |
