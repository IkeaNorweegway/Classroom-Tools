## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Labour market** | All the people looking for work and all the employers looking for workers | Alberta's jobs and workers | One store |
| **Economic cycle** | Times of growth (boom) and slowdown (bust) | Oil prices falling in 2015 | A store sale |
| **Career resilience** | Being able to adapt to change without losing your career | Retraining after a layoff | Never changing jobs |
| **Job security** | Staying in one role for a long time | 20 years at one plant | Finding a new job fast |
| **Career security** | Being able to find new work quickly | Skills that fit many jobs | One job only |
| **Human capital** | Your education, knowledge, skills and experience | A certificate + teamwork skills | Your bank account |
| **Networking** | Building relationships with people who can share ideas and opportunities | Keeping in touch with a coach | Social media likes |
| **Milestone** | A smaller step toward a big goal | Finish Math 10C | The final goal |
