## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Work-integrated learning** | Learning that mixes school with real work | RAP, work experience | A school assembly |
| **Entrepreneurship** | Starting and running your own venture | A bike repair service | Working at a chain store |
| **Break-even** | When income has paid back the costs | 8 tune-ups | The first sale |
| **Fixed cost** | A cost you pay once or every month, no matter how much you sell | $150 of tools | Parts for each tune-up |
| **Formal learning** | Learning from courses or training | A first-aid course | Learning to cook from a parent |
| **Informal learning** | Learning on your own or from others outside courses | Teaching yourself video editing | A certificate program |
| **Cover letter** | A short letter explaining why you fit a job | "I'm applying for…" | A résumé |
| **Digital identity** | What people can find about you online | Your public posts | Your locker combination |
