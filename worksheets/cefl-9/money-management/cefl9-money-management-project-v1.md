## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Debt** | Money borrowed that must be repaid, usually with interest | $2,400 on a credit card | Money in savings |
| **Credit** | The ability to borrow or buy now and pay later | A $2,500 credit limit | Cash |
| **Minimum payment** | The smallest payment allowed each month | 2.5% of the balance or $10 | Paying in full |
| **Credit score** | A 300–900 number showing how you've handled credit | 760 | Your bank balance |
| **Credit utilization** | Balance ÷ limit | $900 of $1,000 = 90% | Your interest rate |
| **Avalanche method** | Pay extra on the highest-interest debt first | Credit card first | Smallest balance first |
| **Snowball method** | Pay extra on the smallest balance first | Phone first | Highest rate first |
| **Predatory lending** | Very costly loans aimed at people short on money | $42 to borrow $300 for 2 weeks | A 9.5% line of credit |
| **Debt-to-income ratio** | Monthly debt payments ÷ monthly gross income | $170 ÷ $3,000 ≈ 5.7% | Your credit score |
