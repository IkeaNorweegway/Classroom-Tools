## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T4 slip** | A summary of a year's pay and deductions from an employer | Box 14: $18,000 | A paycheque stub |
| **Tax return** | The yearly form that works out the tax you really owe | Filed with the CRA | A store refund |
| **Refund** | Your own money back when too much tax was taken off | $244.80 back | A government gift |
| **Deduction** | Lowers the income that is taxed | RRSP contribution | Tuition credit |
| **Credit** | Lowers the tax you owe | 14% of tuition | RRSP contribution |
| **RRSP** | Retirement account: tax break now, taxed when withdrawn | $1,500 in, deducted | A TFSA |
| **TFSA** | Account where growth and withdrawals are tax-free | $7,000 limit (2026) | An RRSP |
| **Self-employed** | Working for yourself, with no employer | Gig delivery driver | A café worker with a T4 |
