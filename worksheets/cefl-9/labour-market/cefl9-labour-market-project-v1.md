## How you will be marked

**Outcome 9-LM:** I can take steps to succeed in a changing labour market.

Your teacher gives each row a level: **4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet.** Meets means you met the outcome.

## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Automation** | Technology doing tasks people used to do | Self-driving haul trucks | Hiring more drivers |
| **Globalization** | Labour markets and businesses connecting across countries | Hiring a designer overseas | A local bake sale |
| **Growing industry** | An industry adding jobs over time | Alberta health care, +15% (2022–2025) | One good month |
| **Human capital** | Your education, skills and experience | First aid + coaching | Your phone |
| **Résumé** | A one-page summary of your human capital for an employer | Leo's résumé | A diary |
| **Personal brand statement** | A short line about who you are and what you offer | "Reliable, upbeat, first-aid certified" | Your favourite food |
| **STAR** | Situation, Task, Action, Result: a way to answer interview questions | The goalie rotation story | "I'm a hard worker." |
| **Self-advocacy** | Clearly explaining your skills, value and needs | Asking for shifts that fit your schedule | Saying nothing |
