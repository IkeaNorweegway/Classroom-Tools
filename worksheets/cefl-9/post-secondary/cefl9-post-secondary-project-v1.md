## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student loan** | Money borrowed for school, repaid later | Federal + Alberta loan | A grant |
| **Interest-free** | No interest charged | The federal part of a Canada Student Loan | A credit card |
| **Prime rate** | A bank's base interest rate; Alberta student loans use it | Sample 4.5% | A fixed fee |
| **Return on investment (ROI)** | What a pathway gives back compared with what it costs | Payback in 2.5 years | Tuition alone |
| **Work-integrated learning** | Learning that mixes school and real work | Co-op, internship, apprenticeship | A summer vacation |
| **Credential recognition** | Whether your qualification is accepted somewhere else | An Alberta nursing licence in BC | Your report card |
| **Contingency** | A backup plan | Practical nursing if RN is full | Giving up |
| **Rationale** | The reasons behind a decision | Fits my interests; nurses are in demand | "Because" |
