**For the adult:** Every answer is different. Look for these signs of a strong plan.
:::

## What strong answers look like

**1.** Questions about technology or the future ("What if school was online-only on Mars?"), not just magic ("What if I could fly?").

**2–3.** Both a benefit and a problem: the problem is where the story comes from.

**4.** A character with a real, hard choice ("tell her parents or fix it herself").

**5.** A flash-forward that shows a consequence of the choice.

**6.** Questions from the adult help your child find gaps before writing.
