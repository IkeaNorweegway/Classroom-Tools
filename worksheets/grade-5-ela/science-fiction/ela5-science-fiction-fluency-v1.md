::: teacher How to use this pack
**Signal from the Future** (Weeks 2–3, performed in Lesson 15) is a radio drama: no costumes, only voices and sound effects, so expression carries everything. Assign a sound-effects team (tapping, a whistle, crumpled paper, a fan). Rehearse 10–15 minutes a day; record and play it back.
**Repeated reading** (fluency group): the passages below, then leveled fiction at about 95% accuracy. **Partner reading** continues; "paragraph shrinking" for fiction: who, and the most important thing that happened, in 10 words or less.
:::

## Signal from the Future: a radio drama

**Parts:** Narrator · Ava (age 11) · Ben (age 10, her brother) · Radio Voice (Ava, 40 years older) · Mom · Sound-effects team

::: script Signal from the Future
SOUND: (wind howling; a door creaks)
NARRATOR: The attic of the old farmhouse was dusty and dark, and in the corner sat a radio older than anyone could remember.
BEN: (whispering) Ava, Mom said not to touch anything up here.
AVA: I'm just looking. Help me with this switch.
SOUND: (a click; static: shhhhhhh)
NARRATOR: The radio crackled to life. For a moment there was only static. Then, a voice.
RADIO VOICE: (crackly) Hello? Is this… is this the attic on Pine Ridge Road?
AVA: (shocked) Who is this?
RADIO VOICE: Ava. It's me. It's you. I'm calling from the year 2066.
BEN: (gasps) That's… forty years from now!
RADIO VOICE: Listen carefully, I don't have much time. Tomorrow, the town will vote on whether to cut down Miller's Woods to build a parking lot.
AVA: I know. Everybody says it doesn't matter.
RADIO VOICE: It matters. In my time, the woods are gone, and the creek is dry, and the birds… (static) …you won't hear them anymore.
SOUND: (static rises, then fades)
BEN: Ava, what are we supposed to do? We're kids.
RADIO VOICE: Kids can speak. Go to the meeting. Tell them what the woods mean to you. One voice can—
SOUND: (static; a long beep)
NARRATOR: And then the radio went silent.
MOM: (calling from downstairs) Ava! Ben! Dinner!
AVA: (slowly) Ben… what if it was real?
BEN: What if it wasn't?
AVA: Then we go to a meeting for nothing. (pause) But what if it was?
NARRATOR: The next evening, the town hall was packed. And when the mayor asked if anyone else wished to speak, a hand went up near the back. A small hand.
AVA: (nervous, then stronger) My name is Ava. I'm eleven. Miller's Woods is where I learned the names of birds…
NARRATOR: Forty years later, in a house on Pine Ridge Road, a woman sat by a window, listening to the birds in a forest that was still standing. She reached over and switched off an old radio. And she smiled.
SOUND: (birdsong)
:::

**Rehearsal checklist:** ☐ My voice shows my character's feelings ☐ I pause where the script says ☐ Sound effects come at the right moment ☐ I slow down at the most important line

## How did it sound? (expression scale)

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expression and volume** | Flat, quiet | Some expression | Mostly matches the meaning | Builds suspense; voices for characters |
| **Phrasing** | Word by word | Two- or three-word chunks | Mostly good phrases | Smooth; dramatic pauses |
| **Smoothness** | Many stops | Some long pauses | A few rough spots | Smooth |
| **Pace** | Slow and laboured | Uneven | Mostly like talking | Slows down for tense moments |
