::: tip
**For the adult:** The **theme** of a story is its message about life. Students often answer with one word ("friendship"); that's the **topic**. A theme is a sentence that says something *about* the topic ("Real friends tell you the truth, even when it's hard"). Use any story, movie or show you both know.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Story: *The Tortoise and the Hare***

1. **Topic (one word):** effort
2. **What does the main character learn or show?** The slow tortoise keeps going and wins; the fast hare stops to nap and loses.
3. **Theme (a sentence):** Steady effort can beat talent that doesn't try.
:::
