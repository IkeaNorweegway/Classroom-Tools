## Cold read 1 · The Last Paper Book

::: passage The Last Paper Book | A new story

It is the year 2110. People read stories that float in the air.

Eli's grandpa has old **paper** books.

Grandpa says, "Stories in the air can be changed. Paper books stay the same."

Eli reads a paper book. Then he reads the same story in the air. The ending is different! Someone changed it.

Eli feels upset. He asks Grandpa, "Can you teach me to keep stories safe?"

Grandpa smiles. "We copy them by hand."
:::

**1.** What year is it? ___ . Is that the future? **Yes / No**

**2.** Who tells the story? Circle. Eli says "I." · A narrator says "Eli" and "he."

**3.** At first, Eli thinks paper books are ___ .

(write 1)

**4.** Does Eli change? **Yes / No**

**5.** Eli feels ___ when he sees the new ending.

(write 1)

**6.** What does the story teach? Circle. Keep the truth safe. · Paper is boring.

**7.** Why did someone change the story? I think ___ .

(write 1)

## Cold read 2 · The Seed

::: passage The Seed | A new story

Rosa lives on **Mars**. She loves the plants in the greenhouse. Her favourite is a little cactus.

A storm breaks the water pipe. There is no water for the plants. The plants die.

But the cactus does not die. It keeps water inside. Then it grows a pink flower!

*Forty years later, Rosa makes the first big garden on Mars. All the plants come from her little cactus.*
