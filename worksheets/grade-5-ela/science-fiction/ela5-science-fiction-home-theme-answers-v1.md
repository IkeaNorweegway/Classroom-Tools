::: tip
**For the adult:** Many themes can fit one story; what matters is that it's a full sentence and backed by a moment from the story.
:::

## What strong answers look like

**2.** One word (friendship, honesty, courage, family).

**3.** What changes: "At the start, she lies to fit in; at the end, she tells the truth."

**4.** A full sentence about the topic: "Being honest is better than fitting in." Not just "honesty."

**5.** A specific moment, not a summary: "When she admits she broke the vase, her friend forgives her."

**6.** Two different themes can both be right if each is supported by the story.
