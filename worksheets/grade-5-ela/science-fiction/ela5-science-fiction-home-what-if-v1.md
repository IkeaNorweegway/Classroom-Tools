::: tip
**For the adult:** Science fiction starts with a "**What if?**" question about technology or the future, then asks how it would change people's lives and choices. Your child is planning stories this way in class. Brainstorm together: the sillier the starting idea, the better, as long as there's a real choice in it.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**What if your fridge could decide what you're allowed to eat?**

1. **Good about it:** you'd eat healthier.
2. **Could go wrong:** it locks the ice cream on your birthday.
3. **A character and a choice:** Mo, age ten, finds out how to hack the fridge. Does he?
4. **What it shows:** sometimes rules help us, and sometimes we need to choose for ourselves.
:::
