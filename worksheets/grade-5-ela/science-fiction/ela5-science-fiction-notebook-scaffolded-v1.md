Use **Anchor note 2**.

**How do you know Story 1 is told in the third person?** Give two clues.

::: frame

:::

(lines 3)

=> The narrator uses the character's name (Maya) and "she/her," never "I." Example: "Maya hesitated" (¶7).

**Rewrite** paragraph 12 of Story 1 in the first person, as if Maya were telling it. Start: *I walked home slowly…*

(lines 4)

## Lesson 6 · Setting and context: Under the Dome

Read Story 2. Use **Anchor note 4**.

| Setting detail | What it tells us about life in 2091 | ¶ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✎ "Twenty-two degrees and sunny" every day | The weather is controlled; it never changes | 1 |
| [[Theo has never been cold or wet]] | [[People are completely protected from nature]] | [[2]] |
| [[The storms outside are wild and sudden]] | [[The dome was built for safety after the climate changed]] | [[5]] |

**Write about it.** Why do Theo's father and Mémère react so differently to the open panel? Use the context.

::: frame
Theo's father ___ because ___ . Mémère ___ because ___ .
:::

(lines 5)

=> Theo's father has always lived safely under the dome and thinks of outside weather as dangerous, so he stays home ("Why would anyone want to be cold?"). Mémère was born before the dome and remembers snow days as the best of her life, so she goes and laughs in the snow.

## Lesson 9 · Flash-forward: Story 4

Read Story 4. Use **Anchor note 6**.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| ✎ Where does the flash-forward begin? | "Sixty years later…" (¶9) |
| How do you know the time changed? | [[Time phrase; italics; Jamal is an old man]] |
| What does it show? | [[His filter design now cleans water around the world]] |
| Where does the story come back? | [["Back in 2027…" (¶11)]] |

**Write about it.** Why do you think the author put the flash-forward *before* the ending instead of at the very end?

(lines 4)

=> So the reader knows what Jamal's choice will lead to while Jamal doesn't; it makes his calm feeling at the end ("he didn't mind at all") feel meaningful, and the "Sent" ending makes sense.

## Lesson 10 · Predict and infer

Before reading the last two paragraphs of Story 4, answer:

**Predict:** What will happen to the message on the old phone?

(lines 2)

**Read the ending. Confirm or revise** your prediction. *(CO7)*

(lines 2)

**Infer.** Who sent the message from 2087, and why did the phone say "Sent" at the end? *(CO6)*

::: frame
I think ___ sent it. The phone says "Sent" because ___ .
:::

(lines 4)

=> Old Jamal sent it ("a message I had to send," ¶10). "Sent" means the message has now been sent back in time from his future, so the loop is complete.
