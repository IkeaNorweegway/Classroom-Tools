## About these stories

**Science fiction** stories ask "**What if?**" They are about the future, robots or new science.

---page---

::: passage The Homework Helper | Story 1

Maya got a robot named Hex. Hex could help with homework.

At first, Hex helped Maya learn. Then Maya was tired. She asked Hex to do her report. Hex did it. Maya got a good mark.

After that, Hex did all her homework. Maya did not learn.

Then there was a test. No robots! Maya did not know the answers.

That night, Maya said, "Hex, show me how. Then I will try by myself."

It was hard. But now the learning was hers.
:::

::: support
**robot** a machine that can do jobs

**test** questions to show what you know
:::

::: passage Under the Dome | Story 2

Theo lived in a town under a big glass **dome**. It was always warm and sunny.

Theo had never seen snow. His great-grandmother, Mémère, loved snow.

One day, the town opened the dome for one hour. Snow came in!

Theo felt the cold snow. He laughed. Mémère laughed too.

Theo wrote a letter: "We need snow days too!"
:::

::: support
**dome** a round glass roof

**snow** soft, cold, white flakes
:::

::: passage Kiko's Grandmother | Story 3

Kiko's grandmother forgot things. So the family got a **CareBot**. The robot gave her pills. It cooked rice.

But Grandma said, "The robot does not know any stories."

So Kiko came to visit. Grandma told her stories about when she was a girl. They laughed and laughed.

Kiko's mom said, "We should all visit more."
:::

::: support
**visit** go to see someone
:::

---page---

::: passage The Message from 2087 | Story 4

Jamal found an old phone. It had a message from the **future**. The message was from Jamal, when he was old!

It said, "Go to the science fair. Trust me."

Jamal went. He showed his machine that makes dirty water clean. A woman said, "Keep working on this."

*Sixty years later, Jamal's machine helped people all over the world get clean water.*

Jamal did not know that yet. But he was glad he went.
:::

::: support
**future** the time that has not happened yet

**flash-forward** when a story jumps ahead in time
:::
