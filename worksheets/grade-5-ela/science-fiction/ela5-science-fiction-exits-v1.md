::: card Ticket 1 · Lesson 5 · Science fiction and theme
Name ____________________

**1.** Give two things that make *The Homework Helper* science fiction. *(TF7)*

(lines 2)

=> Any two: a "what if?" (a robot does your homework); technology that doesn't exist yet (Hex); a changed world (every student gets one); the technology changes a choice.

**2.** Write the theme of *The Homework Helper* as a **sentence**. *(TF9)*

(lines 2)

=> e.g., Shortcuts can take away the learning that makes you proud.
