## 1 · What is science fiction?

::: pair
**I can** tell what science fiction is.

Science fiction asks "**What if?**"

It has **robots**, **new machines** or **the future**.

*What if a robot did your homework?*

**Check:** Circle the science fiction idea.

A dog finds a bone. · A school bus flies to the Moon.
|||
**future**
the time that has not happened yet

**robot**
a machine that can do jobs
:::

## 2 · Who tells the story?

::: pair
**I can** tell who is telling the story.

**I** found a phone. (I am in the story.)

**Jamal** found a phone. **He** was surprised. (Someone else tells it.)

We write with **he**, **she**, **they** and names.
|||
**narrator**
the one who tells the story
:::

---page---

## 3 · What the story teaches

::: pair
**I can** tell what a story teaches.

The **theme** is what the story teaches us.

*Maya let the robot do her work. Then she did not learn.*

The story teaches: **doing it yourself helps you learn.**
|||
**theme**
the lesson or message
:::

---page---

## 4 · When and where

::: pair
**I can** tell how the place changes the story.

Theo lives under a glass dome. It is always warm.

So he has **never** seen [[snow]].
|||
**setting**
when and where a story happens
:::

---page---

## 5 · Characters who change

::: pair
**I can** tell if a character changes.

**Maya** changes. At first she lets the robot do her work. At the end she learns by herself.

**Hex** the robot does **not** change.
|||
**change**
become different
:::

---page---

## 6 · Jumping ahead

::: pair
**I can** find where a story jumps ahead.

A **flash-forward** jumps to the future.

*Sixty years later…*

These words tell me the story jumped [[ahead]].
|||
**flash-forward**
when a story jumps ahead in time
:::

---page---

## 7 · My plan

**I can** plan my story.

::: strategy WWW
**W** **Who**?
**W** **When**? (the future!)
**W** **Where**?
**What** **What** do they want?
**What** **What** happens?
**How** **How** does it end?
**How** **How** do they feel?
:::

---page---

## 8 · Show the future

::: pair
**I can** show the future with details.

Don't say: *It was the future.*

Show it: *The door opened when it saw my face.*

**Check:** Draw something from the future.

(box 4)
|||
**detail**
a small part that helps you picture it
:::
