## 1 · What is a poem?

::: pair
**I can** tell what a lyric poem is.

A **lyric poem** shows a **feeling**.

Poems are written in **lines**.

Read a poem **out loud**. Read it two times.

**Check:** How does *Who Has Seen the Wind?* make you feel? Draw a face.

(box 3)
|||
**poem**
writing in lines that shows a feeling

**feeling**
like happy, calm, sad
:::

## 3 · Word pictures

::: pair
**I can** tell what a comparison means.

**Like** or **as** compares two things.

*The meadowlark goes off like a kettle.*

The bird's song is loud and high, like a kettle whistling.

**Check:** My breath is like a ___ .

(write 1)
|||
**compare**
show how two things are alike
:::

## 4 · Sounds

::: pair
**I can** hear sounds in a poem.

**Rhyme:** words that sound the same at the end: *dip, flip, slip*.

**Repeat:** *Swirl, swirl!*

**Sound words:** *crash, splash*

**Check:** Write a word that rhymes with *crash*: ___

(write 1)
|||
**rhyme**
words that end with the same sound
:::

## 6 · Learn a poem by heart

**I can** say a poem without looking.

1. Read it many times.
2. Learn one stanza at a time.
3. Cover it. Say it. Check it.
4. Say it with a big, clear voice.
