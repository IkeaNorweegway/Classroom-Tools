::: tip
**For the adult:** Poems use comparisons to make us picture and feel things. A **simile** compares with *like* or *as* ("the creek is like a silver thread"); a **metaphor** says one thing *is* another ("hope is the thing with feathers"). Your child is learning to explain **what a comparison means**, not just name it. Ask, "What does that make you picture?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**"My breath hangs in front of me like a word I haven't said yet."**

1. **What two things are compared?** Breath in cold air, and a word you haven't said.
2. **What do they have in common?** Both hang there, waiting.
3. **How does it feel?** Quiet, thoughtful, a little lonely.
:::
