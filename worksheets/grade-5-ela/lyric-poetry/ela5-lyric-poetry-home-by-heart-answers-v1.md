## Signs of strong practice

**2.** Short practice on several days, not one long session.

**3.** A strategy for hard lines: a picture, a rhyme, or linking it to the line before.

**4.** A reason that comes from meaning ("I stress *never* because the poem is saying hope never gives up").

**5–6.** Specific praise and one specific next step (louder, slower at the end, look up).
