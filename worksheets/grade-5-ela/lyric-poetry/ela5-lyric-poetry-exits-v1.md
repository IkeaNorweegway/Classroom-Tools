::: card Ticket 1 · Lesson 5 · Lyric poems, stanzas and sound
Name ____________________

**1.** What is a lyric poem? What is a stanza? *(TF16, TF2)*

(lines 2)

=> A short poem that expresses the speaker's feelings; a stanza is a group of lines about a similar idea.

**2.** Choose one line from *The Song My Paddle Sings*. How does its sound match what's happening? *(TF15)*

(lines 3)

=> e.g., "Dash, dash, / With a mighty crash" sounds loud and fast, like the crashing rapids.
:::
