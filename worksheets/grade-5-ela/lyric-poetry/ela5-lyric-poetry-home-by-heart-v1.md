::: tip
**For the adult:** Your child is memorizing a poem to recite at our class Poetry Café. Practising a little every day works much better than a lot the night before, because the brain stores memories while we sleep. Be the audience: listen, give the next word only if they're really stuck, and tell them one thing they did well.
:::

## How to practise

::: example
1. **Read it aloud** together and talk about what it means.
2. **Picture each line** like a movie.
3. **One stanza at a time.** Say stanza 1 until it's easy; then stanzas 1 and 2; then the whole poem.
4. **Cover and say.** Cover the poem, say it, then check.
5. **Say it with feeling.** Stress the important words. Pause where the meaning pauses (not at every line end).
:::
