## Week 3

**Figurative language (VO13–15).** Write what each means.

| Line | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "the cold sat on us / like a dog that would not move" | [[simile]] | [[The cold stayed for a long time and felt heavy]] |
| "the creek is a silver thread" | [[metaphor]] | [[The creek looks thin and shiny far below]] |
| "August is laughing across the sky" | [[personification]] | [[The month feels bright and happy]] |

**Your turn.** Write one simile and one metaphor about snow.

(lines 2)

**Punctuation for effect (CV3).** Poets use punctuation to control the reader's voice. How does each change how you read?

*Swirl, swirl!* · *Sway, sway,* · *Yet, never, in extremity,*

(lines 3)

=> The exclamation mark makes "Swirl, swirl!" loud and excited; the commas in "Sway, sway," make it slow and gentle; the commas in Dickinson's line make you pause on each word, so "never" gets extra weight.
