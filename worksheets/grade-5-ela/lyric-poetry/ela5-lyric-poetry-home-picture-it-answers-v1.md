::: tip
**For the adult:** For 3–6, any comparison works if the two things really share something and the comparison helps you picture it.
:::

## Answers and examples

**1.** Metaphor. The lake is so calm and smooth that it reflects things perfectly.

**2.** A loud, high, bright whistle, like a kettle when the water boils.

**3.** Example: The clouds are like torn cotton balls.

**4.** Example: The clouds are torn cotton balls.

**5.** Example: Nervous is a bee buzzing in my stomach.

**6.** Your child should notice whether you pictured what they meant; if not, the comparison can be made more precise.
