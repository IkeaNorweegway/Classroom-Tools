## 1 · A script

::: pair
**I can** read a script.

The **name** tells who talks.

**OWEN:** Is this mine?

Words in **( )** tell you what to **do**. Don't read them out loud. Do them!

**(pointing)** = point your finger.
|||
**script** a story to act out

**line** what a character says
:::

## 2 · Characters

::: pair
**I can** tell what a character is like.

**TORTOISE:** (slowly) Just… keep… going.

Tortoise is **calm** and **slow**.

**character**
a person or animal in the play

## 4 · Body and voice

::: pair
**I can** use my body and voice.

**Body:** face, hands, how I stand.

**Voice:** loud or soft, fast or slow.

Stand tall. Breathe. Speak to the **back** of the room.
|||
**project**
speak loud and clear
:::
