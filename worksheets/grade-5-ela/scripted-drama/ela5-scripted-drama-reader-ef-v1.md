::: teacher How to use Edition C
Shorter versions of Plays 1 and 2 with the same characters and events, for decoding and fluency practice. Script lines are short and repeatable, which makes them excellent repeated reading. In group rehearsals, Edition C students can read these lines while others use Edition A; the scenes still fit together.
:::

## About scripts

A **script** is a story people act out. The **name** tells you who talks. Words in **( )** tell you what to do.

---page---

::: script The Lost Lunch
PRIYA: (sad) My lunch is gone!
MARCUS: Don't worry. I will find it.
PRIYA: It is a green box with a dinosaur on it.
JADA: (eating) Hi! What's wrong?
MARCUS: Jada, did you take the lunch?
JADA: No way! I have my own lunch.
MS. LAM: Owen, come here, please.
OWEN: (holding a green box) Is this mine?
MARCUS: (pointing) The dinosaur!
OWEN: I have a dinosaur box too! Look!
PRIYA: (laughing) There are two boxes!
OWEN: I ate one. It was so good.
PRIYA: You can have one more.
MARCUS: Case closed!
:::

::: support
**script** a story to act out

**( )** tells you how to act
:::

::: script The Rematch
HARE: (bouncing) I am fast. I will win!
TORTOISE: (slowly) Good… morning.
OWL: Ready. Set. GO!
HARE: (running) See you later, Tortoise!
HARE: (stopping, sad) What if I lose again?
TORTOISE: (slowly) Just… keep… going.
HARE: Let's go together!
OWL: It's a tie!
TORTOISE: Slow… and steady…
HARE: …and a friend helps!
:::

::: support
**tortoise** a slow animal with a shell

**hare** a fast animal like a rabbit
:::
