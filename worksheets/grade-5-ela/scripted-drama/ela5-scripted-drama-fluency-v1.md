::: teacher How to use this pack
The whole unit is fluency practice: rehearsing a script is repeated reading with a reason. Use the **warm-ups** daily (3–5 minutes) before rehearsal. Students who need fluency support rehearse their lines with you or a partner using repeated reading and feedback, then the **cue cards** help them come in on time. Score performances with the scale on the last page.
:::

## Daily vocal warm-ups

**1. Breath (body):** Hand on your belly. Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out on a long "sssss" for 8. Your belly should move, not your shoulders.

**2. Articulation (tongue twisters).** Say each three times, faster each time, every sound clear:

- Red lorry, yellow lorry.
- Six slippery snails slid slowly seaward.
- Big Joe's boots bounced back.
- Hare's hairy hat hangs high.

**3. Pitch slide:** say "Who has seen the wind?" starting very low and ending very high, then the other way.

**4. Projection:** stand at the back wall. Say "Case closed!" to a partner at the front without shouting: breath, body, energy.

**5. Emotion switch:** say "I found your lunch" as: excited · guilty · bored · suspicious · terrified.

## Line practice: one line, many meanings

Practise each line with a partner. Change **only your voice and body**. Your partner guesses the feeling.

| Line | Try it as… |
| --- | --- |
| What are you eating? | suspicious · curious · disgusted · hungry |
| It's a tie. | confused · thrilled · disappointed · bored |
| The river is frozen. | scared · amazed · worried · matter-of-fact |
| Just keep going. | calm · impatient · encouraging · exhausted |

## Performance scale

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Verbal: clear words** | Hard to understand | Mostly clear | Clear | Clear, even at speed |
| **Paraverbal: voice** | Flat; hard to hear | Some expression | Voice shows the character's feelings | Pace, pitch, volume and pauses all on purpose |
| **Non-verbal: body** | Frozen or turned away | Some movement | Face and gestures match the character | Whole body is the character; reacts when others speak |
| **Teamwork: cues** | Missed many cues | Some late entrances | On time | On time, and listens and reacts to others |
