## The rubric

| Criterion | Has yet to meet | Meets | Proficient | Excellent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. My scene: script form** *(WR2, CV2, CV3)* | Written like a story; hard to perform | Character names and dialogue; some stage directions | Setting, names with colons, no quotation marks, useful stage directions in brackets | Correct form throughout; punctuation and capitals guide the actors' delivery |
| **2. My scene: story and character** *(WR12, WR16, TF19)* | The scene doesn't move the story | The scene has events and fits the play | Events move the story; lines show who each character is | Lines and actions reveal character and build to a strong last line |
| **3. My performance** *(OL3, OL4, TF18)* | Hard to hear or follow | Lines said clearly, with some expression | Voice and body show the character's feelings; heard at the back | Voice, body and timing bring the character to life; reacts to others |
| **4. Teamwork** *(OL5–OL8)* | Didn't contribute, or blocked others | Did my role | Did my role, listened, and gave helpful feedback | Helped the group build consensus and solve problems |

## Samples: Sally Ann finds Davy in the tree (from *Sally Ann Thunder Ann Whirlwind*)

### Sample A · *Meets*

::: script Sample A
SALLY ANN: Who is up there?
DAVY: It's me, Davy. I'm stuck.
SALLY ANN: I will help you.
DAVY: Thank you.
SALLY ANN: (pulls him down)
DAVY: You are very strong.
:::

::: note Why it is "Meets"
**Form:** names and dialogue, one stage direction. **Story and character:** the events are there, but the lines tell (*"You are very strong"*) instead of showing, and there's no setting.
:::

### Sample B · *Proficient*

**Setting:** A forest at sunrise. A tall tree. DAVY's legs dangle from the top branches.

::: script Sample B
DAVY: (calling, upside down) Help! Somebody! Anybody!
SALLY ANN: (strolling in, hornets buzzing around her bonnet) Well, look at that. A boy growing on a tree.
DAVY: I climbed up to see the sunrise. Now my head's stuck.
SALLY ANN: (laughing so hard the branches shake) Hold still!
NARRATOR: She tied six rattlesnakes into a rope and threw it up.
SALLY ANN: (pulling) One… two… THREE!
DAVY: (popping loose, landing in the leaves) Ma'am, you're the strongest person I ever met.
SALLY ANN: (grinning) And you're the stuck-est.
:::

::: note Why it is "Proficient"
**Form:** setting, names, stage directions in brackets, a narrator for what can't be shown. **Character:** Sally Ann's lines and actions show she's bold and funny. **Delivery cues:** ellipses and capitals guide the count.
:::

### Sample C · *Excellent*

**Setting:** A forest at sunrise. A tall pine. Only DAVY's boots are visible at the top, kicking. Birds have stopped singing to watch.

::: script Sample C
DAVY: (muffled, from inside the branches) Hello? Is anyone… anyone at all…
SALLY ANN: (entering, a hornets' nest on her head like a hat, chewing a blade of grass) That depends. Who's asking?
DAVY: (kicking harder) Davy Crockett! Bear wrestler! Lightning rider!
SALLY ANN: (looking up, unimpressed) And tree wearer, by the look of it.
DAVY: (quietly) …Since yesterday.
SALLY ANN: (laughing so hard a pinecone falls on her head; she doesn't notice) Well, Lightning Rider, hold still. (She pulls a handful of rattlesnakes from a rock and knots them, one, two, three.)
DAVY: (nervously) Are those… snakes?
SALLY ANN: (throwing the rope) They're a rope today.
SOUND: (a creak, a groan, a POP)
DAVY: (landing flat on his back in the leaves, staring at the sky) Ma'am. I've wrestled bears. I've never met anyone like you.
SALLY ANN: (offering her hand, then pulling him up with one finger) Nobody has.
:::

::: note Why it is "Excellent"
**Form:** a setting that gives actors something to play (boots kicking, birds watching); directions that guide timing and comedy. **Character:** every line shows something: Davy's bragging collapses ("…Since yesterday"), Sally Ann's calm confidence never cracks. **Story:** the scene builds to a last line that defines her. **Delivery:** ellipses and a sound cue tell the actors exactly how to time the comedy.
:::
