## Answers

**1.** MAX: (dropping his backpack, shouting) I'm starving!

**2.** MOM: (smiling, whispering) Look who's asleep.

**3–4.** Each line starts with a name in capitals and a colon; stage directions in brackets; no quotation marks.

**5.** Acting it out usually shows where the actor needs a direction (a pause, a gesture, a tone of voice).
