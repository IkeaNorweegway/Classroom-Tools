## Cold read 1 · The Last Puzzle Piece

::: script The Last Puzzle Piece
NIA: One piece left!
NIA: (looking everywhere) Where is it?
GRANDPA: (calm) It will turn up.
LEO: (hiding his bear) Nothing!
NIA: Leo, what is in the bear's mouth?
LEO: (quiet) A blueberry?
NIA: (smiling) Let's put it in together.
:::

**1.** Where are they? Circle. a living room · a bus

**2.** What was the problem? The last puzzle piece was ___ .

(write 1)

**3.** How does Nia feel when she can't find it? Circle. upset · sleepy

**4.** Grandpa is **calm / angry**.

**5.** Act out "Where is it?" with your body. ☐ I did it.
