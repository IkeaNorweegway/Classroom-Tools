## Week 3

**Morphology: ex- and trans-, review**

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| exit (stage direction) | [[go out; leave the stage]] |
| expression | [[pushing a feeling out to show it]] |
| transition | [[a change from one scene to the next]] |

**Conventions: punctuation for delivery (CV3).** How should the actor say each line?

1. HARE: What if I lose AGAIN?
2. TORTOISE: Just… keep… going.
3. MARCUS: The dinosaur sticker—

(lines 3)

=> 1. Stress "AGAIN" (capitals). 2. Slowly, with pauses (ellipses). 3. Cut off suddenly (dash), as if interrupted.

## Week 4

**Sentence combining for narrators** with **while**, **as** and **until**:

1. *Hare ran ahead. Tortoise ate a dandelion.* (while)
2. *The panel slid open. The kids looked up.* (as)
3. *Hare sat by the oak tree. Tortoise arrived.* (until)

(lines 4)

=> 1. While Hare ran ahead, Tortoise ate a dandelion. 2. As the panel slid open, the kids looked up. 3. Hare sat by the oak tree until Tortoise arrived.

## Week 6 · Whole-year review

From memory:

1. Tall Tales: *danger* + -ous → [[dangerous]]
2. Information Report: *ex-* means [[out]]; *trans-* means [[across]]
3. Opinion: *important* → idea word [[importance]]
4. Science Fiction: like a robot → [[robotic]]
5. Poetry: *peace* + -ful → [[peaceful]]
6. Drama: *perform* → noun [[performance]]
7. Punctuate the dialogue: *where are you going asked Babe* → [["Where are you going?" asked Babe.]]
8. Make it a script line: [[BABE: Where are you going?]]
