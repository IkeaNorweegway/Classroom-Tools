::: tip
**For the adult:** A **fact** can be checked. An **opinion** is what someone thinks or believes. Ads, reviews, the news and even cereal boxes mix the two. Your child is learning to ask, "Could this be checked?" Opinions aren't wrong; they're just different from facts.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**"This cereal has 9 grams of sugar per serving, and it's the tastiest breakfast ever!"**

1. "9 grams of sugar per serving": can it be checked? Yes, on the label. **Fact.**
2. "The tastiest breakfast ever": can it be proven? No, people disagree. **Opinion.**
3. One sentence can hold both.
:::
