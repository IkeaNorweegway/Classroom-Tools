Each text retells the Edition A text with the same main points. Use it for decoding and fluency practice. For comprehension and writing tasks, read the Edition A text aloud while the student follows, then let them reread this version.
:::

## About these texts

Some texts give **facts**. Some texts give an **opinion**. An opinion is what someone thinks. A good opinion has **reasons**.

The school and people in these texts are made up.

---page---

::: passage Is Recess Long Enough? | Text 1 · News

Kids at Prairie View School want a longer recess. They asked the school to add 10 minutes.

"We don't have time to play," said Jonah.

Doctors say kids need to move a lot every day. Kids should move hard for 60 minutes each day.

The principal said, "If recess gets longer, something else gets shorter."

A parent said, "Maybe we need more balls and games, not more time."
:::

::: support
**fact** something true you can check

**opinion** what someone thinks
:::

---page---

::: passage Give Us Ten More Minutes | Text 2 · Opinion

I think recess should be 10 minutes longer.

**Reason 1:** Kids need to move. Many kids do not move enough.

**Reason 2:** We learn better after we play.

**Reason 3:** At recess, we learn to get along.

Some people say we will lose learning time. But 10 minutes is a small part of the day.

Let's try it!
:::

::: support
**reason** why you think something
:::

---page---

::: passage Better, Not Longer | Text 3 · Opinion

I think recess should be **better**, not longer.

**Reason 1:** The school day is full. More recess means less math or reading.

**Reason 2:** We need more adults outside to keep kids safe.

**Reason 3:** Kids waste time looking for a ball. More balls and games would help.

Let's make recess better!
:::

::: passage A Letter to the Principal | Text 6 · Our model

Dear Principal Delorme,

I think recess should be longer.

First, kids need to move more.

Next, we learn better after we play.

Last, more kids would get to play.

Please try a longer recess.

Sincerely, Leo
:::

::: support
**Dear** how a letter starts

**Sincerely** how a letter ends
