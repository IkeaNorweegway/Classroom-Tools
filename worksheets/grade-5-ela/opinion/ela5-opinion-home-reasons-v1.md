::: tip
**For the adult:** In class, your child is writing an opinion letter using **TREE**: a **T**opic sentence (the opinion), **R**easons, **E**xplanations with evidence, and an **E**nding. A **reason** says *why*; **evidence** *proves* the reason (a fact, a number, an example). Pick a friendly family debate: a pet, a later bedtime, what to have for dinner on Friday.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Opinion: Our family should have pizza night every Friday.**

1. **Reason:** It would save time on a busy day.
2. **Evidence:** On Fridays we get home from swimming at 6:30, and pizza takes 15 minutes to arrive.
3. **The other side:** Someone might say pizza every week isn't healthy. **Answer:** We could add a salad and make it at home.
:::
