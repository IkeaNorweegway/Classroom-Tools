::: tip
**For the adult:** For 5 and 6, check that the "fact" could be checked and the "opinion" is a belief or judgement.
:::

## Answers

**1.** F

**2.** O

**3.** F

**4.** Fact: the team won 5 games this season. Opinion: the coolest jerseys in the league.

**5.** Example: Fact: "Contains 12 granola bars." Opinion: "Deliciously crunchy!"

**6.** Example: Pizza has cheese on it (fact), and it's the best food in the world (opinion).
