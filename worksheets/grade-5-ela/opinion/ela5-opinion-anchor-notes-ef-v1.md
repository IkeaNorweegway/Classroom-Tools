## 1 · Fact or opinion?

::: pair
**I can** tell a fact from an opinion.

A **fact** can be checked.

*Recess is 20 minutes.* (fact)

An **opinion** is what someone thinks.

*Recess is the best!* (opinion)

**Check:** Circle the opinion.

Recess is at 12:20. · Recess is too short.
|||
**fact**
true; can be checked

**opinion**
what someone thinks
:::

---page---

## 2 · News or opinion?

::: pair
**I can** tell news from an opinion piece.

**News** tells what happened. It shows **both sides**.

An **opinion piece** shows **one side**. It says "I think."

**Check:** "I think recess should be longer." Is it **news** or **opinion**?

(write 1)
|||
**news**
tells what happened

**editorial**
a piece that gives an opinion
:::

---page---

## 3 · Opinion, reason, proof

::: pair
**I can** find the opinion and the reasons.

**Opinion:** Recess should be longer.

**Reason:** Kids need to move.

**Proof:** Doctors say kids should move hard for 60 minutes a day.
|||
**reason**
why you think it

**evidence**
proof
:::

how a person sees something

## 5 · Good proof

::: pair
**I can** tell if proof is good.

Good proof can be **checked**.

*Doctors say kids need to move.* ✓ good proof

*Recess is fun.* ✗ not proof; it is an opinion.
|||
**proof**
something that shows it is true
:::

---page---

## 6 · Fair words

::: pair
**I can** use fair words.

✓ Fair: *Ten more minutes would help.*

✗ Not fair: *Only mean people want short recess.*

Do not call people names.
|||
**fair**
honest and kind
:::

## 7 · Numbers

::: pair
**I can** use a number as proof.

126 out of 180 kids want longer recess.

That is **most** of the kids.
|||
**most**
more than half
:::

## 8 · TREE

**I can** plan my opinion with TREE.

::: strategy TREE
**T** **Topic** sentence: what I think
**R** **Reasons**: why
**E** **Explain**: give proof
**E** **Ending**: what I want
:::

**Check:** T = [[Topic sentence]] · R = [[Reasons]] · E = [[Explain]] · E = [[Ending]]
