::: teacher How this booklet fits with Words Their Way
Students keep their own WTW sort. This booklet adds the Grade 5 affixes that build the words of argument (*-ance/-ence, -ity, -ment, per-, com-/con-, ob-, ad-*), Tier 2 words for opinion writing, spelling from memory, sentence combining with *although, because, unless*, and one conventions target per week. Earlier units come back as review. Codes: VO4, VO9, VO10, CV2, CV8, CV10, CV12–14.
:::

(lines 2)

**Conventions: a comma after an opening transition (CV2).** Add commas.

## Week 4

**Morphology: ob- and ad-** (*ob-* = against; *ad-* = toward, to).

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| object (verb) | [[to argue against something]] |
| obstacle | [[something that stands in your way]] |
| advantage | [[something that helps you get ahead]] |

**Extended:** Use *obstacle* and *advantage* in one sentence about recess.

(lines 2)

**Sentence combining** with **unless**: *Recess can't be longer. The school finds more supervisors.*

(lines 2)

=> Recess can't be longer unless the school finds more supervisors.

**Conventions: commas in a letter (CV2).** Fix: *Dear Principal Delorme I think recess should be longer. Sincerely Leo*

(lines 2)
