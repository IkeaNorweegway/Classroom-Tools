## Lesson 2 · Fact or opinion? (Text 1)

Use **Anchor note 1**. Read Text 1, the news article.

| Sentence | F or O | How I know |
| --- | --- | --- |

**Write about it.** Can a news article contain opinions and still be fair? Use Text 1 as evidence.

%% frame: A news article can ___ if it ___ . In Text 1, ___ .

(lines 4)

=> Yes, if the opinions are in quotes from different people and the reporter doesn't take a side. Text 1 quotes Jonah (for), the principal (concerned) and a parent (a different idea), and the reporter never says "I think."
