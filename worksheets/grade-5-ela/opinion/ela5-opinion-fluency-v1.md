::: teacher How to use this pack
**Recess Radio** (whole class, Weeks 2–3, performed in Lesson 15) is a radio call-in show. Persuasive reading needs **emphasis**: stress the opinion word and the key evidence, pause before the most important point. Rehearse 10–15 minutes a day; record and replay if possible.
**Repeated reading** (fluency group): the passages below, then leveled opinion or non-fiction texts at about 95% accuracy. Model, 3–4 reads with error correction, time one read, graph, one question.
**Partner reading** continues; "paragraph shrinking" for opinion text: name the reason, then the evidence, in 10 words or less.
:::

## Recess Radio: a call-in show

**Parts:** Host · Caller 1 (Jonah, student) · Caller 2 (Principal Delorme) · Caller 3 (Mr. Nguyen, parent) · Caller 4 (Ms. Kaur, supervisor) · Caller 5 (Ava, Grade 3) · Producer

::: script Recess Radio
HOST: Good afternoon, Prairie View! You're listening to Recess Radio, where today's question is: should lunch recess be longer? The lines are open. First caller, you're on the air.
JONAH: Hi! I'm Jonah, Grade 5. Here's my point: twenty minutes is not enough. By the time we zip our coats and pick teams, the bell rings. We need ten more minutes.
HOST: Strong words, Jonah. Do you have evidence?
JONAH: Canada's guidelines say kids need sixty minutes of hard activity a day, and most kids don't get it. Recess is where we get it.
HOST: Thank you. Our next caller is a name you'll know.
PRINCIPAL: Hello, it's Principal Delorme. I love that students care about this. But here's my concern: if recess gets longer, something else gets shorter. Where do those ten minutes come from?
HOST: A fair question. Line three, you're on.
MR. NGUYEN: Hi, I'm a parent. I think we're asking the wrong question. Kids spend half of recess looking for a ball! Let's make recess better, not longer.
HOST: Interesting! Line four?
MS. KAUR: Hi, I'm Ms. Kaur, one of the recess supervisors. There are three of us watching more than three hundred students. Before anyone adds time, please think about who's out there in January when it's minus twenty.
HOST: That's a perspective we haven't heard yet. Thank you. One more caller.
AVA: Hi, I'm Ava, I'm in Grade 3. I don't care if it's longer. I just want a turn on the swings!
HOST: (laughing) Ava, that may be the most important point of the day. Producer, any last messages?
PRODUCER: Our survey says one hundred twenty-six out of one hundred eighty students want longer recess, but almost as many want better equipment and games.
HOST: So maybe the answer is: a little longer, and a lot better? The council votes March third. Until then, keep talking, and keep listening. This has been Recess Radio!
:::

**Rehearsal checklist:** ☐ I stress my opinion word ☐ I slow down for numbers ☐ I sound like my character ☐ I pause before my most important point
