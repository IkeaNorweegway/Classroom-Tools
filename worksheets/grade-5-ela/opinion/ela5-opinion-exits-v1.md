::: card Ticket 1 · Lesson 5 · Fact, opinion, news and editorial
Name ____________________

**1.** Write **F** (fact) or **O** (opinion): *Lunch recess starts at 12:20.* ___ · *Lunch recess is the best part of school.* ___ *(TF12)*

=> F · O

**2.** How is a news article different from an editorial? Give one difference. *(TF1)*

(lines 2)

=> A news article informs and shows more than one side; an editorial argues for one side.
:::

::: card Ticket 2 · Lesson 10 · Evidence and perspectives
Name ____________________

**1.** Which is stronger evidence, and why? (a) "Recess is fun." (b) "Statistics Canada reports about 2 in 5 kids get 60 minutes of activity a day." *(WR21)*

(lines 2)

=> (b): it comes from a reliable source and can be checked; (a) is an opinion.

**2.** Name someone whose perspective on recess is different from yours, and explain why they might think that way. *(CO11, CO13)*

(lines 3)

=> e.g., a supervisor might not want longer recess because they'd stand outside longer in the cold watching 300 students.
:::

::: card Ticket 4 · Lesson 18 · Answering the other side
Name ____________________

**1.** Finish: *Some people think recess should stay the same because ___ . That's a fair point. However, ___ .* *(WR6)*

(lines 3)

=> e.g., …because longer recess takes time from learning. However, students who move focus better, so they may learn more in less time.

**2.** Why does answering the other side make an opinion piece stronger? *(WR6)*

(lines 2)

=> It shows you thought about the issue and answers the reader's doubts before they can stop you.
:::
