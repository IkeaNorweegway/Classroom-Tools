## Cold read 1 · A school garden

::: passage Every School Should Have a Garden | An opinion

I think every school should have a garden.

First, kids can learn outside. They can count leaves and look at bugs.

Next, kids eat more vegetables when they grow them.

Last, families can help in the summer.

Some people say a garden is a lot of work. But each family could help for one week.

Let's plant a garden!
:::

**1.** What does the writer think? Every school should have a ___ .

(write 1)

**2.** Write one reason. ___ .

(write 1)

**3.** Circle the **fact**. Kids can count leaves. · Gardens are the best!

**4.** Which reason do you like most? Why?

(write 1)

**5.** Some people say a garden is a lot of ___ .

(write 1)

**6.** Who might not want a garden? Circle. a person who cuts the grass · a kid who loves carrots

**7.** Draw a school garden.

(box 4)

## Cold read 2 · Homework

**News:** Some parents want less homework. Kids are busy at night. A teacher says reading at home helps kids get better at reading.

**Letter:** Dear Editor, I think homework should be reading only. Reading helps you get better. We do math at school every day. Sincerely, Priya
:::

**2.** Why do some parents want less homework? Kids are ___ .

**3.** Priya says reading helps you get ___ .

**5.** Priya says kids do math at ___ .
