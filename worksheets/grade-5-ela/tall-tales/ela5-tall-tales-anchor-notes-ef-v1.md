## 1 · What is a tall tale?

::: pair
**I can** tell what makes a story a tall tale.

A **tall tale** is a story about a hero.

The hero can do things that are **impossible**.

The person telling it acts like it is **true**.

**Paul Bunyan** combed his beard with a [[pine tree]].

It was so cold that **words froze** in the air!

**Check:** Is this a tall tale? Circle one.

*Tom ate a sandwich for lunch.*  **Yes / No**

=> No. Nothing impossible happens.
|||
**impossible**
cannot really happen

**hero**
the main person in the story

**exaggerate**
make it much bigger than real
:::

## 2 · The parts of a story

::: pair
**I can** find the parts of a story.

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginning** | Who is in the story? Where are they? |
| **Problem** | What goes wrong? |
| **Events** | What happens next? |
| **Resolution** | How is the problem [[fixed]]? |
| **Ending** | How does it end? |

**Check:** In *Chinook Charlie*, what is the problem? Circle one.

It is too hot. · It is too cold. · The cows are lost.

=> It is too cold (the Great Freeze).
|||
**problem**
something that goes wrong

**resolution**
how the problem is fixed
:::

## 3 · What is the character like?

::: pair
**I can** tell what a character is like.

We learn about a character from:

- what they **say**
- what they **think**
- what they **do**
- what **others** say about them

Sally Ann walks out to the bear. Everyone else hides.

So Sally Ann is [[brave]].

**Check:** Draw or write one thing John Henry **does**.

(box 4)

=> He races the steam drill / swings two hammers / does not stop.
|||
**trait**
what a person is like inside

brave · kind · determined · funny
trait words
:::

## 4 · Stretching the truth

::: pair
**I can** tell what an exaggeration means.

**Hyperbole** is a big exaggeration.

*It was so cold the cows gave ice cream.*

It really means: it was [[very, very cold]].

A **simile** compares with **like** or **as**.

*His chest got as big as a balloon.*

It really means: his chest got [[very big]].

**Check:** *Joe sneezed and the leaves blew off the trees.* What does it show? Circle one.

Joe is sick. · Joe is very strong. · Joe is sad.

=> Joe is very strong / powerful.
|||
**hyperbole**
a huge exaggeration

**simile**
compares two things with *like* or *as*
:::

## 5 · Telling it short

::: pair
**I can** tell a story in a few words.

A **summary** tells only the big parts.

| Somebody | Wanted | But | So | Then |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John Henry | to save the jobs | a machine came | he raced it and [[won]] | he died; people sing about him |

**Check:** Who is the *somebody* in *Chinook Charlie*?

(lines 1)

=> Chinook Charlie.
|||
**summary**
the most important parts, said short

**gist**
the main idea in one sentence
:::

## 6 · Clues in the story

::: pair
**I can** use clues to figure something out.

**Clue + what I know = I figure it out**

**Clue:** Everyone hides when the bear comes.

**I know:** People hide when they are scared.

**So:** The people think the bear is [[dangerous]].

**Check:** Paul puts Babe inside his coat. How does Paul feel about Babe? Circle one.

He cares about her. · He is angry. · He is bored.

=> He cares about her (he keeps her warm).
|||
**clue**
something that helps you figure it out

**evidence**
words from the story that prove it
:::

## 8 · Show, don't tell

::: pair
**I can** show a feeling with details.

**Tell:** *It was cold.*

**Show:** *Their teeth chattered. Their noses turned blue.*

**Show** with:

- what someone **does**
- what you **hear** or **see**
- what someone **says**

**Check:** Show that Charlie is tired. Draw it or write it.

(box 4)

=> He yawns and falls asleep on the mountain for a week.
|||
**show**
give details so the reader can picture it

**tell**
just say it
:::

**I can** write what people say.

=> "Help!" yelled Davy.
|||
**quotation marks**
" " go around talking words
