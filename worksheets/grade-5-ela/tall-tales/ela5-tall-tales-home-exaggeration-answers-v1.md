::: tip
**For the adult:** Answers vary. A good answer says what the line **really means** in plain words and gives a **reason** for the author's choice. Accept any answer that does both.
:::

## Answers

**1.** A hot-air balloon.

**2.** Very big and round, because he breathed in so much air.

**3.** Her laugh was huge and powerful (she is bigger than life).

**4.** To show how fast and powerful the swing was; a storm wind is strong and makes a whistling sound. It also hints that something big is coming.

**5.** Example: "I'm so hungry I could eat a moose." Really means: I'm very hungry.

**6.** Example: "I've told you a million times!" Really means: I've told you many times and I'm frustrated.
