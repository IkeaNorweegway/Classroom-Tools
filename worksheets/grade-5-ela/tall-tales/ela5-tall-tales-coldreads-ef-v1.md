## Cold read 1 · Mighty Marguerite

::: passage Mighty Marguerite | A new tall tale

In northern Alberta, the mosquitoes got as big as crows! They took picnic baskets. They tried to take the fire truck.

Marguerite was not scared. She was very strong. She could pull a plough faster than a tractor.

The mayor ran to her. "Help! The mosquitoes are all around the school. The kids can't get out!"

Marguerite said, "Somebody has to do something."

She got a giant pot. She filled it with sweet jam.

The mosquitoes smelled the jam. They flew into the pot. They got stuck! Marguerite put on the lid.

The kids ran out to play. The town had a party for Marguerite.
:::

Mosquitoes as big as crows. · Marguerite eats lunch. · She pulls a plough faster than a tractor. · The kids go to school.

## Cold read 2 · Grandma Kowalchuk

::: passage Grandma Kowalchuk and the Bread Road | A new tall tale

It snowed for nine days. The snow went over the houses. The town had no food.

Mr. Dubois said, "We need food from the city. But we can't drive in this snow."

Grandma Kowalchuk was small. But she was strong and clever. She said, "I will bake a road!"

She made a giant ball of dough. It grew and grew. It grew all the way to the city!

She baked it. The bread road was hard on top and soft inside.

Mr. Dubois drove his truck on the bread road. He came back with food. The town was saved!
