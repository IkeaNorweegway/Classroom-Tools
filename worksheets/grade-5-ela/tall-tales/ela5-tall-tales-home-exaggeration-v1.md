::: tip
**For the adult:** Tall tales are full of **exaggeration** (*It was so cold the words froze in the air*) and **comparisons** (*the tree bent like a fishing rod*). Your child is learning to explain what each one **really means** and **why** the author chose it, not just to name it. Read each line aloud together, then ask, "What does it make you picture?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**"It was so cold that people's shadows froze to the sidewalk."**

1. **Is it real?** No, shadows can't freeze. It is an **exaggeration** (also called hyperbole).
2. **What does it really mean?** It was extremely, unbelievably cold.
3. **Why did the author use it?** It helps us picture the cold, and it's funny.
:::
