::: tip
**For the adult:** Answers depend on the story chosen. Check that each part is there and that the summary is short and in your child's own words.
:::

## What a strong answer looks like

**1–4.** Example with *Paul Bunyan*: Somebody: Paul Bunyan · Wanted: to keep his loggers working · But: in the Blue Snow winter they were starving · So: he built a giant griddle for pancakes · Then: the camp cut more wood than ever.

**5.** Example: Paul Bunyan's loggers were starving in the Blue Snow winter, so he built a giant griddle, and they cut more wood than ever.

**6.** Small details to leave out: names of minor characters, exact numbers, descriptions that don't change what happens. Keep: who, the problem, how it's solved, how it ends.
