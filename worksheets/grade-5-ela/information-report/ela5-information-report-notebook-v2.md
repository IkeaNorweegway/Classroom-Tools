## Lesson 6 · Cause and effect: mountains and weather

Read Text 3. Use **Anchor note 4**.

| Cause | Effect | Signal word in the text | ¶ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Air picks up water as it moves across the Pacific]] | [[It arrives at the coast wet and full of clouds]] | [[so]] | [[2]] |
| [[Rising air cools, and cool air can't hold as much water]] | [[Rain and snow fall on the western slopes]] | [[so]] | [[3]] |
| [[The air has dropped most of its water crossing the Rockies]] | [[The land to the east gets much less rain]] | [[as a result]] | [[4]] |
| [[Chinook air is warm and dry]] | [[Snow melts and disappears fast]] | [[because]] | [[6]] |

**Write about it.** Explain why the Grassland gets so little rain. Use at least one signal word.

%% frame: When wet air reaches the mountains, ___ . As a result, ___ .

(lines 4)

=> When wet air from the Pacific reaches the mountains, it rises and cools, so it drops its rain and snow on the western slopes. As a result, the air is dry when it crosses, and the land to the east is in a rain shadow (Text 3 ¶3–4).

## Lesson 7 · Compare and contrast: Parkland and Boreal Forest

Read Text 4. Use **Anchor note 5**.

| | Parkland | Boreal Forest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | [[A band across the middle of Alberta, plus the Peace River area]] | [[The whole north of Alberta]] |
| What the land is like | [[Aspen groves and grassy meadows; rich black soil]] | [[Thick conifer and aspen forest; lakes and muskeg]] |
| People | [[Most Albertans: farms, Calgary, Red Deer, Edmonton]] | [[Few people; towns far apart]] |

**Write about it.** Write a compare-and-contrast paragraph about the Parkland and the Boreal Forest. Use **both**, **but** and **while**.

%% frame: The Parkland and the Boreal Forest both ___ . But the Parkland ___ , while the Boreal Forest ___ .

(lines 5)

=> The Parkland and the Boreal Forest both have trembling aspen, cold winters, and animals like deer and coyotes. But the Parkland is open land with rich soil, farms and cities, while the Boreal Forest is thick forest and muskeg with few people. They sit side by side but are very different places (Text 4 ¶1, ¶6).

**Why do more people live in the Parkland than in the Boreal Forest?** Use evidence.

(lines 3)

=> The Parkland has rich black soil and enough rain for crops, so it became farms, towns and cities (¶3). The Boreal Forest has long, cold winters and muskeg that makes roads hard to build (¶4–5).
