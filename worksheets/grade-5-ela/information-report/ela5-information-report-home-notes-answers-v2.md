::: tip
**For the adult:** Wording will differ; that is the point. Check that notes are short and that the final sentences don't copy the original.
:::

## Example answers

**1.** Mount Columbia · highest in Alberta · 3,747 m

**2.** Example: The tallest spot in Alberta is the top of Mount Columbia, about 3,700 metres up.

**3.** chinook = warm + dry · snow melts fast · animals find grass

**4.** Example: A chinook melts the snow quickly, so animals can get to the grass underneath.

**5–6.** Three to five key words, not whole sentences; a main-idea sentence that sounds like your child, not like the book.
