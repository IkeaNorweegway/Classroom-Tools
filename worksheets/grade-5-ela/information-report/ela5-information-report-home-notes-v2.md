::: tip
**For the adult:** Copying sentences from a book isn't research. Your child is learning to write **key words** as notes, close the book, and then say the fact in their own words. If their sentence sounds exactly like the book, ask, "How would you explain it to a friend?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Book sentence:** "Ice sheets are so heavy that they slowly flow. As the ice moved, it scraped the land like a giant bulldozer."

1. **Notes (key words only):** ice sheets heavy · flow slowly · scraped land
2. **Close the book.**
3. **My own words:** The ice was so heavy that it crept along and scraped the ground as it went.
:::
