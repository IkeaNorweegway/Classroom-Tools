::: tip
**For the adult:** Information texts (non-fiction books, websites, cereal boxes, instructions) use **text features** like headings, bold words, pictures with captions and "Did you know?" boxes. Your child is learning to use them to find facts quickly. Use any non-fiction book, magazine or website you have at home.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Question: How long is the small intestine?**

1. **Look at the headings first,** not the paragraphs. One heading says "Step 4: the small intestine."
2. **Read only that section.** It says "a narrow tube about 6 metres long."
3. **Answer in a sentence:** The small intestine is about 6 metres long. It took 20 seconds, not 5 minutes.
:::
