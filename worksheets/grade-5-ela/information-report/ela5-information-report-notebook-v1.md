## Lesson 7 · Compare and contrast: bones and muscles

Read Text 4. Use **Anchor note 5**.

| | Bones | Muscles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | [[Hold the body up; protect organs; make blood cells]] | [[Move the bones; pump blood; move food]] |
| What they are like | [[Hard; don't change shape]] | [[Soft; contract and relax]] |
| How to keep them strong | [[Running, jumping; calcium]] | [[Exercise / using them]] |

**Write about it.** Write a compare-and-contrast paragraph about bones and muscles. Use **both**, **but** and **while**.

%% frame: Bones and muscles are both ___ . But bones ___ , while muscles ___ .

(lines 5)

=> Bones and muscles are both living tissues that need oxygen and get stronger with use. But bones are hard and give the body its shape, while muscles are soft and contract to move the bones. They work as partners (Text 4 ¶1, ¶7).

**Why do muscles work in pairs?** Use evidence.

(lines 3)

=> Muscles can only pull, never push, so one muscle pulls the bone one way and its partner pulls it back (Text 4 ¶4: biceps and triceps).
