## 1 · Text features

::: pair
**I can** use text features to find facts.

An information text gives **facts**.

**Text features** help you find the important parts.

| Feature | It helps me… |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading** | know what a part is about |
| **Bold word** | see an [[important]] word |
| **Diagram** | see how something works |
| **Caption** | know what a picture shows |
|||
**fact**
something true that can be checked

**heading**
a title for one part
:::

## 2 · Topic and subtopics

::: pair
**I can** find the topic and the parts.

The **topic** is what the whole text is about.

**Subtopics** are the smaller parts. Each one has a **heading**.

**Text 4** topic: the Parkland and the Boreal Forest

Subtopics: The Parkland · The Boreal Forest · [[Same and different]]
|||
**topic**
what it is all about

**subtopic**
one smaller part
:::

## 3 · Main idea

::: pair
**I can** tell what a part is mostly about.

The **main idea** is the most important point.

**Details** are facts that tell more.

*Main idea:* The Grassland is dry.

*Detail:* It does not rain much.

**Check:** Circle the main idea.

Grass has deep roots. · Grass is green.
|||
**main idea**
what it is mostly about

**detail**
a fact that tells more
:::

## 4 · Why it happens

::: pair
**I can** find what happens and why.

**Cause** = why. **Effect** = what happens.

*A warm wind blows* (cause) **so** *the snow melts* (effect).

Words that show cause: **because**, **so**

**Check:** Finish it. The air goes up the mountain and gets cold, **so** ___ .

(write 1)
|||
**cause**
why something happens

**effect**
what happens
:::

## 6 · New words

::: pair
**I can** figure out a new word.

Look **near** the word for a clue.

*ground squirrels (often called **gophers**)*

The clue in ( ) tells me: gophers = [[ground squirrels]].

**Word parts:** **sub-** means *below*. **Subalpine** means [[below]] the alpine zone.
|||
**clue**
a hint
:::

---page---

## 7 · Can I trust it?

::: pair
**I can** tell if a source is good.

Ask:

- **Who** made it? (a park warden? a store?)
- **Why**? To teach me, or to sell?

A park guide is **good** for facts.

An ad that sells tours is **not good** for facts.
|||
**source**
where facts come from

**ad**
something that wants to sell
:::

## 8 · Notes

::: pair
**I can** take notes with key words.

**Notes** are a few key words, not long sentences.

*The ice moved slowly and scraped the land.*

Notes: ice moved · scraped land

Then I say it **my way**.
|||
**notes**
short words to help you remember
:::

## 9 · TIDE

**I can** plan a paragraph with TIDE.

::: strategy TIDE
**T** **Topic** sentence: what it is about
**I** **Important** facts
**D** **Detail**: an example or a number
**E** **Ending** sentence
:::

Check: T = [[Topic sentence]] · I = [[Important facts]] · D = [[Detail]] · E = [[Ending]]

## 10 · Start and finish

::: pair
**I can** start and end my report.

**Start** with a question: *Did you know a wind can melt snow in one afternoon?*

**End** with the big idea and a tip: *The Grassland is full of life. Go see it!*
|||
**hook**
a start that makes people want to read
:::
