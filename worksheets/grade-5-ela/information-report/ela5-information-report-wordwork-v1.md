Students keep their own WTW sort. **Conventions: transition words (WR5, CV10).** Join with *for example*, *as a result* or *also*. Use each once.

1. *Bones protect organs. The skull protects the brain.*
2. *The diaphragm moves down. Air rushes in.*
3. *Muscles move bones. Muscles pump blood.*

(lines 4)

=> 1. Bones protect organs; for example, the skull protects the brain. 2. The diaphragm moves down. As a result, air rushes in. 3. Muscles move bones. Heart muscle also pumps blood.
