## Cold read 1 · Your Skin

::: passage Your Skin | An information text

### A big organ

Your skin is an **organ**. It is the biggest organ in your body.

### A shield

Skin keeps germs out. If you get a cut, germs can get in. So you clean it and cover it.

### Hot and cold

When you are hot, you **sweat**. Sweat helps you cool down.

### Touch

Skin can feel. It feels hot, cold and soft things.
:::

## Cold read 2 · Your Eyes

::: passage Your Eyes | An information text

### Eyes and brain

Your eyes and your brain work together to help you see.

### How you see

First, light goes into your eye. Next, the light goes to the back of the eye. Then, a **nerve** takes a message to your brain. Your brain tells you what you see.

### Bright light

In bright light, the black dot in your eye gets **small**. In the dark, it gets **big**.

### Eyes and cameras

Eyes and cameras both take in light. But your eyes send the picture to your brain.
:::
