::: card Ticket 2 · Lesson 10 · Cause and effect, compare and contrast
Name ____________________

**1.** Fill in: When wet air rises over the mountains, ___ . Which signal word did you use? *(TF11)*

(lines 2)

=> ...it cools, so the water falls as rain and snow on the western slopes. Signal word: so (or when/because).

**2.** Give one way the Parkland and the Boreal Forest are **alike** and one way they are **different**. *(TF11, CO9)*

(lines 2)

=> Alike: both have trembling aspen / cold winters / deer and coyotes. Different: the Parkland is open land with farms and cities, the Boreal Forest is thick forest and muskeg with few people.
:::

(lines 2)

=> Any two: Who made it (an expert)? Why (to inform or to sell)? When? Can I check it in another good source?

::: card Ticket 5 · Lesson 20 · Transitions
Name ____________________

**1.** Join with *as a result*: *The air drops its water on the mountains. The Grassland gets little rain.* *(WR5, CV10)*

(lines 2)

=> The air drops its water on the mountains. As a result, the Grassland gets little rain.

**2.** Join with *for example*: *Fire helps some trees spread. Lodgepole pine cones open in the heat of a fire.* *(WR5)*

(lines 2)
