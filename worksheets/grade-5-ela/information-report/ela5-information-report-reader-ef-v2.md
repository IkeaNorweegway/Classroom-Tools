## About these texts

These texts give **facts**. They have **headings** that tell you what each part is about. Bold words are important words.

---page---

::: passage Six Regions, One Province | Text 1

Alberta is big. The land is not the same everywhere.

There are six **regions**. In one region, the land, plants and animals are alike.

### The six regions

The **Grassland** is dry and flat. The **Parkland** has trees and grass. The **Rocky Mountains** are very high. The **Foothills** are hills by the mountains. The **Boreal Forest** is a big forest in the north. The **Canadian Shield** is old rock and lakes.

### They are connected

Snow falls in the mountains. It melts into a river. The river goes to the city. Then it waters the farms. One drop of water helps three regions!
:::

::: support
**region** a part of the land where things are alike

**forest** a place with lots of trees
:::

::: passage The Grassland | Text 2

The Grassland is flat. You can see very far. There are not many trees.

### A dry, windy land

It does not rain much. The wind blows a lot. Summers are hot.

### Grasses with deep roots

Grass grows here. Its **roots** go deep down. They find water. If a fire comes, the roots live. The grass grows back.

### Animals

The **pronghorn** runs very fast. Owls live in holes in the ground.

### The badlands

Some hills are bare and striped. People find dinosaur bones there.
:::

::: support
**roots** the part of a plant under the ground

**pronghorn** a fast animal like a deer
:::

::: passage Mountains Make the Weather | Text 3

Our weather comes from the west.

### Up and over

Wet air comes to the mountains. The air goes up. It gets cold. Snow falls on the mountains.

### The rain shadow

Now the air is dry. The land past the mountains gets little rain. This dry land is in a **rain shadow**.

### The chinook

Sometimes a warm wind blows down from the mountains. It is called a **chinook**. The snow melts fast!
:::

::: support
**rain shadow** dry land on the far side of mountains

**chinook** a warm, dry wind in winter
:::

::: passage The Parkland and the Boreal Forest | Text 4

### The Parkland

The Parkland has groups of trees and fields of grass. The soil is black and good for farms. Most people in Alberta live here.

### The Boreal Forest

The Boreal Forest is in the north. It is the biggest region. It has lots of trees and wet land called **muskeg**. Moose and wolves live here. Not many people live here.

### Same and different

Both have aspen trees. Both have cold winters. But the Parkland has farms and cities. The Boreal Forest is a thick forest.
:::

::: support
**soil** dirt that plants grow in

**muskeg** wet, soft land in the north
:::

::: passage The Rocky Mountains | Text 5

The Rocky Mountains are in the west. They are the highest land in Alberta.

### Three zones

At the bottom, it is warm. There are trees and grass. In the middle, there are big trees and lots of snow. At the top, it is too cold for trees. Small plants grow on the rocks.

### Ice

Some snow never melts. It turns into ice called a **glacier**.

### Be safe

Stay far away from elk and bears. Never feed wild animals.
:::

::: support
**zone** one part or level

**glacier** a big, thick sheet of ice
:::

::: passage How Ice Shaped Alberta | Text 6

Long ago, ice covered Alberta.

### Step 1: Old rock

Under the land is very old rock.

### Step 2: The ice comes

It got very cold. Snow piled up and turned to ice. The ice was very thick.

### Step 3: The ice moves

The ice moved slowly. It scraped the land. It carried big rocks.

### Step 4: The ice melts

It got warm. The ice melted. The water made rivers and lakes.

### Step 5: A new land

Plants grew. Animals and people came.
:::

::: support
**scrape** rub hard and take off the top

**melt** turn from ice to water
:::
