## About these texts

These texts give **facts**. They have **headings** that tell you what each part is about. Bold words are important words.

---page---

::: passage Your Body Is a Team | Text 1

Your body is made of tiny parts called **cells**.

Cells make **organs**, like the heart. Organs work in teams. A team is called a **system**.

### The systems

The **heart** moves blood. The **lungs** take in air. The **stomach** breaks down food. **Bones** hold you up. **Muscles** move you. The **brain** is the boss.

### They work together

When you kick a ball, your brain tells your muscles to move. You breathe faster. Your heart beats faster. All the systems help!
:::

::: support
**cell** the smallest living part

**organ** a body part with a job

**system** organs that work as a team
:::

::: passage The Heart | Text 2

Your heart is a muscle. It is as big as your fist. It beats all day and all night.

### What it does

The heart **pumps** blood. Blood goes to the lungs to get **oxygen**. Then the heart pumps the blood to the whole body.

### Blood vessels

Blood moves in tubes called **blood vessels**. Some are very thin, thinner than a hair.

> **Try it:** Put two fingers on your wrist. Can you feel your heart beat? That is your **pulse**.
:::

::: support
**pump** push out

**oxygen** a gas in the air we need to live

**pulse** the beat you can feel
:::

::: passage Breathing | Text 3

You breathe in air. The air has **oxygen**. Your body needs oxygen.

### The path of air

Air goes in your nose. It goes down a tube to your **lungs**. In the lungs, oxygen goes into your blood.

### Breathing out

When you breathe out, you get rid of a gas your body does not need.

### When you run

When you run, you breathe faster. Your muscles need more oxygen.
:::

::: support
**lungs** two soft bags in your chest that hold air

**breathe** take air in and out
:::

::: passage Bones and Muscles | Text 4

### Bones

You have 206 bones. Bones hold you up. Your skull keeps your brain safe. Your ribs keep your heart and lungs safe.

### Muscles

Muscles move your bones. Muscles can only **pull**. So they work in pairs. One pulls your arm up. One pulls it down.

### Alike and different

Bones and muscles are both alive. Bones are hard. Muscles are soft and can get short and long.
:::

::: support
**skull** the bone around your brain

**ribs** the bones around your chest
:::

::: passage The Brain | Text 5

Your brain is the **boss** of your body. It helps you think and remember. It keeps your heart beating.

### Messages

Your brain sends messages on **nerves**. Nerves go to every part of your body.

### Ouch!

If you touch something hot, your hand moves away fast. That is called a **reflex**.

### Take care of your brain

Sleep helps your brain. Wear a helmet when you ride a bike.
:::

::: support
**nerve** a thin line that carries messages

**reflex** a very fast move you do not think about
:::

::: passage From Sandwich to Energy | Text 6

Food has to be broken into tiny parts. This is called **digestion**.

### Step 1: Mouth

You chew the food. Spit makes it soft.

### Step 2: Down the tube

You swallow. The food goes down a tube to your stomach.

### Step 3: Stomach

The stomach mixes the food with juices.

### Step 4: Small intestine

The tiny parts go into your blood. Your blood takes them to your cells.

### Step 5: Large intestine

What is left goes out when you use the bathroom.
:::

::: support
**digestion** breaking food down

**stomach** a stretchy bag that mixes food
:::
