::: tip
**For the adult:** Copying sentences from a book isn't research. Your child is learning to write **key words** as notes, close the book, and then say the fact in their own words. If their sentence sounds exactly like the book, ask, "How would you explain it to a friend?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Book sentence:** "Bones are alive. They have blood vessels and nerves, and they can repair themselves when they break."

1. **Notes (key words only):** bones alive · blood vessels + nerves · fix themselves
2. **Close the book.**
3. **My own words:** Bones are living parts of your body, and a broken bone can heal itself.
:::
