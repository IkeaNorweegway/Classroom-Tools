**For the adult:** Answers depend on the book or website. Look for these signs that your child is using the features well.
:::

## What strong answers look like

**1–2.** A heading copied correctly, and a prediction that uses it ("'Hibernation' is probably about how bears sleep through winter").

**3.** The word and a meaning in their own words, often found right after the word or in a glossary.

**4.** A caption that says what the picture **shows**, not just its name ("A bear's den dug under a fallen tree", not "Bear").

**5.** They went to the heading that matched the question instead of reading from the start.

**6.** Any feature, with a reason ("the headings, because I could skip straight to the part I needed").
