## Cold read 1 · The Badlands

::: passage The Badlands | An information text

### Strange hills

The **badlands** are bare, striped hills in a deep valley. They are by the Red Deer River.

### How they were made

Long ago, dinosaurs lived here. Mud covered their bones. The mud turned to rock. Then water cut a deep valley.

### Hot and dry

It is hot. It does not rain much. Few plants grow.

### Bones in the hills

Rain washes the hills away a little each year. This is called **erosion**. Then new bones show up!
:::

## Cold read 2 · Wood Buffalo National Park

::: passage Wood Buffalo National Park | An information text

### A big park

Wood Buffalo is Canada's biggest national park. It is in the north. It keeps rare animals safe.

### Bison

The park was made to keep **wood bison** safe. Wood bison and plains bison are both big and shaggy. But wood bison are bigger.

### The crane's trip

Whooping cranes are tall white birds. First, they live in Texas in winter. Next, they fly north in spring. Then, they make nests in the park. Last, they fly back to Texas in fall.

### Back from the edge

Once, there were very few cranes. Now the park keeps them safe. There are more cranes now.
:::
