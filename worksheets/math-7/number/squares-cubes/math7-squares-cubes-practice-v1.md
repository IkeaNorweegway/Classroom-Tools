### Omar has 40 m of fence. He makes a square pen. What is the area inside?
@viz sqside n=10 side="?" fence=1 fill=sun labels="Fence = 40 m" cell=10

=> Side = 40 ÷ 4 = 10 m. (SC4)
=> Area = 10² = 100 m². The fence (40 m) and the area (100 m²) are different things. Targets the "area and perimeter are the same" mix-up.

### Two numbers from 1 to 125 are both perfect squares and perfect cubes. Find them. Show each one both ways.
(work 2)
=> 1 = 1² = 1³. (SC1, SC3)
=> 64 = 8² = 4³. An 8 by 8 square and a 4 by 4 by 4 cube both use 64 tiles or cubes.

### Ava says, "Roots divide. √16 = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 and ∛27 = 27 ÷ 3 = 9." Use a picture to show she is wrong.
@viz cube n=3 label="27 small cubes"

### Square A has sides of 3 cm. Square B has sides of 6 cm.
a) B's side is 2 times as long. How many times as big is its perimeter?
b) How many times as big is its area?
@viz squares shapes="3x3;6x6" labels="A: 3 cm;B: 6 cm" cell=12
(work 3)
=> a) A: 4 × 3 = 12 cm. B: 4 × 6 = 24 cm. 24 ÷ 12 = 2, so 2 times as big. (SC4)
=> b) A: 3² = 9 cm². B: 6² = 36 cm². 36 ÷ 9 = 4, so 4 times as big. The area does not just double.
