::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is thinking −8 is bigger than −3 "because 8 is bigger than 3." On a number line −8 is further left, so −8 < −3. Think of temperature: −8 °C is colder.
:::

## Answers

**1.** −7 **<** −2. −7 is further left.

**2.** a) 0.5 **>** 0.125. 0.500 has 5 tenths; 0.125 has only 1 tenth.
b) −0.6 **>** −0.65. −0.60 is closer to 0, so it is further right.

**3.** −3/4 **<** −0.7. −3/4 = −0.75, which is further left than −0.70.

**4.** 3/4 = **0.75**. −2/5 = **−0.4** (2/5 = 4/10 = 0.4).

**5.** Decimals: 0.1, −1, −0.25, −0.6, 0.5. Order: **−1, −0.6, −1/4, 0.1, 1/2**

**6.** **Banff** is coldest (−14 °C is the furthest down). Order from coldest: Banff, Red Deer, Lethbridge.
