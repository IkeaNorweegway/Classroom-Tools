::: tip
**For the adult:** To multiply decimals, multiply as if there were no decimal points. Then count the decimal places in both numbers: 0.3 × 0.2 = 0.06. To divide by a decimal, multiply both numbers by 10 (or 100) first: 6 ÷ 0.5 = 60 ÷ 5 = 12. Estimate to check.
:::

::: example
**Find 0.4 × 0.2. Then find 4.8 ÷ 0.6.**
Step 1: 4 × 2 = 8. There are two decimal places in all, so 0.4 × 0.2 = 0.08. The picture shows 8 of 100 squares.

Step 2: Multiply both numbers by 10: 4.8 ÷ 0.6 = 48 ÷ 6 = 8.
:::
