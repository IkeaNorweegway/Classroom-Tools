::: tip
**For the adult:** Build percents from easy ones. 10% means divide by 10. 5% is half of 10%. 25% means divide by 4. 1% means divide by 100. So 35% = 25% + 10%. A percent can be more than 100%: 150% is one and a half times the number.
:::

::: example
**Find 35% of 60.**
Step 1: 25% of 60 = 60 ÷ 4 = 15. And 10% of 60 = 60 ÷ 10 = 6.
@viz percentbar percent=35 top="0%,10%,25%,35%,100%" bottom="0,6,15,21,60" width=440
Step 2: 35% = 25% + 10%. So 35% of 60 = 15 + 6 = 21.
:::
