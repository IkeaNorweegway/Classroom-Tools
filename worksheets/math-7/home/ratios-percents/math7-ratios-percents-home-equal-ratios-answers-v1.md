::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slips are adding the same number to both terms (2:3 → 4:5), and reading 2:3 as "2 out of 3." In 2:3 there are 2 + 3 = 5 parts in all.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 1:4 = **2:8** = **3:12** = **5:20**.
@viz dnl top="0,1,2,3,5" bottom="0,4,[[8]],[[12]],[[20]]" toplabel="concentrate" bottomlabel="water" width=420
**2.** 12:8 = 3:**2**. Divide both by 4.

**3.** **3/7.** There are 3 + 4 = 7 beads in all, and 3 are red.
@viz tape parts="3:red;4:blue" total="[[7]] beads in all"
**4.** 3:5 = 15:**25**. The factor is 5.

**5.** 4:6 = 2:3 (÷ 2). Then × 5: 10:**15**.

**6.** **2:3.** Make the first numbers match: 2:3 = 6:9 and 3:5 = 6:10. Same concentrate, less water.

**7.** **20 adults.** 2 × 4 = 8 kids, so 5 × 4 = 20 adults.

**8.** 2:3 = **4:6**. Multiply both by 2. Adding changes the mix.
