::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slips are reading 0.5% as 50%, thinking a percent can't be over 100%, and thinking 20% off then 20% back on returns to the start. The second 20% is of a smaller number.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 10% of 90 = **9**. 90 ÷ 10 = 9.

**2.** 15% of 40 = 4 + 2 = **6**.
@viz dnl top="0%,5%,10%,15%,100%" bottom="0,[[2]],[[4]],[[6]],40" width=340
**3.** 1% of 800 = 8. Half of 8 is **4**.

**4.** 150% of 30 = 30 + 15 = **45**.

**5.** 3/4 = 75/100 = **75%**.

**6.** 25% of $40 = $10. $40 − $10 = **$30**.

**7.** 10% of $20 = $2, so 5% = $1. Total **$21.00**.
@viz receipt items="Cap:$20.00;GST (5%):[[$1.00]];Total:[[$21.00]]"
**8.** 20% of $100 = $20, so $80. 20% of $80 = $16, so $80 + $16 = **$96**.
