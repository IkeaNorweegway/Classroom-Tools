::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is thinking that asking friends (or one team) is fair. Friends are alike, so the sample is biased. The second is thinking a census is always better. Asking everyone can cost too much or take too long.
:::

## Answers

**1.** **All Grade 7 students in Lethbridge.**

**2.** **Census.** The group is small, so ask all 5 people.

**3.** **Sample.** There are too many fish to catch them all.

**4.** a) **discrete** (you count pets) b) **continuous** (you measure time)

**5.** **No.** Hockey players will likely say hockey. The sample is **biased**. Pick students at random from all of Grade 7.

**6.** **3, 11, 19, 27, 35.** (The next one, 43, is past the end of the list.)
@viz people n=40 cols=10 numbers=1 label="Start at 3."
**7.** Put **names in a hat** and draw without looking, or use a **random number generator**.
