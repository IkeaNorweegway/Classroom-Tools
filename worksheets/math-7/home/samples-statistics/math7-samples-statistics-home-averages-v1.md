::: tip
**For the adult:** **Mean:** add the numbers, then divide by how many there are. **Median:** put the numbers in order and take the middle one. **Mode:** the number that comes up most often. **Range:** biggest − smallest.
:::

::: example
**Find the mean, median, mode and range of 4, 7, 2, 7, 5.**
@viz numstrip rows="4,7,2,7,5 | 2,4,5,7,7" labels="As given;In order" ring="2:3" hot="2:4,5"
Step 1: Mean: 4 + 7 + 2 + 7 + 5 = 25. 25 ÷ 5 = 5.
Step 2: In order: 2, 4, 5, 7, 7. Median: 5 (ringed). Mode: 7 (shaded). Range: 7 − 2 = 5.
:::
