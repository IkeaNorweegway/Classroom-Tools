::: tip
**For the adult:** The **population** is everyone a question is about. A **census** asks all of them. A **sample** asks some of them. A fair sample is picked at random, so everyone has the same chance. One random way: start at a random spot on a numbered list, then pick every 5th (or every nth) name.
:::

::: example
**A class list has 30 students. Leo wants a sample of 6. He starts at 2. Who does he pick?**
@viz people n=30 cols=15 numbers=1 pick="2,7,12,17,22,27" label="Start at 2. Then every 5th."
Step 1: The jump is 30 ÷ 6 = 5. Pick every 5th student.
Step 2: Start at 2 and count on by 5: 2, 7, 12, 17, 22, 27.
:::
