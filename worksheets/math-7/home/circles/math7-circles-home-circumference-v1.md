::: tip
**For the adult:** The radius goes from the centre to the edge. The diameter goes across, through the centre. It is two radii. The circumference is the distance around. Circumference = 3.14 × diameter. Round answers to one decimal place.
:::

::: example
**A hockey puck is 7.6 cm across. Find its circumference.**
@viz circle show=d dlabel="d = 7.6 cm" clabel="C ≈ 23.9 cm" size=40
Step 1: Estimate. 3 × 7.6 = 22.8. So C is a bit more than 22.8 cm.
Step 2: C ≈ 3.14 × 7.6 = 23.864. Round: about 23.9 cm.
:::
