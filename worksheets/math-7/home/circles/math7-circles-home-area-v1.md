::: tip
**For the adult:** Area is the space inside a circle. It is in square units, like cm² or m². Area = 3.14 × radius × radius. Use the radius, not the diameter. The distance around (circumference) is a length, in cm or m.
:::

::: example
**A face-off circle has a radius of 4.5 m. Find its area.**
@viz circle show=r rlabel="r = 4.5 m" alabel="A ≈ 63.6 m²" fill=cold size=46
Step 1: r × r = 4.5 × 4.5 = 20.25.
Step 2: A ≈ 3.14 × 20.25 = 63.585. Round: about 63.6 m².
:::
