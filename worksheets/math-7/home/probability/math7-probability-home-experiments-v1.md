::: tip
**For the adult:** **Experimental probability = times it happened ÷ number of trials.** It is what really happened. Theory says what should happen. A few trials can be far from the theory. Many trials get close to it.
:::

::: example
**Kai flips a coin 20 times. He gets 12 heads. Compare the result to the theory.**
@viz bar data="Heads:12;Tails:8" ymax=12 ystep=4 values=1 ylabel="times" height=80
Step 1: Experimental P(heads) = 12/20 = 0.6 = 60%.
Step 2: Theory: P(heads) = 1/2 = 50%. Close, but not the same. That is normal.
:::
