::: tip
**For the adult:** Two slips are common. One is "after 4 heads, tails is due": a coin has no memory, so it is still 1/2. The other is expecting exactly 5 heads in 10 flips. Results vary, and get close to the theory over many trials.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 6/30 = **1/5 = 0.2 = 20%**.

**2.** Experimental: 8/40 = **0.2 = 20%**. Theory: P(red) = **1/4 = 25%**.
@viz bar data="red:8;blue:11;yellow:10;green:11" ymax=12 ystep=4 values=1 ylabel="times" height=80
**3.** **No.** The coin has no memory. P(tails) is still **1/2**.
@viz flips items="H,H,H,H,?"
**4.** 1/2 × 60 = **about 30 heads**.

**5.** 1/6 × 90 = 90 ÷ 6 = **about 15**.

**6.** **Not for sure.** 10 flips is only a few trials. Results vary. Flip many more times to find out.

**7.** **Yes.** Each player has 3/6 = **1/2**.
