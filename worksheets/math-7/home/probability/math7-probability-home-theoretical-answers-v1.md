::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is saying "it happens or it doesn't, so it's 50-50." Count the outcomes instead: a die has 6 faces, so P(roll a 4) = 1/6. A probability is never more than 1 (100%).
:::

## Answers

**1.** {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}

**2.** 1 and 2 match. P = 2/6 = **1/3**.
@viz dice size=26 labels="[[yes]];[[yes]];[[no]];[[no]];[[no]];[[no]]"
**3.** 5 counters, 3 blue. P(blue) = **3/5 = 0.6 = 60%**.
@viz bag counters="red:2;blue:3"
**4.** a) **impossible** b) **even chance** c) **likely** (5/6)

**5.** 8, 9 and 10 match. P = **3/10 = 0.3 = 30%**.

**6.** **No.** Only 1 of the 6 faces is a 4. P(roll a 4) = **1/6**.

**7.** 1 − 0.6 = 0.4 = **40%**.
