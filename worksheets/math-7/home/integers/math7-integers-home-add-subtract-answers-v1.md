::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is −3 + (−5) = 8. "Two negatives make a positive" is only for × and ÷. Adding two negatives goes further below 0: −8.
:::

## Answers

**1.** −3 + (−5) = **−8**. Same signs: 3 + 5 = 8, keep the − sign.

**2.** **−9 + 4 = −5**. There are 4 zero pairs. 5 red chips are left.
@viz counters pos=4 neg=9 pairs=4
**3.** 7 − (−2) = 7 + **2** = **9**.

**4.** −2 − 6 = −2 + (−6) = **−8**. 5 − 11 = 5 + (−11) = **−6**.

**5.** **−5**. Start at −1 and jump 4 to the left.
@viz numberline min=-7 max=2 width=330 keypoints="-1:start;-5:end"
**6.** **19 degrees.** End − start: 5 − (−14) = 5 + 14 = 19. From −14 up to 0 is 14, and 0 up to 5 is 5.
@viz numberline min=-15 max=6 step=1 every=5 points="-14:7 am;5:noon" width=400
**7.** 3 − (−4) = 3 + 4 = **7**. The answer 7 is bigger than 3, so Jo is wrong. Taking away a negative makes the answer bigger.
