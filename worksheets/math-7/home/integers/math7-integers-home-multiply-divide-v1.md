::: tip
**For the adult:** For × and ÷, count the negative signs. An even number of negatives gives a positive answer. An odd number gives a negative answer. Check a division with multiplication: −15 ÷ 3 = −5 because 3 × (−5) = −15.
:::

::: example
**Find −15 ÷ 3.**
Step 1: Share 15 red chips into 3 equal groups. Each group has 5 red chips.
@viz counters groups=3 each=-5 result="−15 ÷ 3 = −5"
Step 2: Check with multiplication: 3 × (−5) = −15. ✓
:::
