::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is using the sign of the bigger number: (−3) × 7 is not positive. Count the negatives: one negative, so −21.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 4 × (−6) = **−24**. Four groups of −6.

**2.** (−7)(−3) = **21**. Two negatives (even), so positive.

**3.** **4 × (−2) = −8**. Four jumps of −2 land on −8.
@viz numberline min=-10 max=1 jumps="0>-2>-4>-6>-8" width=360
**4.** (−2)(−1)(−5) = **−10**. Three negatives (odd), so negative. 2 × 1 × 5 = 10.

**5.** −42 ÷ 6 = **−7**. Check: 6 × **(−7)** = −42.

**6.** −36 ÷ (−9) = **4**. Same signs, so positive. Check: 4 × (−9) = −36.

**7.** **−3 °C each hour.** −18 ÷ 6 = −3. Check: 6 × (−3) = −18.
@viz numberline min=-20 max=2 step=1 every=2 points="0:9 pm;-18:3 am" width=420
**8.** Some answers: **(4)(−5)**, **(−4)(5)**, **(2)(−10)**, **(−1)(20)**. Each has one negative factor.
