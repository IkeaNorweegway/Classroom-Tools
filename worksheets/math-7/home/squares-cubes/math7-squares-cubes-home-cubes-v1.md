::: tip
**For the adult:** 2³ means 2 × 2 × 2 = 8, not 2 × 3. A cube root goes backwards: ∛27 = 3, because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. The volume of a cube is edge × edge × edge, in cubic units like cm³.
:::

::: example
**A cube has edges of 3 cm. Find its volume.**
Step 1: One layer is a square: 3 × 3 = 9 small cubes.
@viz cube n=3 label="3 × 3 × 3 = 27"
Step 2: There are 3 layers: 9 × 3 = 27. So V = 3³ = 27 cm³.
:::
