::: tip
**For the adult:** 5² means 5 × 5 = 25, not 5 × 2. A square root goes backwards: √25 = 5, because 5 × 5 = 25. Picture a square of tiles. The square is the number of tiles. The root is the length of one side.
:::

::: example
**Find 6² and √36.**
Step 1: 6² means 6 × 6. A 6 by 6 square has 36 tiles.
@viz sqside n=6 side="6" labels="6² = 6 × 6 = 36;√36 = 6" cell=12
Step 2: √36 asks: what number times itself makes 36? 6 × 6 = 36, so √36 = 6.
:::
