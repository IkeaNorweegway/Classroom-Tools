::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is multiplying by the exponent: 2³ is not 6 and 4³ is not 12. Another is mixing up the fence (perimeter, m) and the inside (area, m²) of a square.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 2³ = **8**. 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.
@viz cube n=2 label="2³ = [[8]]"
**2.** 4³ = **64**. 16 × 4 = 64.

**3.** ∛27 = **3**, because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27.

**4.** ∛125 = **5**, because 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

**5.** **125 cm³.** 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

**6.** **4 cm.** ∛64 = 4, because 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.

**7.** Fence: **28 m** (4 × 7). Area: **49 m²** (7 × 7).
@viz sqside n=7 side="7 m" fence=1 fill=plant labels="Fence = [[28 m]];Area = [[49 m²]]" cell=12
**8.** 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = **8**. Zoe did 2 × 3.
