::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is multiplying by the exponent: 9² is not 18. The second is halving for a root: √16 is not 8. Check any root by multiplying it by itself.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 4² = **16**. 4 × 4 = 16.

**2.** 9² = **81**. 9 × 9 = 81.

**3.** √49 = **7**, because 7 × 7 = 49.

**4.** **5 tiles.** √25 = 5, because 5 × 5 = 25.
@viz sqside n=5 side="[[5]]" labels="Area = 25 tiles" cell=12
**5.** √100 = **10**. Check: **10 × 10** = 100.

**6.** **16, 36 and 81** (4², 6² and 9²). 24 and 50 are not: no whole number times itself makes them.

**7.** **11 bales.** √121 = 11, because 11 × 11 = 121.
@viz sqside n=11 side="[[11]]" labels="121 bales" fill=sun cell=8
**8.** **No.** 8 × 8 = 64, not 16. √16 = **4**, because 4 × 4 = 16.
