::: tip
**For the adult:** Domain = all the inputs. Range = all the outputs. Say them in words: "whole numbers from 0 to 5" for counts, "any number from 0 to 4" for time or amounts. Real life sets limits, like a full bus.
:::

::: example
**A trough fills from 0 L to 100 L in 5 minutes. Find the domain and range.**
@viz coord xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=100 ystep=20 points="0,0;5,100" line=1 xlabel="minutes" ylabel="water (L)" width=220
Step 1: Look across at the inputs. Domain: any time from 0 to 5 minutes.
Step 2: Look up at the outputs. Range: any amount from 0 to 100 L.
:::
