::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slips are thinking two inputs can't share an output (they can), and joining the dots for things that are counted. Ask: "Can the input be 2.5?" If not, leave the dots apart.
:::

## Answers

**1.** a) **Yes.** Each input has one output. b) **No.** The input 4 has two outputs, 1 and 2.

**2.** **Dots.** You can't sell part of a ticket.

**3.** **Yes.** Each input has one arrow. 1 and 2 share the output 3, and that is allowed.
@viz mapping in="1,2,3" out="3,5" arrows="1>3;2>3;3>5"
**4.** 1 × 3 = **3**, 2 × 3 = **6**, 4 × 3 = **12** dollars.
@viz machine in="1;2;4" rule="× 3" out="[[3]];[[6]];[[12]]" inlabel="rides" outlabel="cost ($)"
**5.** **0, 20, 40 and 60 L.** Plot (0, 0), (1, 20), (2, 40), (3, 60). **A line:** time can be any amount.
@viz coord xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=60 ystep=20 keypoints="0,0;1,20;2,40;3,60" line=1 xlabel="minutes" ylabel="water (L)" width=220 cell=22
**6.** Input: **the litres of gas**. Output: **the cost**. The cost depends on the litres.
