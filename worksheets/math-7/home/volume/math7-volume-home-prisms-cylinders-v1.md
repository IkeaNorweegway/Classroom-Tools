::: tip
**For the adult:** Volume = base area × height. The base is the shape at each end. Triangle: ½ × base × the triangle's height. Circle: 3.14 × radius × radius. The height h is the distance from one end to the other. To go backwards, divide: height = volume ÷ base area.
:::

::: example
**A tent's triangle end is 2 m wide and 1.5 m high. The tent is 3 m long. Find its volume.**
@viz solid kind=tri along=z b="2 m" th="1.5 m" l="3 m" bv=2 thv=1.5 lv=3 blabel="B" fill=plant size=150
Step 1: Base area: B = ½ × 2 × 1.5 = 1.5 m².
Step 2: V = B × h = 1.5 × 3 = 4.5 m³. (The 3 m is the length, end to end.)
:::
