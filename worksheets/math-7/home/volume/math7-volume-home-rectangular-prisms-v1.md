::: tip
**For the adult:** Volume is the space inside a box. We count it in cubes, so the units are cm³ or m³. One layer of cubes covers the bottom: length × width. The number of layers is the height. So volume = length × width × height.
:::

::: example
**A juice box is 6 cm long, 4 cm wide and 10 cm tall. Find its volume.**
@viz solid kind=box l="6 cm" w="4 cm" h="10 cm" lv=6 wv=4 hv=10 slices=10 size=110
Step 1: One layer: 6 × 4 = 24 cubes.
Step 2: 10 layers: 24 × 10 = 240. The volume is 240 cm³.
:::
