## Answers

**1.** B = ½ × 4 × 3 = 6 cm². V = 6 × 10 = **60 cm³**.

**2.** B ≈ 3.14 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 cm². V ≈ 78.5 × 10 = **785 cm³**.
@viz solid kind=cyl r="r = 5 cm" h="h = 10 cm" rv=5 hv=10 blabel="B" size=90
**3.** 3.8 × 3.8 = 14.44. B ≈ 3.14 × 14.44 = 45.3416 cm². V ≈ 45.3416 × 2.5 = 113.354, about **113.4 cm³**.

**4.** r = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 m. B ≈ 3.14 × 3 × 3 = 28.26 m². V ≈ 28.26 × 4 = 113.04, about **113.0 m³**.

**5.** h = V ÷ B = 60 ÷ 12 = **5 cm**.

**6.** B = V ÷ h = 200 ÷ 2 = **100 m²**.

**7.** 40 cm = 0.4 m and 50 cm = 0.5 m. V = 2 × 0.4 × 0.5 = **0.4 m³**.
