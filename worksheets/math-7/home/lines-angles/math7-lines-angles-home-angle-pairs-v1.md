::: tip
**For the adult:** When two lines cross, angles across from each other (opposite angles) are equal. Two angles side by side on a straight line add to 180°. Matching arrowheads mean two lines are parallel. Then a line crossing both makes equal angles in the same spot at each crossing (corresponding angles).
:::

::: example
**Two lines cross. One angle is 40°. Find the other three.**
@viz angles kind=intersect a=40 labels="40°;140°;40°;140°" highlight="1,3"
Step 1: The angle beside 40° on a line: 180° − 40° = 140°.
Step 2: Opposite angles are equal. Across from 40° is 40°. Across from 140° is 140°.
:::
