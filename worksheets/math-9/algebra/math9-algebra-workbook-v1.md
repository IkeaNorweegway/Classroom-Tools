# Algebra — Practice Workbook
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum · Interleaved practice

**About this workbook:** Questions are mixed up on purpose. Linear relations, linear equations and inequalities, and polynomials all appear together — in scrambled order. Figuring out which skill each question needs is part of the work.

★ Core — attempt all. Some include a hint, word bank, or sentence starter.
★★ Extended — same ideas, higher demand. Explain, find errors, or apply to a new situation.

Show all working. A correct answer with no working earns half marks.
