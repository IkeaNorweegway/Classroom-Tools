Polynomials

THE BIG IDEA

Only like terms combine — same variable, same exponent. Everything else stays separate.

Subtracting Polynomials

Distribute the negative sign to every term in the second bracket, then collect like terms.

(a − b) − (c − d) = a − b − c + d

Multiplying binomials: every term in the first bracket × every term in the second (4 partial products — use the area model).

WORKED EXAMPLE

Find: (4x² + 3x − 1) − (2x² − x + 6)

Distribute the negative:   4x² + 3x − 1 − 2x² + x − 6

Collect:   (4−2)x² + (3+1)x + (−1−6)

Result:   2x² + 4x − 7

⚠ WATCH OUT FOR
MISTAKE(x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 6
FIXNeeds 4 partial products: x² + 5x + 6
MISTAKEx × x = 2x
FIXx × x = x²
MISTAKE−(2x² − x) = −2x² − x
FIXFlip all the signs: −2x² + x
✅ SELF-CHECK

Can you expand (2x − 3)(x + 5) using the area model? Can you subtract (4x² − x + 2) from (6x² + 3x − 1)?
