# Polynomials — Practice Workbook (Sample)
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum · Interleaved practice

**About this workbook:** Questions are mixed up on purpose. You will not know which polynomial skill each question is testing until you read it. Figuring that out is part of the work.

★ Core — attempt all. Some include a hint or sentence starter.
★★ Extended — same ideas, higher demand. Explain, find errors, or apply to a new context. Attempt after finishing Core.

Show all working. A correct answer with no working earns half marks.
