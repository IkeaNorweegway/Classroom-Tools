# Polynomials — Core Worksheet Answer Key ★
Grade 9 Mathematics · Teacher Copy — Not for Students

*Cover the answer. Try the question. Then check.*

---

**1.** Name each polynomial

| Expression | Type | Degree | Leading coefficient |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7x − 3 | **binomial** | **1** | **7** |
| 4x³ + x − 2 | **trinomial** | **3** | **4** |
| −9 | **monomial** | **0** | **−9** |
| 5x² − 3x + 1 | **trinomial** | **2** | **5** |

---

**2.** Identify like terms and combine

**(a) 3x² + 5x − 2x² + 4 − x:**

> Like term groups: {3x², −2x²} and {5x, −x} and {4}
> 3x²−2x²=x² | 5x−x=4x | constant=4
> **Simplified: x²+4x+4**

**(b) 6x² − 3x + 4x² + 2x − 9 + x²:**

> x² terms: 6x²+4x²+x² = 11x²
> x terms: −3x+2x = −x
> Constants: −9
> **Simplified: 11x²−x−9**

---

**3.** Add polynomials

**(a) (2x² + 3x − 1) + (x² − 5x + 4):**

> x² terms: 2x²+x² = **3x²**
> x terms: 3x+(−5x) = **−2x**
> Constants: −1+4 = **3**
> **Sum: 3x²−2x+3**

**(b) (x² + 2x) + (3x − 4) + (−x² + 1):**

> x²: x²+(−x²) = 0. x: 2x+3x=5x. Constants: −4+1=−3.
> **Sum: 5x−3**

---

**4.** Subtract — distribute the negative first

**(a) (5x² + 2x − 3) − (2x² − x + 1):**

> Distribute: 5x²+2x−3 **− 2x² + x − 1**
> **Simplified: 3x²+3x−4**

*Common mistake:* −(−x) = +x. Don't keep it as −x.

**(b) (3x + 7) − (5x − 2):**

> Distribute: 3x+7−5x+2
> **Simplified: −2x+9**

**(c) Subtract (x²−4x+2) from (3x²+x−5):**

> "From B" means B − A: (3x²+x−5)−(x²−4x+2)
> = 3x²+x−5−x²+4x−2
> **= 2x²+5x−7**

---

**5.** Monomial × polynomial

**(a) 4x(3x² − 2x + 1):**

> = 4x(3x²) + 4x(−2x) + 4x(1) = **12x³ − 8x² + 4x**

**(b) −3x(x² + 4x − 5):**

> = **−3x³ − 12x² + 15x**

**(c) 2x²(x − 3):**

> = **2x³ − 6x²**

---

**6.** Binomial × binomial — area model

**(a) (x + 5)(x + 2):**

| × | x | +2 |
|---|---|---|
| x | **x²** | **2x** |
| +5 | **5x** | **10** |

Sum: x²+2x+5x+10 | **Simplified: x²+7x+10**

**(b) (2x + 3)(x − 4):**

| × | x | −4 |
|---|---|---|
| 2x | **2x²** | **−8x** |
| +3 | **3x** | **−12** |

**Simplified: 2x²−5x−12**

**(c) (x − 1)(x − 6):**

> = x²−6x−x+6 = **x²−7x+6**

---

**7.** Error correction

**(a) −(3x²−2x+1) = −3x²−2x+1:**

> Error: sign of −2x not flipped. −(−2x) = +2x.
> **Corrected: −3x²+2x−1**

**(b) 5x × 2x = 10x:**

> Error: only doubled the coefficient — didn't multiply the variables (x×x = x²).
> **Corrected: 10x²**

**(c) (x+4)² = x²+16:**

> Error: missed the middle term. (x+4)(x+4) has four partial products.
> **Corrected: x²+8x+16**

---

**8.** Alberta context — canola field

**(a) Perimeter:**

> 2(2x+3) + 2(x²+4x−1)
> = 4x+6 + 2x²+8x−2
> **= 2x²+12x+4**

**(b) Degree of area (2x+3)(x²+4x−1):**

> **Degree: 3** — the highest-degree terms multiply as (2x)(x²) = 2x³. Degree of product = sum of degrees of factors.

---

**9.** Expand and simplify

**(a) 3x(x+2) + x(x−4):**

> = 3x²+6x + x²−4x = **4x²+2x**

**(b) 2(x+1)(x+3):**

> (x+1)(x+3) = x²+4x+3
> × 2: **2x²+8x+6**

**(c) (x+2)² − (x−1)²:**

> (x+2)² = **x²+4x+4** | (x−1)² = **x²−2x+1**
> Difference: (x²+4x+4)−(x²−2x+1) = **6x+3**

---

**10.** Rectangular garden with square cut

**(a) Area of garden (before cut):**

> (x+7)(x−2) = x²+5x−14 → **x²+5x−14** m²

**(b) Area of square cut:**

> **x²** m²

**(c) Remaining area:**

> (x²+5x−14) − x² = **5x−14** m²

*Note:* The remaining area is linear — once you subtract x² from x², only the boundary terms remain.
