# Polynomials — Concept Summary
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

---

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Polynomial | An expression with one or more terms joined by + or − | 3x² − 2x + 5 |
| Term | A number, variable, or their product | −2x is a term |
| Coefficient | The number in front of the variable | In −2x, coefficient = −2 |
| Degree (of a term) | The exponent on the variable | 5x³ has degree 3 |
| Degree (of a polynomial) | The highest degree among all terms | 3x² − 2x + 5 has degree 2 |
| Like terms | Terms with the same variable raised to the same power | 3x² and −7x² are like terms |
| Standard form | Terms ordered from highest to lowest degree | 4x³ − 2x² + x − 1 |

---

## Key Rules

**Adding:** Remove brackets, collect like terms.
> (3x² + 2x) + (x² − 5x) = 4x² − 3x

**Subtracting:** Distribute the negative sign to EVERY term in the second bracket, then collect.
> (3x² + 2x) − (x² − 5x) = 3x² + 2x − x² + 5x = 2x² + 7x

**Multiplying — monomial × polynomial:** Multiply the monomial by every term inside.
> 3x(2x² − x + 4) = 6x³ − 3x² + 12x

**Multiplying powers:** x^m × x^n = x^(m+n) &nbsp;&nbsp; → &nbsp;&nbsp; x × x = x² (NOT 2x)

**Multiplying — binomial × binomial:** Every term in the first bracket multiplies every term in the second (4 partial products). Use the area model.

---

## Worked Example 1 — Subtracting polynomials

**Find: (4x² + 3x − 1) − (2x² − x + 6)**

> Distribute the negative: 4x² + 3x − 1 **− 2x² + x − 6**
>
> Collect: (4−2)x² + (3+1)x + (−1−6) = **2x² + 4x − 7**

*The most common error: only flipping the sign on the first term inside the bracket.*

---

## Worked Example 2 — Monomial × polynomial

**Expand: −2x(x² − 3x + 5)**

> −2x × x² = −2x³
>
> −2x × (−3x) = +6x²
>
> −2x × 5 = −10x
>
> Result: **−2x³ + 6x² − 10x**

---

## Worked Example 3 — Binomial × binomial (area model)

**Expand: (x + 3)(2x − 1)**

| × | 2x | −1 |
|---|---|---|
| x | 2x² | −x |
| +3 | 6x | −3 |

Sum: 2x² − x + 6x − 3 = **2x² + 5x − 3**

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 6 | Four partial products needed: x² + 5x + 6 |
| (3x)² = 3x² | Apply exponent to both: 3² × x² = 9x² |
| x × x = 2x | Multiplication of x's: x × x = x² |
| −(2x² − x) = −2x² − x | Flip ALL signs: −2x² + x |
| 3x² + 2x = 5x³ | Cannot add unlike terms |
