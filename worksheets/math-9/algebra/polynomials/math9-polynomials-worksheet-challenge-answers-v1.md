**1.** Error analysis

**(a) (3x²+2x−1)−(x²−4x+3) = 3x²+2x−1 − x²−4x+3 = 2x²−2x+2:**

> **Error:** Failed to flip the sign of −4x when distributing the negative. −(−4x) = +4x, not −4x.
>
> **Setup:** = 3x²+2x−1−x²**+4x**−3
>
> **Correct answer: 2x²+6x−4**

**(b) 3x(x+2)² = 3x(x²+4) = 3x³+12x:**

> **Error:** (x+2)² was computed as x²+4 — the middle term 4x was missed. Correct expansion: (x+2)²= x²+4x+4.
>
> **Setup:** 3x(x²+4x+4) = 3x³+12x²+12x
>
> **Correct answer: 3x³+12x²+12x**

**(c) (x+4)(x−4) = x²−8x−16:**

> **Error:** Computed cross-terms incorrectly as −8x. In (x+4)(x−4), the cross-terms are x(−4)+4(x) = −4x+4x = 0. There is no middle term.
>
> **Correct answer: x²−16** (difference of squares pattern)

**2.** What polynomial must be subtracted?

> Set up: (5x²+3x−2) − P = (2x²−x+4)
> Rearrange: P = (5x²+3x−2) − (2x²−x+4)
>
> **Setup:** 5x²+3x−2−2x²+x−4 = 3x²+4x−6
>
> **Final answer: P = 3x²+4x−6**
>
> **Verify:** (5x²+3x−2)−(3x²+4x−6) = 2x²−x+4 ✓

**3.** Expand and simplify

**(a) (2x+3)² − (x−1)²:**

> **Setup:**
> (2x+3)² = 4x²+12x+9
> (x−1)² = x²−2x+1
> Subtract: 3x²+14x+8
>
> **Final answer: 3x²+14x+8**

**(b) (x+2)(x−2) + (x+3)²:**

> **Setup:**
> (x+2)(x−2) = x²−4 (difference of squares)
> (x+3)² = x²+6x+9
> Sum: 2x²+6x+5
>
> **Final answer: 2x²+6x+5**

**(c) 3x(x+1) − (x−2)(2x+1):**

> **Setup:**
> 3x(x+1) = 3x²+3x
> (x−2)(2x+1) = 2x²+x−4x−2 = 2x²−3x−2
> Subtract: (3x²+3x)−(2x²−3x−2) = x²+6x+2
>
> **Final answer: x²+6x+2**

**4.** Swimming pool with path

**(a) Total dimensions** (pool + 2m path on all sides):

> Length: **(x+12) m** [x+8+2+2]
> Width: **(x+7) m** [x+3+2+2]

**(b) Total area:**

> **Setup:** (x+12)(x+7) = x²+7x+12x+84
>
> **= x²+19x+84**

**(c) Pool area:**

> **Setup:** (x+8)(x+3) = x²+3x+8x+24
>
> **= x²+11x+24**

**(d) Path area:**

> **Setup:** Total − Pool = (x²+19x+84)−(x²+11x+24)
>
> **= 8x+60**

**(e) Degree and meaning:**

> **Degree 1** (linear). The path area depends linearly on x because the path width (2 m) is fixed — adding more perimeter width scales area proportionally, not quadratically. The x² terms cancel because both the total area and pool area grow at the same quadratic rate; only the perimeter-based difference remains.

**5.** Proof: (x+a)(x−a) = x²−a²

> **(x+a)(x−a)** = x²−ax+ax−a² = **x²−a²** ✓
> The middle terms cancel because they are equal and opposite.

**(a) 101 × 99 = (100+1)(100−1) = 100²−1² = 10000−1 = **9999****

**(b) 52 × 48 = (50+2)(50−2) = 2500−4 = **2496****

**(c) 97 × 97:**

> This is (97)² — a square, NOT a product of the form (x+a)(x−a). The pattern requires two different numbers equidistant from a centre value.
> Use instead: (100−3)² = 10000−600+9 = **9409**

**6.** Find the value of k

**(a) (x+3)(x+k) = x²+7x+12:**

> **Setup:** Expand left side: x²+(3+k)x+3k. Compare x-terms: 3+k=7 → **k=4**
> Verify constant: 3(4) = 12 ✓

**(b) (2x+k)(x−3) = 2x²+x−15:**

> *Note: There is an inconsistency in this question as written. If 2x²+x−15 is correct, then matching x-coefficients gives k−6=1 → k=7, but matching constants gives −3k=−15 → k=5. These are inconsistent.*
>
> *The intended polynomial is likely 2x²−x−15:*
> (2x+k)(x−3): x-coeff: k−6=−1 → k=5. Constant: −3(5)=−15 ✓
> **k = 5**
>
> *(If students get an inconsistent result, discuss how expanding and matching coefficients provides a system of equations — both conditions must be satisfied.)*

**7.** Disproving (a+b)² = a²+b²

**(a) a=3, b=4:**

> Left: (3+4)² = **49**
> Right: 3²+4² = 9+16 = **25**
> Equal? **No — 49 ≠ 25**

**(b) Correct expansion:**

> (a+b)² = (a+b)(a+b) = a²+ab+ab+b² = **a²+2ab+b²**

**(c) The missing term:**

> The missing term is **2ab** — called the "cross product" or "middle term."
> It appears because multiplying two binomials produces FOUR partial products, not two. The inner and outer terms (a×b and b×a) both equal ab, and together they sum to 2ab.

**8.** Reverse engineering: 2x³+5x²−x−6

**(a) Monomial × something:**

> The GCF of all coefficients is 1, so no monomial factor other than 1 divides all terms. The simplest monomial form is **1 × (2x³+5x²−x−6)**, which is trivial.
>
> *(If students factor x from the first two terms and −1 from the last two: x²(2x+5) − (x+6) — doesn't factor cleanly. The problem may intend part (b) is the primary task.)*

**(b) Binomial × trinomial:**

> Try (x+2) as a factor: 2x³+5x²−x−6 ÷ (x+2):
> (x+2)(2x²+x−3)
>
> Verify: (x+2)(2x²+x−3) = 2x³+x²−3x+4x²+2x−6 = 2x³+5x²−x−6 ✓
>
> **Final answer: (x+2)(2x²+x−3)**
