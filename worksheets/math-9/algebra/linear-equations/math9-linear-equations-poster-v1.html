Linear Equations
& Inequalities

THE BIG IDEA

Every equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other — until x stands alone.

Six Steps to Solve
  1. Distribute — expand any brackets
  2. Combine like terms on the same side
  3. Move variables to one side
  4. Move constants to the other side
  5. Divide to isolate the variable
  6. Verify by substituting back

Inequalities: solve exactly the same way — except flip the sign when you multiply or divide by a negative.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Solve: 4(x − 3) = 2x + 6

Distribute:   4x − 12 = 2x + 6

Move variables:   2x − 12 = 6

Move constants:   2x = 18

Divide:   x = 9

Verify:   4(6) = 24    2(9)+6 = 24 ✓

⚠ WATCH OUT FOR
MISTAKEDistributing to only the first term
FIXMultiply every term inside the brackets
MISTAKENot verifying the answer
FIXAlways substitute back into the original equation
MISTAKEFlipping the sign when adding or subtracting
FIXOnly flip when multiplying or dividing by a negative
✅ SELF-CHECK

Can you solve 2(3x − 1) = −(x + 4) and verify? Can you solve −5x < 20 and graph it?
