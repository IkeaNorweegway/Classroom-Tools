# Linear Equations and Inequalities — Concept Summary
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

---

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Equation | Two expressions set equal | 3x + 5 = 14 |
| Solution | Value that makes the equation true | x = 3 |
| Verify | Substitute back into the original equation to check | 3(3) + 5 = 14 ✓ |
| Inequality | Two expressions compared with <, >, ≤, or ≥ | 2x + 1 > 7 |
| LCD | Least common denominator — use to clear fractions | LCD of 1/3 and 1/4 is 12 |
| Solution set | All values satisfying an inequality | x > 3 |

---

## Solving Equations — Step Order

1. **Distribute** — expand any brackets
2. **Combine like terms** on the same side
3. **Move variables** to one side (add/subtract)
4. **Move constants** to the other side
5. **Divide** to isolate the variable
6. **Verify** by substituting back

**To clear fractions:** Multiply every term by the LCD before doing anything else.

---

## Inequalities — the one new rule

Solving works identically to equations — EXCEPT:

> **When you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.**

This is the only difference. All other steps are the same.

---

## Worked Example 1 — Multi-step equation

**Solve: 4(x − 3) = 2x + 6**

> Distribute: 4x − 12 = 2x + 6
>
> Move variables: 2x − 12 = 6
>
> Move constants: 2x = 18
>
> Divide: **x = 9**
>
> Verify: 4(9 − 3) = 4(6) = 24 &nbsp;&nbsp; 2(9) + 6 = 24 ✓

---

## Worked Example 2 — Rational coefficients

**Solve: x/3 + 1/4 = 5/12**

> LCD of 3, 4, 12 = **12**
>
> Multiply all terms by 12: 4x + 3 = 5
>
> Solve: 4x = 2 → **x = 1/2**

---

## Worked Example 3 — Inequality (sign flip)

**Solve: −3x + 6 ≤ 0**

> −3x ≤ −6
>
> Divide by −3 and **FLIP**: **x ≥ 2**

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Distributing only to the first term: 3(x + 4) = 3x + 4 | Multiply every term inside: 3(x + 4) = 3x + **12** |
| Not verifying — missing the sign error | Always substitute back into the original |
| Flipping inequality when adding/subtracting | Only flip when **multiplying or dividing by a negative** |
| Not multiplying every term by LCD | Every single term on both sides must be multiplied |
