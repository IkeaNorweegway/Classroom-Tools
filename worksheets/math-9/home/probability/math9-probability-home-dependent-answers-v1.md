::: tip
**For the adult:** In each dependent question, both the top and the bottom of the second fraction go down by one.
:::

**1.** 2/5, then **1** green left out of **4**, so 1/4. P(both) = 2/5 × 1/4 = 2/20 = **1/10**

**2.** 2/5 × 2/5 = **4/25**

**3.** 13/52 × 12/51 = 156/2652 = **1/17**

**4.** 3/8 × 2/7 = 6/56 = **3/28**

**5.** (a) **independent** (b) **dependent** (c) **independent** (d) **dependent**

**6.** 4/12 × 3/11 = 12/132 = **1/11**
