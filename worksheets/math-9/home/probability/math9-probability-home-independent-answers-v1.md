::: tip
**For the adult:** Equivalent answers (like 3/12 instead of 1/4) are correct. Simplifying is a bonus.
:::

**1.** 1/2 × 3/6 = 3/12 = **1/4**

**2.** **1/36**

**3.** P(less than 3) = 2/6 each roll: 2/6 × 2/6 = 4/36 = **1/9**

**4.** P(odd) = 3/5: 3/5 × 1/2 = **3/10**

**5.** **1/2**. Each flip is independent; the coin doesn't remember the tails.

**6.** 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12 = **12%**
