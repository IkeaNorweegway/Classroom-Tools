::: tip
**For the adult:** In shadow problems, height ÷ shadow is the same for every object at the same moment. Encourage a quick sketch of both triangles.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A person 1.6 m tall casts a 2 m shadow. A flagpole casts a 15 m shadow at the same time. How tall is the flagpole?**

1. Same sun angle, so the triangles are similar: h/15 = 1.6/2
2. Multiply both sides by 15: h = 15 × 0.8
3. h = 12 m
:::
