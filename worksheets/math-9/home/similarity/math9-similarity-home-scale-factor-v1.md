::: tip
**For the adult:** Scale factor = new length ÷ original length. More than 1 is an enlargement; less than 1 is a reduction.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A 4 cm by 6 cm rectangle is enlarged so the 6 cm side becomes 15 cm. How long is the other side?**

1. Scale factor: 15 ÷ 6 = 2.5
2. Multiply the other side: 4 × 2.5 = 10 cm
3. Check: the copy is bigger, and 10 is bigger than 4. ✓
:::
