::: tip
**For the adult:** Matching sides must correspond: shortest with shortest, longest with longest.
:::

**1.** **6, 9 and 12 cm**

**2.** 2 ÷ 8 = **0.25**, a **reduction**

**3.** Scale factor 15 ÷ 5 = 3, so **21 cm and 27 cm**

**4.** Scale factor 25 ÷ 10 = 2.5, so 15 × 2.5 = **37.5 cm**

**5.** Scale factor 12 ÷ 9 = 4/3, so x = 6 × 4/3 = **8**

**6.** 18 × 24 = 432 cm = **4.32 m**
