::: tip
**For the adult:** A quick check is to put x = 1 into the question and into the answer. Both should give the same number.
:::

**1.** 5x and 4: **5x + 4**

**2.** x² + 4x − 3x² **+** 2x = **−2x² + 6x**

**3.** (5x² − 2x²) + (−3x + x) + (2 − 6) = **3x² − 2x − 4**

**4.** 6x − 4 − 2x + 9 = **4x + 5**

**5.** 3x² + x − 7 − x² + 4x + 2 = **2x² + 5x − 5**

**6.** (3x + 2) + (x − 1) + (3x + 2) + (x − 1) = **8x + 2**
