::: tip
**For the adult:** Encourage a 2 × 2 box for every question: one bracket down the side, the other across the top, then fill in all four cells.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Expand (x + 4)(x − 2).**

1. Box: x and 4 down the side, x and −2 across the top.
2. Cells: x · x = x², x · (−2) = −2x, 4 · x = 4x, 4 · (−2) = −8
3. Add and combine: x² − 2x + 4x − 8 = x² + 2x − 8
4. Check with x = 1: (5)(−1) = −5, and 1 + 2 − 8 = −5 ✓
:::
