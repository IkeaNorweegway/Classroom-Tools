**2.** **No correlation.** Shoe size has nothing to do with math marks.

**3.** (a) **positive** (b) **negative** (c) **none**

**4.** Between 22 (3 years) and 17 (5 years): about **$19 500**. It's **interpolation** (inside the data).

**5.** That's **extrapolation**: 15 years is far outside the data (1 to 9 years). A car's value can't be negative, so the trend can't continue forever.
