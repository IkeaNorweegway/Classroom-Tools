::: tip
**For the adult:** Ask "When one goes up, what does the other do?" Up together is positive, one up and one down is negative, and no pattern is no correlation.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Temperature (°C): 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Iced drinks sold: 12, 20, 31, 38, 50.**

1. As the temperature goes up, sales go up: a positive correlation.
2. The values rise steadily, so it's a strong trend.
3. Predict for 22°C: between 31 (20°C) and 38 (25°C), about 34 drinks.
4. 22°C is inside the data, so this is interpolation and it's reasonable.
:::
