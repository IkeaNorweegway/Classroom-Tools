::: tip
**For the adult:** An even number of negatives multiplied together is positive; an odd number is negative.
:::

**1.** (−4)² = **16**

**2.** −4² = **−16**

**3.** (−2)³ = (−2)(−2)(−2) = **−8**, and −2³ = −(2 × 2 × 2) = **−8**
- They happen to match because there are three negatives (an odd number), which makes the answer negative anyway. With an even exponent they would be different.

**4.** **7⁰ = 1** and **(−5)⁰ = 1**

**5.** 2 + 3 × 8 = 2 + 24 = **26**

**6.** −1 + 16 ÷ 8 = −1 + 2 = **1**

**7.** **10⁻² = 1/100** (or 0.01), and **5⁻¹ = 1/5**
