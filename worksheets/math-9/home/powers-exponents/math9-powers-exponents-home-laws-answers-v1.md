::: tip
**For the adult:** Writing the answer as a power first, then as a number, is exactly what the class does.
:::

**1.** 4² × 4³ = **4⁵ = 1024**

**2.** 7⁶ ÷ 7⁴ = **7² = 49**

**3.** (2³)² = 2³ × 2³ = **2⁶ = 64**
- Power of a power: multiply the exponents, 3 × 2 = 6

**4.** (5⁴ × 5¹) ÷ 5³ = 5⁵ ÷ 5³ = **5² = 25**

**5.** (10² × 10⁵) ÷ (10³)²
- Top: 10⁷. Bottom: 10⁶
- 10⁷ ÷ 10⁶ = **10¹ = 10**

**6.** 2⁸ ÷ 2⁵ = **2³ = 8 copies**
- Check: 256 GB ÷ 32 GB = 8
