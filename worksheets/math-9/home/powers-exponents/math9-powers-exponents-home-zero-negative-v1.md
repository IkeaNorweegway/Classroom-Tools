::: tip
**For the adult:** For every question, ask "What is the exponent attached to?" A negative sign only counts as part of the base when it's inside brackets.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Evaluate −3² + (−3)² + 3⁰.**

1. −3² means −(3 × 3) = −9
2. (−3)² means (−3) × (−3) = 9
3. 3⁰ = 1 (any non-zero number to the power 0 is 1)
4. Add: −9 + 9 + 1 = 1
:::
