## Worked example

::: example
**Simplify (3⁴ × 3²) ÷ 3³, then find its value.**

1. Multiplying, same base: add the exponents. 3⁴ × 3² = 3⁶
2. Dividing, same base: subtract the exponents. 3⁶ ÷ 3³ = 3³
3. Value: 3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27
:::
