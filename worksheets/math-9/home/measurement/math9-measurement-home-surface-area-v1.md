::: tip
**For the adult:** Surface area is everything you could paint on the outside. Where two shapes are stuck together, those faces are hidden, so they aren't counted. Cone side = πrs, where s is the slant height.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A cylinder (radius 3 cm, height 8 cm) has a cone on top (radius 3 cm, slant height 5 cm). Find the outside surface area.**

1. Bottom circle: πr² = 9π
2. Cylinder side: 2πrh = 2π(3)(8) = 48π
3. Cone side: πrs = π(3)(5) = 15π
4. The top of the cylinder is hidden under the cone, so it isn't counted.
5. Total: 9π + 48π + 15π = 72π ≈ 226.2 cm²
:::
