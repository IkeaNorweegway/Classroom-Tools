title: Surface Area of Combined Shapes
topic: measurement
strand: measurement-geometry
skill: finding surface area and leaving out hidden faces
time: about 15 minutes
---

::: tip
**For the adult:** Answers are rounded to one decimal place. Answers in terms of π are also correct.
:::

**1.** 2π(1)² + 2π(1)(4) = 2π + 8π = **10π ≈ 31.4 cm²**

**2.** Each face 4 cm². 2 faces hidden, 10 showing: **40 cm²**

**3.** 2(10 × 4) + 2(10 × 3) + 2(4 × 3) = 80 + 60 + 24 = **164 cm²**

**4.** Base π(5)² = 25π. Side π(5)(13) = 65π. Total **90π ≈ 282.7 cm²**

**5.** Bottom π(4)² = 16π. Side 2π(4)(10) = 80π. No top. Total **96π ≈ 301.6 cm²**

**6.** Walls 2π(3)(6) = 36π. Roof π(3)(5) = 15π. Total **51π ≈ 160.2 m²**
