::: tip
**For the adult:** Answers are rounded to one decimal place. Answers left in terms of π (like 20π) are also correct.
:::

**1.** π × 2² × 5 = **20π ≈ 62.8 cm³**

**2.** ⅓ × π × 3² × 7 = **21π ≈ 66.0 cm³**

**3.** Radius = 5 cm. π × 25 × 12 = **300π ≈ 942.5 cm³**

**4.** 4/3 × π × 27 = **36π ≈ 113.1 cm³**

**5.** ⅓ × 36 × 10 = **120 cm³**

**6.** Cup: ⅓ × π × 16 × 9 = 48π ≈ 150.8 cm³. Glass: π × 16 × 9 = 144π ≈ 452.4 cm³.
- 144π ÷ 48π = **3 cups**
