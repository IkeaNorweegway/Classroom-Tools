::: tip
**For the adult:** Formulas: cylinder V = πr²h, cone V = ⅓πr²h, pyramid V = ⅓ × base area × h, sphere V = 4/3πr³. Check that your child uses the radius, not the diameter.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find the volume of a ball (sphere) with radius 6 cm.**

1. Formula: V = 4/3πr³
2. Substitute: V = 4/3 × π × 6³ = 4/3 × π × 216
3. V = 288π ≈ 904.8 cm³
:::
