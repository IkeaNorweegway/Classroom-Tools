::: tip
**For the adult:** Have your child decide the sign first, then work with the numbers. Same signs give a positive answer; different signs give a negative one.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Calculate (−3/5) ÷ (−9/10).**

1. Sign: a negative divided by a negative is positive.
2. Keep the first fraction, flip the second, and multiply: 3/5 × 10/9
3. Multiply and simplify: 30/45 = 2/3
:::
