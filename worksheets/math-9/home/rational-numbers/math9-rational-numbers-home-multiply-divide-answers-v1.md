::: tip
**For the adult:** Simplifying before multiplying (cross-cancelling) is fine and often easier. The answer should be the same.
:::

**1.** (−1/2) × 4/5
- Sign: negative
- 4/10 = **−2/5**

**2.** 3/4 ÷ (−1/2)
- Sign: negative
- 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = **−3/2** (or −1½)

**3.** (−5/6) × (−3/10)
- Sign: positive
- 15/60 = **1/4**

**4.** (−7/8) ÷ 7/4
- Sign: negative
- 7/8 × 4/7 = 4/8 = **−1/2**

**5.** (−0.4) × 2.5 = **−1**

**6.** 4½ ÷ 3/4
- 9/2 × 4/3 = 36/6 = **6 batches**
