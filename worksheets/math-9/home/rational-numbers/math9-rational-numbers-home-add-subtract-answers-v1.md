::: tip
**For the adult:** Answers in lowest terms are best, but an equivalent fraction (like 3/12 instead of 1/4) shows the right idea.
:::

**1.** −1/2 + 3/4
- −2/4 + 3/4 = **1/4**

**2.** 2/3 − 5/6
- 4/6 − 5/6 = **−1/6**

**3.** −3/5 − 1/10
- −6/10 − 1/10 = **−7/10**

**4.** −1/4 − (−2/3)
- Same as −1/4 + 2/3
- −3/12 + 8/12 = **5/12**

**5.** 1.5 + (−2.75)
- Same as 1.5 − 2.75 = **−1.25**

**6.** −12.5 + 8.75 = **−3.75°C**
- Still below zero, which makes sense: it warmed by less than 12.5 degrees.
