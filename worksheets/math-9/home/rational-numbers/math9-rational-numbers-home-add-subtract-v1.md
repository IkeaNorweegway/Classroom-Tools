::: tip
**For the adult:** Before each question, ask "Will the answer be positive or negative?" Checking against that guess catches most sign mistakes.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Calculate −3/4 + 5/6.**

1. Common denominator: 4 and 6 both go into 12, so −9/12 + 10/12
2. Add the numerators: −9 + 10 = 1, so the answer is 1/12
3. Check the sign: 5/6 is bigger than 3/4, so a small positive answer makes sense. ✓
:::
