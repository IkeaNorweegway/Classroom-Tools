**1.** 80 ÷ 2 = **40°**

**2.** 35 × 2 = **70°**

**3.** **Angle ACB = 90°** (it stands on a diameter)
- **Angle ABC = 180° − 90° − 28° = 62°**

**4.** **47°**. Angles at the edge standing on the same arc are equal, because each is half the same centre angle.

**5.** Triangle AOB is isosceles (two radii), so angle OAB = (180° − 100°) ÷ 2 = **40°**

**6.** A diameter is a straight line through the centre, so the "angle at the centre" is a straight angle, 180°. The edge angle is half of that: **180° ÷ 2 = 90°**.
