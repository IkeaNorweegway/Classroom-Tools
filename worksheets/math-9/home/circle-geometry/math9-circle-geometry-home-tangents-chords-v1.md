## Worked example

::: example
**A circle has radius 13 cm and a chord 24 cm long. How far is the chord from the centre?**

1. The line from the centre at 90° to a chord cuts it in half: 24 ÷ 2 = 12 cm.
2. Right triangle: legs 12 and d, hypotenuse = radius = 13.
3. d² = 13² − 12² = 169 − 144 = 25, so d = 5 cm.
:::
