**For the adult:** Ask your child to sketch each triangle and label the hypotenuse (the longest side, across from the right angle) before they calculate.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A right triangle has legs of 5 cm and 12 cm. How long is the hypotenuse?**

1. Write the theorem: a² + b² = c²
2. Substitute: 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169
3. Take the square root: c = √169 = 13 cm
4. Check: 13 is longer than both legs. ✓
:::
