::: tip
**For the adult:** The key move is finding the two perfect squares on either side. Ask "Which perfect squares is it between?" before anything else.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Estimate √50 to one decimal place.**

1. Perfect squares on either side: 49 = 7² and 64 = 8², so √50 is between 7 and 8.
2. 50 is much closer to 49, so the answer is just above 7: about 7.1
3. Check: 7.1 × 7.1 = 50.41. Close! ✓
:::
