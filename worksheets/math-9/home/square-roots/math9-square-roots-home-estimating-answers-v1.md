::: tip
**For the adult:** Estimates within about 0.1 of the calculator value are good.
:::

**1.** √20
- Between 4 and 5, closer to 4 (16)
- Estimate **about 4.5** (calculator: 4.47)

**2.** √90
- Between 9 and 10 (81 and 100)
- Estimate **about 9.5** (calculator: 9.49)

**3.** √60
- Between 7 and 8 (49 and 64)
- Estimate **about 7.7** (calculator: 7.75)

**4.** **√0.49 = 0.7** and **√1.44 = 1.2**
- Check: 0.7 × 0.7 = 0.49 and 1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44

**5.** **√35, 6, 6.1, √40**
- √35 ≈ 5.92 and √40 ≈ 6.32

**6.** √75 ≈ **8.7 m**
- 75 is between 64 (8²) and 81 (9²)
- Check: 8.7 × 8.7 = 75.69
