::: tip
**For the adult:** On a number line, an open dot means the number itself is not included (< or >). A filled-in dot means it is included (≤ or ≥).
:::

**1.** x + 6 > 10
- Subtract 6: **x > 4**
- Open dot at 4, arrow to the right.

**2.** 2x − 3 ≤ 9
- Add 3: 2x ≤ 12
- Divide by 2: **x ≤ 6**
- Filled-in dot at 6, arrow to the left.

**3.** −4x < 20
- Divide by −4 and flip the sign: **x > −5**
- Test x = 0: −4(0) = 0, and 0 < 20 ✓

**4.** 5 − 2x ≥ 11
- Subtract 5: −2x ≥ 6
- Divide by −2 and flip the sign: **x ≤ −3**
- Test x = −4: 5 − 2(−4) = 13, and 13 ≥ 11 ✓

**5.** x/3 + 2 > 5
- Subtract 2: x/3 > 3
- Multiply by 3: **x > 9**

**6.** 3x − 1 > 8 gives 3x > 9, so x > 3.
- **3.5 and 5** work. 3 does not, because 3(3) − 1 = 8, and 8 is not greater than 8.

**7.** Let g be the number of GB.
- Inequality: 8g ≤ 50
- Divide by 8: g ≤ 6.25
- You can buy at most **6 GB** (6 GB costs $48).
