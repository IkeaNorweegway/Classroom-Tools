# Teacher run sheet and key *(not for students)*

**Data.** Statistics Canada, Census of Population counts, in millions.

| Year | 1951 | 1961 | 1971 | 1981 | 1991 | 2001 | 2011 | 1996 (held back) | 2021 (held back) | 1901 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Population | 14.0 | 18.2 | 21.6 | 24.3 | 27.3 | 30.0 | 33.5 | 28.8 | 37.0 | 5.4 |

**Run of show (about 40 minutes; Reveals 1 to 5 alone make a 10-minute warm-up).**

| Reveal | Appears | Students | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seven dots | Write notice/wonder, then pairs, then share | "Strong positive correlation", "almost a straight line", "evenly spaced across" |
| 2 | Years 1951 to 2021 | Label axis, guess | Guesses that steadily rise: prices, temperature, population. Ask "would that rise this smoothly?" |
| 3 | 0 to 40, no units | Label axis, estimate last dot (about 33 to 34) | Does the number rule a guess out? |
| 4 | "Millions of people" | Write a title | Which country? Canada is not given yet |
| 5 | Title and source | Compare titles, then Q1 to Q3 on paper | Circulate: is the line through the middle of the points, or joined dot to dot? |
| 6 | 1996 and 2021 counts | Record actual, find difference | Both predictions should be within about 1 million |
| 7 | Line of best fit, 1901 callout | Q4 to Q6, reflection | The misconception: "the line is the rule" |

**Key.**

- **Reveal 1:** strong positive correlation; close to linear.
- **Q2:** slope about 0.31 million people per year (accept 0.28 to 0.35). Meaning: Canada's census population grew by roughly 310 000 people per year over this period.
- **Q3:** 1996 is interpolation, about 28.8 (actual 28.8). 2021 is extrapolation, about 36.7 (actual 37.0).
- **Q4:** 14.7 − 50 × 0.31 ≈ −1 million (accept roughly −3 to +1). A population cannot be negative or near zero. Actual 1901 census: 5.4 million.
- **Q5:** 2021 is 10 years past the data, where the pattern is likely to continue. 1901 is 50 years outside it, where there is no data to show the pattern held, and it did not: growth before 1951 was slower. A line of best fit is a model of the data it was fitted to, and it becomes less reliable the further you move from that data.
- **Q6:** any claim tied to the points or the slope. Cannot tell you: why the population grew, where people live, what happened between censuses, what happens after 2021.
- **★:** (50 − 14.7) ÷ 0.31 ≈ 113 years after 1951, so about 2064. Low trust: about 40 years past the data.

**Evidence checklist:** produce from memory (correlation vocabulary, slope) ✓ · explain or justify (Q2, Q5, Q6) ✓ · prior content mixed in (slope as rate of change, from Linear Relations) ✓ · formative signal (prediction vs. actual, self-check) ✓ · worked example: not applicable, this is practice after the Statistics notes.
