Probability

THE BIG IDEA

Independent events have no memory. Past results never change the next probability.

Independent vs. Dependent

P(A) = favourable ÷ total

Independent:   P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

Dependent: recalculate the total after each draw

WORKED EXAMPLE

Bag: 3 red, 5 blue — draw twice, no replacement

P(red on draw 1) = 3/8

After removing 1 red: 2 red left, 7 total left

P(red then red) = 3/8 × 2/7 = 3/28

⚠ WATCH OUT FOR
MISTAKEGambler’s fallacy — “heads is due”
FIXEvery independent event starts fresh
MISTAKENot updating the sample space (dependent events)
FIXRecalculate the total and favourable count after each draw
MISTAKEForgetting to list all outcomes
FIXUse a tree diagram or organized list to be complete
✅ SELF-CHECK

A bag has 4 red and 6 green. Two drawn without replacement. Can you find P(red then green)?
