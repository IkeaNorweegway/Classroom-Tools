# Rational Numbers — Concept Summary
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

---

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Rational number | Any number written as a/b where a and b are integers and b ≠ 0 | −3/4, 5, 0.25 |
| Principal | The positive version (used with square roots and fractions) | |
| LCD | Least common denominator — the smallest shared denominator | LCD of 1/4 and 1/6 is 12 |
| Reciprocal | Flip numerator and denominator | Reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2 |
| BEDMAS | Order of operations: Brackets, Exponents, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction | |

---

## Key Rules

**Comparing negatives:** On the number line, the number closer to zero is greater.
> −1/4 > −3/4 &nbsp; (−1/4 is closer to zero)

**Adding/subtracting fractions:** Find LCD first. Only add/subtract numerators.
> 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6 ✓ &nbsp;&nbsp; NOT 2/5 ✗

**Multiplying fractions:** Multiply numerators, multiply denominators. Sign rules apply.
> (−3/4) × (2/5) = −6/20 = −3/10

**Dividing fractions:** Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.
> (3/4) ÷ (2/5) = (3/4) × (5/2) = 15/8

**Sign rules:**
- Negative × Negative = **Positive**
- Negative × Positive = **Negative**
- Count negative factors: odd count → negative result

---

## Worked Example 1 — Adding with unlike denominators

**Find:** −3/4 + 5/6

> **Step 1:** LCD of 4 and 6 = **12**
>
> **Step 2:** Rewrite: −3/4 = −9/12 &nbsp;&nbsp; 5/6 = 10/12
>
> **Step 3:** −9/12 + 10/12 = **1/12**

*Why do we find the LCD?* Denominators name the size of pieces. You cannot add pieces of different sizes until they're the same size.

---

## Worked Example 2 — Dividing rational numbers

**Find:** (−5/6) ÷ (3/4)

> **Step 1:** Reciprocal of 3/4 is **4/3**
>
> **Step 2:** (−5/6) × (4/3) = −20/18 = **−10/9**
>
> **Step 3:** Sign: negative × positive = **negative** ✓

*Why does dividing by a fraction less than 1 give a larger result?* Dividing by 3/4 asks "how many 3/4-pieces fit in this number?" More fit, so the result is larger.

---

## Worked Example 3 — BEDMAS with rationals

**Evaluate:** (−1/2 + 1/4)² ÷ (−3/4)

> **B:** −1/2 + 1/4 = −2/4 + 1/4 = **−1/4**
>
> **E:** (−1/4)² = **1/16**
>
> **D:** (1/16) ÷ (−3/4) = (1/16) × (−4/3) = **−4/48 = −1/12**

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5 | Find LCD first: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6 |
| Dividing makes it smaller | Dividing by a fraction < 1 gives a larger result |
| Forgetting to flip sign when subtracting | −(−3/4) = +3/4 |
| (−2/3)² is negative | Even power → always positive: (−2/3)² = 4/9 |
