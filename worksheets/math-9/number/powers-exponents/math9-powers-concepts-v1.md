# Powers and Exponents — Concept Summary
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

---

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Shorthand for repeated multiplication | 3⁴ = 3×3×3×3 |
| Base | The factor being repeated | In 3⁴, base = 3 |
| Exponent | How many times to multiply the base | In 3⁴, exponent = 4 |
| Reciprocal | Flip numerator and denominator | Reciprocal of 3 is 1/3 |
| Integral exponent | An exponent that is a positive or negative integer, or zero | 3², 3⁻², 3⁰ |

---

## The Seven Laws

| Law | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product of powers | aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | 2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷ |
| Quotient of powers | aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ | 5⁶ ÷ 5² = 5⁴ |
| Power of a power | (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ | (3²)⁴ = 3⁸ |
| Power of a product | (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ | (2x)³ = 8x³ |
| Power of a quotient | (a/b)ⁿ = aⁿ/bⁿ | (x/3)² = x²/9 |
| Zero exponent | a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0) | 7⁰ = 1 |
| Negative exponent | a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ | 3⁻² = 1/9 |

**Sign rule for negative bases:**
- Even exponent → **positive** result: (−2)⁴ = +16
- Odd exponent → **negative** result: (−2)³ = −8

**−3⁴ ≠ (−3)⁴** &nbsp; −3⁴ = −81 (base is 3) &nbsp; (−3)⁴ = +81 (base is −3)

---

## Worked Example 1 — Product and quotient laws

**Simplify: x⁵ × x³ ÷ x²**

> x⁵ × x³ = x^(5+3) = x⁸ &nbsp;&nbsp; ← product rule (add exponents)
>
> x⁸ ÷ x² = x^(8−2) = **x⁶** &nbsp;&nbsp; ← quotient rule (subtract exponents)

*Why add when multiplying?* Expanded: x⁵ × x³ = (x·x·x·x·x)(x·x·x) = x⁸. Counting factors.

---

## Worked Example 2 — Combined laws

**Simplify: (3x²)⁴ ÷ (9x³)**

> Step 1 — Power of product: (3x²)⁴ = 3⁴ × (x²)⁴ = 81x⁸
>
> Step 2 — Divide: 81x⁸ ÷ 9x³ = (81/9) × x^(8−3) = **9x⁵**

---

## Worked Example 3 — Negative exponents

**Evaluate: (−2)⁻³**

> (−2)⁻³ = 1/(−2)³ = 1/(−8) = **−1/8**

*Key:* The negative exponent says "move to denominator." The base's sign still matters.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| 2³ × 2⁴ = 2¹² | Add exponents when multiplying: 2⁷ |
| (2³)⁴ = 2⁷ | Multiply exponents for power of a power: 2¹² |
| (2 + x)³ = 8 + x³ | Only distributes over multiplication: (2x)³ = 8x³ |
| 3⁻² = −9 | Negative exponent = reciprocal: 3⁻² = 1/9 |
| 5⁰ = 0 | Any nonzero base to power 0 = 1 |
