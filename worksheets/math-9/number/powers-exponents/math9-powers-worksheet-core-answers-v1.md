# Powers and Exponents — Core Worksheet Answer Key ★
Grade 9 Mathematics · Teacher Copy — Not for Students

*Cover the answer. Try the question. Then check.*

---

**1.** Evaluate each power

| Power | Expanded form | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 2⁵ | 2×2×2×2×2 | **32** |
| (−3)³ | (−3)×(−3)×(−3) = 9×(−3) | **−27** |
| (−2)⁴ | (−2)×(−2)×(−2)×(−2) | **16** |
| −2⁴ | −(2×2×2×2) | **−16** |
| (1/2)³ | (1/2)×(1/2)×(1/2) | **1/8** |
| (−1)¹⁰⁰ | Pattern: even exponent → positive | **1** |

*Common mistake:* −2⁴ and (−2)⁴ are NOT the same. −2⁴ = −16; (−2)⁴ = +16. The brackets determine the base.

---

**2.** Zero and negative exponents

**(a)** 8⁰ = **1** | (−5)⁰ = **1** | (xy)⁰ = **1** *(any nonzero quantity raised to 0 = 1)*

**(b)** 4⁻² = 1/4² = 1/**16**

**(c)** (1/3)⁻² = (3/1)² = **9** *(negative exponent flips the fraction)*

**(d)** (−2)⁻³ = 1/(−2)³ = 1/(−8) = **−1/8**

*Common mistake on (d):* The result is negative because the BASE is negative (−2), not because of the negative exponent. The negative exponent alone never creates a negative result.

**3.** Identify rule and simplify

| Expression | Rule | Simplified |
|---|---|---|
| 3⁵ × 3⁴ | Product of powers (add) | **3⁹** |
| (x²)⁵ | Power of a power (multiply) | **x¹⁰** |
| a⁸ ÷ a³ | Quotient of powers (subtract) | **a⁵** |
| (4y)² | Power of a product | **16y²** |
| 6⁰ × 6³ | Zero exponent + product | **6³ = 216** |
| m⁻² | Negative exponent | **1/m²** |

---

**4.** Simplify with steps

**(a) (2x²)³**

> 2³ × (x²)³ = **8 × x⁶ = 8x⁶**

**(b) (a³b²)⁴**

> a^(3×4) × b^(2×4) = **a¹²b⁸**

**(c) (3/x)²**

> 3²/x² = **9/x²**

**(d) (−5m)²**

> (−5)² × m² = **25m²** *(negative squared = positive)*

---

**5.** Identify the error

| Error statement | Error named | Correct answer |
|---|---|---|
| 4³ × 4⁵ = 4¹⁵ | Multiplied exponents — should ADD for products | **4⁸** |
| (x³)⁴ = x⁷ | Added exponents — should MULTIPLY for power of power | **x¹²** |
| 2⁰ = 0 | Confused zero exponent with multiplication by zero | **2⁰ = 1** |
| 3⁻² = −9 | Confused negative exponent with negative result; also squared incorrectly | **3⁻² = 1/9** |

---

**6.** Mixed practice

**(a) x⁴ × x² × x³**

> x^(4+2+3) = **x⁹**

**(b) (m⁵)² ÷ m⁴**

> Power of power: m¹⁰. Quotient: m^(10−4) = **m⁶**

**(c) (2a³)² × a⁴**

> Power of product: 4a⁶. Then × a⁴: 4a^(6+4) = **4a¹⁰**

**(d) (3x⁻²)⁴**

> 3⁴ × x^(−2×4) = 81 × x⁻⁸ = **81/x⁸**

**7.** Sign and magnitude

| Power | Predicted sign | Actual value |
|---|---|---|
| (−3)⁵ | Negative (odd exponent) | **−243** |
| (−3)⁶ | Positive (even exponent) | **729** |
| −3⁶ | Negative (base 3, then negated) | **−729** |
| (−1/2)⁴ | Positive (even exponent) | **1/16** |

*Note:* (−3)⁶ = +729 but −3⁶ = −729 — these are different expressions with different answers.

**8.** Alberta context — light travel

**(a)**

> Time = (3 × 10⁵) ÷ (3 × 10⁸)
> = (3/3) × 10^(5−8)
> = 1 × 10^**−3** seconds

**(b)** This is **0.001 seconds (1 millisecond)** — extremely short. Light travels extraordinarily fast.

**9.** Evaluate using exponent rules

**(a) (2⁴ × 2⁻²)³**

> Inside bracket first: 2^(4+(−2)) = 2². Then (2²)³ = 2^(2×3) = 2⁶ = **64**

**(b) (3² ÷ 3⁴) × 3³**

> 3^(2−4) = 3⁻². Then 3⁻² × 3³ = 3^(−2+3) = 3¹ = **3**

**(c) (−1)²⁰²**

> 202 is even → **1**

**10.** Summary laws table

| Law | Symbol form |
|---|---|
| Product of powers | aᵐ × aⁿ = **aᵐ⁺ⁿ** |
| Quotient of powers | aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = **aᵐ⁻ⁿ** |
| Power of a power | (aᵐ)ⁿ = **aᵐⁿ** |
| Zero exponent | a⁰ = **1** |
| Negative exponent | a⁻ⁿ = **1/aⁿ** |
