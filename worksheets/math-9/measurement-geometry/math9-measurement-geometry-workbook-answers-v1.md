## Q8 ★★ — SA vs V error analysis

Cylinder r=4, h=10.

**(a) SA:**
> SA = 2π(16)+2π(4)(10) = 32π+80π = **112π ≈ 351.9 cm²**
> Not approximately 251 — closer to 352.

**(b) V:**
> V = π(16)(10) = **160π ≈ 502.7 cm³**
> Not approximately 251 — closer to 503.

**(c) Error:**
> The student conflated two fundamentally different measurements. SA measures total outer area (cm²) — amount of metal needed. V measures interior capacity (cm³) — amount it holds. They have **different units**, different meanings, and different values. Even if the numbers appeared similar, they couldn't "be the same thing" — one is 2D, the other is 3D.

## Q9 ★ — Similar triangles, AA

DE∥BC, AD=3, DB=5, DE=4.

**(a) Why similar:**
> Angle A is shared. DE∥BC → angle ADE=angle ABC and angle AED=angle ACB (corresponding angles). **AA criterion.**

**(b) Scale factor:**
> AD/AB = 3/(3+5) = **3/8**

**(c) BC:**
> DE/BC = 3/8 → 4/BC = 3/8 → BC = 4 × 8/3 = **32/3 ≈ 10.7**

## Q14 ★★ — Circle geometry, multi-step

Central AOB = 120°.

> (a) APB = 120°/2 = **60°**
>
> (b) Major arc central = 240°. Inscribed = **120°**
>
> (c) 60° + 120° = **180° ✓**
>
> They always sum to 180° because the two arcs together form the full circle (360°). Their central angles sum to 360°. Each inscribed angle is half its central angle: ½(360°) = 180°. ✓

## Stretch Zone

**S1:** chord=24, d=5. r=√(12²+5²)=√169=**13 cm**. CD=**24 cm** (equal distance → equal chord). APB=40° → central=80°. Arc fraction=80/360=**2/9≈22.2%**.

**S3:** Setup: tangent from P to circle (r=6). Chord AB parallel to tangent. Perpendicular P to AB=10. The distance from P to centre O involves the tangent length and the geometry of the parallel chord. Full solution requires knowing PO first; since OT (tangent-radius)=6, PT=√(PO²-36). The perpendicular from P to AB (a chord) = 10.
