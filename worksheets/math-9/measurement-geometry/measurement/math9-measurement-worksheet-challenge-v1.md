**3.** Alberta context — oil storage.

A cylindrical oil storage tank near Fort McMurray has radius 8 m and height 15 m.

(a) Find the volume and state it in litres (1 m³ = 1000 L).

(b) A hemispherical dome cap is added to the top (same radius). How much additional volume does this add?

(c) The combined structure (cylinder + hemisphere) needs to be painted on all exterior surfaces. Which surfaces are painted and which are not? Calculate the total painted area.

(d) Paint costs $12/m². What is the total cost to paint the exterior?

**4.** Composite object design.

(a) Find the total volume.

(b) List every exterior surface. Calculate each area. Add them for total SA.

(c) The cone has a solid base that sits flush against the cylinder. Is the base circle of the cone included in the SA? Explain.
