# Measurement and Geometry — Practice Workbook
Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum · Interleaved practice

**About this workbook:** Questions are mixed up on purpose. Circle geometry, surface area and volume, and similarity and scale all appear together — in scrambled order. Figuring out which skill each question needs is part of the work.

★ Core — attempt all. Some include a hint, word bank, or sentence starter.
★★ Extended — same ideas, higher demand. Explain, find errors, or apply to a new situation.

Show all working. Include a diagram for every measurement problem — even a rough sketch. A correct answer with no diagram earns half marks.
