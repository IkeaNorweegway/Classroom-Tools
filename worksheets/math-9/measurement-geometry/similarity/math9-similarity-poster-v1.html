Similarity
& Scale

THE BIG IDEA

Similar figures are the same shape at a different size — angles match exactly, sides scale by the same factor.

Two Polygons Are Similar Only If
  1. All corresponding angles are equal, AND
  2. All corresponding sides are proportional

Triangles shortcut — AA: two pairs of equal angles is enough (the third must match too).

WORKED EXAMPLE

△ABC ~ △DEF   AB=6, BC=9, AC=12, DE=4

Scale factor = DE ÷ AB = 4/6 = 2/3

EF = 9 × 2/3 = 6

DF = 12 × 2/3 = 8

⚠ WATCH OUT FOR
MISTAKE“Similar means congruent”
FIXSimilar means proportional — not equal in size
MISTAKESetting up the ratio backwards
FIXMatch image-to-image, original-to-original
