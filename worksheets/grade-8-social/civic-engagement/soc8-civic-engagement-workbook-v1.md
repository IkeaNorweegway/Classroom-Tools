# Civic Engagement — Practice Workbook
Grade 8 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026 · Interleaved practice

**About this workbook:** Questions are scrambled across all four topics in this unit — figuring out which topic each question tests is part of the work. Riverbend, the fictional town in your notes, shows up again here.

★ Core — attempt all. Hints or word banks are provided.
★★ Extended — same concepts, higher demand. Explain, justify, find errors, or apply to a new context.

Show all working. A correct answer with no working earns half marks.

Can vote? Yes / No — Reason: _______________________________________________
