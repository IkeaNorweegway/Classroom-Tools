::: tip
**For the adult:** Most ways of taking part don’t need you to be 18. Help your child pick something small and doable.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Your child is bothered by litter in the local park. What could they do?**

1. Name the issue: litter is building up in the park.
2. Find out who decides: the city looks after parks.
3. List options: a clean-up, asking the city for more bins, writing to a councillor, making posters.
4. Pick one and plan the steps: who to ask, what to bring, when to do it.
:::
