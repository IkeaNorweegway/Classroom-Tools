::: tip
**For the adult:** Global issues include poverty, drought, war and people being forced to move. Ask "How could this reach us here?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How could a drought in another part of the world affect people in Canada?**

1. Prices: if crops fail, some foods cost more here.
2. Help: Canadian charities and the government may send aid.
3. People: some families may have to leave home and could come to Canada.
:::
