---
title: Looking Closely at an Issue
course: social8
unit: Civic Engagement
skill: explaining how a global issue affects people in Canada
time: about 15 minutes
---

::: tip
**For the adult:** Use a news site you trust, and talk about the story together.
:::

**1.** a problem that **affects people in many countries**, not just one place

**2.** Examples: **poverty**, **drought or famine**, **war**, **climate change**, **people forced to leave their homes**.

**3.** Examples: **higher prices** (like fuel), **refugees** arriving, Canadian **soldiers or aid** being sent, people worrying about family overseas.

**4.** People have **different values and priorities**, for example cost, safety, or helping others.

**5.** Answers vary.

**6.** Examples: **raise money** for a trusted charity, **learn and share** reliable information, welcome newcomers at school.
