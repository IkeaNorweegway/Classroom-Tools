::: tip
**For the adult:** Question 5 has several good answers. Deficits are not always a mistake.
:::

**1.** **more** · **less** · **the total amount owed** from borrowing over many years

**2.** **deficit** of **$3** billion

**3.** **Surplus** of **$5 billion**

**4.** $10 + $2 + $3 = **$15 billion**

**5.** Examples: an **emergency** (a flood or pandemic), a slow economy when people lose jobs, or **building** things like hospitals and roads that last a long time.

**6.** Examples: **schools**, **hospitals and health care**, **roads**, **police**, **parks**.
