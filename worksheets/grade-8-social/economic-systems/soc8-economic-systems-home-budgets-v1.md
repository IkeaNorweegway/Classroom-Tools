::: tip
**For the adult:** A deficit is one year’s gap. Debt is all the borrowing added up over the years. These numbers are made up for practice.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Imagine a government collects $40 billion and spends $43 billion this year.**

1. Spending is more than income, so this year is a deficit.
2. The deficit is $43 billion − $40 billion = $3 billion.
3. The government borrows the $3 billion, so its debt grows by $3 billion.
4. If next year it collects $45 billion and spends $42 billion, that’s a $3 billion surplus, which could pay some debt back.
:::
