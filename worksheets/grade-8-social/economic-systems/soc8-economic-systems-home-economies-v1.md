::: tip
**For the adult:** The key question for every economy is "Who decides?" Tradition, the government, individuals and businesses, or a mix.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How does a lemonade stand answer the three economic questions?**

1. What to produce? Lemonade.
2. How to produce it? By hand, with lemons, sugar and water.
3. For whom? Customers who are willing to pay.
4. The owner made the decisions, which is how a market economy works.
:::
