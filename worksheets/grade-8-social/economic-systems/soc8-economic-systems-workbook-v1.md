# Economic Systems — Practice Workbook
Grade 8 Social Studies · Alberta Curriculum (Draft) · Interleaved practice

**About this workbook:** Questions are scrambled across all topics in this unit — figuring out which concept each question tests is part of the work.

★ Core — attempt all. Hints, word banks, or sentence starters are provided.
★★ Extended — same concepts, higher demand. Explain, justify, find errors, or apply to a new context.

Show all working. A correct answer with no working earns half marks.
