::: tip
**For the adult:** Advantages and challenges can vary. Look for a reason with each one.
:::

**1.** What to **produce**? How to **produce** it? For **whom**?

**2.** there **isn’t enough of everything** for everyone’s wants, so choices have to be made

**3.** (a) **traditional** (b) **command** (c) **market** (d) **mixed**

**4.** Advantage: lots of **choice**, and competition can bring lower prices and new ideas.
- Challenge: some people may not be able to afford what they **need**.

**5.** It has **private businesses** (stores, restaurants) and **government services** (health care, public schools).

**6.** Answers vary. Government: schools, roads, health care, parks, libraries. Businesses: groceries, clothes, phones.
