::: tip
**For the adult:** Canadians vote for a local MP, not for the prime minister. The party with the most seats usually forms the government, and its leader becomes prime minister.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How does Canada get a prime minister?**

1. Canada is divided into ridings. Each riding elects one Member of Parliament (MP).
2. The party that wins the most seats usually forms the government.
3. That party’s leader becomes the prime minister.
4. The party with the second-most seats becomes the Official Opposition.
:::
