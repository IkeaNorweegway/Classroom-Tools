::: tip
**For the adult:** Both countries are democracies. The big differences are how the leader is chosen, and whether the leader needs the support of the elected house to stay in power.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How is the leader chosen in each country?**

1. Canada: voters elect local MPs. The leader of the party with the most seats usually becomes prime minister.
2. United States: the president is elected in a separate election, for a fixed four-year term.
3. A Canadian prime minister must keep the support of the House of Commons. A U.S. president serves the full term unless removed by impeachment.
:::
