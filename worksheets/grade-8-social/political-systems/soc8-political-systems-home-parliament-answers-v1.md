**1.** **ridings** (electoral districts) · **Member of Parliament (MP)**

**2.** **government** · **prime minister** · **Opposition**

**3.** Governor General **(iii)** · Prime Minister **(iv)** · Cabinet **(v)** · Senate **(i)** · MPs **(ii)**

**4.** They vote for a **local MP**. The prime minister is the **leader of the party that wins the most seats**.

**5.** **Executive:** carries out the laws (prime minister and Cabinet).
- **Legislative:** makes the laws (Parliament).
- **Judicial:** interprets the laws (the courts).

**6.** Answers vary. Look it up by postal code on the Elections Canada website.
