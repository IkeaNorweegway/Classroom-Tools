::: tip
**For the adult:** Real people and parties mix both views. The goal is to recognise which way an idea leans.
:::

**1.** **the individual (each person)** · **the group (the collective)**

**2.** **left** · **right**

**3.** (a) **I** (b) **C** (c) **I** (d) **C**

**4.** communism **(iii)** · socialism **(ii)** · liberalism **(iv)** · conservatism **(i)**

**5.** An **ideology** is a set of ideas about how society should work.
- A **party** is an organised group of people who share ideas and try to get elected.

**6.** Answers vary. Example: a dress code. An individualist might say students should choose for themselves; a collectivist might say a shared rule helps the whole school.
