title: Reading a Primary Source
course: social8
unit: Ideologies
skill: asking who made a source, why, and what it leaves out
time: about 15 minutes
---

::: tip
**For the adult:** There is rarely one right answer here. Look for questions and reasons, not just a yes or no.
:::

**1.** **at the time** of the event · **later**, by someone looking back

**2.** (a) **P** (b) **S** (c) **P** (d) **S**

**3.** It was made to **persuade**. It shows what the government wanted people to believe and leaves out anything that didn’t fit.

**4.** They saw it from **different points of view**. The government may have wanted to hide or justify what it did; a survivor describes what it was like to live through it.

**5.** Examples: **Who made it, and why?** · **When was it made?** · **Who is missing from it?** · **Can I check it against another source?**

**6.** Answers vary.
