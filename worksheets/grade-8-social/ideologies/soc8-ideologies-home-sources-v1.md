::: tip
**For the adult:** A primary source was made at the time: a letter, photo, poster or diary. Five questions help: Who made it? When? Why? Who was it for? What is left out?
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Read a 1930s government poster showing a smiling family and the words "One people, one nation".**

1. Who made it? The government.
2. When? The 1930s.
3. Why? To persuade people to support the government’s ideas.
4. Who was it for? Ordinary citizens.
5. What is left out? The people the government excluded or persecuted. Whose voices are missing?
:::
