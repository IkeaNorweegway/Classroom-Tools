---

## How to read this schedule

- Each column is one session week.
- Each row is one student.
- Find your name. Find this week's column. That is your role.
- **Know your role before you arrive.**

---

## Production Role Descriptions (quick reference)

| Role | What you do this session |
|---|---|
| **Broadcaster** | Live commentary on the match. Keep the audience engaged. Describe the action, build tension, explain what just happened. |
| **Social Media Manager** | Document the session. Pre-match post, in-match update, post-match reaction. Use team brand assets. |
| **Event Coordinator** | Equipment check, station setup, scoreboard management, bracket admin. Sign off the production readiness checklist before play starts. |
| **Wellbeing Lead** | One-word check-in with every member at the start. Culture monitor during the session. One-word check-out at the end. Report to General Manager if anything needs follow-up. |
| **Player** | In-game. Communicate with team. Follow the IGL's calls. |
| **IGL** *(Weeks 7–9 only)* | Pre-game strategy call. In-game direction. Post-match debrief — leads the team conversation. Stable for the full Regular Season. |
| **Analyst** *(Weeks 7–9 only)* | VOD review before each session. Completes Pre-Match Report with IGL. Pulls one key moment in post-match debrief. |

## Rules for rotation

1. No student holds the same production role two weeks in a row.
2. Every student holds at least one production role by Week 6.
3. The IGL role is assigned by team vote in Week 7 and stays stable through Week 9.
4. The Analyst role (Weeks 7–9) is assigned by the IGL — usually the student with the strongest pattern-recognition.
5. The Vice-President publishes any schedule changes at least one session before they take effect.
