**How this workbook works**

The questions are mixed up on purpose. They come from all parts of the course. For each question, first figure out what it is asking. Then answer it.

★ **Core questions** — for everyone. Some have hints or word banks. Use them if you need help.

★★ **Extended questions** — go deeper. No hints. You need to explain your thinking.

Only do the questions your teacher has opened. Leave the rest for later.
