**Section 3.2 — Election process**
- Any member may nominate themselves for any officer position
- Candidates give a 60-second statement to the club before the vote
- Election is by vote of all members present
- Officers serve for the full season

**Section 3.3 — Removal and replacement**
An officer may be removed by a two-thirds vote of members present if they are not fulfilling their duties. A by-election follows within one session.

## Article IV — Duties of Officers

**President**
- Chairs all club meetings
- Leads The Pledge at the opening and closing of every session
- Represents the club to the school and to external groups (admin, sponsors, other schools)
- Facilitates team decision-making when there is disagreement
- Works with the General Manager to plan the season calendar

**Vice-President**
- Assumes all President duties when the President is absent
- Oversees the role rotation schedule — ensures every member knows their role each week
- Tracks the premiership ladder and posts standings after each session
- Coordinates the Week 9 showcase event logistics with the Event Coordinator

**Secretary**
- Takes brief minutes at club meetings (date, attendance, decisions made)
- Maintains the Charter — updates it when the club votes to amend it
- Manages sign-in sheets with the Wellbeing Lead
- Archives social media posts, broadcast clips, and pre-match reports across the season

## Article VI — The Executive Committee

The Executive Committee consists of the three elected officers and the General Manager.

**Role of the Executive Committee:**
- Resolve disputes that cannot be resolved between team members
- Apply fair play deductions (General Manager confirms)
- Make decisions on competition scheduling, showcase planning, and external communication

**Role of the General Manager (teacher):**
- The General Manager is the adviser and supporter — not the decision-maker for day-to-day operations
- The GM holds final authority on safety, conduct, and eligibility decisions
- The GM does not override officer decisions on internal club matters unless a Charter rule is at stake

## Article VIII — Amendments

This Charter may be amended by a two-thirds vote of members present at any regular or special meeting, provided the amendment has been discussed at least one session before the vote.

Amendments are recorded by the Secretary and signed by all officers.
