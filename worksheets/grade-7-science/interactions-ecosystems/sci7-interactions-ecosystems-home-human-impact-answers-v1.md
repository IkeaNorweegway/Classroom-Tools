::: tip
**For the adult:** Many answers are possible. Look for a clear chain of cause and effect.
:::

**1.** (a) **natural** (b) **human-caused** (c) **natural** (d) **human-caused**

**2.** It comes from **somewhere else** (another region or country) and can crowd out **native (local)** species.

**3.** **Birds** that eat insects lose their food.
- **Plants** that need insects to pollinate them make fewer seeds.

**4.** Example: a **forest fire**. New plants sprout from seeds and roots, and some trees, like lodgepole pine, have cones that open in the heat.
- Over years the forest grows back.

**5.** Answers vary: pick up litter, plant native plants, use less water, protect wetlands, avoid spreading invasive species.

**6.** Problem: elk crossing the road could be hit by cars, or the road could split their habitat.
- Fix: build wildlife crossings (like the overpasses in Banff National Park), fences, or lower speed limits.
