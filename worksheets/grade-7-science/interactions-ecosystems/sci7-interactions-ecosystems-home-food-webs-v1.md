::: tip
**For the adult:** Arrows in a food chain point from the food to the eater, showing where the energy goes. Ask "Who is eating whom?" for every arrow.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Grass → mouse → owl. Name each role and say where the energy started.**

1. The arrow means "is eaten by", so energy moves from grass to mouse to owl.
2. Grass makes its own food, so it is the producer.
3. The mouse eats the producer: primary consumer. The owl eats the mouse: secondary consumer.
4. The energy started with the Sun, which the grass used to make food.
:::
