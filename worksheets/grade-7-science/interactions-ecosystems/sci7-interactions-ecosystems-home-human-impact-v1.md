::: tip
**For the adult:** Ask your child to follow the "ripple": what changes first, who is affected next, and what happens after that.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A wetland is drained to build houses. How does this affect the ecosystem?**

1. What is removed: wetland plants and the habitat they provide.
2. Directly affected: frogs, ducks and insects lose their homes.
3. The ripple: animals that eat frogs lose food, and flooding can get worse because wetlands soak up water.
4. One fix: protect part of the wetland, or build a new one nearby.
:::
