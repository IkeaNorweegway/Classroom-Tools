::: tip
**For the adult:** Temperature is how fast particles are moving. Heat is energy moving from warmer to cooler. Ask "What are the particles doing?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Explain, using particles, why a balloon gets bigger in the sun.**

1. Heat from the sun moves into the air inside the balloon.
2. The air particles gain energy and move faster.
3. Faster particles hit the balloon wall harder and spread out more.
4. So the air takes up more space and the balloon expands.
:::
