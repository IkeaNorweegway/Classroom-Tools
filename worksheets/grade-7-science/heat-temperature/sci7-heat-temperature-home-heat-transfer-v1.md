::: tip
**For the adult:** Conduction needs touching. Convection needs a moving liquid or gas. Radiation needs neither: it can even cross empty space.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Name the heat transfer in a mug of hot chocolate.**

1. The spoon handle gets hot: heat passes through the metal by conduction.
2. Warm liquid rises and cooler liquid sinks: convection.
3. Your hands feel warm near the mug without touching it: radiation.
:::
