**1.** **touching** (particles passing it along) · **liquids or gases** · **rays (waves)**

**2.** **radiation** · **particles (matter)**

**3.** (a) **conduction** (b) **convection** (c) **radiation** (d) **conduction**

**4.** Warm air rises and cool air sinks (**convection**), so warm air collects upstairs.

**5.** The fluffy material **traps air**, which is a poor conductor.
- It **slows the heat** leaving your body. It doesn’t add heat; it keeps yours in.

**6.** Any two: **wall insulation** (conduction), **weather stripping** and caulking (convection, by stopping drafts), **double-pane windows** (conduction), **curtains** (convection and radiation).
