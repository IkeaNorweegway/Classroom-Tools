::: tip
**For the adult:** The pool example is the key idea: more particles means more total energy, even at a lower temperature.
:::

**1.** **particles** · **energy** · **warmer** · **cooler**

**2.** **faster** · **apart** · **expands**

**3.** The **tea** has the higher temperature.
- The **pool** has far more heat energy, because it has far more particles moving.

**4.** Concrete **expands in hot weather**. The gaps give it room so it doesn’t crack.

**5.** **273 K** and **27 °C**

**6.** **Heat moves out** of the drink into the cold air.
- "Cold" isn’t a thing that moves; it’s just less heat.
