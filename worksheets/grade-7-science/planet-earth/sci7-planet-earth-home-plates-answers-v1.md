::: tip
**For the adult:** For question 5: 100 000 cm = 1 km.
:::

**1.** **crust**, **mantle**, **outer core**, **inner core**

**2.** **convection** currents in the **mantle**

**3.** (a) **divergent** (b) **convergent** (c) **transform**

**4.** At boundaries the plates **grind, collide or pull apart**. That builds up stress (earthquakes) and lets magma rise (volcanoes).

**5.** 5 cm × 1 000 000 = 5 000 000 cm
- 5 000 000 ÷ 100 000 = **50 km**

**6.** Plates **pushed together**. The crust was squeezed, folded and pushed up into mountains over millions of years.
