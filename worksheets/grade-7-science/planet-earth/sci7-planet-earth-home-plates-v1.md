::: tip
**For the adult:** Plate boundaries come in three kinds: pulling apart, pushing together and sliding past. Two hands on a table are a good model.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**What happens where two plates pull apart under the ocean?**

1. The plates move away from each other: a divergent boundary.
2. Magma rises up into the gap.
3. It cools to form new ocean floor, building an underwater mountain range (a ridge).
:::
