::: tip
**For the adult:** Igneous = melted and cooled. Sedimentary = bits pressed together in layers. Metamorphic = changed by heat and pressure.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Follow a rock through the rock cycle.**

1. Lava from a volcano cools into basalt: an igneous rock.
2. Wind and water break the basalt into tiny pieces (weathering) and carry them away (erosion).
3. The pieces settle in layers and are pressed together: sedimentary rock.
4. Buried deep, heat and pressure change it into metamorphic rock.
:::
