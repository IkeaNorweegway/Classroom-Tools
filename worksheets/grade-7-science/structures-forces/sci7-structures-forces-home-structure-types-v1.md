::: tip
**For the adult:** Ask: "Is it mostly one solid piece, a skeleton of parts, or a thin curved skin?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**What type of structure is a bike helmet, and why does that help?**

1. It is a thin, hard, curved outer layer: a shell structure.
2. Shells spread a force over a large area.
3. So a hit to one spot is shared by the whole helmet, protecting your head.
:::
