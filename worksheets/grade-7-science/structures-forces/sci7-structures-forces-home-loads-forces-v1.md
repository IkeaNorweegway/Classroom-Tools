::: tip
**For the adult:** Compression squeezes, tension stretches, shear slides, torsion twists. Acting them out with a sponge or a pool noodle helps.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**What forces act on a playground swing set?**

1. The chains are pulled by the rider: tension.
2. The top bar bends: its top side is squeezed (compression) and its bottom side stretched (tension).
3. The legs are pushed down into the ground: compression.
:::
