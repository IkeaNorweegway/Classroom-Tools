::: tip
**For the adult:** Combination structures are common. A clear reason matters more than a single "right" label.
:::

**1.** **one solid piece** of material · **parts joined like a skeleton** · **a thin, curved outer layer**

**2.** brick wall **solid** · ladder **frame** · egg **shell** · power-line tower **frame**

**3.** Inside is a wooden or steel **frame**; the walls and roof cover it like a **shell** (skin).

**4.** A triangle **can’t change shape** without a side breaking, so it stays rigid and spreads the force.

**5.** Examples: solid **tree trunk or mountain** · frame **skeleton or spider web** · shell **egg, turtle shell or nut**

**6.** A **shell** structure, like an igloo or quinzhee.
- The curved dome is strong and spreads the snow’s weight, and snow insulates well.
