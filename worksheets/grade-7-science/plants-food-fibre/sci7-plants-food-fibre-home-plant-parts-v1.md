::: tip
**For the adult:** Use the fridge or the grocery store: most vegetables are a root, stem, leaf, flower or fruit. Guessing which is a fun way to practise.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Follow water and sugar through a plant.**

1. Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil.
2. Xylem tubes carry the water up the stem to the leaves.
3. Leaves use the water to make sugar by photosynthesis.
4. Phloem tubes carry the sugar to the roots, fruits and growing parts.
:::
