::: tip
**For the adult:** Photosynthesis in words: carbon dioxide + water, using light, makes sugar + oxygen. Ask "What goes in, and what comes out?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Write the word equation for photosynthesis and say where it happens.**

1. Inputs: carbon dioxide (from the air) and water (from the soil).
2. Energy: light from the Sun, captured by green chlorophyll.
3. Outputs: sugar (glucose), which the plant uses as food, and oxygen.
4. Where: in the chloroplasts, mostly in the leaves.
:::
