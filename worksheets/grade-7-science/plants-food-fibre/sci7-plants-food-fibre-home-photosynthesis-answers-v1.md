::: tip
**For the adult:** Plants also use some oxygen to live (respiration), but in daylight they make much more oxygen than they use.
:::

**1.** **carbon dioxide** + water → sugar + **oxygen**

**2.** **chlorophyll**, in the **chloroplasts**

**3.** (a) from the **air**, through tiny openings in the leaves
- (b) from the **soil**, through the roots
- (c) from the **Sun** (or a grow light)

**4.** It **can’t photosynthesize** without light, so it can’t make new food.
- It uses up its stored sugar, turns yellow and weak, and may die.

**5.** **False.** Plants **make** their own food (sugar) by photosynthesis.
- Soil gives them water and minerals, not food.

**6.** Trees **take in carbon dioxide and give off oxygen** during photosynthesis, a bit like the reverse of how we breathe.
