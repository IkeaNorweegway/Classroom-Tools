One part I'm still not sure about:
🔑 Word to know
Sexual reproduction — making a new plant using a flower, pollen, and a seed.
🔑 Word to know
Asexual reproduction — making a new plant from a piece of the parent plant. No seed is needed.
🔑 Word to know
Pollination — moving pollen from one flower to another. This has to happen before a seed can form.
🔑 Word to know
Runner — a stem that grows along the ground and starts a new plant.
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think flowers are just pretty. A flower's real job is helping the plant make seeds.
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think making a copy of a plant is not natural. Many plants make copies of themselves in nature, using runners, bulbs, or cuttings. This is called asexual reproduction.
