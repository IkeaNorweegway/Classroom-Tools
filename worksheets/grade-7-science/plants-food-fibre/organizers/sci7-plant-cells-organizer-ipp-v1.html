One part I'm still not sure about:
🔑 Word to know
Cell wall — a stiff outer layer. It gives the cell its shape.
🔑 Word to know
Cell membrane — a thin layer inside the wall. It controls what goes in and out.
🔑 Word to know
Vacuole — a big space that stores water.
🔑 Word to know
Chloroplasts — small green parts. They make food using sunlight.
🔑 Word to know
Nucleus — the control centre of the cell.
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think plant cells and animal cells look the same. Plant cells have a cell wall, a big vacuole, and chloroplasts. Animal cells do not have these parts.
