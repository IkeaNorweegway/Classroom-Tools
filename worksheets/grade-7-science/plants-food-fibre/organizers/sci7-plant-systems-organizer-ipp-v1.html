A plant has two systems. The root system is below the ground. The shoot system is above the ground. Water moves up through the plant. Food moves down through the plant.

One part I'm still not sure about:
🔑 Word to know
Root system — all the roots of a plant. It is under the ground.
🔑 Word to know
Shoot system — the stem and leaves. It is above the ground.
🔑 Word to know
Xylem — the tube that carries water up the plant.
🔑 Word to know
Phloem — the tube that carries sugar (food) down the plant.
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think roots take in food. Roots take in water and minerals. The plant makes its own food in its leaves.
