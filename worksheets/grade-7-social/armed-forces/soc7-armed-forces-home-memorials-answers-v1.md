::: tip
**For the adult:** Questions 1 to 5 depend on the memorial you chose. Check that each answer comes from something on the memorial itself.
:::

**1.** Answers vary.

**2.** Answers vary: local people who served or died.

**3.** Often the First World War (1914–1918) and the Second World War (1939–1945); some add Korea, peacekeeping or Afghanistan.

**4.** Answers vary (often the town, a veterans' group such as the Royal Canadian Legion, or the IODE).

**5.** Example: "Lest We Forget", poppies, a maple leaf or a cross. They show the community wanted to honour and never forget those who served.

**6.** Any one: Remembrance Day (November 11), Indigenous Veterans Day (November 8), wearing a poppy, a moment of silence.
