::: tip
**For the adult:** Canada did not get its own army, navy and foreign policy all at once. Each step, from the North-West Mounted Police in 1873 to the Statute of Westminster in 1931, gave Canada more control over its own affairs. That control is called **sovereignty**.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Which came first, the Royal Canadian Navy or the NWMP? What did each one change?**

1. NWMP: **1873**. Navy: **1910**. So the NWMP came first.
2. The NWMP let Canada enforce its own laws in the West.
3. The navy meant Canada could defend its coasts with its own ships, not Britain's.
:::
