::: tip
**For the adult:** Most Alberta towns have a cenotaph or war memorial. If you can, visit one together; if not, use a photo of one online. Your child is practising reading a memorial as evidence about the past.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A memorial says: "1914–1918. In memory of the men of this district who gave their lives. Lest We Forget." It lists 24 names. It was built by the town in 1921.**

1. **Who is named?** 24 local men who died.
2. **Which war?** The First World War (1914–1918).
3. **Who built it, and when?** The town, in 1921, three years after the war.
4. **What words are used?** "Lest We Forget" means the town wanted the loss remembered forever.
:::
