::: tip
**For the adult:** Canada's regions built different economies from what their land and water offered. Talking about what your own area produces is a great way in.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Why did the Prairies build a wheat economy?**

1. **Resource:** huge areas of flat land with good soil.
2. **Industry:** farming, especially wheat.
3. **Connection:** the railway carried the wheat to ports and buyers around the world.
:::
