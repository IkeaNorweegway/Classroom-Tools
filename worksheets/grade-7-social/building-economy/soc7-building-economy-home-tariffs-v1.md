::: tip
**For the adult:** A tariff is a tax on imported goods. In 1879 Canada's National Policy added tariffs so people would buy Canadian-made goods. Your child is practising the maths and asking "who wins, who pays?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A $100 American plough has a 30% tariff. An Ontario plough costs $120. Which is cheaper?**

1. Tariff: 30% of $100 = $30.
2. New price: $100 + $30 = **$130**.
3. The Ontario plough ($120) is now cheaper. The Ontario factory wins; the farmer pays $20 more than before the tariff.
:::
