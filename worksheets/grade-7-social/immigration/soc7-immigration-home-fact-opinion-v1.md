::: tip
**For the adult:** A **fact** can be checked with evidence. An **opinion** is what someone believes. Old newspapers and posters are full of both, so historians check every source. The statements below were written for this sheet.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Read: "The CPR was finished in 1885, and it is the greatest railway on Earth."**

1. "The CPR was finished in 1885": can it be checked? Yes, in records. **Fact.**
2. "The greatest railway on Earth": can it be proven? No, it is a belief. **Opinion.**
3. One sentence can hold both.
:::
