::: tip
**For the adult:** People move because something **pushes** them away from home (no land, poverty, war) or **pulls** them to a new place (land, work, family). If your family has a migration story, this is a great sheet to talk through together.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A Ukrainian family moves to Alberta in 1900. Find one push and one pull.**

1. **Push:** their farm at home was too small to feed everyone.
2. **Pull:** a 160-acre homestead for a $10 fee under the Dominion Lands Act.
3. **Ask which mattered more:** probably the land, because it was something they could never get at home.
:::
