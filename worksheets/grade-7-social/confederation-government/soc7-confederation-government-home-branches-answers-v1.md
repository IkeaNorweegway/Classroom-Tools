::: tip
**For the adult:** For question 5, any answer about one person having too much power, or no one being able to stop unfair laws, is a good answer.
:::

**1.** **legislative**

**2.** **judicial**

**3.** **executive**

**4.** (a) **judicial** (b) **executive** (c) **legislative**

**5.** Example: one person could make unfair laws, enforce them and judge their own case. Splitting the jobs lets each branch check the others.

**6.** Their local **Member of Parliament (MP)**, who sits in the House of Commons, part of the **legislative** branch.
