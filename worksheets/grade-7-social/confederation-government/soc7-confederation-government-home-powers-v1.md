::: tip
**For the adult:** Since 1867 Canada has had two main levels of government with different jobs. Ottawa handles things like defence, money and criminal law. Provinces like Alberta handle schools, hospitals, cities and natural resources.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Who is responsible for building a new high school in Calgary?**

1. What kind of job is it? **Education.**
2. Under the BNA Act, education belongs to the **provinces**.
3. So the **Alberta government** is responsible (working with the local school board).
:::
