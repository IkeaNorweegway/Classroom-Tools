**4.** Any two:

- reserves (land set aside)
- yearly payments
- farm tools or schools
- rights to hunt and fish
- a "medicine chest" (Treaty 6)

**5.** To open the land for farms, settlers and the railway.

**6.** Example: the written treaty was recorded in English by the Crown, so it shows only one side's understanding. Elders' accounts show what First Nations understood was promised.
