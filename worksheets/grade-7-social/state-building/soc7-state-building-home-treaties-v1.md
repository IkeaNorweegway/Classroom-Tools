::: tip
**For the adult:** The numbered treaties (1871–1921) were agreements between First Nations and the Crown. Most of Alberta is Treaty 6, 7 or 8. The written treaties and First Nations' oral accounts often describe the same agreement differently. Your child is practising seeing both.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Read: "The written Treaty 6 says the land was given up. Many Cree Elders say it was shared." Why are there two versions?**

1. **Who wrote the first?** The Crown, in English.
2. **Where does the second come from?** What First Nations leaders heard and agreed to aloud, passed down by Elders.
3. **Why they differ:** the treaty was negotiated through interpreters, and each side understood the promises in its own way. Both are evidence.
:::
