## How you will be marked

**Outcome 7-LM:** I can explore how careers start and grow.

| Not yet | What we look for | Went further |
|---|---|---|
| | **Know it:** You explain the difference between a job and a career. You name transferable skills. Your career facts (education, wage) are correct. | |
| | **Understand it:** You explain how interests, values, experiences, and education shape a career, and that careers can change. | |

## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Job** | One role or position, often paid | Lifeguard this summer | Thirty years of work |
| **Career** | Your whole path of learning, work, and experiences | Babysitter → college → daycare director | One shift at a store |
| **Transferable skill** | A skill you can use in many jobs | Communication, teamwork | Knowing how one machine works |
| **Interest** | Something you like doing or learning about | Fixing bikes | Something you have to do |
| **Value** | Something that matters to you | Helping people | Your favourite colour |
| **Training** | Learning the skills for a job | An apprenticeship | Watching TV |
| **Apprenticeship** | Learning a trade on the job and in short classes, while getting paid | Electrician apprentice | A 4-year degree with no job |
| **Wage** | Pay for each hour you work | $38.41 an hour | A gift |
