## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Post-secondary** | Education or training after high school | College diploma | Grade 9 |
| **Degree** | What you earn from university, usually in 4 years | Bachelor of Science | A trade ticket |
| **Diploma** | What you earn from a college or polytechnic, usually in 2 years | Vet tech diploma | A driver's licence |
| **Apprenticeship** | Learning a trade on the job, paid, with short classes | Electrician apprentice | Unpaid volunteering |
| **Tuition** | What a school charges for classes | $729.36 a course (U of A, 2026–27) | Rent |
| **True cost** | Tuition + other costs + lost earnings | Tuition + rent + money not earned | Tuition only |
| **Lost earnings** | Money you could have earned by working instead | 8 months × $2,400 | Money you spent |
| **Shortage** | More jobs than workers | Not enough nurses | 300 people for 5 jobs |
| **Surplus** | More workers than jobs | 300 people for 5 jobs | Not enough nurses |
| **Trade-off** | Giving up one thing to get another | Less pay, but start sooner | Getting both |
| **Admission** | Being accepted into a program | Needs Math 30-1 and a 75% average | Signing up for a club |
