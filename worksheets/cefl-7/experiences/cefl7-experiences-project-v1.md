### Your team

You make **your own** Experience Plan. Your team of **4 or 5** gives you ideas and feedback, and makes **one** Welcome and Safety checklist together.

| Job | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Leader** | Keeps the team on track. Makes sure everyone talks. |
| **Finder** | Finds facts: places to volunteer, the rules for your age. |
| **Checker** | Checks that plans are safe and follow the rules. |
| **Designer** | Makes the team checklist clear and easy to read. |
| **Reporter** | Shares the team's questions and ideas with the class. |

## How you will be marked

**Outcome 7-EX:** I can explain how experiences shape what work I like.

Your teacher gives each row a level: **4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet.** Meets means you met the outcome.

## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curricular** | Part of your classes | Science fair project | Hockey practice |
| **Extracurricular** | Outside class, by choice | School band | Math homework |
| **Volunteering** | Unpaid work to help others | Food bank helper | A paid shift |
| **Entrepreneurship** | Starting and running your own business | Snow-shovelling business | Working at a store |
| **Self-employed** | Working for yourself | Dog walker with own clients | A town lifeguard |
| **Part-time** | Fewer hours than a full work week | 15 hours a week | 40 hours a week |
| **Seasonal** | Work only at certain times of year | Summer camp counsellor | A year-round job |
| **Work-life balance** | Fitting work and the rest of life together in a healthy way | Time for sleep, school, and fun | Working every evening |
| **Belonging** | Feeling welcome and included | Being shown around on day one | Being left out |
| **Right to refuse** | You can say no to dangerous work | Saying no to an untrained task | Skipping a boring task |
