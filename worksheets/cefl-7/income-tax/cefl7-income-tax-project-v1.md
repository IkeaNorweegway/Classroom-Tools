## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gross pay** | All the money you earned, before anything comes off | 80 hours × $20 = $1,600 | The money in your bank account |
| **Net pay** | What is left after deductions. Also called "take-home pay." | $1,293.40 goes into the bank | Your hourly wage |
| **Deduction** | Money taken off your pay before you get it | EI, CPP, income tax | A tip from a customer |
| **Wage** | Pay for each hour you work | $15.00 an hour | $52,000 a year |
| **Salary** | The same pay every time, set for the year | $52,000 a year ÷ 26 pays | Pay that changes with your hours |
| **Commission** | A percent of what you sell | 5% of $10,000 in sales | $15.00 an hour |
| **Income tax** | Money from your pay that goes to the governments of Canada and Alberta | $193.35 on Jordan's pay stub | A CPP payment |
| **CPP** | Canada Pension Plan. Saves money for when you retire. | Starts when you turn 18 | A tip |
| **EI** | Employment Insurance. Pays you for a while if you lose your job. | 1.63% of every pay | Money for school |
| **Tax-free amount** | The part of your yearly pay that is not taxed | Canada: the first $16,452 (2026) | All of your pay |
