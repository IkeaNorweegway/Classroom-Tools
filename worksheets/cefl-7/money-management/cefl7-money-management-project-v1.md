## Word cards

| Word | What it means | Example | Not an example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trade-off** | Giving up one thing to get another | Skip the concert to save for a bike | Getting both |
| **Budget** | A written plan for how you'll use your money | $130: $60 savings, $70 spending | Spending without a plan |
| **Need** | Something you must have to live | Food, a place to live | A new video game |
| **Want** | Something nice to have, but not needed | Concert ticket | Winter boots in January |
| **Financial goal** | A money target: what, how much, by when | $360 bike by June | "I want money" |
| **Short-term goal** | A goal for the next few weeks or months | Concert in 2 months | Retirement |
| **Long-term goal** | A goal a year or more away | A car, college | Snacks this week |
| **Emergency fund** | Money saved for surprises | $300 for a broken phone | Money for a planned trip |
| **Financial security** | Having enough for needs and surprises, and being able to plan ahead | Bills paid and savings growing | Owing money every month |
| **Debt** | Money you owe | Borrowing $180 from a friend | Money in savings |
