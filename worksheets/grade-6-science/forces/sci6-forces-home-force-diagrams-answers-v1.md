::: tip
**For the adult:** Neat drawings aren’t needed. Look for the right directions and sensible lengths.
:::

**1.** **push** · **pull** · **arrow**

**2.** **bigger (stronger)**

**3.** An arrow **down** (gravity) and an equal arrow **up** (the string pulling).

**4.** Arrows: **forward** (the pull), **backward** (friction), **down** (gravity), **up** (the snow pushing back).

**5.** (a) **B** (b) **U** (c) **B** (d) **U**

**6.** The forces are **balanced**: both teams pull equally hard in opposite directions.
