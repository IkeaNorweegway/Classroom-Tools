::: tip
**For the adult:** Action and reaction forces act on different objects, so they don’t cancel each other out.
:::

**1.** **equal (the same)** · **opposite**

**2.** **down (backward)** · **up (forward)**

**3.** (a) the **ground pushes you forward** (b) the **water pushes the boat forward** (c) the **wall pushes the ball back**

**4.** They push on each other **equally**. The car moves more because it has **less mass**.

**5.** (a) **E** (b) **P** (c) **E** (d) **P**

**6.** You push the ball forward, so the **ball pushes you backward** just as hard.
