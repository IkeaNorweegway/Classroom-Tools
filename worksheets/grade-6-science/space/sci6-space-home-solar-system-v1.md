## Worked example

::: example
**List the planets in order from the Sun.**

1. Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars: small, rocky planets.
2. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune: giant planets made mostly of gas and ice.
3. Pluto is a dwarf planet, beyond Neptune.
:::
