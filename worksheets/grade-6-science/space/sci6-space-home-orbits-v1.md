::: tip
**For the adult:** A satellite is falling toward Earth all the time, but it is moving sideways so fast that it keeps missing.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Why doesn’t a satellite fall down?**

1. Gravity pulls the satellite toward Earth.
2. It is also moving sideways very fast.
3. As it falls, Earth curves away beneath it, so it keeps falling around Earth. That’s an orbit.
:::
