**1.** the **curved path** an object takes around another · **toward Earth**

**2.** **natural** · **artificial (human-made)**

**3.** Any two: **GPS and maps**, **weather forecasts**, **TV and internet**, **photos of Earth**.

**4.** It would **fall toward Earth**.

**5.** Examples: **muscles and bones get weaker** without gravity, so astronauts exercise every day; food and water must be brought up; tight living space.

**6.** It looks like a **bright, fast-moving star** with no blinking lights.
