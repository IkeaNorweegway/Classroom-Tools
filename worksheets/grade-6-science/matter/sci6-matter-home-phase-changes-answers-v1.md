::: tip
**For the adult:** Water is unusual: it expands when it freezes. That explains questions 5 and 6.
:::

**1.** **solid** to **liquid** · **liquid** to **solid**

**2.** **gas** · **gas**

**3.** (a) **melting** (b) **evaporation** (c) **condensation** (d) **freezing**

**4.** **No.** The particles are the same; they’re just moving differently.

**5.** Water **expands when it freezes**, so ice takes up more space than the same mass of water and is less dense.

**6.** The water inside **freezes and expands**, pushing on the pipe until it cracks.
