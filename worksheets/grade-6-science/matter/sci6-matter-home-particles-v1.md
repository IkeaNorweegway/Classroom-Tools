::: tip
**For the adult:** Particles are much too small to see, with empty space between them. Ask "What are the particles doing?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Describe the particles in ice, liquid water and steam.**

1. Ice (solid): packed tightly in a pattern, jiggling in place.
2. Water (liquid): close together, sliding past each other.
3. Steam (gas): far apart, moving fast in every direction.
:::
