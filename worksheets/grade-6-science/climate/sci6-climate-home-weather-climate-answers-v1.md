::: tip
**For the adult:** One season is weather. Climate change shows up in the pattern over decades.
:::

**1.** **what’s happening in the air right now** · **the usual weather pattern in a place over many years**

**2.** **30**

**3.** (a) **W** (b) **C** (c) **W** (d) **C**

**4.** One winter is **weather**. Climate is the **pattern over many years**, and the long-term pattern is warming.

**5.** Any two: **thermometer**, **rain gauge**, **anemometer** (wind), **barometer**, **weather balloons**, **satellites**, **radar**.

**6.** Example: Alberta has **cold winters and warm summers**, with mostly dry weather. Southern Alberta gets **chinooks**.
