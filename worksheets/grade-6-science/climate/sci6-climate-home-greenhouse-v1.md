::: tip
**For the adult:** The greenhouse effect itself is natural and keeps Earth warm enough for life. The problem is adding extra greenhouse gases.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Why is a parked car warmer inside on a sunny day, and how is Earth similar?**

1. Sunlight passes through the windows and warms the inside.
2. The heat can’t escape easily, so the car warms up.
3. Greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide trap heat around Earth in a similar way.
4. Burning fuels adds more of these gases, so more heat is trapped.
:::
