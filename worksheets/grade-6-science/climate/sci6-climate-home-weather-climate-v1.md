::: tip
**For the adult:** Weather is today. Climate is the usual pattern over 30 years or more.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Sort these: "It’s snowing in Calgary today." "Calgary usually has cold winters with chinooks."**

1. The first is about today: weather.
2. The second is about the usual pattern over many years: climate.
:::
