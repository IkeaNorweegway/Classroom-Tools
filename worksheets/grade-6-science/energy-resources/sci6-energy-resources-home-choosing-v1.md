::: tip
**For the adult:** No energy source is perfect. Good choices weigh cost, reliability, and effects on the environment and people.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A town is choosing between a wind farm and a natural gas plant.**

1. Cost: what it costs to build and run.
2. Reliability: gas works any time; wind only when it’s windy.
3. Environment: gas releases carbon dioxide; turbines use land and can affect birds.
4. Many towns choose a mix, so one source backs up the other.
:::
