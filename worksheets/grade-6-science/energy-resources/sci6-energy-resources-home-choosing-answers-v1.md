::: tip
**For the adult:** Question 6 is an opinion. Look for reasons that mention trade-offs.
:::

**1.** **cost**, **reliability**, **effects on the environment** (also: jobs, safety, what’s available locally)

**2.** **it’s sunny** (long, clear days)

**3.** So that when one source isn’t available, like **wind on a calm day**, another can **back it up**.

**4.** Advantage: **reliable**, works any time, Alberta has a lot. Disadvantage: releases **carbon dioxide**, non-renewable.

**5.** Advantage: **renewable**, no fuel cost or emissions while running. Disadvantage: **doesn’t work at night** and makes less in winter.

**6.** Answers vary.
