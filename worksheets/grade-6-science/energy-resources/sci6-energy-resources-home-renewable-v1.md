::: tip
**For the adult:** Renewable resources won’t run out (wind, sun, moving water). Non-renewable ones took millions of years to form (oil, gas, coal).
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Is wind renewable? Is coal?**

1. Wind keeps blowing and won’t run out: renewable.
2. Coal formed over millions of years and we use it far faster: non-renewable.
:::
