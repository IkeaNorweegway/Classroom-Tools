::: tip
**For the adult:** Biotic = living (or once living). Abiotic = non-living, like sunlight, water, soil and temperature.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**List the biotic and abiotic parts of a pond and how they connect.**

1. Biotic: fish, frogs, algae, cattails.
2. Abiotic: water, sunlight, temperature, rocks.
3. Connection: sunlight lets algae make food, and fish eat the algae.
:::
