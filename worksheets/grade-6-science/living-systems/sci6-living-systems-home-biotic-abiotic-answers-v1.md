::: tip
**For the adult:** Question 5 is a thinking question: fallen leaves came from a living thing, so most scientists call them biotic.
:::

**1.** **living, or once living** · **non-living**

**2.** sunlight **A** · frog **B** · soil **A** · mushroom **B** · water **A** · grass **B**

**3.** Examples: cold water slows down cold-blooded animals; ponds freeze in winter; warm water holds less oxygen.

**4.** They need **sunlight, water and carbon dioxide**. They make **sugar (food) and oxygen**.

**5.** **Biotic**, because they came from a living tree. (Soil is mostly abiotic, though it contains broken-down biotic material.)

**6.** Answers vary.
