**For the adult:** For question 6, any chain that ends at a plant and the Sun is right, e.g. Sun → wheat → bread.
:::

**1.** **make their own food** · **eat other living things** · **break down dead things**

**2.** **food** · **eater**

**3.** Producers: **grass, dandelion**. Consumers: **rabbit, fox**. Decomposers: **mushroom, earthworm**.

**4.** **seeds → mouse → hawk**

**5.** They would **lose food**, so their numbers might drop, or they might eat **more of other animals**.

**6.** Example: **Sun → grass → cow → milk**.
