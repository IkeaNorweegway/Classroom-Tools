::: tip
**For the adult:** Arrows point from the food to the eater. All the energy starts with the Sun.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**grass → grasshopper → bird → hawk. Name each role.**

1. Grass makes its own food from sunlight: producer.
2. Grasshopper, bird and hawk eat other living things: consumers.
3. The arrows show energy moving from the food to the eater.
:::
