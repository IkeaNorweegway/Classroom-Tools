::: tip
**For the adult:** Dividing by a fraction asks "how many of these fit?" How many quarters fit in $3? Twelve. So 3 ÷ 1/4 = 12. The shortcut: keep the first number, change ÷ to ×, flip the second fraction.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find 3/4 ÷ 1/8.**

1. Ask: how many eighths fit in three quarters? 3/4 = 6/8, so 6 eighths fit.
2. Shortcut: keep 3/4, change ÷ to ×, flip 1/8 to 8/1.
3. 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6. Same answer.
:::
