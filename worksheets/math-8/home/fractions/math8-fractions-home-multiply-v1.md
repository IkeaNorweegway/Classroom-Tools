::: tip
**For the adult:** To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms, then simplify. Mixed numbers must be changed to improper fractions first. Multiplying by a fraction smaller than 1 gives a SMALLER answer — that surprises many students.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find 1 1/2 × 2/3.**

1. Change the mixed number: 1 1/2 = 3/2 (1 whole is 2 halves, plus 1 half).
2. Multiply tops and bottoms: 3/2 × 2/3 = 6/6.
3. Simplify: 6/6 = 1.
:::
