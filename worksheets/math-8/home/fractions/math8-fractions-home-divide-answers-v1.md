**For the adult:** Check any answer by multiplying it by the fraction you divided by. You should get back the first number.
:::

**1.** **10** (each whole has 2 halves)

**2.** **8**

**3.** **2** (two pieces of 1/3 fit in 2/3)

**4.** 3/5 × 1/3 = 3/15 = **1/5**

**5.** 5/6 × **3** = 15/6 = **2 1/2**

**6.** 5/2 × 2 = 10/2 = **5**

**7.** 6 × 4/3 = 24/3 = **8 pieces**. Check: 8 × 3/4 = 6 ✓
