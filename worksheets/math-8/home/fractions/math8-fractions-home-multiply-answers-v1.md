::: tip
**For the adult:** Simplifying can happen before or after multiplying. Either way is correct.
:::

**1.** 1 × 1 = 1, 2 × 4 = 8: **1/8**

**2.** 6/15 = **2/5**

**3.** 4/1 × 3/8 = 12/8 = **1 1/2**

**4.** 3/4 × 20 = 60/4 = **15**

**5.** 5/2 × 1/5 = 5/10 = **1/2**

**6.** 4/3 × 3/2 = 12/6 = **2**

**7.** 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = **1/2 cup**
