::: tip
**For the adult:** For any relation, ask three questions: Is it going up or down? Where does it start? How much does it change each step? Those three answers give the equation.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A candle is 24 cm tall. It burns 3 cm every hour.**

1. It goes down, so the relation is decreasing.
2. It starts at 24 cm.
3. It changes by −3 cm each hour.
4. Equation: h = 24 − 3t. After 5 hours: 24 − 15 = 9 cm.
:::
