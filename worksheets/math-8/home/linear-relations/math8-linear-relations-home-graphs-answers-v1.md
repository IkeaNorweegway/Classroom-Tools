::: tip
**For the adult:** Any equation that matches the words is fine, for example s = 5w + 10 or s = 10 + 5w.
:::

**1.** **Up** (increasing). Starts at **$10**. Changes by **+$5** each week.

**2.** **s = 5w + 10**

**3.** 5 × 8 + 10 = **$50**

**4.** **w = 500 − 25t**

**5.** 25t = 500, so t = **20 minutes**.

**6.** **No.** Students and buses are counted in whole numbers. There is no half a student, so plot dots only.
