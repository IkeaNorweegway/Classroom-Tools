**For the adult:** The most common slip is adding the bottoms: −1/2 + 1/3 is not 0/5. The second is working left to right in Q5. Multiply first, then add.
:::

## Answers

**1.** −3/4 + 1/4 = **−2/4 = −1/2**.

**2.** 2.5 + (−6.8) = **−4.3**. Jump 6.8 to the left.
@viz numberline min=-5 max=3 step=0.5 every=1 width=360 points="2.5:start;-4.3:end" jumps="2.5>-4.3" jumplabels="−6.8"

**3.** Same signs, so positive. 1.5 × 4 = **6**.

**4.** Size: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2. One negative, so **−1/2**.
@viz area a=2/3 b=3/4 result="2/3 × 3/4 = [[1/2]]"
**5.** Multiply first: 6 × (−0.5) = −3. Then −3 + (−3) = **−6**. (Not −1.5.)

**6.** Add: −4.5 + 1.5 + (−3) = −6. Divide: −6 ÷ 3 = **−2 °C**.
**7.** Estimate: −5 × 3 = −15. Exact: −4.9 × 3.1 = **−15.19**. **Yes**, it is close to −15 and negative.
