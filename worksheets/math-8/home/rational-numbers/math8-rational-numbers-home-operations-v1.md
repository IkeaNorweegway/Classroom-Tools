::: tip
**For the adult:** To add fractions, find a common bottom number first. Never add the bottoms. For × and ÷: same signs give a positive answer, different signs give a negative answer. Do × and ÷ before + and −.
:::

::: example
**Find −1/2 + 1/3.**
Step 1: Common bottom 6: −1/2 = −3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6.
Step 2: Each chip is 1/6. Two zero pairs cancel.
@viz chips pos=2 neg=3 pairs=2 title="Each chip = 1/6" result="−3/6 + 2/6 = −1/6"
Step 3: One negative sixth is left. −1/2 + 1/3 = −1/6.
:::
