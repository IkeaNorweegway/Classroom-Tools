::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is thinking whole numbers are not rational. They are: 6 = 6/1. The second is with negatives: −0.75 is smaller than −0.7, because it is further left.
:::

## Answers

**1.** a) **7/1** b) **−1/2** (or −5/10) c) **3/2**

**2.** 6 is a natural number. −4 is an integer. 2/5 is rational. (All three are rational.)
@viz rnsets nat="[[6]]" int="[[−4]]" rat="[[2/5]]"
**3.** 2/3 = **0.666…** The 6 **repeats** forever. The remainder 2 keeps coming back.
**4.** −0.333… is further left than −0.3. So **−0.3 is greater**.

**5.** Any two from 0.51 to 0.59, such as **0.55 and 0.58**. (0.501 works too.)

**6.** **−8 °C, −3.5 °C, −0.5 °C, 1 °C.**
@viz numberline min=-10 max=2 step=1 every=2 width=360 points="-8:−8;-3.5:−3.5;-0.5:−0.5;1:1"
**7.** **No.** 0.3 = 3/10 and it stops. 0.333… = 1/3 and never stops. 1/3 is a bit bigger than 0.3.
