::: tip
**For the adult:** A quick sense check: "and" answers are always smaller than each probability on its own.
:::

**1.** **1/4**

**2.** 1/6 × 1/6 = **1/36**

**3.** 1/2 × 1/2 = **1/4**

**4.** 1/4 × 1/4 = **1/16**

**5.** 0.5 × 0.3 = 0.15 = **15%**

**6.** Adding gives 2/3, which is bigger than each chance alone. But both things must happen, so the chance must be smaller. Multiply: 1/2 × 1/6 = **1/12**.
