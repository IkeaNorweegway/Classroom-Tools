::: tip
**For the adult:** HT and TH are different outcomes (first coin heads, second tails, or the other way).
:::

**1.** HH, HT, TH, TT: **4 outcomes**.

**2.** P(two heads) = **1/4**. P(one head, one tail) = HT or TH = **2/4 = 1/2**.

**3.** 3 × 2 = **6 outcomes** (RH, RT, BH, BT, GH, GT).

**4.** **1/6**

**5.** **1/2**. Each flip is independent: the coin doesn't remember the last flips.
