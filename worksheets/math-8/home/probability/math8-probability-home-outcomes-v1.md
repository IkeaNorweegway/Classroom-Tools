::: tip
**For the adult:** When two things happen (flip a coin AND roll a die), list every pair of results in a table or a tree. The number of outcomes is the number for the first thing times the number for the second (2 × 6 = 12). Probability = how many outcomes you want ÷ how many in all.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Flip a coin and roll a die. What is P(heads and an even number)?**

1. Outcomes: H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, H6, T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6 (12 in all).
2. The ones we want: H2, H4, H6 (3 of them).
3. P = 3/12 = 1/4.
:::
