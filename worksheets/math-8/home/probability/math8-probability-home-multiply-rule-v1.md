::: tip
**For the adult:** When two events don't affect each other (independent), the chance that BOTH happen is the two probabilities multiplied. The answer is always smaller than either one, because both have to happen. Fractions, decimals or percents all work.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Roll a die and flip a coin. What is P(a 5 and tails)?**

1. P(5) = 1/6. P(tails) = 1/2.
2. Multiply: 1/6 × 1/2 = 1/12.
3. Check: the table of outcomes has 12 cells, and only one is "5 and tails."
:::
