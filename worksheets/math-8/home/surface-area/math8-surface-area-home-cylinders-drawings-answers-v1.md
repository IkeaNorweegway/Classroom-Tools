::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is making the label as long as the diameter (Q4). It wraps around, so it is about 3.14 times as long. The second is leaving out a circle, or covering a top that isn't there (Q3). In Q6, cubes can hide behind others, so use all three views.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 3.14 × 10 = **31.4 cm**.

**2.** Circles: 2 × 3.14 × 2 × 2 = 25.12 cm². Rectangle: 2 × 3.14 × 2 × 5 = 62.8 cm². SA = 87.92, about **87.9 cm²**.

**3.** Length: 3.14 × 2 = 6.28 m. Area: 6.28 × 3 = **18.84 m²**.

**4.** The label goes around the can. Its length is the circumference: 3.14 × 8 = **25.12 cm**.

**5.** Top: back row 2 squares, front row 1 (left). Front: 1, then 2. Right side: front 1, back 2.
