::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is using the pyramid's height (inside it) instead of the slant height (on the face), as in Q5. The second is finding the volume (8 × 2 × 3 = 48 cm³ for the example box) instead of the surface area. SA is in cm², volume in cm³.
:::

## Answers

**1.** One face: 4 × 4 = 16 cm². SA = 6 × 16 = **96 cm²**.

**2.** Front and back 5 × 3 = 15, sides 2 × 3 = 6, top and bottom 5 × 2 = 10. SA = 15 + 15 + 6 + 6 + 10 + 10 = **62 cm²**.
@viz net type=box lv=5 wv=2 hv=3 n1="15" n2="15" n3="6" n4="6" n5="10" n6="10"
**3.** Base: 4 × 4 = 16 cm². Triangles: 4 × ½ × 4 × 6 = 48 cm². SA = **64 cm²**.
@viz sapnet bv=4 sv=6 b="4 cm" s="6 cm" n1="16" n2="12" n3="12" n4="12" n5="12" size=150

**4.** Ends: 2 × ½ × 6 × 4 = 24 m². Rectangles: 5 × 10 + 5 × 10 + 6 × 10 = 160 m². SA = **184 m²**.

**5.** Use the slant height, 13 cm, not the height. Base: 100 cm². Triangles: 4 × ½ × 10 × 13 = 260 cm². SA = **360 cm²**. (Using 12 cm gives 340 cm², which is wrong.)

**6.** **Yes.** The column of 4 wraps around. The two squares at the top fold to the left and right sides.
