::: tip
**For the adult:** Unroll a can. You get two circles and a rectangle. The rectangle wraps once around the can, so its length is the **circumference**: 3.14 × diameter. SA = the two circles + the rectangle. Use π ≈ 3.14. For a cube building, a **view** shows only the squares you see from that side.
:::

::: example
**A can has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 10 cm. Find its surface area.**
@viz net type=cyl rv=3 hv=10 r="3 cm" h="10 cm" c="2 × 3.14 × 3 = 18.84 cm"
Step 1: Two circles: 2 × 3.14 × 3 × 3 = 56.52 cm².
Step 2: The rectangle: 18.84 × 10 = 188.4 cm².
Step 3: SA = 56.52 + 188.4 = 244.92. About 244.9 cm².
:::
