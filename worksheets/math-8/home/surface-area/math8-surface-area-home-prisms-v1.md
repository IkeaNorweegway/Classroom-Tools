::: tip
**For the adult:** **Surface area** (SA) covers the outside of a solid, like wrapping paper. Unfold it into a flat **net**, find the area of each face and add. Answers are in cm². A pyramid's triangles use the **slant height**, which runs up the face.
:::

::: example
**A box is 8 cm long, 2 cm wide and 3 cm tall. Find its surface area.**
@viz net type=box lv=8 wv=2 hv=3 n1="24" n2="24" n3="6" n4="6" n5="16" n6="16"
Step 1: Front and back: 8 × 3 = 24 cm² each. Sides: 2 × 3 = 6 cm² each. Top and bottom: 8 × 2 = 16 cm² each.
Step 2: SA = 24 + 24 + 6 + 6 + 16 + 16 = 92 cm².
:::
