::: tip
**For the adult:** Volume is how much a container holds. For boxes, triangle-shaped prisms and cans, there is one rule: find the area of the end (the base), then multiply by the height or length. Use 3.14 for π. Volume is in cubic units (cm³).
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A can has a radius of 4 cm and a height of 10 cm. How much does it hold?**

1. The base is a circle. Area = π × r × r = 3.14 × 4 × 4 = 50.24 cm²
2. Multiply by the height: 50.24 × 10 = 502.4 cm³
:::
