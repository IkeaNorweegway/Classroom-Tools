::: tip
**For the adult:** Units matter: surface area is in square units (cm²).
:::

**1.** Front and back: 2 × (5 × 4) = **40**. Top and bottom: 2 × (5 × 2) = **20**. Sides: 2 × (2 × 4) = **16**. Total: **76 cm²**.

**2.** One face: 36 cm². Six faces: **216 cm²**.

**3.** 2(200) + 2(100) + 2(50) = 400 + 200 + 100 = **700 cm²**.

**4.** Only a cube has six equal faces. This box has three different face sizes (200, 100 and 50 cm²).

**5.** Answers vary.
