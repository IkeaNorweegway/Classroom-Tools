::: tip
**For the adult:** To remove brackets, multiply EVERY term inside by the number in front. Watch the signs: −2 × (−3) = +6. When x is on both sides, subtract the smaller x-term from BOTH sides. Answers can be fractions or negative numbers. That is fine.
:::

::: example
**Solve 3(x − 1) = x + 5.**
@viz sedist factor="3" terms="x;− 1" result="3x;− 3"
Step 1: Expand: 3x − 3 = x + 5.
Step 2: Subtract x from both sides: 2x − 3 = 5. Add 3 to both sides: 2x = 8.
Step 3: Divide by 2: x = 4. Check: 3(4 − 1) = 9 and 4 + 5 = 9 ✓
:::
