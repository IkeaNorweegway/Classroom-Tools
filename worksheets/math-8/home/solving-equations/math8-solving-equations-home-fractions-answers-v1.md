::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is multiplying only the fraction term. In Q7, Maya multiplied the (1/3)x and the 6 but not the 4. Every term on both sides must be multiplied. Checking the answer in the first equation catches this every time.
:::

## Answers

**1.** × 2: x + 8 = 14. **x = 6**. Check: 3 + 4 = 7 ✓

**2.** × 4: x − 4 = 8. **x = 12**. Check: (1/4)(12) − 1 = 3 − 1 = 2 ✓

**3.** × 10: 3x + 6 = 24. 3x = 18. **x = 6**.

**4.** One fifth is 6 ÷ 2 = 3. All five fifths: 5 × 3 = **15**. (Or × 5: 2x = 30, x = 15.)

**5.** The LCD of 2, 3 and 6 is 6. × 6: 3x + 2 = 5. 3x = 3. **x = 1**.

**6.** Two decimal places: × 100. 25x + 50 = 300. 25x = 250. **x = 10**.

**7.** She did not multiply the 4 by 3. × 3: x + **12** = 18. **x = 6**. Check: 2 + 4 = 6 ✓
