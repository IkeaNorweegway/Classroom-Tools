::: tip
**For the adult:** Two slips are common. One is multiplying only the first term in the brackets, or missing a sign: −3(x + 4) is −3x − 12. The other is changing one side only. To remove 0.4x from the right, subtract 0.4x from BOTH sides. Adding it (Noor in Q7) makes it worse.
:::

## Answers

**1.** −3 × x = **−3x**. −3 × 4 = **−12**. So −3x − 12.
@viz sedist factor="−3" terms="x;+ 4" result="[[−3x]];[[− 12]]"
**2.** ÷ 5: x − 2 = 3. **x = 5**. (Or 5x − 10 = 15, 5x = 25.)

**3.** − 0.2x: x + 1 = 4. **x = 3**. Check: 3.6 + 1 = 4.6 and 0.6 + 4 = 4.6 ✓

**4.** × 4: 2x + 4 = x + 12. Subtract x: x + 4 = 12. **x = 8**.
@viz semult top="½x;+ 1;=;¼x;+ 3" by="× 4" bottom="[[2x]];+ [[4]];=;x;+ [[12]]"
**5.** Subtract x: 3x + 3 = 8. 3x = 5. **x = 5/3** (about 1.67).

**6.** 3x + 6 = 5x − 10. Subtract 3x: 6 = 2x − 10. 16 = 2x. **x = 8**. Check: 3(10) = 30 and 5(6) = 30 ✓

**7.** She added 0.4x instead of subtracting it. Subtract 0.4x from both sides: 0.5x + 2 = 5. 0.5x = 3. **x = 6**.
