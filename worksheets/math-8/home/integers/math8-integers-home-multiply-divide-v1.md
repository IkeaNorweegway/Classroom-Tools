::: tip
**For the adult:** Multiply or divide the numbers as usual, then decide the sign by counting the negative signs: an even number of negatives gives a positive answer; an odd number gives a negative answer. This rule is only for × and ÷, not for + and −.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find (−6) × (−4) and (−30) ÷ 5.**

1. (−6) × (−4): 6 × 4 = 24. Two negatives (even), so the answer is positive: 24.
2. (−30) ÷ 5: 30 ÷ 5 = 6. One negative (odd), so the answer is negative: −6.
:::
