::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is using "two negatives make a positive" for adding. −3 + (−4) is −7, not 7.
:::

**1.** **−15** (one negative)

**2.** **28** (two negatives)

**3.** **−48**

**4.** **−9**

**5.** **5**

**6.** 2 × 3 × 5 = 30. Three negatives (odd), so **−30**.

**7.** 5 × (−3) = **−15**. The temperature fell 15 °C.
