**1.** 3 + **(−8)** = **−5**

**2.** **−10** − 6 = **−16**

**3.** −4 + 10 = **6**

**4.** (−5) × (−4) = **20**

**5.** 20 ÷ (−4) = −5, then (−5) × (−2) = **10**

**6.** Sum: −4 + 6 + (−10) + 0 = −8. Mean: (−8) ÷ 4 = **−2**.
