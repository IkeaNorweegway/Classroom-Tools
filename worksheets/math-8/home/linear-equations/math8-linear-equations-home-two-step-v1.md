::: tip
**For the adult:** Think of an equation as a balance: do the same thing to both sides. Undo the steps in reverse order: first undo the + or −, then undo the × or ÷. Ask your child to check each answer by putting it back into the equation.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Solve 3x + 4 = 19.**

1. Undo + 4: subtract 4 from both sides. 3x = 15
2. Undo × 3: divide both sides by 3. x = 5
3. Check: 3 × 5 + 4 = 15 + 4 = 19 ✓
:::
