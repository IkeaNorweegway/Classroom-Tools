::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is multiplying by 100 instead of 360, or using the count as the angle. The angles must add to 360°. A circle graph shows parts of one whole at one time, so change over time (Q5 a) needs a line graph.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 0.50 × 360° = **180°**.

**2.** 36 ÷ 360 = 0.1 = **10%**.

**3.** Dog **180°**, cat **90°**, fish **54°**, none **36°**. Check: 180 + 90 + 54 + 36 = 360°.

**4.** 120 ÷ 360 = 1/3. 1/3 × 60 = **20 people**.

**5.** a) **line graph** (change over time) b) **circle graph** (parts of one whole) c) **histogram** (measured data)

**6.** 0.60 × $3000 = **$1800**.
