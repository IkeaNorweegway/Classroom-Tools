::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is thinking any three lengths make a triangle. Test the two shorter sides against the longest: the sum must be more, not equal. For angles, the size of the triangle does not matter: every triangle has 180°.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 68° + 27° = 95°. x = 180° − 95° = **85°**.

**2.** x + x + 30° = 180°, so 2x = 150° and x = **75°**. Both base angles are 75°.
@viz polygon pts="0,0;2,0;1,3.73" ticks="0,1,1" arcs="1,1,0" angles="75°;75°;30°" unit=22
**3.** 180° − 90° − 25° = **65°**.

**4.** 4 + 5 = 9. 9 is less than 10, so there is a gap. **No.**

**5.** 6 + 8 = 14. 14 > 12, so **yes**.

**6.** More than 9 − 5 = 4 and less than 9 + 5 = 14: **4 < x < 14** (open dots at 4 and 14).

**7.** **No.** 3 + 4 = 7 is equal, not more. The straws lie flat on the 7 cm one. No triangle.
