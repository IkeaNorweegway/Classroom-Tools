::: tip
**For the adult:** The three angles of every triangle add to **180°**. An **isosceles** triangle has two equal sides, and the two angles at its base are equal. Three lengths make a triangle only if the two shorter ones add to MORE than the longest. If they add to exactly the longest, the sides lie flat.
:::

::: example
**Find the missing angle x.**
@viz polygon pts="0,0;6,0;4.04,3.39" angles="40°;60°;x" unit=22
Angles add to 180°. 40° + 60° = 100°. x = 180° − 100° = 80°.
:::
