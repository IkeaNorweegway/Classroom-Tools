::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is adding instead of multiplying (Q6). Adding gives a bigger chance than either event alone, which can't be right for "both". The second is adding outcomes: a café with 4 soups and 3 breads has 4 × 3 = 12 combos, not 7.
:::

## Answers

**1.** S = **{HH, HT, TH, TT}**.
@viz tree first="H,T" second="H,T" outcomes=blank
**2.** 4 × 3 = **12 combos**.

**3.** 1/2 × 1/6 = **1/12**. Only T5 matches, 1 of the 12 cells.
@viz outcomes rows="H,T" cols="1,2,3,4,5,6" highlight="T5" rowlabel="coin" collabel="die"
**4.** 1/4 × 1/4 = **1/16** = 0.0625, about 6.3%.

**5.** The bag is the same both times. 2/5 × 2/5 = **4/25** = 0.16 = **16%**.

**6.** **No.** For "and", multiply: 1/2 × 1/6 = **1/12**.
