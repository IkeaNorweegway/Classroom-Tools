::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is dividing the bigger number by the smaller one. k is always second ÷ first, so 8 : 2 gives 0.25, not 4. The second is multiplying when the second amount is known (the map in Q5 b). Then you divide.
:::

## Answers

**1.** k = 12 ÷ 3 = **4**.

**2.** 1.5 × 2 = **3**, 1.5 × 4 = **6**, 1.5 × 6 = **9**.
@viz prk top="2,4,6" bottom="[[3]],[[6]],[[9]]" toplabel="first (a)" bottomlabel="second (b)" k="× 1.5"
**3.** 42 ÷ 30 = 1.4. The unit rate is **$1.40 per litre**.

**4.** 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5 and 16 ÷ 10 = 1.6. **No**, the k values are different, so the ratios are not equivalent.

**5.** k = 5. a) 5 × 9 = **45 km**. b) We know the real length, so divide: 32 ÷ 5 = **6.4 cm**.
@viz prk top="1,9,[[6.4]]" bottom="5,[[45]],32" toplabel="map (cm)" bottomlabel="real (km)" k="× 5"
**6.** k = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75 cups for each person. 0.75 × 10 = **7.5 cups**.

**7.** **No.** k = second ÷ first = 2 ÷ 8 = **0.25**. He divided the wrong way. (For 2 : 8, k would be 4.)
