::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is treating inverse as direct: "twice the workers, twice the time." Ask whether the job gets bigger when more people help. It does not, so the time goes down. A direct proportion graph is a straight line through (0, 0); k is its slope.
:::

## Answers

**1.** a) **Direct.** b) **Inverse** (more friends, smaller share). c) **Inverse** (faster, less time).

**2.** 100 × 2 = **200 km**. 100 × 3.5 = **350 km**.

**3.** Inverse. 3 × 8 = 24 volunteer-minutes. 24 ÷ 6 = **4 min**.
@viz prwork cases="3,8;6,[[4]]" rowlabel="volunteers" collabel="min" labels="3 × 8 = 24;6 × [[4]] = 24"
**4.** 1 × 12 = 2 × 6 = 4 × 3 = 12. **Inverse**, k = 12. When x = 6, y = 12 ÷ 6 = **2**.

**5.** k = 32 ÷ 2 = **$16 per hour**. In 5 h: 16 × 5 = **$80**. **Yes**: it is direct proportion, so the graph is a straight line through (0, 0) with slope 16.

**6.** The gift costs 4 × $15 = $60. $60 ÷ 6 = **$10 each**. Inverse.

**7.** **No.** It is inverse proportion. Twice as many workers share the same job, so it takes **half** as long.
