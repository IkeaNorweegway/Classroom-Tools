::: tip
**For the adult:** In **direct** proportion, both amounts grow together: twice the gas costs twice as much. In **inverse** proportion, one goes up and the other goes down: twice the workers take half the time. Check: divide each pair (direct stays the same) or multiply (inverse stays the same).
:::

::: example
**2 workers shovel the outdoor rink in 6 h. How long do 3 workers take?**
@viz prwork cases="2,6;3,4" rowlabel="workers" collabel="h" labels="2 × 6 = 12;3 × 4 = 12"
Step 1: More workers means less time. This is inverse. The job is 2 × 6 = 12 worker-hours.
Step 2: 3 workers share the 12 hours: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 h.
:::

## Your turn

**1.** Direct or inverse? a) Litres of gas and the cost. b) Friends sharing a $60 gift and the cost for each. c) Speed and time for the same trip. ___ *(Ask: do both go up together?)*

**2.** A car drives at 100 km/h. Fill in the distances. *(Direct: multiply the hours by 100.)*

**3.** 3 volunteers set up the gym chairs in 8 min. How long do 6 volunteers take? *(Find 3 × 8 first.)*
@viz prwork cases="3,8" rowlabel="volunteers" collabel="min" labels="3 × 8 = 24"
**4.** Is this pattern direct or inverse? When x = 1, 2 and 4, y = 12, 6 and 3. Find y when x = 6. ___ *(Try x × y for each pair.)*

**5.** Sam earns $32 in 2 h. Find k. What does he earn in 5 h? Would a graph of his pay be a straight line through (0, 0)? ___

**6.** 4 friends share the cost of a gift. Each pays $15. How much would each pay if 6 friends share it? ___

**7.** Jay says 8 workers take twice as long as 4 workers, because there are twice as many workers. Is he right? Explain. ___
