::: tip
**For the adult:** For a ratio a : b, the **constant of proportionality** is k = b ÷ a (second number ÷ first number). The order matters. Once you know k, multiply by k to go from the first amount to the second. Divide by k to go back.
:::

::: example
**Lee pays $29.00 for 20 L of gas. Find k for litres : dollars. Then find the cost of 40 L.**
@viz prk top="1,20,40" bottom="$1.45,$29.00,$58.00" toplabel="gas (L)" bottomlabel="cost ($)" k="× 1.45" one=1
Step 1: k = 29 ÷ 20 = 1.45. That is $1.45 for each litre (the unit rate).
Step 2: We know the first amount, 40 L. Multiply: 1.45 × 40 = $58.00.
:::
