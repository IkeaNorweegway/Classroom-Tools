::: tip
**For the adult:** The goal is the habit of asking "Is this fair?" when reading a graph.
:::

**1.** The gaps are **uneven** (10, 10, 30, 50). Bars or lines won't be in proportion.

**2.** Doubling the height **and** the width makes the picture **4 times** the area, so it looks like 4 times as much cereal.

**3.** **What happened in the other months?** Three months may not show the real trend.

**4.** **No.** 40% is less than half. "Most" means more than 50%.

**5.** Answers vary.
