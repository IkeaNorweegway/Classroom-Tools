::: tip
**For the adult:** Ads and headlines sometimes use true numbers in a graph that gives the wrong idea. Common tricks: the number line up the side doesn't start at 0; the gaps between numbers are uneven; a picture is made bigger in two directions; only part of the data is shown.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A bar graph shows Brand A at 52 and Brand B at 48. The side axis starts at 45. Brand A's bar looks twice as tall.**

1. Read the numbers: 52 and 48.
2. The real difference is 4, which is small.
3. The axis starting at 45 makes the gap look huge. That is misleading.
:::
