::: tip
**For the adult:** More than one answer can be right if the reason fits.
:::

**1.** **Bar graph** (compare groups). A circle graph also works if you want percents of the whole.

**2.** **Circle graph** (parts of one whole).

**3.** **Line graph** (change over time).

**4.** **Pictograph**.

**5.** Hockey 8 ÷ 20 = **40%**. Soccer 7 ÷ 20 = **35%**. Basketball 5 ÷ 20 = **25%**. (Total 100%.)

**6.** Answers vary.
