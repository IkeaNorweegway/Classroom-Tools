::: tip
**For the adult:** Most numbers are not perfect squares, so we estimate. Keep this list handy: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144. Ask, "Which two perfect squares is it between?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Estimate √30.**

1. Find the perfect squares on each side: 25 < 30 < 36.
2. Take their square roots: √25 = 5 and √36 = 6. So √30 is between 5 and 6.
3. 30 is 5 away from 25 and 6 away from 36. It is a little closer to 25, so √30 is a little less than 5.5.
4. Estimate: about 5.4 or 5.5. (A calculator says 5.477…)
:::
