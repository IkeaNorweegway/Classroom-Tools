::: tip
**For the adult:** Squaring means a number times itself (5² = 5 × 5). A square root goes backwards (√25 = 5). Ask your child to check every square root by multiplying it by itself.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find √49.**

1. Ask: what number times itself makes 49?
2. Try 7: 7 × 7 = 49. Yes!
3. So √49 = 7. Picture a square made of 49 tiles: each side has 7 tiles.
:::
