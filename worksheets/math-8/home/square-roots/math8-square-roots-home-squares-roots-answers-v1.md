**For the adult:** If an answer is wrong, have your child multiply their square root by itself. The check usually shows the mistake.

**1.** 6 × 6 = **36**

**2.** 10 × 10 = **100**

**3.** √36 = **6** because 6 × 6 = 36

**4.** √81 = **9**. Check: 9 × 9 = 81 ✓

**5.** √144 = **12** (12 × 12 = 144)

**6.** Perfect squares: **16** (4 × 4), **25** (5 × 5), **64** (8 × 8). The others are not: no whole number times itself makes 20, 40 or 90.

**7.** √121 = **11**, so each side is 11 dm (11 × 11 = 121).
