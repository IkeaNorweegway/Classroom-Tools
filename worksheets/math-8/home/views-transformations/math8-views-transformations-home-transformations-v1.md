::: tip
**For the adult:** Points are written (x, y): x is across, y is up. A slide (translation) adds to x and y. A flip in the x-axis keeps x and changes the sign of y. A flip in the y-axis changes the sign of x. A half turn (180°) about (0, 0) changes both signs.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Point A(3, 2). Find A′ after each move.**

1. Slide 5 left and 1 up: (3 − 5, 2 + 1) = (−2, 3)
2. Flip in the x-axis: (3, −2)
3. Flip in the y-axis: (−3, 2)
4. Half turn about (0, 0): (−3, −2)
:::
