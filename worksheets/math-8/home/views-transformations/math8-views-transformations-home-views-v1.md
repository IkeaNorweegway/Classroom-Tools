::: tip
**For the adult:** Use any small blocks, dice or sugar cubes. Build a small shape on the table. A view is what you see from one direction, drawn as flat squares. Get down to table level to see the front and side views. Hidden blocks behind others do not show.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Build: a row of 3 blocks, with one more block stacked on the left one.**

1. Top view (looking down): 3 squares in a row.
2. Front view: 3 columns. The left column is 2 squares tall; the others are 1.
3. Right side view: you see only the end of the row: 1 column, 2 squares tall (the tall stack shows above the near block).
:::
