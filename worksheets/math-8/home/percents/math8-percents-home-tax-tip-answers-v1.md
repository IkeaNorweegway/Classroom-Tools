::: tip
**For the adult:** Questions 5 and 6 are the surprising ones. The second change is a percent of the new amount, not the starting amount.
:::

**1.** GST = **$1.00**. Total = **$21.00**.

**2.** **20%** of $60 = **$12**. Total = **$72**.

**3.** 5% of $90 = $4.50. Total = **$94.50**.

**4.** 20% of $50 = $10. Sale price = **$40**.

**5.** 20% of $40 = $8. New price = **$32**. That is $18 off $50, which is **36%, not 40%**.

**6.** $100 → $110 (10% of 100 = 10) → **$121** (10% of 110 = 11).
