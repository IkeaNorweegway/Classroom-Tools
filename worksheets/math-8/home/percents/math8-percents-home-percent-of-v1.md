::: tip
**For the adult:** Your child learned to build percents from easy ones: 10% (divide by 10), 5% (half of 10%), 1% (divide by 100). Ask, "What is 10% first?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Find 15% of $60.**

1. 10% of $60: divide by 10. That is $6.
2. 5% is half of 10%. Half of $6 is $3.
3. 15% = 10% + 5% = $6 + $3 = $9.
:::
