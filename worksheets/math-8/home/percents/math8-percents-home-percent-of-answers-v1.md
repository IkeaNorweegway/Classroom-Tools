::: tip
**For the adult:** A calculator check is fine: 15% of 80 is 0.15 × 80. The mental method matters more, because it shows whether an answer is reasonable.
:::

**1.** 10% of $40 = **$4**. 5% = **$2**.

**2.** 10% of 90 = **9**. 20% = **18**.

**3.** 50% of 64 = **32** (half).

**4.** 10% = $8, 5% = $4, so 15% = **$12**.

**5.** 120 ÷ 4 = **30**.

**6.** 1% = **5**. 3% = **15**.

**7.** 10% of $70 = $7, so 30% = **$21 saved**. You pay $70 − $21 = **$49**.
