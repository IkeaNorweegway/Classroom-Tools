::: tip
**For the adult:** In Alberta, GST is 5%. When two percents are of the same bill (tax and tip), you can add them. When the second percent is of a new price (a sale on a sale), you must do one step at a time.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A $40 dinner. Add 5% GST and a 15% tip.**

1. Both percents are of the same $40, so add them: 5% + 15% = 20%.
2. 20% of $40: 10% is $4, so 20% is $8.
3. Total: $40 + $8 = $48.
:::
