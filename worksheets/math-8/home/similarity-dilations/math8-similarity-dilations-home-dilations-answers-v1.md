::: tip
**For the adult:** The most common slip is adding k instead of multiplying. Adding slides the shape; it does not grow. Check: (0, 0), a corner and its image should sit on one straight line.
:::

## Answers

**1.** **P′(3, 3), Q′(6, 3), R′(3, 6).**
@viz sddilate pts="1,1;2,1;1,2" k=3 names="P,Q,R" image=key rays=1 range=6
**2.** **S′(2, 3)** and **T′(4, 1).**

**3.** He added 2. Multiply instead: (2 × 2, 3 × 2) = **(4, 6)**.

**4.** 8 ÷ 2 = 4 and 4 ÷ 1 = 4. **k = 4.**

**5.** **Yes.** AB = 2 and A′B′ = 4. AC = 2 and A′C′ = 4. Every side is × 2, and both have a right angle at A. Same shape, so similar.
@viz sddilate pts="1,1;3,1;1,3" k=2 names="A,B,C" image=show rays=1 range=6
**6.** 6 ÷ 3 = 2, 8 ÷ 4 = 2, but 11 ÷ 5 = 2.2. Not one k, so **not similar**.
