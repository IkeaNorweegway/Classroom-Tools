::: tip
**For the adult:** Two common slips: subtracting to find k (always divide), and doubling the area when the sides double. Doubling the sides makes the area 2 × 2 = 4 times as big.
:::

## Answers

**1.** k = 5 ÷ 10 = **0.5**. Check: 3 ÷ 6 = 0.5. k is less than 1: a **reduction**.
@viz polygon pts="0,0;10,0;10,6;0,6" sides="10 cm;6 cm;;" cap="Original" pts2="0,0;5,0;5,3;0,3" sides2="5 cm;3 cm;;" cap2="Copy" unit=8
**2.** Copy: 4 × 3 = **12 cm**. Original: 15 ÷ 3 = **5 cm**.

**3.** Long sides: 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5. Short sides: 5 ÷ 4 = 1.25. Two different values of k, so **not similar**.
@viz polygon pts="0,0;6,0;6,4;0,4" sides="6 m;4 m;;" pts2="0,0;9,0;9,5;0,5" sides2="9 m;5 m;;" unit=8
**4.** 5 × 3 = **15 km**.
@viz sdmap a="Farm" b="Town" len=5 angle=10 dist="5 cm" scale="1 cm : 3 km" bar="0;3 km;6 km;9 km"
**5.** First rug: 2 × 3 = **6 m²**. Second rug: 4 m by 6 m = **24 m²**. That is 4 times as big (k² = 2 × 2 = 4), not double.

**6.** Divide, don't subtract: k = 12 ÷ 4 = **3**. The copy is 3 times as long.

**7.** Real sides: 3 × 2 = 6 m and 2.5 × 2 = 5 m. Area: 6 × 5 = **30 m²**.
