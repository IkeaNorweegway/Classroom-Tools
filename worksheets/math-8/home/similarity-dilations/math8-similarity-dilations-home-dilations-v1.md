::: tip
**For the adult:** A **dilation** makes a shape bigger or smaller from a fixed point. Here the point is (0, 0). Multiply BOTH coordinates of every corner by the scale factor k. The image is **similar**: same angles, every side × k.
:::

::: example
**Dilate △ABC about (0, 0) with k = 2.**
@viz sddilate pts="1,1;3,1;1,2" k=2 names="A,B,C" image=show rays=1 range=6
A(1, 1) → A′(2, 2). B(3, 1) → B′(6, 2). C(1, 2) → C′(2, 4). Each side doubled: AB = 2, A′B′ = 4.
:::
