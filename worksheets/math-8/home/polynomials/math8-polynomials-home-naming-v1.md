::: tip
**For the adult:** In 3x², the 3 is the **coefficient** and the 2 is the **exponent**: 3x² = 3 · x · x. Name a polynomial two ways. Count its terms: 1 is a monomial, 2 a binomial, 3 a trinomial. Its **degree** is its highest exponent, not the number of terms.
:::

::: example
**Name 4x³ − 2x + 1.**
Step 1: Count the terms: 4x³, −2x and +1. Three terms: a **trinomial**.
@viz posort bins="4x³:degree 3;−2x:degree 1;+1:degree 0"
Step 2: The highest exponent is 3. It is a **cubic trinomial**, degree 3.
:::
