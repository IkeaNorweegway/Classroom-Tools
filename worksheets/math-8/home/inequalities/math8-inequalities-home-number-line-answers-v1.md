::: tip
**For the adult:** Two slips are common. One is a closed dot for < or >: test the boundary (is 3 < 3 true? No, so the dot is open). The other is pointing x > 2 to the left because the symbol "points left". Greater numbers are always to the right.
:::

## Answers

**1.** **x ≤ 8**.

**2.** Open dot at 2 (2 is not included). Ray to the **right**. Test 5: 5 > 2 ✓
@viz iqline value=2 dir=right closed=0 blank=1 min=-3 max=7 label="x > 2"
**3.** a) **True** (5 equals 5). b) **False** (5 is not greater than 5). c) **False** (−2 is to the right of −5, so −2 > −5).

**4.** 4 is greater than x, so x is less than 4: **x < 4**.

**5.** Closed dot at −2, ray right: **x ≥ −2**.
@viz iqline value=-2 dir=right closed=1 min=-6 max=3 label="[[x ≥ −2]]"
**6.** **y is greater than or equal to −4** (y is at least −4).

**7.** **No.** Test 3: is 3 < 3 true? No. So 3 is not a solution, and the dot is **open**. Only ≤ and ≥ get a closed dot.
