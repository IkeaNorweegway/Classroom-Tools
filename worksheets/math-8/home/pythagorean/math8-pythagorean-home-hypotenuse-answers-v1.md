::: tip
**For the adult:** If the answer is shorter than one of the legs, something went wrong. A common slip is adding the sides (3 + 4 = 7) instead of their squares.
:::

**1.** 9 + 16 = 25. √25 = **5**

**2.** 25 + 144 = 169. √169 = **13**

**3.** 81 + 144 = 225. √225 = **15**

**4.** 16 + 36 = 52. √52 ≈ **7.2**

**5.** 3600 + 2025 = 5625. √5625 = **75 cm**

**6.** Answers vary. A close prediction (within a centimetre or two) shows the theorem works on real objects.
