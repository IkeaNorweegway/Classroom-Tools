::: tip
**For the adult:** In a right triangle, the longest side (the hypotenuse) is across from the square corner. To find it: square the two shorter sides, add, then take the square root. A calculator is fine for the square root.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A right triangle has legs 6 cm and 8 cm. How long is the hypotenuse?**

1. Square each leg: 6² = 36 and 8² = 64.
2. Add: 36 + 64 = 100.
3. Take the square root: √100 = 10. The hypotenuse is 10 cm.
4. Check: 10 is longer than both legs ✓
:::
