::: tip
**For the adult:** Two slips are common. One is halving: √64 is 8, not 32. The other is √(−81) = −9. Check it: (−9) × (−9) = +81. No real number squared is negative.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 0.3 × 0.3 = **0.09**. **Yes**, 0.09 = 0.3² is a perfect square.

**2.** **Yes.** 4 = 2² and 9 = 3², so 4/9 = 2/3 × 2/3.
@viz area a=2/3 b=2/3 result="[[2/3]] × [[2/3]] = 4/9"
**3.** **No.** 32 × 32 = 1024. √64 = **8**, because 8 × 8 = 64.

**4.** a) **9** b) **−9** c) **No real answer.** 9 × 9 and (−9) × (−9) are both +81.

**5.** x = **5 or −5** (x = ±5).
@viz numberline min=-6 max=6 every=1 width=360 keypoints="-5:−5;5:5"
**6.** s² = 144, so s = ±12. A side can't be negative. Each side is **12 m**.
@viz sqside n=12 side="s = 12 m" cell=6 fill=soft labels="Area = 144 m²"

**7.** a) x = **±0.6** b) x = **±3/4**
