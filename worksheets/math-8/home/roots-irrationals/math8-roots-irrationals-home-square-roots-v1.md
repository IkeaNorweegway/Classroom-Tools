::: tip
**For the adult:** The square root of 49 is the number that times itself gives 49. Two numbers do that: 7 and −7. The sign √ means only the positive one: √49 = 7. The equation x² = 49 has both answers: x = ±7. A negative number like −49 has no real square root.
:::

::: example
**Solve x² = 49.**
Step 1: Which numbers squared give 49? 7 × 7 = 49 and (−7) × (−7) = 49.
@viz numberline min=-8 max=8 every=2 points="-7:x = −7;7:x = 7"
Step 2: x = ±7. Both are 7 steps from 0.
:::
