::: tip
**For the adult:** A common slip is "halfway": 70 sits between 64 and 81, but not in the middle, so √70 is not 8.5. Always check by squaring. Answers from the calculator's √ key are fine once your child has made an estimate.
:::

## Answers

**1.** 16 < 20 < 25, so √20 is **between 4 and 5** (about 4.5).

**2.** 50 is just past 49. **√50 ≈ 7.1** (7.07…).
@viz srbench lo=7 hi=8 n=50 keydot=1 est="√50 ≈ [[7.1]]"
**3.** **No.** 8.5 × 8.5 = 72.25, more than 70. 70 is closer to 64. √70 ≈ **8.4** (8.37…).

**4.** Side = √60 ≈ **7.7 m** (7.746…). Fence: 4 × 7.746 ≈ **31.0 m**.

**5.** √49 = 7: **rational**. √7: **irrational**. 0.4545…: **rational** (it repeats; it is 5/11). π: **irrational**.

**6.** **3.1, π, √10, 3.2.** (π ≈ 3.142 and √10 ≈ 3.162.)
@viz numberline min=3 max=3.25 step=0.01 every=0.05 width=520 keypoints="3.1623:√10;3.1:3.1;3.1416:π;3.2:3.2"
**7.** 1.41 × 1.41 = 1.9881, not 2. **No.** 1.41 is close to √2, but √2 never ends. √2 ≈ 1.41.
