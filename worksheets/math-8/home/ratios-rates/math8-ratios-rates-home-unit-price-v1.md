::: tip
**For the adult:** A unit price is the price for ONE item (or one kilogram, one litre). Many grocery shelf tags show it. Look at one with your child and check it together.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Which is the better buy: 4 yogurts for $3.00, or 6 yogurts for $4.20?**

1. 4-pack: $3.00 ÷ 4 = $0.75 per yogurt.
2. 6-pack: $4.20 ÷ 6 = $0.70 per yogurt.
3. $0.70 is less, so the 6-pack is the better buy.
:::
