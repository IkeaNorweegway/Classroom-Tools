::: tip
**For the adult:** Rounding to the nearest cent is fine. The key step is dividing the price by the amount.
:::

**1.** $4.00 ÷ 5 = **$0.80** per pen

**2.** $7.50 ÷ 3 = **$2.50** per kg

**3.** 240 ÷ 3 = **80 km/h**

**4.** 2 L: $1.90 per litre. 3 L: $1.70 per litre. The **3 L** is the better buy.

**5.** 400 g: $1.00 per 100 g. 250 g: $1.10 per 100 g. The **400 g bag** is the better buy.

**6.** 6 × $15.00 = **$90.00**

**7.** 500 km is 5 × 100 km, so 5 × 7 L = **35 L**.
