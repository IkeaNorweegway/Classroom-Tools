::: tip
**For the adult:** If your child added instead of multiplied, ask, "Would that taste the same?" and try it with a small drawing.
:::

**1.** 1 : 4 = 3 : **12** (multiply both by **3**)

**2.** 2 : 5 = 6 : **15**

**3.** 3 : 7 = **12** : 28

**4.** 10 : 4 = 5 : **2**

**5.** **No.** 2 × 2 = 4, but 3 × 2 = 6, not 5. Adding 2 to each number changes the mix.

**6.** 12 ÷ 4 = 3, so 1 × 3 = **3 cups of mix**.

**7.** 9 ÷ 3 = 3, so 2 × 3 = **6 cups of raisins**.
