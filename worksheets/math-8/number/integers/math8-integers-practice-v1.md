### The temperature in Grande Prairie drops 2 °C every hour from 6 p.m. to midnight. What is the total change? Write a multiplication.
(work 2)
=> 6 hours × (−2) = −12. The temperature drops 12 °C.

### A golfer scored −2, −1, +1, −3 and 0 on five holes. What was her mean score per hole?
(work 2)
=> Sum = −5. Mean = (−5) ÷ 5 = −1.

## Challenge

### Explain with counters why (−2) × (−3) = 6. Draw your model.
(work 4)
=> Start with 6 zero pairs. Take away 2 groups of −3 (6 red counters). 6 yellow counters are left: +6.

### Zoe wrote: (−4) + (−6) = 10 and (−4) × (−6) = −24. Both are wrong. Fix each one and explain the rule she mixed up.
(work 4)
=> (−4) + (−6) = −10: adding two negatives goes further below zero. (−4) × (−6) = 24: two negatives multiply to a positive. She swapped the rules.

### Without multiplying, decide if each product is positive or negative. Explain.
a) (−1) × (−2) × (−3) × (−4) × (−5)
b) (−7) × 0 × (−9)
(work 3)
=> a) Five negatives (odd) → negative. b) Anything times 0 is 0, which is neither positive nor negative.

### Find two integers that multiply to −24 and add to 2.

### In Calgary, a chinook wind raised the temperature from −22 °C to 8 °C in 5 hours. What was the mean change per hour? Then the temperature fell 3 °C per hour for 4 hours. What was the final temperature?
@viz numberline min=-24 max=10 step=2 every=4 points="-22:start;8:after chinook" ends="colder,warmer"
(work 4)
=> Change = 8 − (−22) = 30. 30 ÷ 5 = +6 °C per hour. Then 4 × (−3) = −12, so 8 + (−12) = −4 °C.
