## Challenge

### Ella makes orange juice with 2 cans of concentrate and 5 cans of water. Ravi uses 3 cans of concentrate and 8 cans of water. Whose juice tastes stronger? Show how you know.
(work 4)
=> Ella: 2 out of 7 = 28.6% concentrate. Ravi: 3 out of 11 = 27.3%. Ella's is a little stronger. (Or compare water per can of concentrate: Ella 2.5, Ravi 2.67.)

### Tom says, "3:5 is the same as 5:7 because I added 2 to both." Explain why Tom is wrong. Use a picture.
(work 4)
=> Adding changes the comparison. 3:5 → water is 5/3 ≈ 1.67 times the concentrate. 5:7 → 1.4 times. Multiply instead: 3:5 = 6:10.

### Write a different situation for each meaning of {3/4}: a ratio, a rate, and a probability.
(work 4)
=> Ratio: 3 cats to 4 dogs. Rate: $3 for 4 pencils. Probability: 3 chances out of 4 of spinning blue.

### Explain why "80 km/h" cannot be written as a percent, but "4 out of 5 shots" can.
(work 3)
=> A percent is a part out of 100 of the same kind of thing. 4 of 5 shots is shots out of shots (80%). km per hour compares different units.

### A farmer gets $1260 for 4.5 tonnes of canola. How much would 10 tonnes earn at the same price?
(work 3)
=> $1260 ÷ 4.5 = $280 per tonne. 10 × $280 = $2800.

### Bus A goes 210 km in 3 h. Bus B goes 280 km in 3.5 h. Which bus is faster? By how much?
(work 3)
=> A: 70 km/h. B: 80 km/h. Bus B is faster by 10 km/h.
