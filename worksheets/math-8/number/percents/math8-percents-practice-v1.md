### Your family's restaurant bill is $60. Add 5% GST and a 15% tip. What is the total?
(work 3)
=> 5% + 15% = 20%. 20% of $60 = $12. Total = $72.

### A $50 sweater is 10% off. Then you pay 5% GST on the sale price. What do you pay?
(work 3)
=> 10% off: $50 − $5 = $45. GST: 5% of $45 = $2.25. Pay $47.25.

## Challenge

### Mila says, "0.5% of 1000 is 500." Explain her mistake and find the right answer.
(work 3)
=> She used 0.5 (which is 50%). 0.5% = 0.005. 0.005 × 1000 = 5.

### A school had 400 students. Now it has 520. What percent of the old number is the new number? Is that more than 100%? Explain.
(work 3)
=> 520 ÷ 400 = 1.3 = 130%. Yes: the school is all of its old size plus 30% more.

### The price of a $100 hockey stick goes up 10% in June and up 10% again in July. Liam says it now costs $120. Is he right? Show your work.
@viz flow steps="$100|+10%|[[$110]]|+10%|[[$121]]"
(work 3)
=> No. 10% of 100 = 10 → $110. 10% of 110 = 11 → $121.

### A store has a "40% off" sale. Another store has "20% off, then 20% off the sale price." Which is the better deal on a $150 jacket? Show both.
(work 4)
=> 40% off: $150 × 0.6 = $90. 20% then 20%: 150 × 0.8 = 120; 120 × 0.8 = $96. The 40% off store is better by $6.

### 12 is 15% of what number? Explain using a double number line.
@viz dnl top="0%,5%,15%,100%" bottom="0,[[4]],12,[[80]]" toplabel="percent" bottomlabel="number"
(work 2)
=> 15% is 12, so 5% is 4. 100% is 20 × 4 = 80.

### Canola prices went down 10% and then up 10%. Did they end up where they started? Use a starting price of $600 per tonne.
(work 3)
=> $600 × 0.9 = $540. $540 × 1.1 = $594. No, they end $6 lower, because the 10% increase was of a smaller number.
