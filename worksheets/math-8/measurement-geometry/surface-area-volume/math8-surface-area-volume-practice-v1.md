### Find the surface area of the box.
@viz prism l="12 cm" w="5 cm" h="8 cm" lv=12 wv=5 hv=8
(work 3)
=> 2(12 × 5) + 2(12 × 8) + 2(5 × 8) = 120 + 192 + 80 = 392 cm²

### Find the volume of the box in Question 2.
(work 1)
=> 12 × 5 × 8 = 480 cm³

### A tent is a triangular prism. Find its surface area (including the floor).
@viz triprism b="4 m" th="1.5 m" s="2.5 m" l="5 m" bv=4 thv=1.5 lv=5
(work 4)
=> Triangles: 2 × ½ × 4 × 1.5 = 6 m². Floor: 4 × 5 = 20 m². Sides: 2 × 2.5 × 5 = 25 m². SA = 51 m².

### Find the volume of the tent in Question 4.
(work 2)
=> Base area ½ × 4 × 1.5 = 3 m². V = 3 × 5 = 15 m³

## Challenge

### Sam says the SA of a 10 cm × 4 cm × 2 cm box is 6 × (10 × 4) = 240 cm². Explain his mistake and find the right SA.
(work 3)
=> Only a cube has 6 equal faces. SA = 2(40) + 2(20) + 2(8) = 80 + 40 + 16 = 136 cm².

### A cylinder's net has a rectangle 31.4 cm long and 10 cm tall. What is the radius of the circles? Explain.
@viz net type=cyl r="?" h="10 cm" rv=5 hv=10 c="31.4 cm"
(work 3)
=> The rectangle's length is the circumference: 2πr = 31.4, so r = 31.4 ÷ 6.28 = 5 cm.

### A round hay bale has diameter 1.5 m and length 1.2 m. How much plastic wrap covers it? How much space does it take up?
@viz cylinder r="1.5 m" h="1.2 m" rv=0.75 hv=1.2 diameter=1
(work 4)
=> r = 0.75. SA = 2(3.14)(0.5625) + 3.14(1.5)(1.2) = 3.53 + 5.65 ≈ 9.2 m². V = 3.14 × 0.5625 × 1.2 ≈ 2.1 m³.

### Two boxes both hold 1000 cm³. Box A is 10 × 10 × 10. Box B is 25 × 8 × 5. Which uses less cardboard? Show your work.
(work 4)
=> A: SA = 6 × 100 = 600 cm². B: 2(200) + 2(125) + 2(40) = 400 + 250 + 80 = 730 cm². Box A uses less cardboard.

### A box sits on a table. Ella turns it on its side and says, "Now it holds more, because it is taller." Is she right? Explain.
(work 3)
=> No. The volume is l × w × h, the same three numbers in any order. Turning it changes how it looks, not the space inside.

### A grain bin is a cylinder 8 m across and 6 m tall. A truck holds 20 m³ of grain. How many truckloads fill the bin?
(work 3)
=> r = 4. V = 3.14 × 16 × 6 = 301.44 m³. 301.44 ÷ 20 ≈ 15.1, so about 16 truckloads (15 full ones and part of another).
