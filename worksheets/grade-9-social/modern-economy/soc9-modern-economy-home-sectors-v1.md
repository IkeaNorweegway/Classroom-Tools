::: tip
**For the adult:** Primary industries take resources from nature. Secondary industries make products. Tertiary industries provide services.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Follow a tree from forest to store.**

1. Logging cuts the tree down: primary industry.
2. A pulp and paper mill turns it into paper: secondary industry.
3. A store sells you a notebook: tertiary industry (a service).
:::
