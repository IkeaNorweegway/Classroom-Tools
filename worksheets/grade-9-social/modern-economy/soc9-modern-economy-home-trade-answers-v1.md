::: tip
**For the adult:** NAFTA (1994) was replaced by a similar agreement, CUSMA, in 2020.
:::

**1.** **sell** to other countries · **buy** from other countries

**2.** **fewer or no taxes (tariffs)** on goods crossing borders

**3.** It’s **next door**, it’s a **huge market**, and we share a **language** and close business ties.

**4.** Gain: **shoppers** (lower prices) and **exporters**. Lose: some **workers** whose jobs move to places where products are cheaper to make.

**5.** If that country **stops buying or adds tariffs**, Canada’s economy is hurt badly.

**6.** Answers vary.
