::: tip
**For the adult:** Exports are things Canada sells to other countries. Imports are things we buy from them.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Why does Canada trade so much with the United States?**

1. Geography: it’s right next door, with the world’s longest shared border.
2. Canada sells it oil, gas, cars and lumber (exports).
3. Canada buys machinery, food and many other goods from it (imports).
4. Agreements like NAFTA lowered taxes on trade between the countries.
:::
