**2.** oil well **P** · car factory **S** · bank **T** · farm **P** · pulp mill **S** · hospital **T**

**3.** Any two: **car-making**, **oil and gas**, **pulp and paper**, **hydroelectric power**.

**4.** Any two: **overproduction**, the **1929 stock market crash**, **trade collapsing**, **drought on the Prairies**.

**5.** With **public works** (building roads and other projects to create jobs), **relief and social programs**, and **spending more than they collected** (deficits).
