::: tip
**For the adult:** A perspective is a point of view shaped by someone’s experiences. Ask "How would this look to someone else?"
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How might a Chinese Canadian soldier in 1944 see the war?**

1. The fact: many Chinese Canadians volunteered, even though they couldn’t vote in federal elections.
2. A perspective: some hoped that serving would prove their loyalty and win equal rights.
3. What happened: Chinese Canadians gained the federal vote in 1947.
:::
