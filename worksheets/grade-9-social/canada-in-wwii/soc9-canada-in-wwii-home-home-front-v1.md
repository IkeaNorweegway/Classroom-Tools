::: tip
**For the adult:** The "home front" means life in Canada during the war: factories, rationing, Victory Bonds and new jobs for women.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How did people in Canada help the war effort without going overseas?**

1. Factories switched to making ships, planes, trucks and supplies.
2. Rationing limited goods like sugar, butter and gasoline so more could go to the war.
3. People bought Victory Bonds to lend the government money.
4. Many women took factory and other jobs that had mostly gone to men.
:::
