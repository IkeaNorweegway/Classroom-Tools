**For the adult:** Some areas, like the environment, are shared between levels, which is part of why they disagree.
:::

**1.** **defence, money, citizenship** · **schools, hospitals, natural resources** · **roads, garbage, parks, local services**

**2.** (a) **F** (b) **P** (c) **M** (d) **F** (e) **P** (f) **P**

**3.** Alberta **owns its natural resources**, but the federal government handles **trade, the environment and projects that cross provincial borders**, like pipelines.

**4.** (a) the **city or town** (b) the **province**

**5.** The government must keep the **support (confidence) of the elected House of Commons**. If it loses a key vote, it must resign or call an election.
