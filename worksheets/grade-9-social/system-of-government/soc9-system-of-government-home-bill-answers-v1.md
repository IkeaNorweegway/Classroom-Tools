::: tip
**For the adult:** The Senate is often called the chamber of "sober second thought".
:::

**1.** **bill**

**2.** **first reading, second reading, committee stage, third reading, Senate, royal assent**

**3.** A small group of MPs **studies the bill in detail**, hears from **experts and the public**, and can suggest **changes**.

**4.** The Senate gives a **second look** and can suggest changes before it becomes law.

**5.** If a law **breaks the Constitution** (including the Charter), the courts can **strike it down**.

**6.** Answers vary.
