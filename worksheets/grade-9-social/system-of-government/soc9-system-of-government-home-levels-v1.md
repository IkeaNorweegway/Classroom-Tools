::: tip
**For the adult:** Federal = the whole country. Provincial = Alberta. Municipal = your city or town.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Who handles a pothole, a new school and a passport?**

1. Pothole: municipal (the city).
2. New school: provincial (Alberta runs education).
3. Passport: federal (citizenship and travel).
:::
