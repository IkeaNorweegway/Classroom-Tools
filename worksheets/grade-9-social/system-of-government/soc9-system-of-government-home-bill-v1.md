::: tip
**For the adult:** A bill is a proposed law. It has to pass the House of Commons and the Senate, then get royal assent.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Follow a bill through Parliament.**

1. First reading: the bill is introduced.
2. Second reading: MPs debate the main idea.
3. Committee stage: a small group studies it closely and may change it.
4. Third reading: a final debate and vote.
5. Senate: senators go through the same steps. Then royal assent makes it law.
:::
