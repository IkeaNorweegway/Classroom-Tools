**For the adult:** This era had real progress and real harm at the same time. Encourage your child to include both.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**How did health care change after the war?**

1. Before: families paid doctors and hospitals themselves.
2. Saskatchewan started public hospital and medical insurance.
3. By the early 1970s every province had joined a national plan.
4. Now most medical care is paid for through taxes.
:::
