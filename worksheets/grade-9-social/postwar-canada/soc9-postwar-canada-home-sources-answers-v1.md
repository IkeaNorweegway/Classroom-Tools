skill: asking who made a source, why, and for whom
time: about 15 minutes
---

::: tip
**For the adult:** There is rarely one right answer here. Look for thoughtful questions.
:::

**1.** **at the time** of the event · **later**, looking back

**2.** (a) **P** (b) **S** (c) **P** (d) **S**

**3.** Different **audiences**, **owners** or **points of view**; they may choose different facts to highlight.

**4.** It shows **how some people felt** at the time, even if it isn’t a fair or complete picture.

**5.** Examples: **Who made it, and why?** · **Who is it for?** · **What is left out?** · **Can I check it elsewhere?**

**6.** Answers vary.
