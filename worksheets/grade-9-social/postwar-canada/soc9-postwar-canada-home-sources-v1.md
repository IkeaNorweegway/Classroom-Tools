::: tip
**For the adult:** Before trusting a source, ask: Who made it? When? Why? Who was it for? What does it leave out?
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A 1980 Alberta newspaper cartoon criticises the federal National Energy Program. How should we read it?**

1. Who made it? An Alberta cartoonist.
2. When? 1980, just after the program was announced.
3. Why? To show Albertans’ anger that Ottawa was controlling oil prices and revenue.
4. What is left out? Why the federal government thought the program would help the rest of Canada.
:::
