::: tip
**For the adult:** Push factors make people leave a place. Pull factors draw them to a new one. Most moves have both.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**In 1948 a family leaves a war-damaged city in Europe and settles in Edmonton. Why?**

1. Push factors: war damage, few jobs, hard living conditions.
2. Pull factors: jobs in Alberta’s growing oil industry, safety, family already in Canada.
3. Both together explain the move.
:::
