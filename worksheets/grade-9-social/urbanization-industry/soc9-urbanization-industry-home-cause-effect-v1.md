::: tip
**For the adult:** Ask "And then what happened?" to follow a chain of effects. Some effects are good and some are not.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Oil is struck at Leduc in 1947. What were the effects?**

1. Cause: a major oil discovery near Edmonton.
2. Effects: thousands of new jobs, workers moving to Edmonton and Calgary.
3. Further effects: new suburbs, more roads and services.
4. Costs: pollution, and farmland and habitat turned into housing.
:::
