::: tip
**For the adult:** The Persons Case was about women holding offices like Senator, not about voting. Many women could already vote in federal elections by 1918.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**What did the Famous Five ask, and what happened?**

1. They asked whether the word "persons" in Canada’s constitution included women.
2. 1928: the Supreme Court of Canada said no.
3. 1929: they appealed to the Privy Council in Britain, which said yes.
4. Women could now be appointed to the Senate.
:::
