::: tip
**For the adult:** Citizens, permanent residents, temporary residents and refugees all live in Canada, but with different rights. Only citizens can vote.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**Who can vote? A citizen, a permanent resident, a student here for two years, and a refugee.**

1. Citizen: yes, if 18 or older.
2. Permanent resident: can live and work here for good, but can’t vote until they become a citizen.
3. Temporary resident (like an international student): no.
4. Refugee: given protection in Canada; can’t vote unless they later become a citizen.
:::
