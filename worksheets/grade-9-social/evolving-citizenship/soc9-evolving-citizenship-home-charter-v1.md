::: tip
**For the adult:** The Charter groups rights into fundamental freedoms, democratic, mobility, legal and equality rights. Section 1 allows reasonable limits.
:::

## Worked example

::: example
**A school bans students from wearing a religious head covering. Which rights are involved?**

1. Fundamental freedoms: freedom of religion.
2. Equality rights: not being treated unfairly because of religion.
3. Section 1: any limit must be reasonable and justified, for example for a real safety reason. Courts decide.
:::
