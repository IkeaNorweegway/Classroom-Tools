# Research
*Evidence on assessment and delivery practices*

**Pedagogical framework:** Research summaries in this folder feed into `_meta-context.md`. When a summary is complete, check whether it introduces new principles or thinkers that should be reflected in the meta framework.

---

## Purpose
This folder holds research summaries and evidence syntheses on what makes assessment and instructional delivery effective. It informs everything else in this workspace — the worksheets, tests, notes, and prompts should all trace back to something in here.

## What belongs here
- Summaries of research on formative assessment, feedback, retrieval practice, spaced practice, cognitive load
- Evidence on delivery practices: explicit instruction, worked examples, questioning techniques, wait time
- Research on specific populations: ELL students, students with learning differences, Indigenous learners
- Critiques and limitations — what the research doesn't support, where evidence is weak

## What does NOT belong here
- Full-text PDFs (link to them or cite them, don't reproduce them)
- Opinion pieces without evidence
- Anything that hasn't been checked against at least one primary or high-quality secondary source

## File naming
`[topic]-research.md`
Example: `formative-assessment-research.md`
Example: `retrieval-practice-research.md`
Example: `feedback-delivery-research.md`

## Research summary format
Each file should include:
1. **Topic** and why it matters for classroom practice
2. **Core finding** — the one thing the evidence most clearly establishes
3. **Key studies** — 3–5 named, with author, year, and the specific finding
4. **Effect sizes or outcome data** where available
5. **What it looks like in practice** — concrete, classroom-level implications
6. **What it doesn't support** — honest limitations
7. **Alberta/Canada angle** where relevant
