## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** a percent is a number out of 100. The hundred grid and the percent bar carry every lesson; the symbols come after the picture.
**Sequence:** percents, fractions and decimals including < 1% and > 100% (PC1) → percent of a number (PC2) → combined percents (PC3) → percent of a percent (PC4).
**Materials:** hundred grids (print extras), receipts or flyers from local stores, calculators from Lesson 2.
**Depth ceiling:** no compound-interest formula. Alberta has 5% GST only; other provinces' taxes are a contrast, not a requirement.
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Percents can't go over 100%" | Ask about a sports team that doubled its fans, or a town that grew. Shade two grids. |
| 0.5% read as 50% or 0.5 | Zoom into one small square. Half of it is half of one percent. |
| Adding percents of different amounts | Run the two-step example with real numbers ($100 → $80 → $64) before any rule. |
| 25% of 80 = 80 ÷ 25 | Back to the bar: four equal parts, each one is 25%. |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — Percents as pictures (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check, then hundred grids: shade 45%, 7%, 100%.
SHOW: Two grids for 150%. "Can a percent be more than 100%?" Vote first, then reveal.
SAY: "100% is one whole. More than one whole is more than 100%."
SHOW: The zoom picture for 0.5%.
WATCH: 0.5% written as 0.5. Ask: "Is that half of the grid or half of one square?"
DO: Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Percent of a number (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: write 30%, 3%, 300% and 0.3% as decimals.
SHOW: Percent bar for 25% of $80. Then the double number line for 15% of $60.
SAY: "10% is easy: divide by 10. Build other percents from it."
CUE: "What percent is 18 out of 24?" Students try before Worked Example 4.
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Combined percents (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: 10%, 5% and 15% of $40.
SHOW: A real receipt (or the notes receipt). "How much is the GST? The tip?"
SAY: "When both percents are of the same bill, you can add the percents."
WATCH: Students adding percents of different amounts. That is tomorrow's lesson; flag it now: "Same bill?"
DO: Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Percent of a percent (60 min)
DO: Warm-up prediction: "$100 is 20% off, then 20% off again. What do you pay?" Most say $60.
SHOW: Work it out on the board: $80, then $64. Let the result surprise them.
SAY: "Each percent is of the number in front of you right now."
DO: Multipliers (× 0.80, × 1.10) for students ready for them.
DO: Core worksheet; exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.

## Connections
- **Ratios & Rates:** a percent is a ratio out of 100; double number lines work for both.
- **Data Displays:** circle graphs use percents of a whole.
- **Grade 9:** percents with rational numbers and in probability.
