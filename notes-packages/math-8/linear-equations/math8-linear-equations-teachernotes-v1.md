## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** an equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other, and undo the steps in reverse order.
**Sequence:** one-step (LE1) → two-step (LE2) → brackets (LE3) → writing and checking (LE4). Each section starts with a balance or tile picture, then the symbols.
**Materials:** algebra tiles or a pan balance with bags and blocks; integer chips for negatives.
**Depth ceiling:** the five PR2 forms with integer a, b, c. Solutions may be negative or fractions. No variables on both sides and no inequalities (Grade 9).
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| 3x = 12 → subtract 3 | Read 3x aloud as "three times x." |
| Dividing first in 2x + 3 = 11 | Show the build order (× 2, then + 3) and undo it backwards. |
| 2(x + 3) = 2x + 3 | Draw 2 groups of (bag + 3 blocks). Count the blocks. |
| No verify step | Every answer gets a ✓ line. Make it part of "done." |
| Answer without words | "x = 8" is not an answer to "how many rides?" |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — One-step equations (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check. Balance demo: 3 bags = 12 blocks.
SHOW: Worked Examples 1–3. Write "÷ 3 both sides" beside each line.
SAY: "Whatever you do to one side, you do to the other."
WATCH: Subtracting the coefficient. Use the misconception box.
DO: Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Two-step equations (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: three one-step equations, one with a negative.
SHOW: Flowchart x → × 2 → + 3 → 11; then the two balance pictures.
SAY: "Undo the last step first. Shoes off before socks."
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Brackets (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: expand 2(x + 5) using a picture.
SHOW: 3(x + 2) as three groups. Solve both ways side by side.
CUE: "Which way is easier for 3(x + 2) = 10?"
DO: Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Writing and checking (60 min)
DO: Warm-up interleaved: one of each form.
SHOW: The four-step flowchart; the Stampede example; the error-hunt example.
DO: Pairs: one writes a story, the other writes and solves the equation; swap roles.
DO: Core worksheet; exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.

## Connections
- **Linear Relations:** finding x when you know y (ordered pairs) is solving an equation.
- **Integers:** every equation with negatives uses integer × and ÷.
- **Grade 9:** variables on both sides, rational coefficients and inequalities.
