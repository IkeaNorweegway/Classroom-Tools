## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** multiplying is repeated groups; a negative first factor means "take away groups." Counters and number lines come first; the sign rules come from a pattern, not from "because I said so."
**Sequence:** × with models (IN1) → sign rules from patterns (IN2) → ÷ as the inverse of × (IN3) → order of operations and problems (IN4).
**Materials:** two-colour counters (yellow +, red −), number lines on desks, calculators for 2-digit ÷ 2-digit.
**Depth ceiling:** integers only. 2-digit ÷ 1-digit by hand; 2-digit ÷ 2-digit with a calculator.
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Two negatives make a positive" used for adding | Put −3 + (−4) and (−3) × (−4) side by side every day this week. |
| Sign from the "bigger" number when multiplying | "Count the negatives." Make students say it. |
| Order of operations ignored with negatives | Underline the × and ÷ first, in colour. |
| Negative × with counters feels impossible | Model zero pairs first. "We need something to take away." |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — Multiplying with models (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check. Warm-up: −3 + (−4), 5 + (−8), −2 − 3 (adding review).
SHOW: 3 groups of −2 with counters; then the number line.
SAY: "3 × (−2) means three groups of negative two."
DO: Temperature-drop example. Then zero pairs: take away groups for (−3) × 2 and (−3) × (−2).
WATCH: Students who can't start the "take away" model. Have them build 6 zero pairs first.
DO: Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Sign rules (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: two counter models from yesterday.
SHOW: The pattern table and its graph. Students predict the next line before you write it.
SAY: "Count the negatives. Even means positive. Odd means negative."
WATCH: The addition mix-up. Use the misconception box before practice.
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Dividing (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: 4 × (−5), (−4) × (−5), and "what times 3 is −12?"
SHOW: Sharing a $12 debt; then "how many groups of −3 make −12?"
SAY: "Division undoes multiplication, so the sign rules are the same."
CUE: "Check every quotient with a multiplication."
DO: Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Order of operations and problems (60 min)
DO: Warm-up interleaved: one ×, one ÷, one + with integers.
SHOW: BEDMAS flowchart; Worked Example 7; the Lake Louise mean.
DO: Core worksheet. Challenge: the chinook problem.
DO: Exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.

## Connections
- **Linear Equations** need integer × and ÷ (solving −3x = 12).
- **Grade 9:** the same rules for negative fractions and decimals (rational numbers) and for powers like (−2)³.
