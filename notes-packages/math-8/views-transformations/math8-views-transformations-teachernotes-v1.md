## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** moving between 3-D objects and flat pictures. Views flatten an object from three directions; transformations move flat shapes without changing their size or shape.
**Sequence:** views (VT1) → building from views and turning (VT2) → translations, reflections, rotations on a grid (VT3) → congruence and combined moves (VT4).
**Materials:** linking cubes (10 per student), isometric dot paper, square grid paper, tracing paper for rotations, mirrors (optional).
**Depth ceiling:** objects made of cubes; rotations of objects in 90° steps. Grid rotations of 90°, 180°, 270° about the origin. No dilations (Grade 9 similarity).
**Conventions in these materials:** front = the side the "front" arrow points at; side view = the right side (front on the left); 90° rotations are counterclockwise unless the question says clockwise.
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| Hidden cubes counted in a view | Put the cube model on the desk and crouch to eye level. |
| Views drawn in perspective | "A view is flat: squares only." |
| Reflection changes the wrong coordinate | Fold the grid along the mirror line and press through. |
| Rotation direction | Trace the shape on tracing paper and turn it with a pencil point on (0, 0). |
| "Flipped isn't congruent" | Cut out the shape and lay it on the image. |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — Views (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check. Each student builds the 6-cube object from Section 1.
SHOW: Isometric picture beside the three views. Crouch to the desk to see each view.
WATCH: Side views that count hidden cubes. Use the misconception box.
DO: Students build their own 5-cube object and draw its views; partners check. Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Building and turning (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: draw the views of a 3-cube L.
SHOW: Build from views (Worked Example 2). Then turn a model a quarter turn and redraw.
CUE: "Which side swings to the front when you turn it clockwise?"
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Transformations on a grid (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: plot (3, −2), (−4, 1), (0, 5).
SHOW: Translation, reflection, rotation with the same triangle ABC.
SAY: "Write the rule, then test it on one point in the picture."
DO: Tracing paper for rotations. Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Congruence and combined moves (60 min)
DO: Warm-up interleaved: one view, one translation, one reflection.
SHOW: Two moves in a row; working backwards from an image.
SAY: "Slides, flips and turns never stretch. The image is always congruent."
DO: Core worksheet; exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.
