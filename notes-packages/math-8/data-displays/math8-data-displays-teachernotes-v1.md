## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** every graph is a choice. Students learn what each graph is for, then become graph critics: spotting broken axes, stretched pictures and conclusions the data doesn't support.
**Sequence:** four kinds of graphs (DD1) → choosing a graph (DD2) → misleading graphs (DD3) → checking conclusions (DD4).
**Materials:** graphs clipped from flyers, news sites or ads (print a few); grid paper; protractors or a circle-graph template for Lesson 2 (optional).
**Depth ceiling:** circle, line, bar and pictographs only. No scatter plots (Grade 9). Data in these materials marked "sample" is made up for teaching; the Edmonton temperatures are rounded long-term averages.
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| Circle graph for change over time | "Where is the time axis?" Redraw as a line graph. |
| Reading bar heights, not numbers | Cover the axis numbers, then uncover them. |
| Twice as tall = twice as much | Cut out the two pictures and fit the small one inside the big one: 4 fit. |
| "Most" used for less than 50% | "Most" means more than half. |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — Four kinds of graphs (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check. Gallery of 4 graphs on the walls; students write what each one is for.
SHOW: Notes Section 1, one graph at a time.
WATCH: A circle graph used for change over time. Use the misconception box.
DO: Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Choosing a graph (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: name the graph for three situations.
SHOW: The fruit data as a bar graph and a circle graph. "What does each one make easy to see?"
DO: Class survey (favourite season or snack); groups draw two graphs of the same data and present one strength of each.
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Misleading graphs (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: a broken-axis graph on the board with no comment. "What does it say?"
SHOW: Graph 1 and Graph 2 of the same data. The scaled-picture trick.
SAY: "Read the numbers, not just the picture."
DO: Pairs make a fair and a misleading graph of the same data. Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Checking conclusions (60 min)
DO: Warm-up interleaved: graph type, strength, and one misleading trick.
SHOW: Ava's books and the half-year temperature story.
DO: Core worksheet; real ad or news graph critique. Exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.

## Connections
- **Percents:** circle graph slices are percents of a whole.
- **Probability:** experimental results are often shown in bar graphs.
- **Grade 9:** scatter plots, sampling, bias in data collection.
