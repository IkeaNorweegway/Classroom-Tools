## Overview
::: overview
**Big idea:** multiplying by a fraction takes part of something; dividing by a fraction counts how many pieces fit. Models first (bars, area models, "how many fit"), then the rules.
**Sequence:** mixed numbers and estimating (FR1) → multiplying (FR2) → dividing (FR3) → choosing the operation and order of operations (FR4).
**Materials:** fraction strips or paper strips, grid paper for area models.
**Depth ceiling:** positive fractions and mixed numbers only. Order of operations with positive answers only.
:::

| Watch for | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Multiplying makes bigger" | Ask for half of 8 before any rule. Then {1/2} × {3/4} with an area model. |
| Mixed numbers multiplied part by part | Estimate first (2.5 × 1.3 ≈ 3). Show the four-part rectangle. |
| "Dividing makes smaller" | 3 ÷ {1/4}: how many quarter-dollars in $3? |
| Flipping the first fraction | Check every quotient with multiplication. |

## Lessons
### Lesson 1 — Mixed numbers and estimating (60 min)
DO: Pre-knowledge check. Fraction strips: build {7/4}.
SHOW: Bars for 7 quarters = 1{3/4}. Then the number line with 0, ½ and 1.
SAY: "Before you calculate, estimate: close to 0, a half, or 1?"
DO: Exit ticket Section 1.

### Lesson 2 — Multiplying (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: 3 groups of {2/5} with strips.
SHOW: Area model for {2/3} × {3/4}. Students shade their own on grid paper.
SAY: "Tops times tops, bottoms times bottoms. The area model shows why the bottoms multiply: rows × columns."
WATCH: Multiplying mixed numbers part by part. Use the four-part rectangle.
DO: Exit ticket Section 2.

### Lesson 3 — Dividing (60 min)
DO: Warm-up prediction: "Is 3 ÷ {1/4} bigger or smaller than 3?" Vote.
SHOW: The "how many fit" bars. Then sharing ({3/4} ÷ 3).
SAY: "Keep, change, flip: flip the number you divide BY."
CUE: "Check by multiplying: quotient × divisor should give the first number."
DO: Exit ticket Section 3.

### Lesson 4 — Choosing the operation (60 min)
DO: Warm-up: one ×, one ÷, one mixed-number question.
SHOW: The flowchart. Sort six story problems into "part of" (×) and "how many fit / share" (÷) before solving.
DO: Core worksheet; exit ticket Section 4. Quiz next class.

## Assessment
