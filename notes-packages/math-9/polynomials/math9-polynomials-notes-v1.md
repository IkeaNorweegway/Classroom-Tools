**Degree of a term** = the exponent on the variable.
- The degree of 5x² is ___
- The degree of 3x is ___
- The degree of 7 (a constant) is ___

**Degree of a polynomial** = the degree of the _______________ term (the one with the highest exponent).

> Example: 4x³ + 2x² − x + 9
> Degrees of each term: ___, ___, ___, ___
> Degree of the polynomial: ___

**Leading coefficient** = the coefficient of the term with the _______________ degree.
In 4x³ + 2x² − x + 9, the leading coefficient is _______________.

---

> **MISCONCEPTION: Degree is not the same as the leading coefficient.**
>
> Wrong idea: "3x² has degree 3 because the coefficient is 3."
>
> Why it fails: The degree tells you the power of the variable, not how many of something you have. In 3x², the 3 is the coefficient (how many x²-groups) and the 2 is the degree (the variable's power).
>
> Correct: 3x² has **degree 2** and leading **coefficient 3**.

### Guided Notes

**Like terms** share the same _______________ raised to the same _______________. The coefficients can be different — only the variable part needs to match.

| Like terms? | Why? |
|---|---|
| 3x² and −7x² | Yes — both have _______________ |
| 4x and 4x² | No — different _______________ |
| 5x and 3y | No — different _______________ |
| 6 and −2 | Yes — both are _______________ |

**To combine like terms:** Add or subtract the _______________ only. The variable part stays the same.

> Example: 3x² + 7x² = ___x²
> The x² part does not change. Only the coefficients combine.

---

> **MISCONCEPTION: You cannot add unlike terms.**
>
> Wrong idea: "3x² + 2x = 5x²" or "3x² + 2x = 5x³"
>
> Why it fails: 3x² and 2x have different powers. They represent different things — you cannot combine them any more than you can add 3 apples and 2 oranges and call the result "5 apples."
>
> Correct: 3x² + 2x stays as **3x² + 2x** — it is already simplified.

---

### Guided Notes

Subtracting a polynomial means adding its _______________.

To find the opposite of a polynomial, change the sign of _______________ term.

> Opposite of (2x² − 3x + 1) = ___________________________

**The key step: distribute the negative sign.**

> (5x² + 3x − 4) − (2x² − x + 6)
>
> = 5x² + 3x − 4 ___ 2x² ___ x ___ 6 &nbsp;&nbsp; ← distribute the negative
>
> = ___________________________

---

> **MISCONCEPTION: The negative sign must be distributed to every term.**
>
> Wrong idea: A student writes (5x² + 3x − 4) − (2x² − x + 6) = 5x² + 3x − 4 − 2x² − x + 6
>
> Why it fails: The subtraction sign applies to the entire second polynomial — every term inside the bracket gets its sign flipped. −(2x² − x + 6) = −2x² **+** x **−** 6, not −2x² − x + 6.
>
> Correct sign change: −(2x² − x + 6) = _________________________

---

**Step-by-step:**

1. Write the subtraction: (\_\_\_\_\_\_) − (\_\_\_\_\_\_)
2. Rewrite as addition: (\_\_\_\_\_\_) + (\_\_\_\_\_\_) — change all signs in the second bracket
3. Combine like terms: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

### Guided Notes

**The distributive property** says: a(b + c) = _______________ + _______________

To multiply a monomial by a polynomial: multiply the monomial by _______________ term inside the brackets.

> Example: 3x(2x² − 4x + 1)
>
> = 3x × 2x² + 3x × (−4x) + 3x × 1
>
> = _______________ + _______________ + _______________

**Exponent rule — multiplying powers with the same base:**

x^m × x^n = x^(___)

> x × x = x^___ (not 2x)
> x² × x³ = x^___

---

> **MISCONCEPTION: x × x = 2x**
>
> Wrong idea: When multiplying x by x, students often write 2x (adding the coefficient) instead of x² (multiplying the exponents).
>
> Why it fails: x × x means "x groups of x" — the result is x², the area of a square with side x. Adding the exponents is multiplication. Doubling the coefficient is addition (x + x = 2x, a completely different operation).
>
> Correct: x × x = x² &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; x + x = 2x

---

To multiply two binomials, every term in the first bracket must multiply every term in the _______________.

**(x + 3)(x + 4)** has four partial products:

| | x | +4 |
|---|---|---|
| **x** | x × x = ___ | x × 4 = ___ |
| **+3** | 3 × x = ___ | 3 × 4 = ___ |

Simplified: ___________________________

**The area model connects multiplication to geometry.**
The total area = sum of the four rectangular sections.

Each cell in the table is one _______________ product. Combine the _______________ terms to simplify.

---

> **MISCONCEPTION: (x + a)(x + b) ≠ x² + ab**
>
> Wrong idea: "Just multiply the numbers: (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 6"
>
> Why it fails: There are four partial products, not two. The middle terms (2x and 3x) are missed entirely. This is the most common binomial multiplication error.
>
> Correct: (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6

---
