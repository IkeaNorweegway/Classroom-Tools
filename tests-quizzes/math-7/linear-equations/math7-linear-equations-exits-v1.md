## Section 1: Simplifying expressions
Target: I can combine like terms and use the distributive property. (LE1)
### Simplify 5x − 2 + x + 7. Use the tiles.
@viz tiles terms="5x;-2;x;+7"
(lines 1)
=> Like terms: 5x + x = 6x and −2 + 7 = 5. So 6x + 5.
### Simplify −4(x − 3).
(lines 1)
=> −4 × x = −4x and −4 × (−3) = +12. So −4x + 12.
### Is 3x + 5x equal to 8x²? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No. 3x + 5x is 8 x tiles, so it is 8x. Adding like terms adds the coefficients. It does not make x².

## Section 2: Keeping the balance
Target: I can do the same thing to both sides to solve an equation. (LE2)
### Solve 2x − 5 = 9.
@viz balance left="2x;-5" right="+9"
(lines 2)
=> Add 5 to both sides: 2x = 14. Divide both sides by 2: x = 7. Check: 14 − 5 = 9. ✓
### Solve 3(x + 1) = −9.
(lines 2)
=> 3x + 3 = −9. Subtract 3: 3x = −12. Divide by 3: x = −4.
### Ava solves x + 4 = 10. She writes x = 10 after taking 4 off the left side. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> She did it to one side only. The right side must lose 4 too: x = 10 − 4 = 6. Check: 6 + 4 = 10. ✓

## Section 3: Solving with x on both sides
Target: I can solve an equation with x on both sides. (LE3)
### Solve 6x + 3 = 2x + 15.
@viz balance left="6x;+3" right="2x;+15"
(lines 2)
=> Subtract 2x: 4x + 3 = 15. Subtract 3: 4x = 12. x = 3.
### Solve 2(x + 4) = 5x + 14.
(lines 2)
=> 2x + 8 = 5x + 14. Subtract 2x: 8 = 3x + 14. Subtract 14: −6 = 3x. x = −2.
### Sam's first step for 6x + 3 = 2x + 15 is 8x + 3 = 15. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> He moved 2x and kept its sign. To take 2x off the right side, he must take 2x off the left side too: 4x + 3 = 15.

## Section 4: Checking solutions and word problems
Target: I can check a solution and use equations to solve problems. (LE4)
### Is x = −3 the solution of 5x + 4 = 2x − 5? Verify.
(lines 2)
=> Left: 5(−3) + 4 = −15 + 4 = −11. Right: 2(−3) − 5 = −6 − 5 = −11. Equal, so yes.
### Stream A costs $10 a month plus $4 a movie. Stream B costs $22 a month plus $1 a movie. When do they cost the same?
@viz balance left="4x;+10" right="x;+22" caption="Each bag is one movie."
(lines 2)
=> Let x = movies. 10 + 4x = 22 + x. Subtract x: 10 + 3x = 22. 3x = 12. x = 4 movies.
### Explain how you know your answer is right.
(lines 2)
=> Substitute 4 into both sides. Stream A: 10 + 16 = $26. Stream B: 22 + 4 = $26. They are equal.
