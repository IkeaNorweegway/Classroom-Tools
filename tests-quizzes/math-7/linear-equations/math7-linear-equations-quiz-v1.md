### [LE2 · 2] Which equations are equivalent to 3x − 1 = 11? Write yes or no. a) 3x = 12 b) 3x = 10 c) 6x − 2 = 22 d) 4x − 1 = x + 11
(lines 2)
=> a) yes (+ 1 both sides) b) no (− 1 on the right only) c) yes (× 2 both sides) d) yes (+ x both sides). ½ mark each.

### [LE2 · 3] Raj takes 5 off the left side of 2x + 5 = 13. He writes 2x = 13. Explain why the balance is not level. Then solve.
@viz balance left="2x;+5" right="+13"
(lines 3)
=> The left side lost 5 blocks and the right side did not, so the right side is heavier (1 mark). Take 5 off both sides (1 mark).
=> 2x = 8, so x = 4 (1 mark).
