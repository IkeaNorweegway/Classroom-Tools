### [ST1 · 2] "How many minutes do Grade 7 students in Alberta spend outside each day?" Name the population. Is this a statistical question?
(lines 2)
=> All Grade 7 students in Alberta (1 mark).
=> Yes: the answers vary from student to student (1 mark).

### [ST1 · 2] Census or sample? a) The shoe sizes of the 22 players on a soccer team. b) The mass of every potato grown in Alberta this year.
(lines 2)
=> a) Census: a small group, easy to measure (1 mark).
=> b) Sample: far too many potatoes to weigh (1 mark).

(lines 2)
=> a) discrete b) continuous c) discrete d) continuous (½ mark each).

### [ST2 · 3] Explain your thinking. Noor wants to know how Grade 7 students get to school. She asks only the students on her bus. Is her sample biased? Explain. How could she fix it?
@viz people n=30 cols=15 groups="1-8:sun" pick="1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8" label="Noor asks only the 8 students on her bus"
(lines 3)
=> Yes, it is biased (1 mark).
=> Everyone she asks rides the bus. Students who walk, bike or get a ride get no chance (1 mark).
=> Fix: pick a random sample from all Grade 7 students, such as names in a hat (1 mark).

### [ST2 · 3] A list has 60 names. You want a sample of 12. What is the jump? Start at 2. Write the first 4 picks.
@viz people n=60 cols=15 numbers=1 label="Start at 2."
(lines 2)
=> Jump: 60 ÷ 12 = 5 (1 mark).
=> 2, 7, 12, 17 (2 marks; 1 mark if one pick is wrong).

### [ST3 · 2] A Banff gift shop counts visitors for 5 hours: 18, 25, 30, 22 and 15. Find the mean.
(lines 2)
=> 18 + 25 + 30 + 22 + 15 = 110 (1 mark). 110 ÷ 5 = 22 visitors an hour (1 mark).

### [ST3 · 3] Find the median of 11, 4, 9, 15, 7, 6.
@viz numstrip rows="11,4,9,15,7,6 | ___,___,___,___,___,___" labels="As given;In order"
(lines 2)
=> In order: 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 15 (1 mark). The middle two are 7 and 9 (1 mark).
=> (7 + 9) ÷ 2 = 8 (1 mark). No mark for 9 or 15 (middle of the list as given).

### [ST3 · 3] Find the mode and the range of 5, 8, 5, 12, 3, 5, 8. Ben says the mode is 12. Why is he wrong?
(lines 3)
=> Mode: 5 (3 times) (1 mark). Range: 12 − 3 = 9 (1 mark).
=> 12 is the biggest value. The mode is the value that comes up most often (1 mark).
