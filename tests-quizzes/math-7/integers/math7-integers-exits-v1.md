## Section 1: Adding integers
Target: I can add integers with chips, a number line and a rule. (IN1)
### Write the addition for the chips. Find the sum.
@viz counters pos=3 neg=8
(lines 1)
=> −8 + 3 = −5. Make 3 zero pairs. 5 red chips are left.
### Find −12 + 5 and −4 + (−9).
(lines 1)
=> −7 and −13
### Zoe says −5 + (−5) = 10. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> Adding two negatives moves further left: −5 + (−5) = −10. "Two negatives make a positive" is only for × and ÷.

## Section 2: Subtracting integers
Target: I can subtract integers with chips, a number line and a rule. (IN2)
### Use chips to find −1 − 3. Show the zero pairs you add.
@viz chips neg=1 title="Start: −1"
(work 2)
=> There are no yellow chips to take. Add 3 zero pairs. Take away 3 yellow chips. 4 red are left: −1 − 3 = −4.
### Write 6 − (−7) as adding the opposite. Then find it.
(lines 1)
=> 6 + 7 = 13
### Ben says 2 − (−4) must be less than 2, because you subtract. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> 2 − (−4) = 2 + 4 = 6, and 6 > 2. Taking away a negative makes the answer bigger.

## Section 3: Multiplying integers
Target: I can multiply integers and find the sign of a product. (IN3)
### Write a multiplication for the jumps. Find the product.
@viz numberline min=-16 max=2 step=1 every=2 jumps="0>-5>-10>-15" jumplabels="−5,−5,−5"
(lines 1)
=> 3 × (−5) = −15
### Find (−6)(−7) and (−2)(5)(−3)(−1).
(lines 1)
=> 42. Then 3 negatives (odd), 2 × 5 × 3 × 1 = 30, so −30.
### Is (−4) × 9 positive or negative? Explain how you know without multiplying.
(lines 2)
=> Negative. There is one negative factor, and 1 is odd. The size of 9 does not decide the sign.

## Section 4: Dividing integers and problems
Target: I can divide integers, check my answer and solve problems. (IN4)
### Find −18 ÷ 3. Use the chips.
@viz counters groups=3 each=-6
(lines 1)
=> 3 groups of 6 red chips: −18 ÷ 3 = −6. Check: 3 × (−6) = −18.
### Find −72 ÷ (−8). Write the related multiplication.
(lines 1)
=> 9, because 9 × (−8) = −72.
### A hiker goes down 450 m in 5 hours, the same each hour. Raj says the change is −9 m per hour. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> Estimate: −500 ÷ 5 = −100, so the answer is near −100. −450 ÷ 5 = −90 m per hour. Check: 5 × (−90) = −450.
