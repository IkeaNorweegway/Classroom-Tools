### [CI1 · 3] Name each labelled part of the circle.
@viz circle show=rd rlabel="part A" dlabel="part B" centre="point C" size=62
(lines 2)
=> A: radius. B: diameter. C: centre. 1 mark each.

### [CI1 · 2] a) A circle has a radius of 8.5 cm. Find the diameter. b) A circle has a diameter of 9 m. Find the radius.
(lines 2)
=> a) d = 2 × 8.5 = 17 cm (1 mark). b) r = 9 ÷ 2 = 4.5 m (1 mark).

### [CI1 · 2] Explain how to draw a circle with a diameter of 10 cm with a compass.
(lines 3)
=> Open the compass to the radius, 5 cm (1 mark). Put the point on the centre and turn the pencil all the way around (1 mark).

### [CI2 · 2] Leo measures a bowl. C = 47.1 cm and d = 15 cm. Find C ÷ d. What is this number called?
(lines 2)
=> 47.1 ÷ 15 = 3.14 (1 mark). It is pi, π (1 mark).

### [CI2 · 2] Find the circumference.
@viz circle show=d dlabel="d = 12 cm" size=50
(lines 2)
=> C ≈ 3.14 × 12 = 37.68 (1 mark). About 37.7 cm (1 mark, with units).

### [CI2 · 3] A bike wheel has a radius of 35 cm. How far does it roll in one turn?
(lines 2)
=> d = 2 × 35 = 70 cm (1 mark). C ≈ 3.14 × 70 (1 mark) = 219.8 cm (1 mark). No mark for 3.14 × 35 as the final step.

### [CI2 · 2] A tree has a circumference of 125.6 cm. Find its diameter.
(lines 2)
=> d ≈ 125.6 ÷ 3.14 (1 mark) = 40 cm (1 mark).

### [CI3 · 3] Find the area of this circle.
@viz circle show=r rlabel="r = 9 m" size=50
(lines 2)
=> r² = 9 × 9 = 81 (1 mark). A ≈ 3.14 × 81 = 254.34, about 254.3 (1 mark). Units m² (1 mark).

### [CI3 · 3] A plate has a diameter of 24 cm. Find its area.
(lines 3)
=> r = 24 ÷ 2 = 12 cm (1 mark). r² = 144 (1 mark). A ≈ 3.14 × 144 = 452.16, about 452.2 cm² (1 mark).
=> Using the diameter (3.14 × 24 × 24 = 1808.64) earns no marks for the last two steps.

### [CI3 · 2] Explain your thinking. A circle is cut into slices and laid out like this. Why is the base of the shape half the circumference?
@viz circlearea sectors=12 hlabel="r" blabel="base = ?"
(lines 3)
=> Half the slices point up, half point down. So half the crust (circumference) is along the bottom and half along the top (1 mark).
=> The base is πr and the height is r, so A = πr × r = πr² (1 mark).
