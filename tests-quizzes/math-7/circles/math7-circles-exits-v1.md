## Section 1: Parts of a circle
Target: I can name the parts of a circle. I can draw a circle with a compass. (CI1)
### Find the diameter of this circle.
@viz circle show=rd rlabel="r = 6.5 cm" dlabel="d = ?" size=56
(lines 1)
=> d = 2 × 6.5 = 13 cm.
### Jo wants a circle 12 cm across. She opens her compass to 12 cm. Is she right? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No. A compass is set to the radius. 12 ÷ 2 = 6, so she should open it to 6 cm.

## Section 2: Circumference and π
Target: I can find the circumference from the diameter or the radius. I can find the diameter from the circumference. (CI2)
### Find the circumference.
@viz circle show=d dlabel="d = 20 cm" size=50
(lines 1)
=> C ≈ 3.14 × 20 = 62.8 cm.
### A round pond has a circumference of 31.4 m. Find its diameter.
(lines 1)
=> d ≈ 31.4 ÷ 3.14 = 10 m.
### A wheel is 50 cm across. Sam says one turn is about 50 cm. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> One turn is the circumference. It is about 3.14 diameters: 3.14 × 50 = 157 cm. 50 cm is only one diameter.

## Section 3: Area of a circle
Target: I can explain where A = πr² comes from. I can find the area of a circle from its radius or diameter. (CI3)
### Find the area.
@viz circle show=r rlabel="r = 7 cm" size=50
(lines 2)
=> r² = 49. A ≈ 3.14 × 49 = 153.86. About 153.9 cm².
### A plate has a diameter of 10 cm. Find its area.
(lines 2)
=> r = 5 cm. A ≈ 3.14 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 cm².
### Ben finds the area of the plate as 3.14 × 10 × 10 = 314 cm². Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> A = πr² uses the radius, 5 cm, not the diameter. Using the diameter makes the answer 4 times too big.

## Section 4: Circle problems
Target: I can choose circumference or area to solve a problem. I can use the right units. (CI4)
### A sprinkler waters a circle of lawn. The water reaches 5 m from the sprinkler. a) Find the area of lawn it waters. b) A fence goes around the circle. How long is the fence?
@viz circle show=r rlabel="r = 5 m" fill=plant size=50
(lines 3)
=> a) A ≈ 3.14 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 m².
=> b) d = 10 m. C ≈ 3.14 × 10 = 31.4 m.
### Which answer is in m² and which is in m? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> The lawn is in m². It is the inside, so it counts squares. The fence is in m. It is a length around the edge.
