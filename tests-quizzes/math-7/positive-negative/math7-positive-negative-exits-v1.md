## Section 1: Opposites and absolute value
Target: I can find the opposite and the absolute value of a number. (PN1)
### What number is at the point? Find its opposite and its absolute value.
@viz numberline min=-6 max=6 step=0.5 every=1 points="-4.5:"
(lines 1)
=> The point is −4.5. Its opposite is 4.5. |−4.5| = 4.5.
### Find |−12| and |12|.
(lines 1)
=> 12 and 12
### Ella says |−9| = −9. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> Absolute value is the distance from 0. −9 is 9 steps from 0, and a distance can't be negative. |−9| = 9.

## Section 2: Fractions and decimals on the number line
Target: I can put negative fractions and decimals on a number line. (PN2)
### Mark −0.75 and −1{1/2} on the number line.
@viz numberline min=-2 max=1 step=0.25 every=1 keypoints="-1.5:−1 1/2;-0.75:−0.75"
=> −0.75 is 3 quarters left of 0. −1{1/2} is half way from −1 to −2.
### Write −{4/5} two other ways.
(lines 1)
=> {4/−5} and −({4/5}) (also −0.8)
### Explain how you know −1.5 is not between −1 and 0.
(lines 2)
=> −1.5 is more than 1 step from 0, so it is past −1. It is half way from −1 to −2.

## Section 3: Fractions to decimals and back
Target: I can change fractions to decimals and decimals to fractions. (PN3)
### Fill in the decimals.
@viz dnl top="−1,−3/4,−1/2,−1/4,0" bottom="−1,[[−0.75]],[[−0.5]],[[−0.25]],0" toplabel="fraction" bottomlabel="decimal"
=> −{3/4} = −0.75, −{1/2} = −0.5, −{1/4} = −0.25
### Write −0.6 as a fraction in simplest form. Write −{7/8} as a decimal.
(lines 2)
=> −0.6 = −{6/10} = −{3/5}. 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875, so −{7/8} = −0.875.
### Leo says {1/2} = 1.2. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> {1/2} means 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5. Half is less than 1, but 1.2 is more than 1.

## Section 4: Comparing and ordering
Target: I can compare and order positive and negative fractions and decimals. (PN4)
### Order from least to greatest: −0.4, {1/4}, −{1/2}, −0.05, 0.3
@viz numberline min=-1 max=1 step=0.25 width=600
(lines 1)
=> Decimals: −0.4, 0.25, −0.5, −0.05, 0.3. Order: −{1/2}, −0.4, −0.05, {1/4}, 0.3
### Which is colder: −12.5 °C or −12.05 °C? Explain how you know.
@viz thermo min=-15 max=0 step=5 values="-12.5,-12.05" labels="−12.5 °C;−12.05 °C"
(lines 2)
=> −12.5 °C is colder. Line up places: −12.50 and −12.05. −12.50 is further from 0, on the cold side, so it is less.
