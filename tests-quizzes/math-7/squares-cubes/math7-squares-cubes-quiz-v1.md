### [SC1 · 3] Is 20 a perfect square? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No (1 mark). 4² = 16 and 5² = 25 (1 mark). 20 is between them, so no whole number times itself makes 20 (1 mark).

@viz sqside n=8 side="?" labels="Area = 64 tiles" cell=12

### [SC2 · 3] Find √25, √100 and √144.
(lines 1)

### [SC2 · 2] Find √(7²).
(lines 1)
=> 7² = 49 (1 mark). √49 = 7 (1 mark).

### [SC2 · 2] List the factor pairs of 49. Use them to find √49.
(lines 1)
=> 1 × 49 and 7 × 7 (1 mark). The pair with equal factors is 7 × 7, so √49 = 7 (1 mark).

### [SC2 · 3] Raj says √16 = 8. Is he right? Explain your thinking. You may use the pictures.
@viz squares shapes="4x4;8x2" labels="A;B" cell=12
(lines 3)
=> No (1 mark). Check by squaring: 8 × 8 = 64, not 16 (1 mark). √16 = 4, because 4 × 4 = 16; picture A is the square (1 mark).
