## Section 1: Perfect squares
Target: I can show a perfect square as a picture and write it as a power. (SC1)
### Write the tiles as a product, a power and a number.
@viz squares shapes="6x6" labels="?" cell=14
(lines 1)
=> 6 × 6 = 6² = 36
### Find 9² and 12². Name the base and the exponent in 9².
(lines 1)
=> 81 and 144. In 9², the base is 9 and the exponent is 2.
### Leo says 7² = 14. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> 7² means two 7s multiplied: 7 × 7 = 49. A 7 by 7 square has 49 tiles. Leo did 7 × 2.

## Section 2: Square roots
Target: I can find a square root as the side of a square. (SC2)
### The square has an area of 81 tiles. How long is one side?
@viz sqside n=9 side="?" labels="Area = 81 tiles" cell=11
(lines 1)
=> √81 = 9, because 9 × 9 = 81.
### Find √49 and √121.
(lines 1)
=> 7 and 11
### Mia says √36 = 18. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> Check by squaring: 18 × 18 = 324, not 36. √36 = 6, because 6 × 6 = 36. The root is the side, not half.

## Section 3: Perfect cubes and cube roots
Target: I can build a perfect cube and find a cube root. (SC3)
### How many small cubes? Write it as a power.
@viz cube n=3 label=" "
(lines 1)
=> 3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27
### Find 5³ and ∛64.
(lines 1)
=> 125 and 4
### Sam says ∛8 = 4 and 2³ = 6. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8, not 6. So ∛8 = 2, not 4. Check: 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.

## Section 4: Area, perimeter and volume problems
Target: I can find the perimeter and area of a square and the volume of a cube. (SC4)
### A square sandbox has sides of 5 m. Find its perimeter and its area. Write the units.
@viz sqside n=5 side="5 m" fence=1 fill=sun cell=18
(lines 1)
=> Perimeter = 4 × 5 = 20 m. Area = 5² = 25 m².
### A cube has edges of 4 cm. Find its volume.
(lines 1)
=> 4³ = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64 cm³
### A square garden has an area of 100 m². Noor says it needs 100 m of fence. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> 100 m² is the area, not the fence. The side is √100 = 10 m. The fence is 4 × 10 = 40 m.
