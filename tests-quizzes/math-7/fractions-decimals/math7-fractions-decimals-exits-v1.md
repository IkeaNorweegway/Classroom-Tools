## Section 1: Multiplying fractions
Target: I can multiply fractions with a picture and with a rule. (FD1)
### Use the area model. Find {2/3} × {1/4}. Write it in simplest form.
@viz area a=2/3 b=1/4 result="2/3 × 1/4 = [[2/12]]"
(lines 1)
=> {2/12} = {1/6}. The square has 12 parts. The overlap is 2 parts.
### A recipe uses {2/3} cup of milk. Ben makes half a recipe. How much milk does he need?
(lines 1)
=> {1/2} × {2/3} = {2/6} = {1/3} cup.
### Is {1/2} × {3/5} more or less than {3/5}? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> Less. It is half of {3/5}, which is {3/10}. Times a proper fraction takes only part.

## Section 2: Dividing fractions
Target: I can divide with fractions using groups, common denominators and reciprocals. (FD2)
### How many {1/4}s fit in 2?
@viz groups total=2 piece=1/4 numbers=0 count="2 ÷ 1/4 = [[8]]"
(lines 1)
=> 8. Each whole has 4 quarters. 2 × 4 = 8.
### Find {4/5} ÷ {2/5} and {2/3} ÷ {1/4}.
(lines 1)
=> Same bottoms: 4 ÷ 2 = 2. Flip the divisor: {2/3} × 4 = {8/3} = 2{2/3}.
### Zoe wrote {1/2} ÷ {1/3} = {2/1} × {1/3} = {2/3}. Explain how you know she is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> She flipped the first fraction. Flip the divisor: {1/2} × 3 = {3/2} = 1{1/2}. A third fits in a half more than once, so the answer must be more than 1.

## Section 3: Multiplying and dividing decimals
Target: I can multiply and divide decimals and check with an estimate. (FD3)
### Use the area model. Find 0.4 × 0.3.
@viz area a=3/10 b=4/10 alabel="0.3" blabel="0.4" note1="columns: 4 tenths" note2="rows: 3 tenths" result="0.4 × 0.3 = [[0.12]]"
(lines 1)
=> The overlap is 12 of 100 squares. 0.4 × 0.3 = 0.12.
### Find 9 ÷ 0.5 and 3.6 ÷ 0.4. Then find the cost of 10 L of gas at $1.45 per litre.
(lines 2)
=> 9 ÷ 0.5 = 90 ÷ 5 = 18. 3.6 ÷ 0.4 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9.
=> 10 × 1.45 = $14.50.
### Leo says 0.2 × 0.4 = 0.8. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> 0.8 is bigger than both numbers. Part of a part is smaller. 2 × 4 = 8, with two decimal places: 0.08.

## Section 4: Order of operations
Target: I can evaluate an expression in the right order. (FD4)
### Evaluate 5 + 4 × 3. Fill in each step.
@viz flow steps="5 + 4 × 3|5 + [[12]]|[[17]]" width=420
(lines 1)
=> × first: 4 × 3 = 12. Then 5 + 12 = 17.
### Evaluate.
a) (8 − 2)² ÷ 4
b) −2 + 10 ÷ (−5)
c) {1/2} × 4 − {1/4}
(lines 2)
=> a) 6² ÷ 4 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9. b) −2 + (−2) = −4.
=> c) 2 − {1/4} = 1{3/4}.
### Kai says 18 ÷ 3 × 2 = 3. Explain how you know he is wrong.
(lines 2)
=> He did × before ÷. × and ÷ go left to right: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12.
