### [PB1 · 2] Kai says his chance of winning a raffle is 150%. Is he right? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> No (1 mark).
=> A probability is from 0% to 100%. The winning tickets can't be more than all the tickets (1 mark).

### [PB2 · 3] Explain your thinking. Ava says P(roll a 2) = {1/2}, because "you roll a 2 or you don't." Is she right? Explain.
@viz dice hot="2" size=30
(lines 3)
=> No (1 mark).
=> A die has 6 equally likely outcomes. Only one is a 2. "Roll a 2" and "not a 2" are not equally likely (1 mark).
=> P(roll a 2) = {1/6} (1 mark).

### [PB2 · 4] A bag has 3 red and 5 blue counters. Find P(red) as a ratio, a fraction, a decimal and a percent.
(lines 2)
=> Ratio 3:8, red to all (1 mark). Fraction {3/8} (1 mark).
=> Decimal 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 (1 mark). Percent 37.5% (1 mark).

### [PB2 · 2] Find P(red) on this spinner. Write it as a fraction and a percent.
@viz spin sections="green:2;red:1;blue:1" size=130
(lines 2)
=> Split green into 2 equal pieces. There are 4 equal pieces, and red is 1 (1 mark).
=> P(red) = {1/4} = 25% (1 mark).

### [PB2 · 2] The chance of rain in Red Deer tomorrow is 0.3. Find P(no rain).
(lines 1)
=> 1 − 0.3 (1 mark) = 0.7, or 70% (1 mark).
