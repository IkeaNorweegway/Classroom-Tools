### [VO1 · 2] Name this prism. How many lateral faces does it have?
@viz solid kind=ngon n=6 sv=1.5 hv=3 shade=0 size=130
(lines 1)
=> A hexagonal prism (1 mark). 6 lateral faces (1 mark).

### [VO1 · 2] This box lies on a rectangle face. Which faces are its bases? Name the prism.
@viz solid kind=tri bv=4 thv=3.5 lv=9 ground=1 shade=0 size=200
(lines 2)
=> The two triangle ends (1 mark). A triangular prism (1 mark).
=> No marks for "the bottom rectangle" as a base.

### [VO1 · 2] A hockey puck is a cylinder. How many bases does it have, and what shape are they? What is its side called?
(lines 2)
=> 2 circle bases (1 mark). The curved surface (1 mark).

### [VO2 · 3] Find the volume of this box of centimetre cubes.
@viz layers l=6 w=3 h=2 llabel="one layer: ___ cubes" blabel="___ layers"
(lines 2)
=> One layer: 6 × 3 = 18 cubes (1 mark). 2 layers (1 mark). V = 18 × 2 = 36 cm³ (1 mark).

### [VO2 · 2] A box is 9 cm long, 4 cm wide and 5 cm tall. Find its volume.
(lines 2)
=> 9 × 4 × 5 = 180 (1 mark). 180 cm³ (1 mark, with units).

### [VO2 · 2] Explain your thinking. Leo writes the volume of that box as 180 cm². Is he right? Explain.
(lines 3)
=> No: the unit should be cm³ (1 mark).
=> Volume counts cubes, or three lengths are multiplied: length × width × height (1 mark).

### [VO3 · 3] Find the volume of this triangular prism.
@viz solid kind=tri along=z b="5 cm" th="4 cm" l="9 cm" bv=5 thv=4 lv=9 blabel="B" size=170
(lines 2)
=> B = ½ × 5 × 4 = 10 cm² (1 mark). h = 9 cm (1 mark). V = 10 × 9 = 90 cm³ (1 mark).
=> No mark for the last step if the ½ is missing (V = 180 cm³).

### [VO3 · 4] A grain bin is 10 m across and 4 m tall. Find its volume.
@viz solid kind=cyl r="d = 10 m" h="h = 4 m" rv=5 hv=4 diameter=1 blabel="B" fill=cold size=130
(lines 3)
=> r = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 m (1 mark). B ≈ 3.14 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 m² (1 mark).
=> V ≈ 78.5 × 4 (1 mark) = 314 m³ (1 mark).
=> Using 10 m as the radius (1256 m³) earns only the mark for multiplying by h.

### [VO3 · 3] A juice can has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 11 cm. Find its volume. Round to one decimal place.
(lines 3)
=> B ≈ 3.14 × 3 × 3 = 28.26 cm² (1 mark).
=> V ≈ 28.26 × 11 = 310.86 (1 mark). About 310.9 cm³ (1 mark).
