### [IN1 · 2] Draw counters to show 2 × (−5). What is the product?
(work 2)
=> 2 groups of 5 red counters. −10.

### [IN1 · 2] The water in a tank drops 4 cm every hour for 6 hours. Write a multiplication for the change and find it.
(lines 1)
=> 6 × (−4) = −24 cm

### [IN2 · 2] Find (−8) × (−5) and (−8) × 5.
(lines 1)
=> 40 and −40

### [IN2 · 2] Is (−2) × (−2) × (−2) positive or negative? Explain without multiplying.
(lines 2)
=> Negative: three negatives is an odd number.

### [IN3 · 2] Find (−54) ÷ 6 and (−54) ÷ (−6).
(lines 1)
=> −9 and 9

### [IN3 · 2] A hiker went down 360 m in 4 hours at a steady pace. What was the change per hour?
(lines 1)
=> (−360) ÷ 4 = −90 m per hour

### [IN4 · 2] Find 6 − 4 × (−3).
(work 1)
=> 6 − (−12) = 18

### [IN4 · 4] The low temperatures in Fort McMurray for 4 days were −15 °C, −9 °C, −12 °C and −4 °C. Find the mean low. Show your steps.
(work 3)
=> Sum: −40 (2 marks). Mean: (−40) ÷ 4 = −10 °C (2 marks).
