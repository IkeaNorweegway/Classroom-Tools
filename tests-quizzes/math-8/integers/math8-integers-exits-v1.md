## Section 1: Multiplying with models
Target: I can show the product of two integers with counters or a number line. (IN1)
### Draw a model for 3 × (−4). What is the product?
(work 3)
=> 3 groups of 4 red counters (or 3 jumps of −4): −12

## Section 2: Sign rules
Target: I can use the sign rules for multiplying integers. (IN2)
### Find (−6) × (−7) and (−6) + (−7).
(lines 1)
=> 42 and −13
### Explain why the two answers have different signs.
(lines 2)
=> Multiplying two negatives gives a positive. Adding two negatives gives a bigger negative.

## Section 3: Dividing integers
Target: I can divide integers. (IN3)
### Find (−63) ÷ 9. Write the multiplication that checks it.
(lines 2)
=> −7; check: (−7) × 9 = −63

## Section 4: Order of operations and problems
Target: I can use the order of operations with integers. (IN4)
### Find 8 + (−12) ÷ 4 × (−2).
(work 2)
=> (−12) ÷ 4 = −3; −3 × (−2) = 6; 8 + 6 = 14
