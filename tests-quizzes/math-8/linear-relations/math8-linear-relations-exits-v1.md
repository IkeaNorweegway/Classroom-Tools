## Section 1: Patterns to tables
Target: I can turn a growing pattern into a table and describe how it grows. (LR1)
### Fill in the table and describe the pattern in words.
@viz pattern start=2 add=3 figures=3
| Figure | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiles | [[5]] | [[8]] | [[11]] | [[14]] |
(lines 1)
=> Starts at 2 and goes up by 3.

## Section 2: Equations and ordered pairs
Target: I can write an equation and find a missing value in an ordered pair. (LR2)
### Write an equation for: x 1 2 3 4, y 6 10 14 18.
(lines 1)
=> y = 4x + 2
### For y = 4x + 2, find the missing values: (5, ___) and (___, 30).
(lines 1)
=> (5, 22) and (7, 30)

## Section 3: Graphing a relation
Target: I can graph a relation and decide whether to connect the points. (LR3)
### Graph y = 2x + 1 for x = 0 to 4.
@viz coord xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=10 keypoints="0,1;1,3;2,5;3,7;4,9"
=> (0, 1), (1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7), (4, 9)

## Section 4: Reading a relation
Target: I can describe a relation from its graph. (LR4)
### A graph starts at 60 and goes down by 4 each step. Describe it and write an equation.
(lines 2)
=> Decreasing; starting value 60; changes by −4 each step. y = 60 − 4x.
