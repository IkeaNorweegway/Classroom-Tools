### [LR2 · 2] For y = 3x − 4, find y when x = 6, and x when y = 11.
(work 2)
=> y = 14; 3x − 4 = 11 → x = 5

### [LR2 · 2] Mo says the equation for x 1 2 3, y 4 6 8 is y = x + 2. Is he right? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> No. Test x = 3: 3 + 2 = 5, not 8. Up by 2 means y = 2x + 2.

### [LR4 · 4] A 30 cm candle burns 3 cm every hour. Describe the relation (increasing or decreasing, starting value, change per hour), write an equation, and find when it burns out.
(work 3)
=> Decreasing, starts at 30 cm, −3 cm each hour (2). h = 30 − 3t (1). Burns out at 10 hours (1).
