@viz rnsets nat="[[6]]" int="[[−11]]" rat="[[−5/8]]"
=> 6 is natural. −11 is an integer. −{5/8} is rational (not an integer).

## Section 2: Repeating decimals and the number line

@viz rndiv a=8 b=11 steps=3 blank=1
(lines 1)
=> 80 ÷ 11 = 7 R 3, 30 ÷ 11 = 2 R 8. The 8 is back. {8/11} = 0.{bar:72}.

## Section 3: Operations with rational numbers
Target: I can add, subtract, multiply and divide positive and negative fractions and decimals. (RN3)
### Find −{2/3} + {1/4}.
@viz chips pos=3 neg=8 pairs=3 title="Each chip = 1/12" result="−8/12 + 3/12 = [[−5/12]]"
(lines 1)
=> −{8/12} + {3/12} = −{5/12}
### A diver at −8.4 m rises 2.5 m, then dives 3.1 m. Where is she?
(lines 1)
=> −8.4 + 2.5 − 3.1 = −9.0. She is at −9 m.
### Explain how you know (−{3/5}) ÷ (−{3/10}) is positive.
(lines 2)
=> Both numbers are negative. Same signs give a positive answer. It equals 2.
