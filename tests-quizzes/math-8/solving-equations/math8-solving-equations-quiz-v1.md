### [SE1 · 2] What number clears the fractions in {1/4}x + {2/3} = 1? Write the new equation.
(lines 2)
=> 12, the LCD of 4 and 3 (1 mark). 3x + 8 = 12 (1 mark).

### [SE1 · 2] Solve {1/2}x + 4 = 7.
@viz semult top="{1/2}x;+ 4;=;7" by="× 2" bottom="[[x]];+ [[8]];=;[[14]]"
(lines 2)
=> × 2: x + 8 = 14 (1 mark). x = 6 (1 mark).

### [SE1 · 3] Solve 0.2x − 1.4 = 0.6. Check your answer.
(lines 3)
=> × 10: 2x − 14 = 6 (1 mark). 2x = 20, x = 10 (1 mark).
=> Check: 0.2(10) − 1.4 = 2 − 1.4 = 0.6 ✓ (1 mark).

### [SE1 · 3] Solve {2/3}x − {1/2} = {3/2}.

### [SE1 · 2] Tia solves {1/3}x + 5 = 6. She writes x + 5 = 18. Explain her mistake. Then fix it.
@viz semult top="{1/3}x;+ 5;=;6" by="× 3" bottom="x;+ 5;=;18" miss="2"
(lines 3)
=> She did not multiply the 5 by 3. Every term must be multiplied (1 mark).
=> x + 15 = 18, so x = 3 (1 mark).

### [SE2 · 3] Solve 2(x + 1) − 3(x − 2) = 5. Check your answer.
