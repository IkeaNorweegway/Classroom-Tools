## Section 1: Clearing fractions and decimals
Target: I can multiply both sides by the same number to clear fractions and decimals. (SE1)
### Solve {1/5}x + 3 = 7.
@viz semult top="{1/5}x;+ 3;=;7" by="× 5" bottom="[[x]];+ [[15]];=;[[35]]"
(lines 2)
=> × 5: x + 15 = 35. x = 20. Check: 4 + 3 = 7. ✓
### Solve 0.3x − 0.9 = 1.5.
(lines 2)
=> × 10: 3x − 9 = 15. 3x = 24. x = 8. Check: 2.4 − 0.9 = 1.5. ✓
### In the first question, why must the 3 be multiplied by 5 too? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> Both sides must be multiplied by the same number. Every term is part of a side. If the 3 stays 3, the sides are not equal any more.

## Section 2: Brackets
Target: I can solve equations with brackets, even with negative or fraction factors. (SE2)
### Solve −4(x + 1) = 10.
@viz sedist factor="−4" terms="x;+ 1" result="[[−4x]];[[− 4]]"
(lines 2)
=> ÷ −4: x + 1 = −2.5. x = −3.5. Check: −4(−2.5) = 10. ✓
### Solve {1/2}(x − 4) = 7.
(lines 2)
=> × 2: x − 4 = 14. x = 18. Check: {1/2}(14) = 7. ✓
### Jo says {1/3}(x + 9) = {1/3}x + 9. Is she right? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No. Every term is multiplied: {1/3}(x + 9) = {1/3}x + 3. Test x = 0: {1/3}(9) = 3, not 9.

## Section 3: Variables on both sides
Target: I can solve equations with x on both sides and fraction or decimal coefficients. (SE3)
### Solve 0.7x + 2 = 0.2x + 4.5.
(lines 2)
=> Subtract 0.2x: 0.5x + 2 = 4.5. 0.5x = 2.5. x = 5. Check: 5.5 and 5.5. ✓
### Solve {1/3}x + 2 = {1/2}x − 1.
@viz semult top="{1/3}x;+ 2;=;{1/2}x;− 1" by="× 6" bottom="[[2x]];+ [[12]];=;[[3x]];− [[6]]"
(lines 2)
=> × 6: 2x + 12 = 3x − 6. 18 = x. Check: 6 + 2 = 8 and 9 − 1 = 8. ✓
### Zack solves 0.7x + 2 = 0.2x + 4.5. He writes 0.5x + 2 = 0.2x + 4.5. Is it equivalent? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No. He took 0.2x off the left side only. The right side still has 0.2x. Both sides must change the same way.

## Section 4: Equation problems
Target: I can write and solve an equation for a problem, and check the answer in the story. (SE4)
### Plan A costs $15 a month plus $3.25 per GB. Plan B costs $27 a month plus $1.75 per GB. When do they cost the same?
@viz flow steps="Let x = GB|A: 15 + 3.25x|B: 27 + 1.75x|A = B"
(lines 3)
=> 15 + 3.25x = 27 + 1.75x. 1.5x = 12. x = 8 GB. Both cost $41.
### Use F = 1.8C + 32. A winter morning in Red Deer is 14 °F. What is it in °C?
@viz thermo min=-10 max=40 step=10 values="14" labels="Red Deer (°F)"
(lines 2)
=> 14 = 1.8C + 32. −18 = 1.8C. C = −10 °C.
### How do you know your answer to the phone plan question is right? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> Put 8 GB in the story. Plan A: 15 + 3.25(8) = 15 + 26 = $41. Plan B: 27 + 1.75(8) = 27 + 14 = $41. They match.
