## Part B — Show Your Work

**9.** **3x = 15**, so x = 15 ÷ 3 = **5**. Check: 3 × 5 = 15 ✓
- 1 mark each

**10.** x = 42 ÷ (−6) = **−7**. Verify: −6 × (−7) = 42 ✓
- 1 mark: divides by −6
- 1 mark: −7
- 1 mark: verifies

**11.** x/4 = 1 − 3 = −2, so x = −2 × 4 = **−8**. Check: −8 ÷ 4 + 3 = 1 ✓
- 1 mark: subtracts 3
- 1 mark: multiplies by 4
- 1 mark: −8

**12.** Sam divided only some of the terms (he didn't divide the 10). Undo the + 10 first: 5x = 25, so **x = 5**. Check: 5(5) + 10 = 35 ✓
- 1 mark: names the mistake
- 1 mark: 5x = 25
- 1 mark: x = 5

**13.** x − 3 = 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so **x = 8**. (Or 4x − 12 = 20, 4x = 32.) Verify: 4(8 − 3) = 4 × 5 = 20 ✓
- 1 mark: divides first or expands correctly
- 1 mark: 8
- 1 mark: verifies

**14.** x + 5 = 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, so **x = −1.5** (or −3/2). (Or 2x + 10 = 7, 2x = −3.)
- 1 mark: first step
- 1 mark: second step
- 1 mark: −1.5

**15.** Let r be the number of rides. **3r + 5 = 26**. 3r = 21, so r = 7. **Ana went on 7 rides.**
- 1 mark: equation
- 1 mark: r = 7
- 1 mark: sentence

## Part C — Put It Together

**16.** (a) **C = 2g + 25**
- 1 mark: 2g
- 1 mark: + 25

(b) 2g + 25 = 41, so 2g = 16, so **g = 8 GB**
- 1 mark each: steps, answer

(c) g + 20 = 78 ÷ 3 = 26, so **g = 6**
- 1 mark each: divides by 3, answer

(d) 3(6 + 20) = 3 × 26 = 78 ✓. **Her brother used 6 GB each month.**
- 1 mark: verifies
- 1 mark: sentence

## Part D — Explain Your Thinking

**17.** Model answer: Kim is not right. Check: 2(5.5 + 3) = 2 × 8.5 = 17, not 14. Her mistake: 2(x + 3) is 2x + 6, not 2x + 3. She only multiplied the x. Correct: 2x + 6 = 14, so 2x = 8, so **x = 4**. Check: 2(4 + 3) = 14 ✓
