## Section 1: Nets
Target: I can draw and recognize nets. (SV1)
### Sketch the net of a cylinder. Label the length of the rectangle.
(work 3)
=> Two circles and a rectangle; the rectangle's length is the circumference (2πr or πd).

## Section 2: Surface area of prisms
Target: I can find the surface area of a prism. (SV2)
### Find the SA of a box 6 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm.
(work 2)
=> 2(24) + 2(18) + 2(12) = 48 + 36 + 24 = 108 cm²

## Section 3: Surface area of cylinders
Target: I can find the surface area of a cylinder. (SV3)
### Find the SA of a cylinder with r = 2 cm and h = 5 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14.
(work 2)
=> 2(3.14)(4) + 2(3.14)(2)(5) = 25.12 + 62.8 = 87.92 cm²

## Section 4: Volume of prisms and cylinders
Target: I can find volume with area of base × height. (SV4)
### Find the volume of a triangular prism: triangle base 6 cm, height 5 cm; prism length 10 cm.
(work 2)
=> Base area = 15 cm². V = 150 cm³
### Is your answer in cm² or cm³? Why?
(lines 1)
=> cm³, because volume measures space (three dimensions).
