## Part C — Put It Together

**16.** (a) Box: 10 × 6 × 20 = **1200 cm³**. Cylinder: 3.14 × 5 × 5 × 15 ≈ **1177.5 cm³**

(c) Ends: 2 × 3.14 × 25 = 157. Side: 2 × 3.14 × 5 × 15 = 471. Total **628 cm²**

(d) The **box holds a little more** (1200 vs 1177.5 cm³), but the **cylinder needs much less cardboard** (628 vs 760 cm²). Either choice is fine with a reason: for example, the cylinder, because it holds almost as much and costs less to make.
- 1 mark: both comparisons right

## Part D — Explain Your Thinking

**17.** Model answer: Sam is not right. Surface area is the covering on the outside (cm²); volume is the space inside (cm³). A 1 cm cube has SA = 6 cm² and V = 1 cm³. A 6 cm cube has SA = 6 × 36 = 216 cm² and V = 216 cm³. The numbers are different for the 1 cm cube, and even when the numbers match, the units are different: one is square units, one is cubic units.
