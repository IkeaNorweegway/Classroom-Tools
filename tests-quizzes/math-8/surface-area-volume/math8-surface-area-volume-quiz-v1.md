### [SV1 · 2] How many faces of each shape are in the net of a triangular prism?
(lines 1)
=> 2 triangles and 3 rectangles

### [SV1 · 2] A can has a diameter of 10 cm. How long is the rectangle in its net? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> About 31.4 cm. The rectangle wraps around the circle, so its length is the circumference, π × 10.

### [SV2 · 4] Find the surface area of a box 15 cm × 10 cm × 4 cm.
(work 3)
=> 2(150) + 2(60) + 2(40) = 300 + 120 + 80 = 500 cm² (1 mark per pair, 1 for the total)

### [SV3 · 4] Find the surface area of a cylinder with r = 5 cm and h = 8 cm.
(work 3)
=> Circles: 2(3.14)(25) = 157 (2). Rectangle: 2(3.14)(5)(8) = 251.2 (1). SA = 408.2 cm² (1).

### [SV4 · 2] Find the volume of the box in Question 3.
(work 1)
=> 15 × 10 × 4 = 600 cm³

### [SV4 · 2] Find the volume of the cylinder in Question 4.
(work 1)
=> 3.14 × 25 × 8 = 628 cm³

### [SV4 · 2] A student says a box that is 2 × 3 × 4 holds more when it stands on its 2 × 3 end. Is that true? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> No. 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 in any position; the space inside does not change.
