### [RR1 · 2] A pond has 5 frogs and 8 ducks. Write the ratio of frogs to ducks, and frogs to all the animals.
(lines 1)
=> 5:8 and 5:13

### [RR1 · 2] A drink is 1 part juice to 3 parts water. Leah says 1 out of 3 parts is juice. Is she right? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> No. 1 + 3 = 4 parts in all, so 1 out of 4 parts is juice.

### [RR2 · 2] Fill in: 4 : 7 = 20 : ___ and 4 : 7 = ___ : 49
(lines 1)
=> 35 and 28

### [RR2 · 2] Explain why 2:5 and 3:6 are NOT equal ratios.
(lines 2)
=> 2:5 × anything never gives 3:6. (2:5 = 0.4; 3:6 = 0.5.) Adding 1 to each term is not allowed.

### [RR3 · 2] A cyclist rides 54 km in 3 h. What is the unit rate?
(lines 1)
=> 18 km/h

### [RR3 · 4] Cereal: a 500 g box costs $4.50 and a 750 g box costs $6.00. Which is the better buy? Show both unit prices.
(work 3)
=> 500 g: $0.90 per 100 g (or $0.009/g). 750 g: $0.80 per 100 g. The 750 g box is the better buy.

### [RR4 · 4] A recipe uses 3 cups of flour to make 24 cookies. How much flour for 40 cookies? Show your strategy.
(work 3)
=> 1 cup makes 8 cookies. 40 ÷ 8 = 5 cups.
