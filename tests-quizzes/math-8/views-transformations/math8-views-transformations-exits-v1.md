## Section 2: Building from views and turning objects

## Section 3: Transformations on a grid
Target: I can translate, reflect and rotate a shape and give the new coordinates. (VT3)
### Point A(4, −2). Give A′ after: a) translate 5 left, b) reflect in the y-axis, c) rotate 180° about (0, 0).
(lines 2)
=> a) (−1, −2) b) (−4, −2) c) (−4, 2)

## Section 4: Congruence and combined moves
Target: I can do two moves in a row and explain congruence. (VT4)
### B(1, 3) is reflected in the x-axis, then translated 2 up. Where is B′? Is the image congruent? Why?
(lines 2)
=> (1, −3) then (1, −1). Yes: reflections and translations keep size and shape.
