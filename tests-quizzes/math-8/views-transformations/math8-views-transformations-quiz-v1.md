### [VT1 · 2] Explain why the front view of a stack of 2 cubes with a stack of 4 cubes behind it shows 4 squares, not 6.
(lines 2)
=> From the front, the tall stack hides the short one. The view shows the outline (4 tall), not hidden cubes.

### [VT4 · 4] After a reflection in the y-axis and then a translation 1 up, the image is Q′(−3, 4). Find Q. Is QR the same length as Q′R′? Explain.
(work 3)
=> Undo the translation: (−3, 3) (1). Undo the reflection: Q(3, 3) (1). Yes (1): reflections and translations keep lengths, so the image is congruent (1).
