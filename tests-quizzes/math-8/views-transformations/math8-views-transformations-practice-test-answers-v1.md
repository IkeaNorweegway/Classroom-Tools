## Part B — Show Your Work

**9.** **Top:** back row 3 squares; front row 2 squares (left and right ends), with a gap in the middle. **Front:** columns 2, 1, 1 tall. **Right side:** columns 1 tall (front) and 2 tall (back).
- 1 mark for each view

**10.** **5 cubes.** The top view shows 4 stacks. The front view says the left column is 2 tall. The side view says the back row is 2 tall and the front row is 1 tall. So the back-left stack is 2 and the other three stacks are 1: 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 5.
- 1 mark: uses the top view (4 stacks)
- 1 mark: places the 2-tall stack at back left
- 1 mark: 5 cubes

## Part D — Explain Your Thinking

**17.** Model answer: Ben is not right. A reflection is like a mirror image. The shape faces the other way and it moves, but every side length and every angle stays the same. Shapes with the same size and shape are congruent, so the reflected shape is congruent to the original. (Example: in Q16, TU and T″U″ are both 2 units.)

| Level | Marks | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Full | 3 | Says Ben is wrong; says lengths and angles stay the same; says the position or direction changes |
| Partial | 1–2 | Says the size and shape stay the same with no mention of what changes (2); says he is wrong only (1) |
| Not shown | 0 | Agrees with Ben |
