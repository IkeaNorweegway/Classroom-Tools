## Part B — Show Your Work

**9.** (a) 36 + 64 = **100 cm²** (b) √100 = **10 cm** (c) The two smaller squares together have the same area as the biggest square.
- 1 mark each

**10.** c² = 4² + 9² = 16 + 81 = 97, so c = √97 ≈ **9.8 cm**
- 1 mark: 16 + 81
- 1 mark: 97
- 1 mark: 9.8 cm

**11.** d² = 80² + 45² = 6400 + 2025 = 8425, so d = √8425 ≈ **91.8 cm**

- 1 mark: 8425

**13.** A leg must be shorter than the hypotenuse, and 13.9 cm is longer than 13 cm. Ben added the squares when he needed to subtract. a² = 169 − 25 = 144, so **a = 12 cm**.
- 1 mark: the leg can't be longer than the hypotenuse
- 1 mark: subtracts

**14.** 12² + 16² = 144 + 256 = 400. 20² = 400. They are equal, so **yes**, it is a right triangle.
- 1 mark: 400 from the two short sides
- 1 mark: 20² = 400
- 1 mark: yes

**15.** If the corner were square, the diagonal would be √(60² + 80²) = √10 000 = 100 cm. It is 98 cm, so **the corner is not square** (it is a little less than 90°).
- 1 mark: 3600 + 6400 = 10 000

## Part C — Put It Together

**16.** (a) d² = 27² + 27² = 729 + 729 = 1458, so d = √1458 ≈ **38.2 m**
- 1 mark each: set-up, answer

(b) The throw is the hypotenuse. 33² − 27² = 1089 − 729 = 360, so the distance is √360 ≈ **19.0 m**
- 1 mark: subtracts
- 1 mark: 19.0 m

(c) For a right angle the distance should be about 38.2 m (38² = 1444, not 1458). 38.0 m is a little short, so the corner is **not quite** a right angle (it is a bit less than 90°). Accept "very close to a right angle" if the comparison is shown.
- 1 mark: compares 38.0 with 38.2 (or 1444 with 1458)
- 1 mark: a conclusion

(d) Home → first → second = 27 + 27 = 54 m. 54 − 38.2 = **15.8 m** farther.
- 1 mark: 54 m
- 1 mark: 15.8 m
