### [SR3 · 3] Estimate √55 to one decimal place. Show your benchmarks. Then check by squaring.

=> Check: 7.4² = 54.76, close to 55 (1 mark). (√55 = 7.416…)

### [SR3 · 3] A square field has an area of 90 m². Find the side to the nearest tenth, then the perimeter.
@viz sqside n=9.49 grid=0 side="s" fill=plant cell=14 fence=1 labels="Area = 90 m²"
(lines 3)
=> s = √90 = 9.486… ≈ 9.5 m (2 marks: 1 for √90, 1 for the rounded value).
=> Perimeter: 4 × 9.486… ≈ 37.9 m (1 mark; 38.0 m from 4 × 9.5 also accepted).
