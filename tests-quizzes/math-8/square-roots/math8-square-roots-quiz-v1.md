### [SQ1 · 2] Find 12². Show your work.
(lines 1)
=> 12 × 12 = 144 (1 mark for the multiplication, 1 for 144)

### [SQ1 · 2] Is 40 a perfect square? Explain how you know.
(lines 2)
=> No. 6² = 36 and 7² = 49, so no whole number squared makes 40. (1 for no, 1 for the reason)

### [SQ2 · 2] Find √169. Show how you can check your answer.
(lines 1)
=> 13, because 13 × 13 = 169

### [SQ2 · 2] Mia says √64 = 32. Explain her mistake and give the right answer.
(lines 2)
=> She halved 64. 32 × 32 = 1024. √64 = 8, because 8 × 8 = 64.

### [SQ3 · 2] √52 is between which two whole numbers? Which one is it closer to?
(lines 2)
=> Between 7 and 8 (49 < 52 < 64); closer to 7.

### [SQ3 · 2] Estimate √20 to one decimal place. Explain your thinking.
(lines 2)
=> About 4.5 (16 < 20 < 25, a bit closer to 16). Accept 4.4 or 4.5 with a reason.

### [SQ4 · 4] A square floor has an area of 81 m². How long is one wall? How long are all four walls together?
(work 3)
=> One wall: √81 = 9 m (2 marks). All four: 4 × 9 = 36 m (2 marks).
