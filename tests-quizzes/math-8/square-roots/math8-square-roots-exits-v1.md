## Section 1: Perfect squares
Target: I can show a perfect square as a square of tiles, and find the square of a number. (SQ1)
### What is 7²? Show the multiplication.
(lines 1)
=> 7 × 7 = 49
### Is 24 a perfect square? Draw or explain.
(work 2)
=> No. 4 × 4 = 16 and 5 × 5 = 25. No square of tiles has exactly 24.

## Section 2: Square roots
Target: I can find the square root of a perfect square, and explain what it means. (SQ2)
### √81 = ___ because ___ × ___ = 81.
(lines 1)
=> 9, because 9 × 9 = 81
### A square has an area of 121 cm². How long is one side?
(lines 1)
=> √121 = 11 cm

## Section 3: Estimating square roots
Target: I can estimate the square root of a number that is not a perfect square. (SQ3)
### √60 is between which two whole numbers? Which one is it closer to?
(lines 2)
=> Between 7 and 8 (49 and 64). Closer to 8, because 60 is closer to 64.

## Section 4: Square root problems
Target: I can use squares and square roots to solve real problems. (SQ4)
### A square patio has an area of 49 m². How much edging goes all the way around?
(work 2)
=> Side = √49 = 7 m. Edging = 4 × 7 = 28 m.
