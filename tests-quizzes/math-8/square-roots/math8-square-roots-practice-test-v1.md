**16.** Jade is building a square patio in Calgary. She uses square tiles that are 1 m by 1 m. She has 150 tiles.

*[2]* (a) What is the biggest square patio she can make? How many tiles does it use? Why can't she make a bigger one?

*[2]* (b) Her neighbour has a square patio with an area of 50 m². Estimate the length of one side to one decimal place. Show the perfect squares you used.

*[2]* (c) Jade also builds a square sandbox with an area of 36 m². How long is one side? How much wood does she need to go all the way around it?

*[2]* (d) Jade says: “If I double the area of my patio, each side will be twice as long.” Test her idea with the patio from (a). Is she right?
