## Section 1: Outcomes and probability
Target: I can find the probability of an event. (PB1)
### A spinner has 8 equal parts: 3 are yellow. Write P(yellow) as a fraction, a decimal and a percent.
(lines 1)
=> 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%

## Section 2: Two events — trees and tables
Target: I can list the outcomes of two independent events. (PB2)
### Draw a tree for flipping two coins. List the outcomes.
(work 3)
=> HH, HT, TH, TT (4 outcomes)
### A coin landed tails 4 times in a row. What is P(heads) on the next flip? Why?
(lines 1)
=> 1/2. The flips are independent.

## Section 3: The multiply rule
Target: I can use P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B). (PB3)
### Roll a die and spin a 4-part spinner (one part red). Find P(a 2 and red).
(work 2)
=> 1/6 × 1/4 = 1/24

## Section 4: Testing probability
Target: I can compare experiment results with theory. (PB4)
### A die was rolled 12 times. A 1 came up 4 times. Is the die unfair? Explain.
(lines 2)
=> Not necessarily. Theory says about 2 in 12, but 12 rolls is a small sample. Roll many more times before deciding.
