## Part A — Choose the Answer
Circle the best answer. 1 mark each.

### [PR1 · 1] A pontoon boat holds 15 people. A canoe holds 3. How many times as many people does the boat hold?
- A) 12
- B) 5
- C) {1/5}
- D) 18
=> **B.** 15 ÷ 3 = 5.
=> Common mistakes: A is how many more (15 − 3). C compares the other way.

### [PR1 · 1] Maya ran 2 km. Noah ran 5 km. Maya ran how many times as far as Noah?
- A) 0.4
- B) 2.5
- C) 3
- D) 0.25
=> **A.** 2 ÷ 5 = 0.4.
=> Common mistakes: B compares Noah to Maya. C is the difference.

### [PR2 · 1] What is k for the ratio 4 : 10?
- A) 0.4
- B) 6
- C) 40
- D) 2.5
=> **D.** k = second ÷ first = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5.
=> Common mistakes: A divides the wrong way. B subtracts.

### [PR2 · 1] Which ratio has k = 3?
- A) 3 : 1
- B) 6 : 3
- C) 5 : 15
- D) 9 : 6
=> **C.** 15 ÷ 5 = 3.
=> Common mistakes: A has k = {1/3}. B has k = 0.5.

### [PR3 · 1] For a ratio a : b, k = 1.5. The first term is 8. What is the second term?
- A) 12
- B) 9.5
- C) 5.3
- D) 16
=> **A.** b = k × a = 1.5 × 8 = 12.
=> Common mistakes: B adds 1.5. C divides by 1.5.

### [PR3 · 1] On a map, 1 cm stands for 25 km. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they?
- A) 31 km
- B) 4.2 km
- C) 150 km
- D) 125 km
=> **C.** 6 × 25 = 150 km.
=> Common mistakes: A adds. B divides 25 by 6.

### [PR4 · 1] Which situation is an inverse proportion?
- A) the number of apples and their cost
- B) the number of painters and the time to paint a fence
- C) the distance driven and the gas used
- D) the hours worked and the pay
=> **B.** More painters means less time. The product (painters × hours) stays the same.
=> Common mistakes: A, C and D are direct: both amounts grow together.

### [PR4 · 1] Which table shows an inverse proportion?
- A) 1 : 6, 2 : 12, 3 : 18
- B) 1 : 5, 2 : 6, 3 : 7
- C) 2 : 4, 4 : 8, 8 : 16
- D) 2 : 9, 3 : 6, 6 : 3
=> **D.** 2 × 9 = 18, 3 × 6 = 18 and 6 × 3 = 18. The product stays the same.
=> Common mistakes: A and C are direct (the quotient stays the same). B is neither.

## Part B — Show Your Work
Show your steps.

### [PR1 · 3] A moose has a mass of about 450 kg. A deer has a mass of about 90 kg. a) How many times the deer's mass is the moose's? b) What decimal of the moose's mass is the deer's? c) A moose calf has 0.3 times the mass of the moose. Find the calf's mass.
@viz prtimes rows="Moose:1;Calf:0.3" value="450"
(lines 3)
=> a) 450 ÷ 90 = 5 times (1 mark).
=> b) 90 ÷ 450 = 0.2 (1 mark).
=> c) 0.3 × 450 = 135 kg (1 mark).

### [PR2 · 3] Is the cost proportional to the mass of apples? Find k for each column, then decide.
@viz prk top="2,3,5" bottom="$5.00,$7.50,$12.00" toplabel="apples (kg)" bottomlabel="cost ($)"
(lines 3)
=> 5.00 ÷ 2 = 2.5 and 7.50 ÷ 3 = 2.5 (1 mark).
=> 12.00 ÷ 5 = 2.4 (1 mark).
=> Not proportional: k is not the same in every column (1 mark).

### [PR2 · 3] Store A sells 750 g of cereal for $4.50. Store B sells 1.2 kg for $6.60. Use the cost per 100 g to find the better buy.
(lines 3)
=> Store A: 4.50 ÷ 7.5 = $0.60 per 100 g (1 mark).
=> Store B: 1.2 kg = 1200 g, and 6.60 ÷ 12 = $0.55 per 100 g (1 mark).
=> Store B is the better buy (1 mark).

### [PR3 · 3] Aiden earns $16.50 an hour at a garden centre. a) What is k for hours : pay? b) How much does he earn in 12 hours? c) How many hours does he work to earn $264?
@viz prk top="1,12,?" bottom="$16.50,?,$264" toplabel="hours" bottomlabel="pay ($)"
(lines 3)
=> a) k = 16.50 (1 mark).
=> b) 12 × 16.50 = $198 (1 mark).
=> c) 264 ÷ 16.50 = 16 hours (1 mark).

### [PR3 · 3] A smoothie uses berries and yogurt in the ratio 3 : 2. a) Find k for berries : yogurt. b) How much yogurt goes with 4.5 cups of berries? c) How many cups of berries go with 5 cups of yogurt?
(lines 3)
=> a) k = 2 ÷ 3 = {2/3} (about 0.67) (1 mark).
=> b) {2/3} × 4.5 = 3 cups of yogurt (1 mark).
=> c) 5 ÷ {2/3} = 7.5 cups of berries (1 mark).

### [PR4 · 3] Is each one direct or inverse proportion? Give a reason for each. a) the number of movie tickets and the total cost b) the speed and the time for a 300 km trip c) the number of friends sharing a $60 pizza bill and each person's share
(lines 3)
=> a) Direct: twice the tickets, twice the cost (1 mark).
=> b) Inverse: faster means less time; speed × time = 300 (1 mark).
=> c) Inverse: more friends means a smaller share; friends × share = 60 (1 mark).

### [PR4 · 3] It takes 4 volunteers 6 hours to pack the food hampers. a) Find k = volunteers × hours. b) How long would 3 volunteers take? c) How many volunteers would finish in 2 hours?
@viz prwork cases="4,6" labels="4 × 6 = [[24]]" rowlabel="volunteers" collabel="hours"
(lines 3)
=> a) k = 4 × 6 = 24 (1 mark).
=> b) 24 ÷ 3 = 8 hours (1 mark).
=> c) 24 ÷ 2 = 12 volunteers (1 mark).

## Part C — Put It Together
A Grade 8 class takes a bus from Edmonton to the Royal Tyrrell Museum in Drumheller. The trip is about 280 km.

### [PR3 · 2] (a) The bus uses 35 L of fuel for every 100 km. How much fuel does it use for the trip?
(work 2)
=> k = 35 ÷ 100 = 0.35 L per km (1 mark).
=> 0.35 × 280 = 98 L (1 mark).

### [PR4 · 2] (b) At 80 km/h the trip takes 3.5 hours. How long would it take at 100 km/h? Is this direct or inverse proportion?
(work 2)
=> Speed × time = 80 × 3.5 = 280, so time = 280 ÷ 100 = 2.8 hours (2 h 48 min) (1 mark).
=> Inverse: going faster takes less time (1 mark).

### [PR1 · 2] (c) An adult ticket costs $21. A youth ticket costs $14. How many times the youth price is the adult price? What fraction of the adult price is the youth price?
(lines 2)
=> 21 ÷ 14 = 1.5 times (1 mark).
=> 14 ÷ 21 = {2/3} (1 mark).

### [PR2 · 2] (d) The gift shop sells 3 postcards for $4.50 and 8 postcards for $12.00. Is the price proportional? Use k to explain.
(lines 2)
=> 4.50 ÷ 3 = 1.50 and 12.00 ÷ 8 = 1.50 (1 mark).
=> Yes, proportional: k = 1.5, $1.50 per postcard both times (1 mark).

## Part D — Explain Your Thinking

### [PR4 · 3] Ella says, "2 people take 6 hours to paint a room. So 4 people will take 12 hours, because both numbers double." Is she right? Explain.
(lines 4)
=> Model answer: No. This is inverse proportion: more people do the same job faster. The total work is 2 × 6 = 12 person-hours. With 4 people, 12 ÷ 4 = 3 hours. Doubling the people halves the time.
