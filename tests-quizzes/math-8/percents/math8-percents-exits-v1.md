## Section 1: Percents, fractions and decimals
Target: I can write a percent as a fraction and a decimal, even below 1% or above 100%. (PC1)
### Write 140% as a decimal and draw it on hundred grids.
@viz hundred shaded=0 grids=2
=> 1.4. One full grid and 40 squares of a second grid.
### Write 0.3% as a decimal.
(lines 1)
=> 0.003

## Section 2: Percent of a number
Target: I can find a percent of a number. (PC2)
### Find 15% of $80. Show how you used benchmarks.
(work 2)
=> 10% = $8, 5% = $4, so 15% = $12.
### Kira scored 18 out of 20. What percent is that?
(lines 1)
=> 18 ÷ 20 = 0.9 = 90%

## Section 3: Combined percents
Target: I can solve problems that add two percents, like tax and tip. (PC3)
### A $30 meal in Alberta. Add 5% GST and a 15% tip. What is the total?
(work 2)
=> 20% of $30 = $6. Total $36.

## Section 4: Percent of a percent
Target: I can explain why two percent changes in a row do not just add. (PC4)
### $80 goes down 25%, then up 25%. What is the final amount? Why isn't it $80?
(work 3)
=> $80 × 0.75 = $60. $60 × 1.25 = $75. The increase is 25% of $60, which is smaller than 25% of $80.
