*[3]* **12.** A Calgary Flames player scored on 14 of his 40 shots. What percent of his shots went in?

*[3]* **13.** A haircut in Edmonton costs $35. Ana pays 5% GST and leaves a 15% tip. Both are on the $35. What is her total?

*[3]* **14.** A bike costs $240 before tax. In Alberta the tax is 5% GST. In Ontario the tax is 13% HST. How much more does the bike cost in Ontario?

**15.** An $80 video game is 25% off. Then the store takes another 10% off the sale price.

*[2]* (a) What is the final price?

*[1]* (b) Is that the same as 35% off $80? Explain.

## Part C — Put It Together

*Show your work. [8 marks] &nbsp; /8*

**16.** A school fair in Red Deer had 400 visitors in Year 1. In Year 2, it had 20% more visitors than Year 1. In Year 3, it had 25% more visitors than Year 2.

*[2]* (a) How many visitors came in Year 2?

*[2]* (b) How many visitors came in Year 3?

*[2]* (c) Year 3 is what percent of Year 1? Write it as a percent and as a decimal.

*[2]* (d) Liam says: “The fair grew 20% + 25% = 45% from Year 1 to Year 3.” Is Liam right? Explain.
