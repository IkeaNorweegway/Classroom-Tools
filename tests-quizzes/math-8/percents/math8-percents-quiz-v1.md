### [PC1 · 2] Write 6% as a fraction and as a decimal.
(lines 1)
=> 6/100 (or 3/50) and 0.06

### [PC1 · 2] Write 0.9% as a decimal. Explain how you know it is less than 1%.
(lines 2)
=> 0.009. 1% = 0.01, and 0.9% is less than one whole percent.

### [PC1 · 2] A garden grew 160% as many carrots as last year. Write 160% as a decimal. Did it grow more or fewer carrots than last year?
(lines 2)
=> 1.6. More: 160% is more than 100%.

### [PC2 · 2] Find 30% of 90.
(work 2)
=> 0.3 × 90 = 27 (or 10% = 9, × 3 = 27)

### [PC2 · 2] 21 of 28 students walked to school. What percent is that?
(work 2)
=> 21 ÷ 28 = 0.75 = 75%

### [PC3 · 4] A $45 pair of shoes in Alberta: 5% GST. A $45 meal: 5% GST and a 15% tip. Find the total for each.
(work 3)
=> Shoes: 5% of $45 = $2.25 → $47.25 (2 marks). Meal: 20% of $45 = $9 → $54 (2 marks).

### [PC4 · 4] A $200 TV is 10% off. Then it is 10% off again. Sam says the price is now $160. Is Sam right? Show your work and explain.
(work 4)
=> No (1). $200 × 0.9 = $180 (1). $180 × 0.9 = $162 (1). The second 10% is of $180, so it is only $18 (1).
