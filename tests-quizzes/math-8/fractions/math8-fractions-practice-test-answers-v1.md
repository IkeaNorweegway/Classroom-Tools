## Part C — Put It Together

**16.** (a) 9/4 × 3/2 = 27/8 = **3 3/8 cups**
- 1 mark: improper fractions and multiplies
- 1 mark: 3 3/8

(b) 9/2 ÷ 3/4 = 9/2 × 4/3 = 36/6 = **6 batches**
- 1 mark: divides
- 1 mark: 6

(c) 5 ÷ 1/4 = **20 pieces**. Divide, because the question asks how many 1/4 m pieces fit in 5 m.
- 1 mark: 20
- 1 mark: the reason

(d) About 2 × 1 1/2 = 3 (or 2 1/2 × 1 1/2 ≈ 3 3/4). 3 3/8 is close, so **yes, it is reasonable**.
- 1 mark: an estimate
- 1 mark: compares it with (a)
