## Section 1: Mixed numbers and estimating
Target: I can change between mixed numbers and improper fractions, and estimate. (FR1)
### Write 3{2/5} as an improper fraction.
(lines 1)
=> {17/5}
### Is {9/10} × {1/8} closer to 0, {1/2} or 1? Why?
(lines 2)
=> Close to 0, because {1/8} is close to 0.

## Section 2: Multiplying fractions
Target: I can multiply fractions and mixed numbers. (FR2)
### Find {3/4} × {2/3}. Simplify.
(lines 1)
=> {6/12} = {1/2}
### Find 1{1/3} × 1{1/2}. Show your steps.
(work 2)
=> {4/3} × {3/2} = {12/6} = 2

## Section 3: Dividing fractions
Target: I can divide fractions. (FR3)
### How many {1/3} cups fit in 4 cups? Draw it or explain.
@viz bars bars="1/1;1/1;1/1;1/1"
=> 12 (each cup holds 3 thirds)
### Find {5/6} ÷ {1/3}.
(work 2)
=> {5/6} × 3 = {15/6} = 2{1/2}

## Section 4: Fraction problems
Target: I can choose the right operation for a fraction problem. (FR4)
### A bag holds 2{1/4} kg of rice. Each serving is {1/4} kg. Write the operation you need and solve.
(work 2)
=> Divide: 2{1/4} ÷ {1/4} = {9/4} × 4 = 9 servings.
