## Part A: Selected Response (15 marks — 1 mark each)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** Which is a biotic factor?

   a) soil moisture   b) air temperature   c) a decomposer bacterium   d) wind speed

**2.** An organism that makes its own food using sunlight is called:

   a) consumer   b) decomposer   c) producer   d) omnivore

**3.** A secondary consumer eats:

   a) only producers   b) only primary consumers   c) both producers and primary consumers   d) only decomposers

**4.** The arrows in a food web represent:

   a) which organism is the predator   b) the direction that energy flows
   c) which organism is bigger   d) the path that nutrients travel when an organism dies

**5.** Using the 10% rule, if primary consumers have 5,000 units of energy, secondary consumers have approximately:

   a) 50,000   b) 5,000   c) 500   d) 50

**6.** Which trophic level contains the LEAST energy?

   a) producers   b) primary consumers   c) secondary consumers   d) tertiary consumers

**7.** Biodiversity refers to:

   a) the size of an ecosystem   b) the number of producers in an area
   c) the variety of species and habitats in an ecosystem   d) the amount of abiotic material in an area

**8.** A keystone species is one that:

   a) is found only in Alberta   b) is the largest predator in an ecosystem
   c) has a disproportionately large effect on its ecosystem for its population size
   d) is endangered or at risk

**9.** The process by which a simple community gradually becomes more complex after a disturbance is called:

   a) erosion   b) ecological succession   c) biodiversity   d) nutrient cycling

**10.** Which of the following is a human-caused change to an ecosystem?

   a) a forest fire sparked by lightning   b) a beaver dam flooding a valley
   c) introduction of purple loosestrife to a wetland   d) a drought reducing grassland cover

**11.** What makes an invasive species harmful compared to a native competitor?

   a) invasive species are larger   b) invasive species lack natural predators in their new environment
   c) invasive species always destroy habitat directly   d) invasive species only affect humans

**12.** Ecosystem services include:

   a) only the plants and animals humans eat   b) clean water, clean air, soil formation, and other benefits ecosystems provide for free
   c) only services provided by managed parks   d) the commercial value of natural resources only

**13.** Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK):

   a) is less reliable than Western science   b) is built from generations of careful observation of specific ecosystems
   c) is only relevant to historical land use   d) has been proven incorrect by modern science

**14.** In Alberta, which biome has the highest biodiversity?

   a) grassland   b) wetland   c) parkland/foothills transition   d) tundra

**15.** A species is classified as "at risk" when:

   a) its population has dropped below 100 individuals   b) its population or habitat is threatened to the degree that extinction is a possibility
   c) it has been hunted in Alberta   d) a single individual has been found dead
