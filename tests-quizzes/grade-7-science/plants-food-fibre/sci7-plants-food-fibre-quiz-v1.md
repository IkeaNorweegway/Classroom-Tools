## Part A: Selected Response (10 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** Which is NOT part of cell theory?

   a) All living things are made of cells   b) Cells are the basic unit of life
   c) Cells produce their own food   d) All cells come from pre-existing cells

**2.** Which structure is found in plant cells but NOT in animal cells?

   a) nucleus   b) cell membrane   c) cell wall   d) DNA

**3.** The large central vacuole in plant cells primarily:

   a) produces food for the cell   b) stores water and maintains cell pressure
   c) controls what enters the cell   d) contains the cell's DNA

**4.** Chloroplasts are found in:

   a) all plant cells   b) root cells only   c) green parts of the plant   d) animal cells

**5.** Xylem transports:

   a) sugars from leaves downward   b) water and minerals from roots upward
   c) oxygen to the roots   d) glucose to all parts of the plant

**6.** Phloem transports:

   a) water upward from roots   b) sugars made by photosynthesis downward
   c) minerals from soil to leaves   d) oxygen from the atmosphere into leaves

**7.** The root system's main functions are:

   a) photosynthesis and reproduction   b) absorption, anchoring, and storage
   c) producing seeds and fruit   d) transporting sugars to all parts

**8.** The inputs to photosynthesis are:

   a) glucose, water, oxygen   b) carbon dioxide, water, light energy
   c) glucose, carbon dioxide, light   d) oxygen, glucose, minerals

**9.** Why do plant leaves appear green?

   a) they reflect all colours of light   b) they absorb green light and reflect other colours
   c) chlorophyll absorbs most colours but reflects green   d) they contain green dye in their vacuoles

**10.** Photosynthesis is important to ecosystems because:

   a) it produces water for animals   b) it converts solar energy into food energy that enters food chains
   c) it creates carbon dioxide for the atmosphere   d) it allows all organisms to make their own food
