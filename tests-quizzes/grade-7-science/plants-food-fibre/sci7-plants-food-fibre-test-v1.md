## Part A: Selected Response (15 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** Which is NOT a tenet of cell theory?

   a) All living things are made of cells   b) Cells are the basic unit of life
   c) All cells produce their own energy   d) All cells come from pre-existing cells

**2.** Which structure is found in plant cells but NOT in animal cells?

   a) nucleus   b) cell membrane   c) cell wall   d) mitochondria

**3.** The central vacuole in plant cells is responsible for:

   a) photosynthesis   b) storing water and maintaining turgor pressure
   c) producing proteins   d) copying DNA during cell division

**4.** Xylem tissue carries:

   a) sugars from leaves to roots   b) water and minerals from roots to leaves
   c) oxygen from leaves to roots   d) food to all parts of the plant

**5.** Which structure produces pollen?

   a) pistil   b) ovary   c) sepal   d) stamen

**6.** What is the direct product of pollination?

   a) a fruit   b) a seed   c) transfer of pollen to the stigma   d) germination

**7.** Vegetative reproduction (runners, bulbs, tubers) produces offspring that are:

   a) genetically unique   b) genetically identical to the parent
   c) more diverse than sexually reproduced offspring   d) always weaker than sexually reproduced offspring

**8.** Which statement about photosynthesis is correct?

   a) CO₂ is a product of photosynthesis   b) photosynthesis occurs in all cells of a plant
   c) glucose is a product of photosynthesis   d) photosynthesis requires no light

**9.** Why do plant leaves appear green?

   a) chlorophyll absorbs green light   b) chlorophyll reflects green light
   c) the central vacuole is green   d) the cell wall contains green pigment

**10.** Which Alberta crop is BOTH a food and fibre source?

   a) barley   b) wheat   c) flax   d) canola

**11.** Selective breeding involves:

   a) inserting a gene from one organism into another   b) breeding only plants with desired traits over generations
   c) exposing plants to radiation to cause mutations   d) cloning plants in a laboratory

**12.** About 95% of Alberta canola is genetically modified to be:

   a) drought-tolerant   b) herbicide-resistant   c) pest-resistant   d) higher in Omega-3

**13.** Which of the following best describes a monoculture?

   a) growing a diverse mix of crops   b) growing a single crop species over large areas
   c) using only organic methods   d) rotating crops every year

**14.** Selective breeding differs from genetic modification in that selective breeding:

   a) is faster   b) is more precise   c) has been used for thousands of years   d) is done in a laboratory only

**15.** Which role does phloem tissue play?

   a) It transports water from roots upward   b) It transports sugars made in leaves downward
   c) It provides structural support to the stem   d) It absorbs minerals from the soil
