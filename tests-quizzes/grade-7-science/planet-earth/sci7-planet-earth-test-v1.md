## Part A: Selected Response (15 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** Which layer of Earth is liquid?

   a) crust   b) mantle   c) outer core   d) inner core

**2.** The inner core is solid (not liquid) even though it is extremely hot because:

   a) it has cooled over billions of years   b) extreme pressure keeps it solid
   c) it has no iron or nickel   d) it is surrounded by the liquid outer core

**3.** Scientists know about Earth's interior primarily by studying:

   a) volcanic eruptions   b) deep drilling   c) seismic waves from earthquakes   d) satellite imagery

**4.** Igneous rock with large crystals formed:

   a) quickly at the surface from lava   b) slowly underground from magma
   c) from compressed sediments   d) from heat and pressure transforming existing rock

**5.** The law of superposition states that:

   a) igneous rock is always older than sedimentary rock   b) in undisturbed rock layers, older layers are deeper
   c) metamorphic rock is always the youngest type   d) all rock layers are the same age throughout the world

**6.** Fossils are most commonly found in:

   a) igneous rock   b) metamorphic rock   c) sedimentary rock   d) mantle rock

**7.** Tectonic plates are driven by:

   a) Earth's rotation   b) convection currents in the mantle
   c) the gravitational pull of the Moon   d) differences in plate thickness

**8.** Mid-ocean ridges form at:

   a) transform boundaries   b) convergent boundaries (ocean-ocean)
   c) divergent boundaries   d) convergent boundaries (continent-continent)

**9.** The Rocky Mountains formed because:

   a) a volcano erupted repeatedly in the same location   b) oceanic plates pushed under North America and compressed the crust
   c) a divergent boundary split the continent   d) glaciers deposited rock material in a range

**10.** What is the difference between the focus and epicenter of an earthquake?

   a) the focus is on the surface; the epicenter is underground   b) the focus is underground; the epicenter is on the surface directly above
   c) they are the same thing   d) the focus measures size; the epicenter measures shaking

**11.** A shield volcano has gentle, fluid lava flows because:

   a) it is located far from plate boundaries   b) its lava has low viscosity and releases gas gradually
   c) its lava has high viscosity and traps gas   d) it is smaller than a composite volcano

**12.** Freeze-thaw cycling is an example of:

   a) chemical weathering   b) erosion   c) mechanical weathering   d) deposition

**13.** The flat prairie landscape of central Alberta was primarily shaped by:

   a) wind erosion   b) river erosion   c) glaciation — ice sheets that covered Alberta and deposited till
   d) plate tectonics

**14.** Index fossils are useful in geology because:

   a) they are the largest fossils found   b) they allow correlation of rock layers across distant locations
   c) they only form in metamorphic rock   d) they show us what plants look like

**15.** Alberta's oil sands are best described as:

   a) volcanic rock from ancient eruptions   b) metamorphic rock from tectonic pressure
   c) ancient marine sediment from the Cretaceous period   d) glacially deposited material from the last ice age
