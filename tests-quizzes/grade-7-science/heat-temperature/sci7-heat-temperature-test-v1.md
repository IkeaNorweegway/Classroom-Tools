## Part A: Selected Response (15 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** Temperature measures:

   a) total energy transferred between objects   b) average kinetic energy of particles in a substance
   c) the rate of heat flow   d) how much a substance expands when heated

**2.** 0°C in Kelvin is:

   a) 0 K   b) 273 K   c) 373 K   d) −273 K

**3.** Absolute zero (0 K) is:

   a) the freezing point of water   b) the temperature at which particles theoretically stop moving
   c) the coldest temperature ever recorded on Earth   d) −100°C

**4.** A bathtub and a cup both contain water at 60°C. Which statement is correct?

   a) The bathtub has more heat energy because it has more particles
   b) The cup has more heat energy because the water is denser
   c) Both have the same heat energy because they are the same temperature
   d) Heat cannot be compared between different-sized containers

**5.** "Cold comes in through the window" is scientifically inaccurate because:

   a) windows reflect cold   b) cold is not a substance — heat is leaving through the window
   c) air cannot move through glass   d) cold travels only downward

**6.** In a heating-cooling curve, the plateau at 0°C during melting of ice occurs because:

   a) the particles stop absorbing energy   b) the stove runs out of power temporarily
   c) heat energy is used to break bonds between particles, not increase temperature   d) water reflects heat at 0°C

**7.** Which material is the best conductor of heat?

   a) wood   b) rubber   c) copper   d) air

**8.** Heat transfer by convection requires:

   a) direct contact between particles   b) electromagnetic waves   c) a fluid (liquid or gas) that can circulate   d) a vacuum

**9.** The Sun heats the Earth by radiation because:

   a) the solar wind conducts heat to Earth   b) radiation does not require a medium — it travels through the vacuum of space
   c) convection currents carry heat across space   d) Earth absorbs cold from outer space

**10.** Which material has a higher specific heat capacity?

   a) iron   b) sand   c) water   d) aluminum

**11.** Oceans moderate the climate of coastal cities because water:

   a) evaporates faster than land   b) has a high specific heat capacity and stores and releases heat slowly
   c) reflects sunlight more than land does   d) absorbs radiation better than soil

**12.** Insulation works by:

   a) producing heat inside the insulated object   b) slowing heat transfer in both directions
   c) reflecting all radiation back   d) storing cold air near the surface

**13.** Thermal pollution in a river most directly harms aquatic life by:

   a) making the water toxic   b) increasing acidity   c) decreasing dissolved oxygen levels
   d) introducing invasive species

**14.** A shiny space blanket (emergency blanket) primarily reduces heat loss by:

   a) conducting heat away from the body   b) blocking convection currents
   c) reflecting infrared radiation back toward the body   d) creating a vacuum layer

**15.** Which of the following is NOT an application of thermal expansion?

   a) expansion gaps in railroad tracks   b) mercury rising in a thermometer
   c) slack in power lines installed in winter   d) a metal pot heating water by convection
