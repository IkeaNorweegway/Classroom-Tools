## Part A: Selected Response (10 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** A bicycle is an example of a:

   a) solid structure   b) frame structure   c) shell structure   d) combination structure

**2.** An egg is best classified as:

   a) solid   b) frame   c) shell   d) combination

**3.** Most real-world structures are combination structures because:

   a) engineers are too lazy to use one type   b) one type alone is rarely optimal for all parts of a structure
   c) combination structures always cost less   d) building codes require it

**4.** The weight of a bridge's own steel is classified as:

   a) live load   b) dynamic load   c) dead load   d) thermal load

**5.** An earthquake shaking a building is classified as:

   a) live load   b) dead load   c) static load   d) dynamic load

**6.** Dynamic loads are more dangerous than static loads because:

   a) they are always heavier   b) they can be unpredictable in direction and cause resonance
   c) they cannot be calculated by engineers   d) they always occur in pairs

**7.** Compression is:

   a) forces pulling material apart   b) forces squeezing material together
   c) forces acting in opposite directions on parallel planes   d) a twisting force

**8.** A cable in a suspension bridge is primarily under:

   a) compression   b) tension   c) shear   d) torsion

**9.** Concrete is strong in compression but weak in tension. Steel rebar is added to make:

   a) coloured concrete   b) lighter concrete   c) reinforced concrete that handles both forces
   d) waterproof concrete

**10.** Torsion is best described as:

   a) a squeezing force   b) a twisting force   c) a pulling-apart force   d) a sliding force
