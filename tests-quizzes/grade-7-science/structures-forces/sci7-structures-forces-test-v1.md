## Part A: Selected Response (15 marks)

Circle the best answer.

**1.** A shell structure carries load through:

   a) internal solid material   b) a rigid framework of members   c) a curved outer surface
   d) underground pilings

**2.** The Lethbridge Viaduct (a large railway bridge built on a steel skeleton) is best classified as:

   a) solid   b) frame   c) shell   d) solid-shell

**3.** Most real-world structures are combination structures because:

   a) it is cheaper   b) engineers are legally required to use combinations
   c) different parts of a structure have different functional needs   d) combination structures are always lighter

**4.** The weight of people walking on a bridge is classified as:

   a) dead load   b) dynamic load   c) live load   d) compression load

**5.** Which is the BEST example of a dynamic load?

   a) the weight of a roof's shingles   b) 50 students sitting in a classroom
   c) an earthquake shaking a building's foundation   d) a car parked in a parking garage

**6.** Compression forces in a structure:

   a) pull material apart   b) push material together   c) slide material sideways   d) twist material

**7.** Cables in a suspension bridge are primarily experiencing:

   a) compression   b) shear   c) tension   d) torsion

**8.** Concrete is reinforced with steel because:

   a) steel makes concrete harder   b) steel makes concrete resist tension, which plain concrete cannot
   c) steel reduces the weight of concrete   d) steel improves concrete's appearance

**9.** A structure is most stable when:

   a) it is very heavy   b) its centre of gravity is high   c) its centre of gravity is within its base of support
   d) it is made of a single material

**10.** A triangle is the strongest polygon because:

   a) it has the most sides   b) it cannot change shape without changing side lengths
   c) it is always the heaviest   d) it distributes tension better than compression

**11.** A lever's mechanical advantage is GREATER than 1 when:

   a) the effort arm is shorter than the load arm   b) the effort arm is longer than the load arm
   c) the fulcrum is between the effort and load   d) the machine is a third-class lever

**12.** A mechanical advantage of 5 means:

   a) you do 5 times less work   b) you apply 5 times less force but must apply it over 5 times the distance
   c) the machine creates 5 times the energy   d) you apply force over 1/5 the distance

**13.** Which simple machine is a screw most closely related to?

   a) lever   b) wheel and axle   c) pulley   d) inclined plane

**14.** Steel is preferred over concrete in earthquake-prone buildings because steel:

   a) is heavier and more stable   b) is flexible and does not shatter under sudden lateral forces
   c) has better compressive strength   d) is waterproof

**15.** Which material property is most important for a suspension bridge cable?

   a) hardness   b) tensile strength   c) compressive strength   d) brittleness
