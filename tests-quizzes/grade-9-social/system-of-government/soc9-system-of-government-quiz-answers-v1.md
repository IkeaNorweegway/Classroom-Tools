# Canada's System of Government — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **The elected House of Commons.** (1 mark)
2. The government must resign, or call an election; a loss of confidence means the government no longer has the support it needs to govern. (2 marks)
3. Any one: first reading, second reading, committee study, third reading, both houses, royal assent. (1 mark)
4. Royal assent is the Governor General's formal approval; a bill does not become law until it has royal assent. (2 marks)
5. Federal: any one, e.g. defence, banking, criminal law, citizenship. Provincial: any one, e.g. health, education, property. (2 marks: one each)
6. Dividing power lets each level of government deal with matters closest to the people it serves, and reflects Canada's regions. (3 marks: each level's role, regions, balance)
7. **The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.** (1 mark)
8. A court can review a law and declare it invalid if it breaks a Charter right and cannot be justified under section 1. (4 marks: review, invalidate, section 1, example)

**Common errors to watch for:** "the Charter is an act of Parliament" (Q7); "the provinces can make laws about defence" (Q5).
