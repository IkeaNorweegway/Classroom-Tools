# Urbanization and the Growth of Industry — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **War in a person's home country.** (1 mark)
2. A push factor pushes a person out of their home country (war, poverty); a pull factor draws them to a new country (jobs, safety). (2 marks: one each)
3. Oil and gas (any one industry named in the unit). (1 mark)
4. Suburbs need cars to be practical, so more households buying cars increased demand for car-making. (2 marks)
5. Benefit: jobs, services, infrastructure. Cost: pollution, land loss, traffic. (2 marks: one each)
6. Benefits go to people with the jobs, land, or wealth; people without these may pay the costs (pollution, rising housing costs). (3 marks: benefit, cost, unequal distribution)
7. Any clearly stated opinion. (1 mark)
8. Evidence from the unit, explained as support for the opinion. (4 marks: evidence, explanation, link to opinion, a fair counter-point)

**Common errors to watch for:** "push and pull are the same thing" (Q2); "cities only have benefits" (Q5, Q6).
