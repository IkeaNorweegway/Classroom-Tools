# Canada's Independence and Democratic Rights — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Statute of Westminster.** (1 mark)
2. The Balfour Declaration recognized Britain's Dominions, including Canada, as autonomous and equal in status to the UK. (2 marks)
3. Women were recognized as "persons" eligible to sit in the Senate. (1 mark)
4. Any one of the Famous Five (Emily Murphy, Nellie McClung, Louise McKinney, Henrietta Muir Edwards, Irene Parlby) and what she fought for: women's right to be appointed to the Senate. (2 marks)
5. Control over foreign policy lets Canada make its own treaties and decisions, rather than having Britain decide for it. (2 marks)
6. Foreign policy shows a country's independence: controlling it means making its own decisions about alliances and agreements. (3 marks: definition, link to independence, example)
7. **Balfour Declaration (1926), Persons Case (1929), Statute of Westminster (1931).** (1 mark)
8. Ordering the events shows a sequence: recognition of equal status, then rights and legal changes, then the power to amend. The order shows steady growth in independence. (4 marks)

**Common errors to watch for:** "the Statute of Westminster made Canada fully independent in all respects" (Q6); "the Persons Case made women full citizens in 1929" (Q3).
