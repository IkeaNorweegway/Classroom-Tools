# Evolving Citizenship and Rights — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **A person granted the right to live in Canada permanently but not a citizen.** (1 mark)
2. A temporary resident has permission to stay for a set time (e.g. a student or worker); a refugee is someone who cannot safely return home and seeks protection. (2 marks: one each)
3. **The Canadian Citizenship Act.** (1 mark)
4. The Charter entrenched rights in the constitution, so governments and courts must respect them, and gave courts power to strike down laws that break them. (2 marks)
5. Any one right (e.g. fundamental freedoms, legal rights, equality rights, mobility rights) with a correct example. (2 marks: right, example)
6. Freedom of expression lets people speak, publish and protest, which a democracy depends on; it is listed among the fundamental freedoms. (3 marks: right, why it matters, Charter status)
7. Section 1 guarantees rights and freedoms subject only to reasonable limits that can be justified in a free and democratic society. (1 mark)
8. A reasonable limit: e.g. limits on speech that incites violence. The limit must be justified and proportionate, balancing the right against public safety. (4 marks: limit, justification, balance, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "rights can be limited at any time" (Q7); "citizens and permanent residents have identical rights" (Q1, Q2).
