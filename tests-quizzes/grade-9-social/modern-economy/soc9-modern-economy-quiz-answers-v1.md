# Building a Modern Economy — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **1930s** (the crash was 1929; the Depression deepened through the 1930s). (1 mark)
2. Any two: stock market crash, falling prices, bank failures, unemployment, drought in the Prairies. (2 marks: one each)
3. Any one: oil and gas, car-making, manufacturing, hydroelectric power. (1 mark)
4. Post-war demand for consumer goods, new technology and the need to rebuild raised demand for manufactured goods. (2 marks)
5. **The United States.** Trade with the US supports jobs and exports and shapes Canada's economy. (2 marks: country, why it matters)
6. Dependence on one market can leave Canada exposed when the US economy slows or when trade rules change. (3 marks: dependence, exposure, example)
7. Free trade: trade between countries with few or no tariffs or other barriers. (1 mark)
8. A strong answer weighs at least one benefit (lower prices, new markets) against at least one cost (competition, job loss) for the chosen group, and reaches a reasoned conclusion. (4 marks: benefit, cost, group, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "the Depression was caused only by the stock market crash" (Q2); "free trade has no losers" (Q8).
