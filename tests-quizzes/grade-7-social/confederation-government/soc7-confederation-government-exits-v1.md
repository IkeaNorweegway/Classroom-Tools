# Creating Canada's Government — Exit Tickets
Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

**Unit Learning Outcome (LO):** Students examine how the Quebec Conference, London Conference, and the British North America Act (1867) led to the creation of Canada.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (CG1–CG5, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.
