# Grade 6 Science — Climate: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **The average weather in a place over many years.** (1 mark)
2. Weather is what the atmosphere is doing at a place over a short time (hours or days). Climate is the average weather in a place over a long period (at least 30 years). (2 marks: one each)
3. Carbon dioxide, methane or water vapour (any one). (1 mark)
4. Sunlight passes through the atmosphere and warms the surface; some heat is radiated back out but greenhouse gases trap part of it, keeping the planet warmer than it would be. (2 marks)
5. Natural: volcanic eruptions, changes in the Sun's output, ocean cycles (any one). Human: burning fossil fuels, deforestation, agriculture (any one). (2 marks)
6. Trees take in carbon dioxide and store carbon; cutting them down releases stored carbon and removes a way of taking carbon out of the air. (3 marks: stored carbon released, less carbon absorbed, conclusion)
7. Any reasonable answer, e.g. drought reducing crop yields, a shorter growing season. (1 mark)
8. A warmer, drier summer increases evaporation, so less water flows into rivers and reservoirs; the town's water supply shrinks. Any answer that describes the full chain counts. (4 marks: each link, plus a conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "the ozone layer causes the greenhouse effect" (Q4); "weather and climate are the same" (Q2).
