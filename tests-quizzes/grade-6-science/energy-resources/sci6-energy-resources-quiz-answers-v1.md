# Grade 6 Science — Energy Resources: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Wind.** (1 mark)
2. Oil forms over millions of years and is used far faster than it is replaced, so it cannot be renewed on a human timescale. (2 marks)
3. It is changed into a more useful form, e.g. crude oil is refined into fuels. (1 mark)
4. Crude oil is refined (separated into products like gasoline and diesel), then transported to a station. (2 marks: refining, transport or distribution)
5. Any two: cost, environmental impact, reliability, availability, renewability. (2 marks)
6. Solar: higher upfront cost, low emissions, depends on sunlight. Coal: lower running cost, high emissions. Any cost factor and any environmental factor. (3 marks: one cost, one environment, a fair comparison)
7. Advantage: clean, renewable, reliable. Disadvantage: changes river ecosystems, needs a dam and flooding. (2 marks: one for each side)
8. Every energy source has a trade-off, e.g. wind is renewable but depends on wind and can affect birds; coal is reliable but pollutes. (4 marks: an example, a trade-off, a second example, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "renewable means free" (Q5, Q8); "processing makes a resource bigger" (Q3).
