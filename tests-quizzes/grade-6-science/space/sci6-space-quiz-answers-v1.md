# Grade 6 Science — Space: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **The Sun.** (1 mark)
2. Any planet (e.g. Mars, Jupiter) and any non-planet object (e.g. the Moon, an asteroid, a comet). (2 marks: one each)
3. Telescope, probe, rover or satellite (any one). (1 mark)
4. A telescope observes from a distance; a rover moves across a planet's surface to collect samples and images. (2 marks: one each)
5. The curved path an object follows around another object because of gravity. (2 marks)
6. Gravity pulls the satellite toward Earth, while its forward motion keeps it moving; the two balance so it keeps circling. (3 marks: gravity, motion, balance)
7. Any reasonable answer, e.g. food, water, oxygen, supplies. (1 mark)
8. Astronauts are in orbit, so they and the station fall toward Earth together, which makes them seem weightless. A challenge: the body changes without gravity, e.g. muscles and bones weaken; astronauts exercise daily. (4 marks: cause of floating, challenge, management, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "there is no gravity in space" (Q6, Q8); "satellites need an engine to stay up" (Q6).
