# Grade 6 Science — Matter: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Liquid.** (1 mark)
2. A gas has particles far apart, moving quickly and in all directions, colliding with each other and the container. (2 marks: one for spacing, one for movement)
3. **Melting.** (1 mark)
4. Particles at the surface of the liquid gain enough energy to break free and become a gas (water vapour); they move apart. (2 marks)
5. The particles vibrate or move faster and spread farther apart, so the metal expands. (2 marks)
6. Particles move faster and take up more space when heated, so the bridge expands. Without expansion joints, the bridge would buckle or crack. (1 mark for particles move/spread; 1 for expansion; 1 for consequence)
7. **Liquid water.** Ice is less dense than liquid water. (1 mark)
8. In ice, particles are held in a regular, open pattern with more space between them than in liquid water. The same mass takes up more space, so ice is less dense and floats. (4 marks: arrangement, spacing, mass vs volume, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "particles get bigger when heated" (Q5, Q6); "mass changes during melting" (Q8 reasoning); "ice is denser" (Q7).
