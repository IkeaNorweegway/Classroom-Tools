# Grade 6 Science — Forces: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **An arrow whose length shows its size.** (1 mark)
2. A 10 N arrow pointing left and a 4 N arrow pointing right, drawn to scale (left arrow 2.5 times as long as right). Net force 6 N left. (2 marks: one for both arrows correct, one for net force)
3. **Balanced.** (1 mark)
4. **Unbalanced.** The forces are not equal, so the ball speeds up in the direction of the larger force. (2 marks)
5. **Elastic.** (1 mark)
6. The paper clip does not return to its original shape after the force is removed, so the change is permanent. (2 marks)
7. **Action and reaction** (an action-reaction pair). (1 mark)
8. The two forces act on different objects (your hand on the wall; the wall on your hand), so they do not cancel. (3 marks: different objects, equal and opposite, both forces exist at the same time)

**Common errors to watch for:** "the forces cancel out" (Q8); "a moving object needs a force to keep moving" (Q4); "elastic and plastic are the same" (Q5, Q6).
