# Grade 6 Science — Living Systems: Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **A rock.** (1 mark)
2. Biotic: any living thing (e.g. a tree, a deer). Abiotic: any non-living part (e.g. soil, water, sunlight, air). (2 marks: one each)
3. **Oxygen.** (1 mark)
4. Carbon dioxide, water and sunlight. Sunlight provides the energy to make food (sugar). (2 marks)
5. The arrow points from the food (what is eaten) to the organism that eats it, showing the flow of energy. (2 marks)
6. Grass → mouse → hawk, with arrows pointing in the direction energy flows. Energy decreases at each level, because each organism uses energy for its own life processes. (3 marks: arrows, direction, energy decreases)
7. Any reasonable answer, e.g. fish, frogs, aquatic plants. (1 mark)
8. Without hawks, the mice population rises. More mice eat more grass, so the grass decreases. Any answer that describes the chain of cause and effect counts. (4 marks: each link in the chain, plus a conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "arrows point to the predator" (Q5) — the arrow shows the flow of energy, which goes from prey to predator; "energy is recycled up the food chain" (Q6).
