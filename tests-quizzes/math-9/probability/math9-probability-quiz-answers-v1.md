1. P(4) = **1/6**. (2 marks)
2. 26 red cards out of 52: P(red) = **1/2**. (2 marks)
3. **Independent.** The result of the first flip does not change the chance of the second. (2 marks: answer, reason)
4. P(both red) = (3/4) × (2/3) = **1/2**. (2 marks: setup, answer)
5. (1/6) × (1/6) = **1/36**. (2 marks)
6. (1/2) × (1/2) = **1/4**. (2 marks)
7. Expected heads = 10 × 1/2 = **5**. The actual result of 7 is higher, which is normal variation over a small number of flips. (2 marks: expected value, comparison)
8. Small samples vary; chance can produce results that differ from the expected value, and the results get closer to the expected value with more trials. (2 marks)

**Common errors to watch for:** adding probabilities when both events must happen (Q5, Q6); treating dependent events as independent (Q4); expecting exact agreement in small samples (Q7, Q8).
