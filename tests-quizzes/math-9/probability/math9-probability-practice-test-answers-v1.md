## Part C — Extended Response

**19.** (a) P(gold) = **3/10**. P(not black) = 1 − 1/10 = **9/10**.
- 1 mark each

(b) 3/10 × 2/9 = 6/90 = **1/15** (about 0.067)

- 1 mark: 1/15

(c) 3/10 × 3/10 = **9/100** (0.09). It is higher than (b) because the gold ticket goes back in, so the second draw still has 3 gold out of 10. In (b) the second draw had only 2 gold out of 9.

- 1 mark: 0.14 compared with 0.09
- 1 mark: explains variation in a small number of trials

## Part D — Thinking

**20.** Model answer: With replacement: 2/5 × 2/5 = 4/25 = 0.16. Without replacement: 2/5 × 1/4 = 2/20 = 0.10. That is a big difference, so Lee is wrong for a small bag: removing one red marble changes the second draw from 2 red out of 5 to 1 red out of 4. Lee is nearly right only when the bag is very large. For example, with 200 red and 300 blue, the second draw changes from 200/500 = 0.4 to 199/499 ≈ 0.399, which hardly changes anything. But removing an item always changes the probability a little.
