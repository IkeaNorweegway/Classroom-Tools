## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** The degree of 6x² − 4x³ + x − 9 is
A) 2 &nbsp; B) 3 &nbsp; C) 6 &nbsp; D) 4

**2.** In −2x² + 5x − 7, the constant term is
A) −2 &nbsp; B) 5 &nbsp; C) −7 &nbsp; D) 7

**3.** Which polynomial is a trinomial?
A) x² + 1 &nbsp; B) 4x &nbsp; C) 3x² − x + 2 &nbsp; D) x³ − x² + x − 1

**4.** Which pair are like terms?
A) 3x and 3x² &nbsp; B) −5x² and 2x² &nbsp; C) 4x and 4y &nbsp; D) 7 and 7x

**5.** (2x² + 3x) + (x² − 5x) =
A) 3x² − 2x &nbsp; B) 3x² + 8x &nbsp; C) 3x⁴ − 2x² &nbsp; D) x² − 2x

**6.** The opposite of (x² − 4x + 3) is
A) −x² − 4x + 3 &nbsp; B) −x² + 4x − 3 &nbsp; C) x² + 4x − 3 &nbsp; D) −x² + 4x + 3

**7.** 4x(x − 2) =
A) 4x² − 2 &nbsp; B) 4x² − 8x &nbsp; C) 4x² − 8 &nbsp; D) 4x − 8

**8.** (x + 5)(x − 2) =
A) x² − 10 &nbsp; B) x² + 3x − 10 &nbsp; C) x² − 3x − 10 &nbsp; D) x² + 7x − 10

## Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

*Show your working. [3 marks each] /30*

**9.** Look at the polynomial 5 − 2x + 3x².
(a) Write it in standard form. *[1]*
(b) State its degree and its leading coefficient. *[1]*
(c) A student says the degree is 5, "because 5 is the biggest number." Explain the mistake. *[1]*

**10.** Simplify 4x² − 3x + 6 − x² + 5x − 8. *[3]*

**11.** A student wrote 3x² + 4x = 7x³.
(a) Explain why this is wrong. *[2]*
(b) What is the simplified form of 3x² + 4x? *[1]*

**12.** Add (3x² − 2x + 5) + (−x² − 4x − 7). *[3]*

**13.** Subtract (5x² + x − 2) − (2x² − 3x + 4). *[3]*

**14.** A student subtracted like this: (6x − 1) − (2x − 5) = 6x − 1 − 2x − 5 = 4x − 6.
Find the mistake, and give the correct answer. *[3]*

**15.** Expand.
(a) 3(2x² − x + 4) *[1]*
(b) −2x(x² − 3x + 1) *[2]*

**16.** A rectangle is 2x cm wide and (3x + 5) cm long.
(a) Write a simplified polynomial for its area. *[2]*
(b) Find the area when x = 3. *[1]*

**17.** Use an area model to expand and simplify (2x + 3)(x + 4). *[3]*

**18.** Expand and simplify (3x − 2)(x − 1). Then check your answer by substituting x = 2 into the question and into your answer. *[3]*

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** A family in Medicine Hat floods a backyard rink. The rink is (2x + 6) m long and (x + 3) m wide. [Diagram: rectangle labelled 2x + 6 and x + 3, with a shaded x by x square in one corner.]

(a) Write and simplify a polynomial for the perimeter of the rink. *[2]*
(b) Write and simplify a polynomial for the area of the rink. *[2]*
(c) A square corner, x m by x m, is left unflooded for a snowbank. Write and simplify a polynomial for the area that gets flooded. *[2]*
(d) The neighbours build a rink with the same width, (x + 3) m, and a length of 3x m. Write its area as a simplified polynomial. *[2]*
