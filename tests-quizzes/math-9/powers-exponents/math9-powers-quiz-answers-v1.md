# Powers and Exponents — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **32** (2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2). (1 mark)
2. **3⁴**. (1 mark)
3. **x⁷** (add the exponents). (2 marks)
4. **y¹⁰** (multiply the exponents). (2 marks)
5. **1.** Any non-zero number to the power 0 is 1: dividing a power by itself gives x^(n−n) = x⁰, and any number divided by itself is 1. (2 marks: value, reason)
6. 6⁻² = 1/6² = **1/36**. (2 marks)
7. 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8, which is positive. A negative exponent means reciprocal, not a negative answer. (3 marks: rule, calculation, why it is not −8)
8. (2x²)³ = 2³ · x⁶ = **8x⁶**. (3 marks: cube the coefficient, multiply the exponent, answer)

**Common errors to watch for:** 2⁵ calculated as 2 × 5 (Q1); 7⁰ = 0 (Q5); negative exponent giving a negative answer (Q6, Q7); coefficient not cubed (Q8).
