## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** The solution of x/8 = 15/24 is
A) x = 45 &nbsp; B) x = 5 &nbsp; C) x = 3 &nbsp; D) x = 120

**2.** A photo 4 cm wide is enlarged to 10 cm wide. The scale factor is
A) 2.5 &nbsp; B) 0.4 &nbsp; C) 6 &nbsp; D) 40

**3.** A scale factor of 0.5 makes
A) an enlargement &nbsp; B) a shape that is not similar &nbsp; C) a congruent copy &nbsp; D) a reduction

**4.** Two polygons are similar when
A) they have the same perimeter &nbsp; B) all their sides are equal &nbsp; C) corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional &nbsp; D) they have the same number of sides

**5.** Which rectangle is similar to a 2 cm by 5 cm rectangle?
A) 6 cm by 15 cm &nbsp; B) 4 cm by 7 cm &nbsp; C) 3 cm by 6 cm &nbsp; D) 5 cm by 8 cm

**6.** Two triangles have two pairs of equal angles. They are similar by
A) SSS &nbsp; B) SAS &nbsp; C) they may not be similar &nbsp; D) AA

**7.** One triangle has sides 3, 4 and 5. Another has sides 6, 8 and 10. The triangles are
A) congruent &nbsp; B) similar by SSS &nbsp; C) similar only if the angles are measured &nbsp; D) not similar

**8.** On a map with a scale of 1 : 50 000, a distance of 4 cm is a real distance of
A) 20 km &nbsp; B) 200 m &nbsp; C) 2 km &nbsp; D) 12.5 km

## Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

*Show your working. [3 marks each] /30*

**9.** Solve. (a) 12/x = 3/5 *[1]* (b) (x − 1)/6 = 10/4 *[2]*

**10.** A truck uses 6 L of fuel to drive 80 km. At the same rate, how much fuel does it need to drive from Edmonton to Calgary, about 300 km? Set up a proportion. *[3]*

**11.** Quadrilateral ABCD is similar to quadrilateral EFGH. AB = 6 cm, BC = 9 cm, EF = 10 cm and ∠B = 110°.
(a) Find the scale factor from ABCD to EFGH. *[1]*
(b) Find FG. *[1]*
(c) Find ∠F. *[1]*

**12.** Is a 4 cm by 6 cm rectangle similar to a 6 cm by 8 cm rectangle? Show the ratios you used. *[3]*

**13.** A triangle with sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 8 cm is enlarged by a scale factor of 2.5.
(a) Find the side lengths of the new triangle. *[2]*
(b) Show that the ratio of the perimeters equals the scale factor. *[1]*

**14.** In △ABC, ∠A = 40° and ∠B = 75°. In △DEF, ∠D = 40° and ∠F = 65°. Are the triangles similar? Explain, and write a similarity statement if they are. *[3]*

**15.** In △PQR, PQ = 6 cm, QR = 9 cm and ∠Q = 70°. In △STU, ST = 4 cm, TU = 6 cm and ∠T = 70°. Are the triangles similar? Name the criterion and show your ratios. *[3]*

**16.** In the diagram, DE ∥ BC. AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and DE = 6 cm. Find BC. *[3]* [Diagram.]

**17.** A floor plan uses a scale of 1 : 250. A room is 2.4 cm by 3.2 cm on the plan. What are the real dimensions of the room, in metres? *[3]*

**18.** A student 1.6 m tall casts a shadow 2.4 m long. At the same time, a flagpole casts a shadow 15 m long. How tall is the flagpole? *[3]* [Diagram.]

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** A Grade 9 class builds a scale model of the school gym at a scale of 1 : 120. The real gym is 36 m long, 24 m wide and 9 m tall.

(a) Find the length, width and height of the model in centimetres. *[2]*
(b) A door on the model is 1.75 cm tall. How tall is the real door, in metres? *[2]*
(c) A basketball court is 28 m by 15 m. Is the gym floor similar to the court? Show your ratios. *[2]*
(d) At one time of day, the 9 m gym wall casts a shadow 6 m long, and a nearby tree casts a shadow 10 m long. Explain why the two triangles are similar, then find the height of the tree. *[2]*
