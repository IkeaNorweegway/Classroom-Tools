# Measurement — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. V = πr²h = π(4)(5) = **20π cm³**. (2 marks)
2. SA = 4πr² = 4π(9) = **36π cm²**. (2 marks)
3. Surface area is the total area of all the outside faces (measured in square units); volume is the space inside (measured in cubic units). (2 marks: one each)
4. V = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)π(9)(4) = **12π cm³**. (2 marks)
5. V = (1/3)(2 × 2)(3) = **4 cm³**. (2 marks)
6. The two faces where the cubes touch are hidden, so they are not counted in the combined surface area. (2 marks)
7. V = (1/3)(4 × 4)(6) = **32 cm³**. (2 marks)
8. The cylinder has three times the volume of the cone with the same base and height; the cone is **one-third** of the cylinder. (2 marks)

**Common errors to watch for:** forgetting the 1/3 for cones and pyramids (Q4, Q5, Q7); counting the joined faces in the surface area (Q6); using the diameter instead of the radius (Q1, Q4).
