*Read each question fully before answering. Show the formula you use and all working. Round answers to one decimal place and include units. Formulas for pyramids, cones and spheres are given below.*

| Solid | Surface area | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Square pyramid (base side s, slant height l, height h) | SA = s² + 2sl | V = (1/3)s²h |
| Cone (radius r, slant height l, height h) | SA = πr² + πrl | V = (1/3)πr²h |
| Sphere (radius r) | SA = 4πr² | V = (4/3)πr³ |

## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** A triangle has a base of 10 cm and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. Its area is
A) 60 cm² &nbsp; B) 30 cm² &nbsp; C) 16 cm² &nbsp; D) 32 cm²

**2.** The area of a circle with a radius of 5 cm, to the nearest tenth, is
A) 31.4 cm² &nbsp; B) 78.5 cm² &nbsp; C) 15.7 cm² &nbsp; D) 25.0 cm²

**3.** Which measurement tells you how much cardboard you need to make a closed box?
A) volume &nbsp; B) surface area &nbsp; C) perimeter &nbsp; D) capacity

**4.** When you unroll the curved surface of a cylinder, it makes a
A) circle &nbsp; B) triangle &nbsp; C) rectangle &nbsp; D) semicircle

**5.** The volume of a rectangular prism 4 cm by 5 cm by 6 cm is
A) 120 cm³ &nbsp; B) 15 cm³ &nbsp; C) 148 cm³ &nbsp; D) 60 cm³

**6.** Which formula gives the volume of a cylinder?
A) 2πrh &nbsp; B) πr²h &nbsp; C) 2πr² + 2πrh &nbsp; D) (1/3)πr²h

**7.** A cone and a cylinder have the same radius and the same height. The cone's volume is
A) the same as the cylinder's &nbsp; B) half the cylinder's &nbsp; C) one-third of the cylinder's &nbsp; D) double the cylinder's

**8.** Two cubes with 2 cm sides are glued face to face. The surface area of the new shape is
A) 48 cm² &nbsp; B) 40 cm² &nbsp; C) 24 cm² &nbsp; D) 44 cm²

**11.** Find the surface area of a closed cylinder with a radius of 3 cm and a height of 10 cm. *[3]*

**13.** A water tank is a cylinder with a radius of 1.5 m and a height of 4 m. Find its volume. *[3]*

**14.** A shed is a rectangular prism 6 m wide, 10 m long and 4 m tall, with a triangular prism roof. The roof's triangle has a base of 6 m and a height of 3 m. Find the total volume of the shed. *[3]* [Diagram.]

(a) Find its volume. *[2]*

(a) Find its surface area. *[1]*

**17.** A cone has a radius of 5 cm and a height of 12 cm.
(a) Find its slant height. *[1]*
(b) Find its surface area. *[2]*

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working. Round to one decimal place and include units. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** A Lethbridge honey company is choosing a closed container.
- **Container A:** a cylinder with a radius of 4 cm and a height of 10 cm.
- **Container B:** a rectangular prism 7 cm by 7 cm by 10 cm.

(a) Find the volume of each container. *[2]*
(b) Find the surface area of each container. *[2]*
(c) Which container holds more honey? Which uses less material? Which would you recommend, and why? *[2]*
(d) A designer suggests a cone with a radius of 4 cm and a height of 10 cm, and says it holds "half as much as Container A." Find the cone's volume and correct the designer. *[2]*
