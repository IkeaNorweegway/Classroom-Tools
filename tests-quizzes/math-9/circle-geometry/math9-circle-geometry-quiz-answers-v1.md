# Circle Geometry — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. (a) **Radius.** (b) **Tangent.** (2 marks: one each)
2. **90°.** A tangent meets the radius at right angles. (2 marks)
3. Inscribed angle = half the central angle = **40°**. (2 marks)
4. The tangent-radius rule: the tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so the angle is 90°. (2 marks: rule named, reason)
5. Inscribed angle = 120° ÷ 2 = **60°**. (2 marks)
6. Chord: any line segment joining two points on the circle. Arc: any part of the circumference. (2 marks: one each)
7. x = 110° ÷ 2 = **55°**. (2 marks)
8. The inscribed-angle rule (the inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc), because the inscribed angle subtends the arc from the other side of the circle. (2 marks: rule, reason)

**Common errors to watch for:** inscribed angle equal to the central angle (Q3, Q5, Q7); tangent meeting the radius at an angle other than 90° (Q2).
