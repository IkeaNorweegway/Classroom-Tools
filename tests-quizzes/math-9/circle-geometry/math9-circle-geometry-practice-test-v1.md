## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** A chord that passes through the centre of a circle is a
A) radius &nbsp; B) diameter &nbsp; C) tangent &nbsp; D) arc

**2.** An angle with its vertex on the circle and its arms along two chords is
A) a central angle &nbsp; B) an inscribed angle &nbsp; C) always a right angle &nbsp; D) a tangent angle

**3.** A circle has a diameter of 26 cm. Its radius is
A) 13 cm &nbsp; B) 52 cm &nbsp; C) 26 cm &nbsp; D) 6.5 cm

**4.** A line from the centre perpendicular to a chord
A) bisects the chord &nbsp; B) is always a radius &nbsp; C) is a tangent &nbsp; D) is parallel to the chord

**5.** In one circle, chords are drawn at different distances from the centre. Which chord is longest?
A) the one 2 cm from the centre &nbsp; B) the one 5 cm from the centre &nbsp; C) the one 7 cm from the centre &nbsp; D) they are all the same length

**6.** A central angle is 110°. The inscribed angle on the same arc is
A) 220° &nbsp; B) 110° &nbsp; C) 55° &nbsp; D) 70°

**7.** An inscribed angle that stands on a diameter is always
A) 45° &nbsp; B) 90° &nbsp; C) 180° &nbsp; D) different for each circle

**8.** A tangent and the radius to the point of tangency meet at
A) 45° &nbsp; B) 60° &nbsp; C) 90° &nbsp; D) 180°

## Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

*Show your working. [3 marks each] /30*

**9.** Use the diagram to name: (a) a radius (b) a diameter (c) a chord that is not a diameter. *[1 each]* [Diagram: centre O; diameter AB; chord CD; radius OE.]

**10.** A chord AB is 24 cm long in a circle with a radius of 13 cm. How far is the chord from the centre? *[3]* [Diagram: OM ⊥ AB at M; OA = 13 cm; AB = 24 cm.]

**11.** A circle has a radius of 10 cm. Chord AB is 6 cm from the centre. How long is the chord? *[3]* [Diagram: OM ⊥ AB, OM = 6 cm, OB = 10 cm.]

**12.** Two chords in a circle with a radius of 17 cm are each 30 cm long. How far is each chord from the centre? Name the chord property that lets you answer for both chords at once. *[3]*

**13.** In the diagram, ∠ACB = 38°. [Diagram: A, B, C, D on the circle; chords from C and from D to A and B; radii OA and OB.]
(a) Find ∠AOB. *[1]*
(b) Find ∠ADB. *[1]*
(c) Name the property you used in (b). *[1]*

**14.** AB is a diameter and C is on the circle. ∠CAB = 32°. Find ∠ABC. Explain each step. *[3]*

**15.** A student wrote: "The inscribed angle is 50°, so the central angle on the same arc is 25°." Explain the mistake and give the correct central angle. *[3]*

**16.** PT is tangent to the circle at T. The radius is 8 cm and OP = 17 cm. Find the length of PT. *[3]*

**17.** PT is tangent to the circle at T, and ∠TOP = 52°. Find ∠OPT. Explain each step. *[3]*

**18.** PA and PB are tangent to the circle at A and B. PA = 11 cm and the radius is 6 cm.
(a) How long is PB? *[1]*
(b) What is the size of ∠OAP? *[1]*
(c) Find the perimeter of the shape OAPB. *[1]*

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working and name each property you use. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** A circular skating pond in an Edmonton park has a radius of 25 m. [Diagram: centre O, rope AB, bench P outside the pond, path PT touching the pond at T.]

(a) A straight rope barrier, AB, is a chord 48 m long. How far is the rope from the centre of the pond? *[2]*
(b) A second rope, also 48 m long, is put across the pond somewhere else. How far is it from the centre? Explain how you know without calculating again. *[2]*
(c) A bench at P is 65 m from the centre. A path goes from P and just touches the edge of the pond at T. How long is the path PT? *[2]*
(d) Two lifeguard chairs, L and M, are on the edge of the pond. The central angle ∠LOM is 100°. A photographer stands at Q on the edge, on the far side, and fits both chairs exactly in the frame. What angle, ∠LQM, does the camera need to take in? *[2]*

Is Maria correct? Use the inscribed angle theorem and at least one example to explain when an inscribed angle is 90° and when it is not.
