## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** Which of these is **bivariate** data?
A) hours studied and test mark for 30 students &nbsp; B) the heights of 30 students &nbsp; C) favourite colours of 30 students &nbsp; D) the number of pets in 30 homes

**2.** Which graph is best for seeing whether two numerical variables are related?
A) bar graph &nbsp; B) circle graph &nbsp; C) pictograph &nbsp; D) scatter plot

**3.** On a scatter plot, as x increases, y tends to decrease. The correlation is
A) positive &nbsp; B) negative &nbsp; C) none &nbsp; D) perfect

**4.** The points on a scatter plot lie close to a straight line. The correlation is
A) zero &nbsp; B) weak &nbsp; C) strong &nbsp; D) proof of cause

**5.** A good line of best fit
A) goes through as many points as possible &nbsp; B) has about the same number of points above and below it &nbsp; C) always goes through the origin &nbsp; D) joins the first and last points

**6.** A line of best fit has the equation y = 2x + 5. The predicted value of y when x = 10 is
A) 70 &nbsp; B) 20 &nbsp; C) 15 &nbsp; D) 25

**7.** A data set has x-values from 10 to 50. Using the line of best fit to predict y when x = 65 is
A) extrapolation &nbsp; B) interpolation &nbsp; C) correlation &nbsp; D) an outlier

**8.** A strong correlation between two variables does **not** prove
A) a relationship in the data &nbsp; B) a trend &nbsp; C) causation &nbsp; D) that a line of best fit can be drawn

## Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

*Show your working. [3 marks each] /30*

**9.** Is a scatter plot a good choice for each data set? Answer yes or no, with a reason. *[1 each]*
(a) the number of students in each grade at a school
(b) the arm span and height of 20 students
(c) the daily high temperature and the number of jackets sold at a store

**10.** A study compares hours of screen time per day and hours of sleep for 25 students.
(a) Which is the independent variable? *[1]*
(b) Which axis does it go on? *[1]*
(c) What does one point on the scatter plot stand for? *[1]*

**11.** The scatter plot shows the distance a truck has driven since filling up and the fuel left in the tank. [Scatter plot: distance 0–500 km, fuel 0–60 L, nine points falling steadily.]
(a) What is the direction of the correlation? *[1]*
(b) What is the strength? *[1]*
(c) Describe the relationship in a sentence that uses the context. *[1]*

**12.** In Alberta towns, the number of coffee shops and the number of dentists have a strong positive correlation. Does that mean coffee shops cause more dentists? Explain, and name a third variable that could explain the pattern. *[3]*

**13.** Two students draw a line of best fit for the same 10 points. Ana's line passes through 4 of the points, with the other 6 all below it. Ben's line passes through 1 point, with 5 points above and 4 below. Whose line is better? Explain using the rules for a line of best fit. *[3]*

**14.** The scatter plot shows hours of practice and free throws made out of 20. Draw a line of best fit, then find its equation. *[3]* [Scatter plot: (1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 7), (4, 9), (5, 10), (6, 13), (7, 14), (8, 15).]

**15.** A coach uses the line of best fit y = 1.5x + 3, where x is hours of practice and y is free throws made out of 20.
(a) Predict the free throws made after 5.5 hours of practice. *[1]*
(b) What does the slope, 1.5, mean here? *[1]*
(c) What does the y-intercept, 3, mean here? *[1]*

**16.** The line of best fit for the number of electric cars registered in a town is E = 40t + 100, where t is the number of years after 2015. The data covers 2015 to 2023.
(a) Predict the number for 2020. Is this interpolation or extrapolation? *[1]*
(b) Predict the number for 2040. Is this interpolation or extrapolation? *[1]*
(c) Which prediction is more reliable? Why? *[1]*

**17.** A student measured a sunflower for 30 days and found the line of best fit h = 9d + 5, where h is the height in cm after d days.
(a) Use the line to predict the height after 200 days. *[1]*
(b) Is this prediction reasonable? Explain why this kind of prediction can go wrong. *[2]*

**18.** A line of best fit passes through (10, 35) and (30, 75). Find its equation. *[3]*

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** A Lethbridge outdoor pool recorded the daily high temperature and the number of visitors on 10 days.

| Temperature (°C) | 18 | 20 | 21 | 23 | 24 | 26 | 27 | 29 | 30 | 32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visitors | 110 | 135 | 130 | 160 | 170 | 195 | 190 | 220 | 235 | 250 |

(a) Plot the data on the grid. Label both axes. *[2]* [Grid: temperature 15–35 °C, visitors 80–280.]
(b) Describe the correlation: direction, strength, and what it means here. *[2]*
(c) Draw a line of best fit and find its equation. *[2]*
(d) Use your equation to predict the number of visitors at 25 °C and at 40 °C. Which prediction is more reliable? Explain. *[2]*

Is the headline's conclusion justified? Explain the difference between correlation and causation, suggest one other variable that could explain the pattern, and describe what kind of study could test the claim.
