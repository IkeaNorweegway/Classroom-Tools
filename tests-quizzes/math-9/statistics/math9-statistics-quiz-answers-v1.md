# Statistics and Scatter Plots — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Positive** trend: as x increases, y increases. (2 marks)
2. **Negative** trend: as x increases, y decreases. (2 marks)
3. Close to the points, with about as many points above as below, following the overall trend. (2 marks)
4. y = 2(10) + 1 = **21**. (2 marks)
5. **30.** (2 marks)
6. Correct it if the true value can be found; otherwise remove it and say so. A typing error is not real data. (2 marks: action, reason)
7. Both rise in summer because of a third factor: hot weather makes people both buy more ice cream and swim more. Correlation does not show cause. (2 marks: third factor, correlation not cause)
8. Any reasonable example, e.g. shoe size and reading level in children (a third factor, age, links them). (2 marks)

**Common errors to watch for:** calling any trend "strong" without evidence (Q1, Q2); removing an outlier without a reason (Q6); "correlation proves causation" (Q7).
