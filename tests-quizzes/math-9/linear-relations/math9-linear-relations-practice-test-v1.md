## Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

*Circle the best answer. [1 mark each] /8*

**1.** Each table has x = 0, 1, 2, 3. Which y-values show a **linear** relation?
A) 1, 2, 4, 8 &nbsp; B) 2, 5, 8, 11 &nbsp; C) 0, 1, 4, 9 &nbsp; D) 5, 4, 2, −1

**2.** "The cost of gas depends on the number of litres you buy." The independent variable is
A) the cost &nbsp; B) the number of litres &nbsp; C) the price per litre &nbsp; D) the gas station

**3.** The slope of the line through (2, 1) and (6, 9) is
A) 2 &nbsp; B) 1/2 &nbsp; C) 8 &nbsp; D) 4

**4.** A horizontal line has a slope that is
A) positive &nbsp; B) negative &nbsp; C) zero &nbsp; D) undefined

**5.** In y = −4x + 7, the y-intercept is
A) −4 &nbsp; B) 4 &nbsp; C) 7 &nbsp; D) 7/4

**6.** Which equation has a slope of 3 and a y-intercept of −2?
A) y = −2x + 3 &nbsp; B) y = 3x − 2 &nbsp; C) y = 3x + 2 &nbsp; D) y = −3x − 2

**7.** Which equation shows **direct** variation?
A) y = 5x &nbsp; B) y = 5x + 1 &nbsp; C) y = x + 5 &nbsp; D) y = 5

**8.** The graph of a partial variation
A) passes through (0, 0) &nbsp; B) crosses the y-axis somewhere other than (0, 0) &nbsp; C) is always horizontal &nbsp; D) is always a curve

## Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

*Show your working. [3 marks each] /30*

**9.** (a) Complete the table for y = −3x + 4. *[2]*

| x | −1 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | | | | |

(b) Describe the pattern in the y-values. *[1]*

**10.** Graph y = 2x − 1. Make a table of at least three points first. *[3]* [Grid: x −3 to 4, y −6 to 7.]

**11.** Find the slope of the line on the grid. Show the rise and the run. *[3]* [Grid: x −3 to 5, y −6 to 5; line through the marked points (−2, 4) and (4, −5).]

**12.** A hiker near Canmore walks down a mountain trail at a steady rate. At 10:00 a.m. she is at an elevation of 2400 m. At 1:00 p.m. she is at 1800 m.
(a) Find the slope (rate of change), with units. *[2]*
(b) What does the sign of the slope tell you? *[1]*

**13.** Kim found the slope of the line through (1, 3) and (5, 11) like this: (5 − 1)/(11 − 3) = 4/8 = 1/2.
What did Kim do wrong? Find the correct slope. *[3]*

**14.** A line passes through (0, −3) and (2, 5). Write its equation in the form y = mx + b. *[3]*

**15.** For the line y = 2x − 8:
(a) State the y-intercept as a point. *[1]*
(b) Find the x-intercept as a point. *[1]*
(c) Jo says the y-intercept is 4, "because that's where the line hits zero." Explain Jo's mistake. *[1]*

**16.** A line has a slope of −2 and passes through (3, 1). Find b, then write the equation of the line. *[3]*

**17.** Here are two tables.

| x | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Table P: y | 0 | 4 | 8 | 12 |
| Table Q: y | 3 | 7 | 11 | 15 |

(a) Which table shows direct variation, and which shows partial variation? How can you tell? *[1]*
(b) Write an equation for each table. *[2]*

**18.** The graph shows the cost of renting a canoe at a lake in Banff. [Grid: hours 0 to 5, cost 0 to $80 by $10; line from (0, 20) to (5, 70).]
(a) What is the y-intercept, and what does it mean here? *[1]*
(b) What is the slope, and what does it mean here? *[1]*
(c) Is this direct or partial variation? Explain. *[1]*

## Part C — Application (Extended Response)

*Show all working. [8 marks] /8*

**19.** Two Calgary shops rent snowboards.
- **Shop A:** $30 per day.
- **Shop B:** a $40 one-time fee plus $20 per day.

(a) Write an equation for the cost, C, of renting for d days at each shop. *[2]*
(b) Make a table of the cost at each shop for 0 to 5 days. *[2]*
(c) Graph both relations on the same grid. Label each line. *[2]* [Grid: days 0 to 6, cost 0 to $200 by $20.]
(d) After how many days do the two shops cost the same? Which shop is cheaper for a 6-day trip? Use your work to explain. *[2]*
