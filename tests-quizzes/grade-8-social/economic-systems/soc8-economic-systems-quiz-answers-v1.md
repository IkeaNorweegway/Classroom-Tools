# Economic Systems — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Having limited resources for unlimited wants.** (1 mark)
2. What to produce; how to produce it; for whom to produce it. (2 marks: one each)
3. **A command economy.** (1 mark)
4. In a market economy, prices and private choice decide what is produced; in a mixed economy, markets operate alongside significant government involvement. (2 marks: one each)
5. Any two: taxes (income, sales, property), fees, resource royalties. (2 marks: one each)
6. Governments fund services such as health care, education and roads, which benefit citizens; spending on these supports people's well-being. (3 marks: spending, service, benefit)
7. **A deficit.** (1 mark)
8. A deficit is the shortfall in one year (spending exceeds revenue that year). Debt is the total of all past deficits that remains unpaid. (4 marks: deficit defined, debt defined, the link, example)

**Common errors to watch for:** "deficit and debt are the same" (Q8); "a command economy means no government" (Q3).
