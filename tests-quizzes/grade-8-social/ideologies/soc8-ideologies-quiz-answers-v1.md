# Foundations and Influences of Ideologies — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. **Putting individual rights and choices first.** (1 mark)
2. Individualism puts the individual's rights and choices first; collectivism puts the needs of the group first. (2 marks: one each side)
3. **Left.** (1 mark)
4. Liberalism is usually placed toward the centre-left (more faith in individual choice with some government role); conservatism toward the right (more emphasis on tradition and limited change). Any reasonable reasons. (2 marks)
5. Socialism: an economic system in which the community or state owns or controls major industries, to share wealth more equally. (2 marks)
6. Communism aims for a classless society in which the state owns all means of production; socialism usually keeps private property and uses government to redistribute wealth. (3 marks: communism, socialism, the difference)
7. Any one: total control of the government, one party, censorship, secret police, no free elections. (1 mark)
8. An extreme ideology gives the state the right to decide every area of life; opposition is punished, the press is censored and the state controls the economy and the people. (4 marks: a mechanism, an example, the result, conclusion)

**Common errors to watch for:** "ideology and political party are the same" (Q1–Q4); "communism and socialism are interchangeable" (Q6).
