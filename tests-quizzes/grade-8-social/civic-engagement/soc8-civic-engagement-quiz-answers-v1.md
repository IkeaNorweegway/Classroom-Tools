# Civic Engagement — Mid-Unit Quiz — Answer Key
Teacher use only · Keep off student copies

1. Any one: volunteering, joining a group, petitioning, contacting a representative, attending a council meeting. (1 mark)
2. Any reasonable answer, e.g. joining a community clean-up, writing to a city councillor, organizing a school petition. (2 marks)
3. **Canadian citizens aged 18 or older.** (1 mark)
4. Any one requirement: Canadian citizenship, age 18 or older on election day, and being on the voters list. (2 marks)
5. Drought can cut crop supplies, raising food prices in Canada or affecting trade. (2 marks)
6. War can disrupt trade routes and energy supplies, so the costs of goods and fuel rise for Canadians. (3 marks: disruption, supply, price)
7. Any reasonable issue and step. (1 mark)
8. A strong answer names a clear action, a partner, and a measure of success. (4 marks: action, partner, measure, reasoning)

**Common errors to watch for:** "anyone who lives in Canada can vote" (Q3); "global issues don't affect Canadians" (Q5).
