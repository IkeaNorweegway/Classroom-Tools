Creating Canada's Government — Exit Tickets

Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

Unit Learning Outcome (LO): Students examine how the Quebec Conference, London Conference, and the British North America Act (1867) led to the creation of Canada.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (CG1–CG5, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.

Exit Ticket 1 — Designing a Country

LO: CG1, sequencing the events that led to Confederation.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Put the three conferences in order. (1 mark)

2. What was decided at the Quebec Conference? (2 marks)

3. Why did the plan for Canada have to be passed by Britain's Parliament? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — The Ideas Behind the Plan

LO: CG1, the democratic features the BNA Act set up.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What is a riding? (1 mark)

2. Explain representation by population in your own words. (2 marks)

3. Under responsible government, what happens if the government loses an important vote in the House of Commons? Why is that important? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — The Three Branches

LO: CG4, the roles of the executive, legislative and judicial branches.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the three branches. (1 mark)

2. Match each to a branch: Parliament, the courts, the Prime Minister and Cabinet. (2 marks)

3. Why split government into three branches? (2 marks)

LO: CG4, how the legislative and executive branches are organized.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

2. How is a senator chosen? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 5 — Federalism

LO: CG2 and CG3, the division of powers.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Federal or provincial: schools? the army? (1 mark)

2. What is federalism? (2 marks)

3. Give one benefit and one challenge of dividing power between the two levels of government. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 6 — The Courts

LO: CG5, why a national court system and a general court of appeal matter.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

2. Explain why a country needs one top court of appeal. (2 marks)
