One part I'm still not sure about:
🔑 Word to know
Photosynthesis — how a plant makes its own food.
🔑 Word to know
Chlorophyll — the green colour in leaves. It captures light energy.
🔑 Word to know
Carbon dioxide — a gas in the air. The plant takes it in through its leaves.
🔑 Word to know
Glucose — the sugar (food) the plant makes.
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think a plant gets its food from the soil. A plant makes its own food using light, water, and carbon dioxide. Soil gives the plant water and minerals — not food.
