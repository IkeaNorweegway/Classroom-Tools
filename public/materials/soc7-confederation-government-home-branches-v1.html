The Three Branches

One skill: matching government jobs to the executive, legislative and judicial branches · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: The BNA Act (1867) split Canada's government into three branches: one makes laws (Parliament), one carries them out (the Prime Minister and Cabinet), and one interprets them (the courts).

Worked example

Worked example

Which branch is at work? "Parliament votes to pass a new law about bike helmets."

  1. What is the job? Making a law.
  2. Who makes laws? Parliament: the House of Commons and the Senate.
  3. So it is the legislative branch.
