Armed Forces and Sovereignty — Exit Tickets

Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

Unit Learning Outcome (LO): Students relate the development of armed forces in Canada to sovereignty.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (AF1–AF6, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.

Exit Ticket 1 — Policing the West

LO: AF1, the evolution of national police forces.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In what year was the NWMP created? (1 mark)

2. Name one reason the NWMP was sent west. (2 marks)

3. How did the NWMP change between 1873 and 1920? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Building a Military

LO: AF2, how Canada's military changed after Confederation.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. When was the Royal Canadian Navy created? (1 mark)

2. What happened in 1871, and why did it matter? (2 marks)

3. Explain how building its own military gave Canada more control over its own affairs. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — The Boer War

LO: AF3, the significance of Canada's role in the Boer War.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the battle where Canadians won fame in 1900. (1 mark)

2. Why did Canadians disagree about the Boer War? (2 marks)

3. Why was the Boer War significant for Canada? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 5 — Who Served, and How We Remember

LO: AF4 and AF5, diverse contributions and honouring sacrifice.
