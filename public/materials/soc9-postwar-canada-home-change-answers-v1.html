What Changed After the War — Answers

One skill: describing post-war changes in Canada, good and harmful · about 15 minutes

For the adult: Residential schools are a painful topic. It’s fine to talk about it gently and listen.

1. government programs that protect people from hardship, like health care, pensions and unemployment insurance

2. peacekeeping · Suez

3. NATO (iii) · NORAD (ii) · Korean War (iv) · peacekeeping (i)

4. More people came from outside Europe, from places like India, China, the Caribbean and Vietnam, and Canada adopted a multiculturalism policy.

5. Children were taken from their families and often punished for speaking their languages. Many suffered abuse, and the loss of family, language and culture has affected generations since.
