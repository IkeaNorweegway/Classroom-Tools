Building Canada's Economy — Exit Tickets

Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

Unit Learning Outcome (LO): Students examine the Canadian government's role in developing the economy post-Confederation.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (BE1–BE5, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.

Exit Ticket 1 — Expansion and Regional Economies

LO: BE1, how expansion west and natural resources shaped the economy.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What was the main crop grown on the Prairies? (1 mark)

2. Name two regions of Canada and one resource or industry for each. (2 marks)

3. Why do different regions build different economies? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — The National Policy

LO: BE2, consequences of the National Policy.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What is a tariff? (1 mark)

2. Name the three parts of the National Policy. (2 marks)

3. Which region gained the most from the National Policy, and which paid more? Explain. (3 marks)

LO: BE3, opportunities and challenges of tariffs and free trade.
