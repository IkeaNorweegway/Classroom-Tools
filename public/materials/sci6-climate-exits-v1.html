Grade 6 Science — Climate: Exit Tickets

Grade 6 Science · Alberta Curriculum

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Weather and climate

LO 1: I can explain the difference between weather and climate.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Is "it snowed yesterday" a statement about weather or climate? (1 mark)

2. Explain the difference between weather and climate in one sentence each. (2 marks)

3. Why does a single cold week not tell us that the climate is getting colder? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — The greenhouse effect

LO 2: I can explain the greenhouse effect.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name two greenhouse gases. (1 mark)

2. Describe what happens to sunlight as it reaches Earth and is reflected back out. (2 marks)

3. Explain why a greenhouse keeps plants warmer than the air outside. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Causes of climate change

LO 3: I can describe natural and human causes of climate change.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Give one natural cause and one human cause of climate change. (2 marks)

2. How does burning fossil fuels for electricity add to the greenhouse effect? (2 marks)

3. Why do scientists say that human causes are changing the climate faster than natural causes alone? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Climate change in Alberta

LO 4: I can describe how climate change affects Alberta.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one way climate change could affect Alberta's farms. (1 mark)

2. Describe one effect of a warmer, drier summer on a community near a river. (2 marks)

3. Why might a change in Alberta's snowpack matter for people far from the mountains? (3 marks)
