The Greenhouse Effect — Answers

One skill: explaining the greenhouse effect and climate change · about 15 minutes

For the adult: Keep it hopeful: lots of people and communities are working on solutions.

1. carbon dioxide (or methane, water vapour) · heat

2. too cold for most life

3. Any two: burning gasoline in cars, burning coal or natural gas for electricity and heat, cutting down forests.

4. The greenhouse effect is about trapping heat. The ozone hole is a thin spot in the ozone layer that lets in more harmful UV light.

5. Any two: shrinking glaciers, more wildfires and smoke, droughts, changing seasons.

6. Examples: walk, bike or carpool, combine errands, turn the heat down a little, use less hot water.
