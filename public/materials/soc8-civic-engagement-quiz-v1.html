4. [LO 2] Explain one requirement a person must meet to vote in Canada. (2 marks)

5. [LO 3] Explain how a drought in another country could affect Canadians. (2 marks)

6. [LO 3] Explain how a war in one part of the world can affect the prices Canadians pay. (3 marks)

7. [LO 4] Name one issue you care about and one step you could take to act on it. (1 mark)

8. [LO 4] Plan one way to take action on an issue you care about. Include what you would do, who you would work with, and how you would know it worked. (4 marks)
