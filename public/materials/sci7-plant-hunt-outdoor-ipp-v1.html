🔑 Word to know
Shoot system: the parts of a plant above the ground.
🔑 Word to know
Root system: the parts of a plant below the ground.
💡 Remember
You cannot see roots. They are under the ground. Draw the roots you think are there.

ReadHow a plant makes food

A plant makes its own food. This is called photosynthesis. It happens in the green leaves.

The leaf takes in light, water, and carbon dioxide. The leaf makes food and oxygen.

🔑 Word to know
Photosynthesis: how a plant makes its own food.
light + water + carbon dioxide → food (glucose) + oxygen
Where does a plant make its food? (circle one)
rootssoilleaves
⚠️ Watch out
Many people think plants get food from soil. Plants make their own food in their leaves.
