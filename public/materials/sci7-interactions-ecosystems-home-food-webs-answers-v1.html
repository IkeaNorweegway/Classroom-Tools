Food Chains and Food Webs — Answers

One skill: following energy through food chains and webs · about 15 minutes

For the adult: Food chains can be drawn in different directions on the page. What matters is that the arrows point toward the eater.

1. Producer algae, primary consumer minnow, secondary consumer pike

2. Arrows point from the food to the eater.

  • Correct: rabbit → fox

3. grass → grasshopper → snake → hawk

4. plants → snails, plants → ducks, snails → ducks

  • Ducks are both primary and secondary consumers.

5. Primary consumer: 10% of 5000 = 500

  • Secondary consumer: 10% of 500 = 50 units

6. They break down dead plants and animals and return nutrients to the soil.

  • Plants need those nutrients to grow, so without decomposers the ecosystem would run out.
