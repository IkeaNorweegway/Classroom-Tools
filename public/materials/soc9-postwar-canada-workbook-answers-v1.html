Part B — Extended Questions

Q7
Sample response Like the company scenario, the National Energy Program favoured the federal government and central Canadian consumers. It reduced revenue and control for Alberta's oil economy, which fed directly into Western alienation. I think it can be fair for a policy to affect one region more, if the affected region has a real voice in shaping it. The core unfairness in the NEP wasn't that Alberta was affected — it was that Alberta felt shut out of the decision.
Q8
Sample response I would argue these cannot be judged separately. Canada built a strong global reputation through NATO, the Korean War, and Pearson's peacekeeping work. At the same time, residential schools and the Sixties Scoop were separating Indigenous children from their families. A country's reputation should reflect its full record, not just its most flattering chapters.
Q9
Sample response One example from this unit is limiting hate speech, such as racism or antisemitism, even though this restricts freedom of expression. I think this strikes a fair balance, because it protects a group from real, direct harm rather than just an inconvenience.
