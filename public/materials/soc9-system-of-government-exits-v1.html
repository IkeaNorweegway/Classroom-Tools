Canada's System of Government — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Responsible government

LO 1: I can explain responsible government.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Who must a government have the confidence of in a responsible government? (1 mark)

2. What happens to a government that loses the confidence of the House of Commons? (2 marks)

3. Why does responsible government mean the government answers to elected representatives? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — How a bill becomes law

LO 2: I can describe the steps for a bill to become law.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the two houses that must pass a bill in the federal Parliament. (1 mark)

2. What must happen to a bill after both houses pass it? (2 marks)

3. Put these steps in order: royal assent, first reading, third reading, committee study. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Federal and provincial powers

LO 3: I can explain which powers belong to the federal government and which to the provinces.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one power that belongs to the federal government. (1 mark)

2. Name one power that belongs to the provinces. (2 marks)

3. Why does Canada divide power between two levels of government? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Courts and rights

LO 4: I can explain how courts protect rights under the Charter.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What document sets out the rights that courts protect in Canada? (1 mark)

2. What can a court do if a law breaks a right in the Charter? (2 marks)

3. Why is it important that courts, not only Parliament, can check laws against the Charter? (3 marks)
