Writing Algorithms — Answers

One skill: writing exact steps with sequence, loops and conditionals · about 15 minutes

For the adult: There are many right answers. The test is whether someone could follow the steps exactly without guessing.

1. a list of exact steps in order · repeats steps · makes a choice: if something is true, do this

2. Example: walk 5 steps forward, turn left, walk 3 steps, stop at the door.

3. Example: pick up toothbrush, put toothpaste on it, wet it, brush top teeth, brush bottom teeth, spit, rinse brush, put it away.

4. repeat 4 times: forward, then turn left

5. If the robot sees a wall (or gets too close), then turn; otherwise keep going.

6. Examples: microwave (count down, then beep), washing machine (fill, wash, rinse, spin), traffic light.
