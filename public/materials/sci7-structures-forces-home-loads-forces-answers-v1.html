Loads and Internal Forces — Answers

One skill: naming loads and the forces inside materials · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 2: Loads · Section 3: Internal Forces

For the adult: A dynamic load changes or moves, like wind, an earthquake or traffic.

1. squeezes · stretches · slides one part past another · twists

2. Dead load: the structure’s own weight. Live load: what it carries, like people or cars.

3. (a) tension (b) compression (c) torsion (d) shear

4. Dead: the bridge’s own weight. Live: cars and people. Dynamic: wind, an earthquake or moving traffic.

5. Its centre of gravity is higher and its base is narrower, so a small push moves the centre of gravity past the edge of the base.

6. The ramp spreads the work over a longer distance, so you need a smaller force.

  • You do about the same work either way. A simple machine doesn’t make energy.
