The hook

Every spring, volunteers in many Canadian communities run free tax clinics to help people file their taxes. Some people miss out on refunds and benefits because they never file.

  • Food-delivery gig (self-employed), after expenses: $2,500.
  • Tuition paid (T2202 form): $4,000.

How you will be marked

Outcome 9-IT: I can explain how income tax, deductions, registered accounts and benefits affect my finances.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Reads the T4 accurately and identifies all reportable income, including tips and self-employment.
Understanding (U): Explains why credits, deductions and the choice of RRSP or TFSA change the client's financial position.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Calculates tax owed, the effect of credits and an RRSP deduction, self-employed CPP, and the refund or balance accurately, in a workable order.
Communication (S&P): Explains the return and advice so the client can follow it in plain words.

Hand-in checklist (not marked): ☐ I did my own math before comparing ☐ I did my team job each class ☐ We made at least 2 changes after feedback
