Step 7Pitch panel

Your audience

Pitch to a panel of staff or community members (like a friendly "Dragons' Den"). You have 4 minutes. They ask questions and tell you what's strong and what to fix. If your venture is realistic, your teacher may help you try it for real.

Questions the panel might ask. Plan your answers.

QuestionOur answer

Outcome 9-EX: I can connect experiences and employment opportunities to career pathways.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes volunteering, work-integrated learning and entrepreneurship, and tells formal, informal, job-specific and employability skills apart.
Understanding (U): Explains how the venture's experience connects to members' career pathways and the skills it builds.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Plans the venture's costs, price, break-even and risks accurately, and prepares a cover letter and digital identity check.
Communication (S&P): Pitches the venture so the panel can follow the idea, the numbers and the career connections.
