The hook

Two friends each save $1,000.

Ana starts at age 15 and leaves it to grow at 6% a year until she's 65.
Ben waits until he's 25, then does the same until 65.

How you will be marked

Outcome 8-MM: I can use costs, interest and savings choices to plan for financial security.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Uses fixed cost, variable cost, principal, rate, interest, saving, investing and risk accurately.
Understanding (U): Explains why managing costs and choosing between saving and investing affect financial security, linking risk to each goal's timeline.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Builds a budget that separates fixed and variable costs, and calculates simple and compound interest accurately.
Communication (S&P): Presents the plan so a visitor can follow the budget, the growth, and where each goal's money goes.

Hand-in checklist (not marked): ☐ My budget adds up ☐ I did my team job each class ☐ I made at least 2 changes after feedback
