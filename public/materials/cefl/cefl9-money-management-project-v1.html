The case

Use Jordan's case, or a case card from your teacher.

The situation

Jordan, 22 (sample)

  • Earns $3,000 a month before deductions.
  • Credit card: $2,400 owing at 20.99%, minimum $60. Credit limit $2,500.
  • Phone financing: $600 owing at 0%, minimum $50.
  • Line of credit: $3,000 owing at 9.5%, minimum $60.
  • Has been late on the credit card twice this year.
  • Can put $400 a month toward debt.
  • Needs $300 for a car repair next week and is thinking about a payday loan.

Outcome 9-MM: I can analyze how debt and credit work and plan to borrow responsibly.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes the kinds of credit, the credit score factors, and what makes a loan predatory, accurately.
Understanding (U): Explains how compound interest and minimum payments make debt grow, and how responsible borrowing protects financial security.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Calculates interest, credit utilization and debt-to-income ratio accurately, and compares repayment strategies and borrowing options to build a plan.
Communication (S&P): Presents the case and advice so a Grade 8 student can follow the cost of debt and the plan.
