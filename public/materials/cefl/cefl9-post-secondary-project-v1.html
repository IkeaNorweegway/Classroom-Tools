Driving question

How do I know if a post-secondary pathway is worth the cost?

The hook

Two friends, one career goal: working with computers.

Alex takes a 4-year university degree and borrows $30,000.
Riley takes a 2-year polytechnic diploma with a paid co-op term and borrows $6,000.

Five years after high school, who is better off? What would you need to know to decide?

Step 7Pitch it

Your audience

Pitch your plan in 3 minutes to a panel: the counsellor, a teacher, or family volunteers. They ask questions like a family deciding whether to help pay. They tell you whether you convinced them.

Outcome 9-PS: I can evaluate post-secondary options by weighing personal goals, financial costs and labour market outcomes.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

Skills & Procedures (S&P): Calculates true cost and payback using labour market income data, and builds a plan with a primary pathway, a contingency and a rationale.
