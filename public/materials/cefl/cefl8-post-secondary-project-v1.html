How you will be marked

Outcome 8-PS: I can use program requirements, costs and job information to set realistic education and career goals.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes the admission requirements and costs of both programs accurately, and tells scholarships, bursaries, grants and loans apart.
Understanding (U): Explains how early course choices and competitive admission affect which programs stay open.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Builds a Grade 10–12 course plan that meets the diploma and both programs, and compares their costs with ways to pay.
Communication (S&P): Presents the plan so a counsellor can follow the courses, the requirements they meet, and the costs.

Hand-in checklist (not marked): ☐ I did my team job each class ☐ I talked to someone about a program ☐ I made at least 2 changes after feedback
