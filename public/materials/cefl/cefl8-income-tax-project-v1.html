How you will be marked

Outcome 8-IT: I can analyze income tax and total compensation to compare job offers and explain the role taxes play in society.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes tax brackets and progressive, flat and regressive taxes accurately.
Understanding (U): Explains why total compensation and taxes matter when comparing offers, and how taxes fund shared services.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Calculates income tax across brackets and total compensation for both offers accurately, in a workable order.
Communication (S&P): Presents the recommendation so Sam can follow the numbers, the non-money factors and the trade-off.

Hand-in checklist (not marked): ☐ I did my own tax math before comparing ☐ I did my team job each class ☐ We made at least 2 changes after feedback
