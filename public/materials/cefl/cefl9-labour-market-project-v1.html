How you will be marked

Outcome 9-LM: I can take steps to succeed in a changing labour market.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes how automation and globalization affect the chosen job, and classifies its industry using labour market data with a source.
Understanding (U): Explains why knowing where the labour market is moving, and communicating your human capital, help you succeed.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Writes a résumé and brand statement tailored to the posting, matching evidence to its requirements.
Communication (S&P): Answers interview questions with clear, specific examples and advocates for own needs politely.
