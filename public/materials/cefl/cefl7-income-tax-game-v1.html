Payday: Where Does the Paycheque Go?

I can work out where a paycheque goes, from gross pay to take-home pay, and explain why.

This is a model. The pay rules are the real 2026 rates from your Pay Card. The workers, jobs and businesses are made up. Bills are counted every two weeks to keep the math simple.
