Outcome 8-LM: I can explain how a changing labour market shapes the skills and goals I need for career resilience.

Your teacher gives each row a level: 4 Excellent · 3 Proficient · 2 Meets · 1 Has yet to meet. Meets means you met the outcome.

✗ Has yet to meet: what's missing✓ Meets (the outcome)★ Beyond: Proficient or Excellent
Knowledge (K): Describes how economic cycles, technology and social change affect the labour market, using the terms career resilience and human capital accurately.
Understanding (U): Explains why adaptability and career security protect a career better than job security alone, linking it to the chosen career.
Skills & Procedures (S&P): Builds a career plan with short- and long-term milestones and a backup, and ranks transferable skills with reasons.
Communication (S&P): Presents the plan so a Grade 7 student can follow how change could affect the career and how the plan prepares for it.
