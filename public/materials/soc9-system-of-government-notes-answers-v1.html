Section 2: How Laws Are Made

Legislative process, in order: 1. Bill drafting 2. First reading 3. Second reading 4. Committee stage 5. Third reading 6. Approval by the Senate 7. Royal assent by the Crown.

Retrieval Check answers:

  1. Bill drafting → first reading → second reading → committee stage → third reading → Senate approval → royal assent.
  2. Orders in council, regulations and directives, or ministerial orders/decisions (any one).

Section 3: Federalism and Natural Resources

Federal GovernmentProvincial Government
Defence, Banking and Currency, Trade and CommerceEducation, Healthcare, Property and Civil Rights
Criminal Law, CitizenshipMunicipalities, Natural Resources

Guided notes answers: relies heavily on oil and gas; controls resource revenues such as royalties.

Connect-back answer: Because provinces, not the federal government, control natural resources, any federal policy touching resources (like the National Energy Program) is stepping into provincial territory — which almost guarantees conflict when their interests differ.

Retrieval Check answers:

  1. Federal: defence, banking, trade, criminal law, citizenship. Provincial: education, healthcare, property/civil rights, municipalities, natural resources (any three each).
  2. Alberta's economy relies heavily on oil and gas, and the province controls the revenue and decisions that come with it.

Retrieval Check answers:
