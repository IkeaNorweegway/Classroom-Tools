Building the Canadian State — Exit Tickets

Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

Unit Learning Outcome (LO): Students examine the deliberate acts of state building.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (SB1–SB7, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.

Exit Ticket 1 — British North America Before 1867

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB1, shifting political boundaries.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name two colonies or territories that were part of British North America in 1864. (1 mark)

2. How did Britain gain control of New France? (2 marks)

3. Why was Rupert's Land different from the colonies around it? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Why the Colonies Joined Together

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB2 and SB3, reasons for and goals of Confederation.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Put the three conferences in order. (1 mark)

2. Explain how the American Civil War helped convince leaders to support Confederation. (2 marks)

3. A classmate says Canada became fully independent in 1867. Are they right? Explain. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Expanding West

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB4, why and how Canada expanded west.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. From whom did Canada take over Rupert's Land? (1 mark)

2. Give two benefits Canada gained from taking over Rupert's Land. (2 marks)

3. Why is it wrong to call the West "empty land" in 1869? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — The Red River Conflict and the Northwest Resistance

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB5, the Red River Conflict and the government's response.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What did Louis Riel form at Red River in 1869? (1 mark)

2. Give one reason the Métis resisted in 1869. (2 marks)

3. How did the government respond in 1885, and how did the railway help? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 5 — Treaties and the Indian Act

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB6, how expansion changed control over land for First Nations.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. How many numbered treaties were signed, and in what years? (1 mark)

2. Name one thing the written treaties promised First Nations. (2 marks)

3. Explain why First Nations and the Crown sometimes understood the same treaty differently. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 6 — Filling the West and New Provinces

LO: Students examine the deliberate acts of state building — SB7, settlement and key government decisions.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In what year did Alberta become a province? (1 mark)

2. Name the three parts of the National Policy. (2 marks)

3. Explain how the CPR helped lead to Alberta becoming a province. (3 marks)
