Action and Reaction Pairs

One skill: finding the push-back force, and elastic versus plastic · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: Every push has an equal push back in the opposite direction, on the other object. Ask "What’s pushing back?"

Worked example

Worked example

A swimmer pushes off the pool wall. What are the forces?

  1. Action: the swimmer’s feet push the wall backward.
  2. Reaction: the wall pushes the swimmer forward, just as hard.
  3. The wall doesn’t move because it’s attached to the pool; the swimmer shoots forward.
