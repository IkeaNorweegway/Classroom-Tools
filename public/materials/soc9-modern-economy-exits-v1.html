Building a Modern Economy — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — The Great Depression

LO 1: I can explain what caused the Great Depression and how governments responded.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In what decade did the Great Depression begin? (1 mark)

2. Name one cause of the Great Depression. (2 marks)

3. Describe one way the government responded and say why it was needed. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Industries after the war

LO 2: I can describe the industries that grew after the war.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one industry that grew in Canada after the Second World War. (1 mark)

2. Why did manufacturing grow after the war? (2 marks)

3. How did the growth of one industry create jobs in another? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Trade with the United States

LO 3: I can explain why trade with the United States matters to Canada.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the country that is Canada's largest trading partner. (1 mark)

2. Why does Canada depend on trade with the United States? (2 marks)

3. Explain one risk to Canada's economy when trade with one country is so large. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Who gains and who loses

LO 4: I can weigh who gains and who loses from free trade.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What is free trade? (1 mark)

2. Name one group that might gain from free trade and one that might lose. (2 marks)

3. Weigh the benefits and costs of free trade for a small factory town. (3 marks)
