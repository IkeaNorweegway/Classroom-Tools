Guided Notes

A polynomial is an expression made of one or more terms, joined by addition or subtraction. Each term has a coefficient (the number in front) and a variable raised to a whole-number exponent.

Degree of a term = the exponent on the variable.

Misconception

"3x² has degree 3 because the coefficient is 3."

The degree tells you the power of the variable, not the coefficient. In 3x², the 3 is the coefficient and the 2 is the degree.

✓ 3x² has degree 2 and leading coefficient 3.

Section 2 — Like Terms

Learning target: I can identify like terms in a polynomial and combine them correctly.

Vocabulary

TermDefinition in your own wordsOne example
Like termsTerms with the same variable(s) raised to the same exponents3x² and −7x²

Guided Notes

Like terms share the same variable raised to the same exponent. Coefficients can differ — only the variable part must match.

To combine like terms: add or subtract the Yes — both constants only. The variable part stays the same.

Example
3x2 + 7x2 = x2
The x² part does not change. Only the coefficients are added.
Misconception

"3x² + 2x = 5x²"  or  "3x² + 2x = 5x³"

3x² and 2x have different powers. They represent different things — you cannot combine them any more than you can add 3 apples and 2 oranges and call the result 5 apples.

✓ 3x² + 2x stays as 3x² + 2x — it is already fully simplified.

Practice — Circle like terms, then simplify

1.   4x2    −2x2    3x    5x2

Answer Like: {4x², −2x², 5x²} and {3x}. Simplified: 7x² + 3x

2.   7x    −x    4    2x2

Answer Like: {7x, −x} and {4} and {2x²}. Simplified: 2x² + 6x + 4

3.   9 − 3x + 2x2 − 5 + x

Answer Constants: 9−5=4. x terms: −3x+x=−2x. x² terms: 2x². Simplified: 2x²−2x+4

Algebra tile connection

Section 4 — Subtracting Polynomials

Learning target: I can subtract polynomials by distributing the negative sign and combining like terms.

Vocabulary

TermDefinition in your own wordsOne example
DifferenceResult of subtracting two polynomials(5x²+3x)−(2x²+x) = 3x²+2x
Opposite (of a polynomial)
DistributeApply to every term inside brackets−(2x²−x) = −2x²+x

Guided Notes

Subtracting a polynomial means adding its opposite. To find the opposite, change the sign of every.

Opposite of (2x2 − 3x + 1) = −2x²+3x−1

Example — distribute the negative sign
(5x2 + 3x − 4) − (2x2 − x + 6)
= 5x2 + 3x − 4 2x2 x − 2x² + x − 6 6  ← distribute
= 3x²+4x−10  ← simplified
Misconception

"−(2x² − x + 6) = −2x² − x + 6"

The subtraction sign applies to every term in the bracket. Missing even one sign change gives a wrong answer for every subsequent step.

✓ −(2x² − x + 6) = −2x² + x − 6

Misconception

"x × x = 2x"

x + x = 2x (addition doubles the coefficient). But x × x means "x groups of x" — that is x², the area of a square with side x. Addition and multiplication are different operations.
