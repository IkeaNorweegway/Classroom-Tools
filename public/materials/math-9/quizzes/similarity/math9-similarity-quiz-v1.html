Similarity and Scale — Mid-Unit Quiz

Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

Name: _________________________ Date: _____________ Marks: /16

Show your working. Include units.

1. [LO 1] Rectangle A is 2 cm by 3 cm. Rectangle B is 4 cm by 6 cm. Are they similar? Explain. (2 marks)

2. [LO 2] A length of 3 cm on a drawing represents 9 cm in real life. Find the scale factor from the drawing to real life. (2 marks)

3. [LO 2] A triangle has sides 3, 4 and 5 cm. A similar triangle has a scale factor of 2. Find the length of its longest side. (2 marks)

4. [LO 3] Two similar triangles have bases of 2 cm and 6 cm. The smaller triangle is 3 cm tall. Find the height of the larger triangle. (2 marks)

5. [LO 3] Explain the steps you would take to find a missing side using similar triangles. (2 marks)

6. [LO 4] A 1.8 m tall student casts a 2.4 m shadow. At the same time, a tree casts a 12 m shadow. Find the height of the tree. (2 marks)

7. [LO 4] Describe how you could find the height of a flagpole you cannot reach, using its shadow and your own height. (2 marks)

8. [LO 1] Explain what makes two shapes similar. (2 marks)
