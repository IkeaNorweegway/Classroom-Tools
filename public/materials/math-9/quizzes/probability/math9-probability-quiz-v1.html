Probability — Mid-Unit Quiz

Grade 9 Mathematics · Alberta Curriculum

Name: _________________________ Date: _____________ Marks: /16

Give probabilities as fractions in simplest form.

1. [LO 1] A fair six-sided die is rolled. Find the probability of rolling a 4. (2 marks)

2. [LO 1] A card is drawn from a standard deck of 52 cards. Find the probability of drawing a red card. (2 marks)

3. [LO 2] A coin is flipped twice. Are the two flips independent or dependent? Explain. (2 marks)

4. [LO 2] A bag has 3 red and 1 blue marble. Two marbles are drawn without replacement. Find the probability that both are red. (2 marks)

5. [LO 3] A fair die is rolled twice. Find the probability of a 2 then a 5. (2 marks)

6. [LO 3] A fair coin is flipped twice. Find the probability of heads both times. (2 marks)

7. [LO 4] A fair coin is flipped 10 times and lands on heads 7 times. How many heads would you expect? Compare this with the result. (2 marks)

8. [LO 4] Explain why 10 flips may not match the expected result exactly. (2 marks)
