1. [LO 1] Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 2 cm and height 5 cm. Leave your answer in terms of π. (2 marks)

2. [LO 1] Find the surface area of a sphere with radius 3 cm. Leave your answer in terms of π. (2 marks)

3. [LO 2] Explain the difference between surface area and volume. (2 marks)

4. [LO 1] Find the volume of a cone with radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. Leave your answer in terms of π. (2 marks)

7. [LO 1] Find the volume of a square pyramid with a 4 cm by 4 cm base and a height of 6 cm. (2 marks)

8. [LO 2] A cone and a cylinder have the same base and height. Which has the greater volume, and by what fraction? (2 marks)
