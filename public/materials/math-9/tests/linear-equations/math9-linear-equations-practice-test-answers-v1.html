Part D — Thinking

20. Model answer: Ali is not correct. In x − 4 > −6 there are negative numbers, but I add 4 to both sides and get x > −2. No flip is needed. Check: x = 0 works (0 − 4 = −4, which is greater than −6). In −2x > 6 I divide both sides by −2, so I flip: x < −3. Check: x = −4 gives 8 > 6 ✓, but x = 0 gives 0 > 6 ✗, so the flip was needed. The sign flips only when you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, because that reverses the order of numbers: 2 < 3, but −2 > −3.
