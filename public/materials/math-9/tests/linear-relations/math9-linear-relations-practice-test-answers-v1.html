Part D — Thinking

20. Model answer: Jordan is not correct. Compare y = 2x and y = −5x. The line y = −5x is steeper (it drops 5 for every 1 across, while y = 2x only rises 2), but −5 is smaller than 2. Steepness depends on how big the slope is when you ignore its sign. The sign only tells you the direction: positive goes up to the right, negative goes down to the right. Jordan's rule only works when both slopes are positive: y = 5x is steeper than y = 2x, and 5 > 2.
