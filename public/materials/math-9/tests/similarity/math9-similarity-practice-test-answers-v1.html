Part D — Thinking

20. Model answer: Ryan is not correct. Similar polygons need equal angles and sides in the same ratio. Rectangles always have equal angles, so the angle condition is met, but the sides might not be in proportion. A 2 cm by 4 cm rectangle and a 2 cm by 8 cm rectangle: 2/2 = 1 but 8/4 = 2. The ratios are different, so they are not similar (one is long and thin). Some rectangles are similar, like 2 by 4 and 3 by 6 (both ratios 1.5), but not all of them.
