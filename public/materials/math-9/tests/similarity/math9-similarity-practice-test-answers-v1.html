Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. The solution of x8 = 15/24 is

A) x = 45B) x = 5C) x = 3D) x = 120
Answer B · Why: 24x = 120, so x = 5 · Common errors: A: multiplied across the wrong way. D: forgot to divide

2. A photo 4 cm wide is enlarged to 10 cm wide. The scale factor is

A) 2.5B) 0.4C) 6D) 40
Answer A · Why: New ÷ original = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 · Common errors: B: original ÷ new. C: subtracted

3. A scale factor of 0.5 makes

A) an enlargementB) a shape that is not similarC) a congruent copyD) a reduction
Answer D · Why: A scale factor less than 1 shrinks the shape · Common errors: A: thinks any scale factor enlarges

4. Two polygons are similar when

A) they have the same perimeterB) all their sides are equalC) corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportionalD) they have the same number of sides
Answer C · Why: Both conditions are needed · Common errors: B: that is congruent (for sides). A, D: not enough

5. Which rectangle is similar to a 2 cm by 5 cm rectangle?

A) 6 cm by 15 cmB) 4 cm by 7 cmC) 3 cm by 6 cmD) 5 cm by 8 cm
Answer A · Why: 6 ÷ 2 = 3 and 15 ÷ 5 = 3, the same scale factor · Common errors: B, D: added the same amount to each side. C: ratios 1.5 and 1.2

6. Two triangles have two pairs of equal angles. They are similar by

A) SSSB) SASC) they may not be similarD) AA
Answer D · Why: Two pairs of equal angles is enough (the third must match too)

7. One triangle has sides 3, 4 and 5. Another has sides 6, 8 and 10. The triangles are

A) congruentB) similar by SSSC) similar only if the angles are measuredD) not similar
Answer B · Why: 6/3 = 8/4 = 10/5 = 2 · Common errors: A: different sizes, so not congruent

8. On a map with a scale of 1 : 50 000, a distance of 4 cm is a real distance of

A) 20 kmB) 200 mC) 2 kmD) 12.5 km
Answer C · Why: 4 × 50 000 = 200 000 cm = 2000 m = 2 km · Common errors: B, A: unit conversion slips

Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

Show your working. [3 marks each]   /30

9. Solve.

[1] (a) 12x = 3/5[2] (b) x − 16 = 10/4
Answer (a) 3x = 60, so x = 20 (b) 4(x − 1) = 60, so x − 1 = 15, so x = 16
  • (a) 1 mark
  • (b) 1 mark: cross-multiplies correctly; 1 mark: x = 16

[3]10. A truck uses 6 L of fuel to drive 80 km. At the same rate, how much fuel does it need to drive from Edmonton to Calgary, about 300 km? Set up a proportion.

Answer 6/80 = x/300, so 80x = 1800, so x = 22.5 L
  • 1 mark: proportion with matching units in matching places
  • 1 mark: solves it
  • 1 mark: 22.5 L

11. Quadrilateral ABCD is similar to quadrilateral EFGH. AB = 6 cm, BC = 9 cm, EF = 10 cm and ∠B = 110°.

[1](a) Find the scale factor from ABCD to EFGH.  

[1](b) Find FG.

[1](c) Find ∠F.  

Answer (a) 10/6 = 5/3 (about 1.67) (b) FG = 9 × 5/3 = 15 cm (c) ∠F = 110° (corresponding angles are equal)
  • 1 mark each

[3]12. Is a 4 cm by 6 cm rectangle similar to a 6 cm by 8 cm rectangle? Show the ratios you used.

Answer No. 6/4 = 1.5 but 8/6 ≈ 1.33. The sides are not in the same ratio, so the rectangles are not similar (even though all their angles are 90°).
  • 1 mark: 6/4 = 1.5
  • 1 mark: 8/6 ≈ 1.33
  • 1 mark: not similar, because the ratios differ

13. A triangle with sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 8 cm is enlarged by a scale factor of 2.5.

[2](a) Find the side lengths of the new triangle.

[1](b) Show that the ratio of the perimeters equals the scale factor.

Answer (a) 12.5 cm, 17.5 cm, 20 cm (b) Perimeters: 20 cm and 50 cm. 50 ÷ 20 = 2.5, the scale factor.
  • (a) 2 marks: all three sides (1 mark for two)
  • (b) 1 mark

[3]14. In △ABC, ∠A = 40° and ∠B = 75°. In △DEF, ∠D = 40° and ∠F = 65°. Are the triangles similar? Explain, and write a similarity statement if they are.

Answer ∠C = 180° − 40° − 75° = 65°. ∠E = 180° − 40° − 65° = 75°. So ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E and ∠C = ∠F. Similar by AA: △ABC ~ △DEF.
  • 1 mark: finds the missing angles
  • 1 mark: matches two pairs of angles (AA)
  • 1 mark: similarity statement in the right order

[3]15. In △PQR, PQ = 6 cm, QR = 9 cm and ∠Q = 70°. In △STU, ST = 4 cm, TU = 6 cm and ∠T = 70°. Are the triangles similar? Name the criterion and show your ratios.

Answer ST/PQ = 4/6 = 2/3 and TU/QR = 6/9 = 2/3, and the included angles ∠Q = ∠T = 70°. Similar by SAS.
  • 1 mark: both ratios
  • 1 mark: notes the angle is between the two sides
  • 1 mark: SAS

[3]16. In the diagram, DE ∥ BC. AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and DE = 6 cm. Find BC.

Answer DE ∥ BC, so △ADE ~ △ABC (AA). AB = 4 + 6 = 10. BC/DE = AB/AD, so BC = 6 × 10/4 = 15 cm.
  • 1 mark: uses AB = 10 (not DB = 6)
  • 1 mark: correct proportion
  • 1 mark: 15 cm

[3]17. A floor plan uses a scale of 1 : 250. A room is 2.4 cm by 3.2 cm on the plan. What are the real dimensions of the room, in metres?

Answer 2.4 × 250 = 600 cm = 6 m. 3.2 × 250 = 800 cm = 8 m. The room is 6 m by 8 m.
  • 1 mark: multiplies by 250
  • 1 mark: converts to metres
  • 1 mark: both dimensions

[3]18. A student 1.6 m tall casts a shadow 2.4 m long. At the same time, a flagpole casts a shadow 15 m long. How tall is the flagpole?

Answer The sun's rays hit both at the same angle, so the triangles are similar. h/15 = 1.6/2.4, so h = 15 × 1.6 ÷ 2.4 = 10 m.
  • 1 mark: proportion with heights and shadows matched
  • 1 mark: solves it
  • 1 mark: 10 m

Show all working. [8 marks]   /8

19. A Grade 9 class builds a scale model of the school gym at a scale of 1 : 120. The real gym is 36 m long, 24 m wide and 9 m tall.

[2](a) Find the length, width and height of the model in centimetres.

[2](b) A door on the model is 1.75 cm tall. How tall is the real door, in metres?

[2](c) A basketball court is 28 m by 15 m. Is the gym floor similar to the court? Show your ratios.

[2](d) At one time of day, the 9 m gym wall casts a shadow 6 m long, and a nearby tree casts a shadow 10 m long. Explain why the two triangles are similar, then find the height of the tree.

Answer (a) 36 m = 3600 cm, and 3600 ÷ 120 = 30 cm. 2400 ÷ 120 = 20 cm. 900 ÷ 120 = 7.5 cm.
  • 1 mark: converts to cm and divides by 120
  • 1 mark: all three dimensions

(b) 1.75 × 120 = 210 cm = 2.1 m

  • 1 mark: multiplies by 120
  • 1 mark: 2.1 m

(c) No. 36/28 ≈ 1.29 and 24/15 = 1.6. The ratios are different, so the floor and the court are not similar.

  • 1 mark: both ratios
  • 1 mark: not similar, with the reason

(d) The wall and the tree both stand straight up (right angles), and the sun's rays hit both at the same angle, so the triangles are similar by AA. h/10 = 9/6, so h = 15 m.

  • 1 mark: AA reason (right angle and the same sun angle)
  • 1 mark: 15 m

Part D — Thinking

One question that asks you to judge a claim. [4 marks]   /4

20. Ryan says:

“All rectangles are similar, because all their angles are 90°.”

Is Ryan correct? Use the definition of similar polygons and at least one example to explain.

Answer Model answer: Ryan is not correct. Similar polygons need equal angles and sides in the same ratio. Rectangles always have equal angles, so the angle condition is met, but the sides might not be in proportion. A 2 cm by 4 cm rectangle and a 2 cm by 8 cm rectangle: 2/2 = 1 but 8/4 = 2. The ratios are different, so they are not similar (one is long and thin). Some rectangles are similar, like 2 by 4 and 3 by 6 (both ratios 1.5), but not all of them.
