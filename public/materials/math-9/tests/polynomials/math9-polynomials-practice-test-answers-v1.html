Part A — Knowledge (Selected Response)

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. The degree of 6x² − 4x³ + x − 9 is

A) 2B) 3C) 6D) 4
Answer B · Why: The highest power is x³ · Common errors: A: used the first term. C: used the biggest coefficient. D: counted the terms

2. In −2x² + 5x − 7, the constant term is

A) −2B) 5C) 7D) −7
Answer D · Why: The term with no variable, including its sign · Common errors: A, B: coefficients. C: dropped the sign

3. Which polynomial is a trinomial?

A) 3x² − x + 2B) 4xC) x² + 1D) x³ − x² + x − 1
Answer A · Why: Three terms · Common errors: C: two terms (binomial). B: one term (monomial). D: four terms

4. Which pair are like terms?

A) 3x and 3x²B) 4x and 4yC) −5x² and 2x²D) 7 and 7x
Answer C · Why: Same variable, same power · Common errors: A: different powers. B: different variables. D: one has no variable

5. (2x² + 3x) + (x² − 5x) =

A) 3x⁴ − 2x²B) 3x² + 8xC) 3x² − 2xD) x² − 2x
Answer C · Why: 2x² + x² = 3x² and 3x − 5x = −2x · Common errors: B: added 5x instead of −5x. A: added the exponents. D: dropped a term

6. The opposite of (x² − 4x + 3) is

A) −x² + 4x − 3B) −x² − 4x + 3C) x² + 4x − 3D) −x² + 4x + 3
Answer A · Why: Every sign changes · Common errors: B: changed only the first sign. C: kept the x² positive. D: missed the last term

7. 4x(x − 2) =

A) 4x² − 2B) 4x − 8C) 4x² − 8D) 4x² − 8x
Answer D · Why: 4x × x = 4x² and 4x × (−2) = −8x · Common errors: A: multiplied only the first term. C: lost the x on −8x. B: x × x is not x

8. (x + 5)(x − 2) =

A) x² − 10B) x² + 3x − 10C) x² − 3x − 10D) x² + 7x − 10
Answer B · Why: x² − 2x + 5x − 10 = x² + 3x − 10 · Common errors: A: missed the middle terms. C: sign of the middle term. D: added 5 + 2

Part B — Understanding (Short Answer)

Show your working. [3 marks each]   /30

9. Look at the polynomial 5 − 2x + 3x².

[1](a) Write it in standard form.  

[1](b) State its degree and its leading coefficient.   degree:   leading coefficient:

[1](c) A student says the degree is 5, “because 5 is the biggest number.” Explain the mistake.

Answer (a) 3x² − 2x + 5 (b) degree 2, leading coefficient 3 (c) The degree is the highest power of x, not the biggest number. 5 is the constant term.
  • 1 mark each

[3]10. Simplify 4x² − 3x + 6 − x² + 5x − 8.

Answer (4x² − x²) + (−3x + 5x) + (6 − 8) = 3x² + 2x − 2
  • 1 mark for each correct term

11. A student wrote:

3x² + 4x = 7x³

[2](a) Explain why this is wrong.

[1](b) What is the simplified form of 3x² + 4x?  

Answer (a) 3x² and 4x are unlike terms: x² and x stand for different things (an x²-tile and an x-tile are different shapes), so they can't be combined. A number check shows it too: with x = 1, 3x² + 4x = 7, but with x = 2 it is 12 + 8 = 20, while 7x³ = 56.
  • 1 mark: says they are unlike terms (different powers)
  • 1 mark: a reason (tiles, meaning, or a number check)

(b) 3x² + 4x: it is already simplified.

  • 1 mark

[3]12. Add (3x² − 2x + 5) + (−x² − 4x − 7).

Answer (3x² − x²) + (−2x − 4x) + (5 − 7) = 2x² − 6x − 2
  • 1 mark for each correct term

[3]13. Subtract (5x² + x − 2) − (2x² − 3x + 4).

Answer 5x² + x − 2 − 2x² + 3x − 4 = 3x² + 4x − 6
  • 1 mark: distributes the negative to every term
  • 2 marks: the answer (1 mark if one term is wrong)

[3]14. A student subtracted like this:

(6x − 1) − (2x − 5) = 6x − 1 − 2x − 5 = 4x − 6

Find the mistake, and give the correct answer.

Answer The student didn't change the sign of the −5. Subtracting −5 is adding 5: 6x − 1 − 2x + 5 = 4x + 4.
  • 1 mark: names the error
  • 1 mark: correct signs after removing the brackets
  • 1 mark: 4x + 4

15. Expand.

[1](a) 3(2x² − x + 4)

[2](b) −2x(x² − 3x + 1)

Answer (a) 6x² − 3x + 12 (b) −2x³ + 6x² − 2x
  • (a) 1 mark
  • (b) 2 marks (1 mark if one sign or one term is wrong)

16. A rectangle is 2x cm wide and (3x + 5) cm long.

[2](a) Write a simplified polynomial for its area.

[1](b) Find the area when x = 3.

Answer (a) 2x(3x + 5) = 6x² + 10x cm²
  • 1 mark: sets up 2x(3x + 5)
  • 1 mark: 6x² + 10x

(b) 6(9) + 10(3) = 54 + 30 = 84 cm² (check: 6 cm × 14 cm = 84 cm²)

  • 1 mark

[3]17. Use an area model to expand and simplify (2x + 3)(x + 4).

Answer Area model with 2x and 3 across, x and 4 down: 2x² | 3x over 8x | 12. Total 2x² + 11x + 12.
  • 1 mark: area model with the four parts labelled
  • 1 mark: the four partial products 2x², 8x, 3x, 12
  • 1 mark: 2x² + 11x + 12

[3]18. Expand and simplify (3x − 2)(x − 1). Then check your answer by substituting x = 2 into the question and into your answer.

Answer 3x² − 3x − 2x + 2 = 3x² − 5x + 2. Check with x = 2: (3(2) − 2)(2 − 1) = 4 × 1 = 4, and 3(4) − 5(2) + 2 = 12 − 10 + 2 = 4 ✓
  • 1 mark: four partial products
  • 1 mark: 3x² − 5x + 2
  • 1 mark: both sides checked with x = 2

Follow-through: across Part B, if one early slip is carried correctly through later steps, take off only the mark for the slip.

Part C — Application (Extended Response)

Show all working. Write each answer with its units. [8 marks]   /8

19. A family in Medicine Hat floods a backyard rink. The rink is (2x + 6) m long and (x + 3) m wide.

[2](a) Write and simplify a polynomial for the perimeter of the rink.

[2](b) Write and simplify a polynomial for the area of the rink.

[2](c) A square corner, x m by x m (shaded), is left unflooded for a snowbank. Write and simplify a polynomial for the area that gets flooded.

[2](d) The neighbours build a rink with the same width, (x + 3) m, and a length of 3x m. Write its area as a simplified polynomial.

Answer (a) 2(2x + 6) + 2(x + 3) = 4x + 12 + 2x + 6 = 6x + 18 m
  • 1 mark: adds all four sides (or doubles each side)
  • 1 mark: 6x + 18

(b) (2x + 6)(x + 3) = 2x² + 6x + 6x + 18 = 2x² + 12x + 18 m²

  • 1 mark: four partial products
  • 1 mark: 2x² + 12x + 18

(c) (2x² + 12x + 18) − x² = x² + 12x + 18 m²

  • 1 mark: subtracts x² from the area in (b)
  • 1 mark: x² + 12x + 18

(d) 3x(x + 3) = 3x² + 9x m²

  • 1 mark: sets up 3x(x + 3)
  • 1 mark: 3x² + 9x

Units: take off 1 mark once if units are missing throughout. Follow-through applies from (b) to (c).

Part D — Thinking

One question that asks you to judge a claim. [4 marks]   /4

20. Nina says:

“(x + 4)² = x² + 16, because you square each term.”

Is Nina correct? Use an area model and a number you choose for x to explain.

Answer Model answer: Nina is not correct. (x + 4)² means (x + 4)(x + 4). The area model has four parts: x², 4x, 4x and 16. So (x + 4)² = x² + 8x + 16. Nina left out the two 4x rectangles. Number check with x = 3: (3 + 4)² = 7² = 49, but 3² + 16 = 9 + 16 = 25. The correct expansion gives 9 + 24 + 16 = 49 ✓.
