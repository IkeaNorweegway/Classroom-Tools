Part C — Application (Extended Response)

Show all working and name each property you use. [8 marks]   /8

19. A circular skating pond in an Edmonton park has a radius of 25 m.

[2](a) A straight rope barrier, AB, is a chord 48 m long. How far is the rope from the centre of the pond?

[2](b) A second rope, also 48 m long, is put across the pond somewhere else. How far is it from the centre? Explain how you know without calculating again.

[2](c) A bench at P is 65 m from the centre. A path goes from P and just touches the edge of the pond at T. How long is the path PT?

[2](d) Two lifeguard chairs, L and M, are on the edge of the pond. The central angle ∠LOM is 100°. A photographer stands at Q on the edge, on the far side, and fits both chairs exactly in the frame. What angle, ∠LQM, does the camera need to take in?
