Part D — Thinking

20. Model answer: The conclusion is not justified. The data may show a correlation (students who eat breakfast tend to have higher marks), but correlation doesn't prove that one thing causes the other. A third variable could explain both: for example, students with more regular routines or more sleep may be more likely to eat breakfast and also do better in school. To test the claim, you would need a controlled experiment: randomly put similar students into two groups, give one group breakfast and not the other, and compare marks over time, keeping other things the same.
