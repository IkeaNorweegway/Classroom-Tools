1. Determine whether each pair of rectangles is similar. Show your reasoning.

(a) Rectangle A: 4 cm × 6 cm. Rectangle B: 6 cm × 9 cm.

Ratio of lengths: Ratio of widths: Are they equal? Similar?

(b) Rectangle A: 3 m × 8 m. Rectangle B: 6 m × 15 m.

Ratio of lengths: Ratio of widths: Similar?

Answer
(a) A: 4×6, B: 6×9:
Ratio of lengths: 6/4 = 1.5 | Ratio of widths: 9/6 = 1.5
Equal? Yes | Similar — scale factor 3/2
(b) A: 3×8, B: 6×15:
Ratio of lengths: 6/3 = 2 | Ratio of widths: 15/8 = 1.875
Equal? No | NOT similar
Common mistake: Checking only one pair of sides. Both dimension ratios must be equal.

6. Scale diagrams — map. A map of Banff National Park uses scale 1 : 250 000.

(a) Two campsites are 3.2 cm apart on the map. Real distance = km.

(b) A trail is 8.5 km long in real life. Map distance = cm.

(c) A lake appears 2.4 cm × 1.8 cm on the map. Actual dimensions = km × km.

Answer
(a) 3.2 cm on map:
3.2 × 250,000 = 800,000 cm = 8 km

(b) Trail 8.5 km real:
8.5 km = 850,000 cm. Map = 850,000/250,000 = 3.4 cm

(c) Lake 2.4 cm × 1.8 cm:
2.4 × 250,000 = 600,000 cm = 6 km × 1.8 × 250,000 = 4.5 km

7. Indirect measurement — shadow method. At 1 PM in Edmonton, a 2 m measuring stick casts a 1.2 m shadow. A nearby building casts a 36 m shadow.

(a) Set up the proportion:

Building height / 36 = 2 /

(b) Solve: building height = m

(c) Why must both shadows be measured at the same time?

Answer
(a) Proportion: Building height / 36 = 2 / 1.2
(b) Building height: h = 36 × (2/1.2) = 36 × (5/3) = 60 m
(c) Why same time? The sun's angle changes throughout the day — measuring at different times creates different-sized angles, so the triangles are no longer similar and the proportion doesn't hold.

9. Similar triangle — find x. △ABC ~ △DEF. AB = 2x + 1, DE = 3x − 1, BC = 5, EF = 10.

(a) Set up proportion: AB/DE = BC/EF

2x + 1/3x − 1 = 5/10 =

(b) Solve for x using cross-multiplication:

2(2x + 1) = 1(3x − 1)

x =

Answer

△ABC ~ △DEF, AB=2x+1, DE=3x-1, BC=5, EF=10

(a) Set up proportion: 2x+1/3x-1 = 5/10 = 1/2
(b) Cross-multiply: 2(2x+1) = 1(3x-1) → 4x+2 = 3x-1 → x = −3

⚠️ Note for teacher: x=−3 produces negative side lengths (AB=−5, DE=−10), which are geometrically invalid. The expressions chosen in this question are inconsistent — there is no positive solution. This appears to be a typographical error in the worksheet. The question is valuable for practising proportion setup and cross-multiplication, but the expressions should be checked before assigning.

10. Mirror method — Alberta context. A student places a mirror on the ground and steps back until she can see the top of a Calgary grain elevator in it. The mirror is 3 m from her feet. Her eye height is 1.65 m. The mirror is 72 m from the base of the elevator.

(a) Set up the proportion (similar triangles):

elevator height / 72 = 1.65 /

(b) Elevator height = m

Answer
(a) Proportion: Elevator height / 72 = 1.65 / 3
(b) Elevator height: h = 72 × (1.65/3) = 72 × 0.55 = 39.6 m
