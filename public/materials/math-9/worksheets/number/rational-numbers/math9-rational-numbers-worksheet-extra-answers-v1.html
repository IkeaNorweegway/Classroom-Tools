Teacher Answer Key — Not for Distribution

Cover the answer. Try the question. Then check.

1. Order: −5/6, 1/3, −1/2, 0, 2/3

Convert to decimals: −5/6 ≈ −0.833 | −1/2 = −0.5 | 0 | 1/3 ≈ 0.333 | 2/3 ≈ 0.667

Least → greatest: −5/6, −1/2, 0, 1/3, 2/3

Number line: −5/6 sits left of −0.5 toward −1; −1/2 at −0.5; 0 at centre; 1/3 and 2/3 to the right.

2. Adding and subtracting

(a) 5/6 + 1/4

LCD of 6 and 4 = 12
5/6 = 10/12 · 1/4 = 3/12
10/12 + 3/12 = 13/12

(b) −7/10 − (−1/5)

Rewrite: −7/10 + 1/5
LCD = 10 · 1/5 = 2/10
−7/10 + 2/10 = −1/2

Common Mistake

Forgetting to flip the sign of the subtracted term. −7/10 − (−1/5) is NOT −7/10 − 1/5.

(c) 2/5 + (−5/6)

LCD = 30 · 2/5 = 12/30 · −5/6 = −25/30
12/30 + (−25/30) = −13/30

3. Multiplying

(a) (−3/4) × (2/9)

Sign: neg × pos = negative
3 × 2/4 × 9 = 6/36 = −1/6

(b) (−9/16) × (−4/3) — cross-cancel

Sign: neg × neg = positive
Cancel 9 ↔ 3 (÷3): 3 and 1 · Cancel 4 ↔ 16 (÷4): 1 and 4
(3/4) × (1/1) = 3/4

(c) (−2/5)2

= (−2/5) × (−2/5)
neg × neg = positive
= 4/25

Common Mistake

(−2/5)2 ≠ −4/25. The negative base squared always gives a positive result.

4. Dividing

(a) (5/8) ÷ (1/4)

= (5/8) × (4/1)
= 20/8 = 5/2

(b) (−7/12) ÷ (7/6)

= (−7/12) × (6/7)
Cancel 7 ↔ 7 and 6 ↔ 12 (÷6): (−1/2) × (1/1)
= −1/2

(c) (−4) ÷ (−2/3)

= (−4/1) × (−3/2)
neg × neg = positive
= 12/2 = 6

Common Mistake

Forgetting to flip the divisor. (−4) ÷ (−2/3) ≠ (−4) × (−2/3).

5. Alberta context — Lake Louise snowpack

Starting: 40.5 cm | Day 1: −2.25 | Day 2: +5.5 | Day 3: −1.75 | Day 4: +3

(a) Total change:

−2.25 + 5.5 + (−1.75) + 3
= 5.5 + 3 − 2.25 − 1.75
= 8.5 − 4 = 4.5 cm

(b) Final depth:

40.5 + 4.5 = 45 cm

6. Order of operations

(a) 3/4 × (−2/5) + 1/3

Step 1 (×): 3/4 × (−2/5) = −6/20 = −3/10
Step 2 (+): −3/10 + 1/3 → LCD 30: −9/30 + 10/30 = 1/30

(b) (1/3 + 1/6)2 ÷ (−1/2)

Bracket first: 1/3 + 1/6 = 2/6 + 1/6 = 1/2
Exponent: (1/2)2 = 1/4
Divide: (1/4) ÷ (−1/2) = (1/4) × (−2) = −1/2

7. Error analysis

(a) "−2/5 > −1/5 because 2 > 1."

Error: Compared the numerators as if these were positive numbers. For negatives, the value closer to 0 is greater — a bigger "size" of the negative means a smaller value.
Correct: −2/5 = −0.4 and −1/5 = −0.2, and −0.2 is to the right of −0.4, so −1/5 > −2/5

(b) "3/5 − 1/4 = 2/1 = 2"

Error: Subtracted numerators and denominators separately — not a valid operation. Denominators must be equalized first.
Correct: LCD = 20 · 3/5 = 12/20 · 1/4 = 5/20
12/20 − 5/20 = 7/20

8. Water tank drain rate

(−5/12) × 13/4 = (−5/12) × (7/4)
= −35/48
Total change = −35/48 m

(Negative indicates the level dropped. The magnitude 35/48 m is the amount drained.)

9. Exponents

(a) (−1/3)3

= (−1/3) × (−1/3) × (−1/3)
= (1/9) × (−1/3)
= −1/27
(odd power of a negative → negative)

(b) (2/5)2 − (−1/4)2

= 4/25 − 1/16
= 64/400 − 25/400
= 39/400

(c) −(3/5)2

= −(9/25)
= −9/25
(exponent applies to 3/5 only; the leading negative is NOT inside the square)

10. Paint coats

(a) 45/6 ÷ 2/3

45/6 = 29/6
(29/6) × (3/2) = 87/12 = 7.25
Full coats = 7

(b) Fraction of a coat with what's left

Used: 7 × 2/3 = 14/3 = 28/6
Remaining: 29/6 − 28/6 = 1/6 L
As a fraction of a coat: (1/6) ÷ (2/3) = (1/6) × (3/2) = 3/12 = 1/4 of a coat
