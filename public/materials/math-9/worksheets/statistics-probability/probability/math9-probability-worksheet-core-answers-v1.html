6. Dependent events — without replacement.

Same bag (5 yellow, 3 purple). Draw two WITHOUT replacement.

(a) P(yellow on draw 1) = /8

(b) After drawing yellow: yellow remain, total remain.

P(yellow on draw 2 | yellow on draw 1) = /7

(c) P(yellow then yellow) = (/8) × (/7) =

(d) P(purple then yellow) = (/8) × (/7) =

Answer

(a) P(Y draw 1) = 5/8

(b) After yellow drawn: 4 yellow remain, 7 total remain. P(Y draw 2 | Y draw 1) = 4/7

(c) P(Y then Y) = (5/8)(4/7) = 20/56 = 5/14

(d) P(P then Y): P(P) = 3/8. After purple drawn: 5Y remain, 7 total. P(Y|P) = 5/7. P = (3/8)(5/7) = 15/56

Common Mistake

Using 5/8 for the second draw (forgetting it's without replacement).

8. Experimental probability.

A spinner was spun 60 times: Red = 22, Blue = 18, Green = 20.

(a) Experimental probability of each:

P(Red) =    P(Blue) =    P(Green) =

(b) If the spinner has 3 equal sections, what is the theoretical probability of each?

(c) Are the experimental results close to theoretical?

Answer

Experimental probability — spinner spun 60 times (R=22, B=18, G=20)

(a) P(R) = 22/60 = 11/30 ≈ 0.367 | P(B) = 18/60 = 3/10 = 0.3 | P(G) = 20/60 = 1/3 ≈ 0.333

(b) Theoretical (equal sections) = 1/3 each ≈ 0.333

(c) Yes, close — each result is near 20 (expected for equal thirds). Differences (22,18,20) are due to random chance in 60 trials.
