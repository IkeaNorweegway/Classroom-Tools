Answer

(a) (3x²+2x−1)−(x²−4x+3) = 3x²+2x−1 − x²−4x+3 = 2x²−2x+2:

Error: Failed to flip the sign of −4x when distributing the negative. −(−4x) = +4x, not −4x.

Setup: = 3x²+2x−1−x²+4x−3

Correct answer: 2x²+6x−4

(b) 3x(x+2)² = 3x(x²+4) = 3x³+12x:

Error: (x+2)² was computed as x²+4 — the middle term 4x was missed. Correct expansion: (x+2)²= x²+4x+4.

Setup: 3x(x²+4x+4) = 3x³+12x²+12x

Correct answer: 3x³+12x²+12x

(c) (x+4)(x−4) = x²−8x−16:

Error: Computed cross-terms incorrectly as −8x. In (x+4)(x−4), the cross-terms are x(−4)+4(x) = −4x+4x = 0. There is no middle term.

Correct answer: x²−16 (difference of squares pattern)

Answer

Proof: (x+a)(x−a) = x²−a²

(x+a)(x−a) = x²−ax+ax−a² = x²−a² ✓

The middle terms cancel because they are equal and opposite.

(b) 52 × 48 = (50+2)(50−2) = 2500−4 = 2496

(c) 97 × 97:

This is (97)² — a square, NOT a product of the form (x+a)(x−a). The pattern requires two different numbers equidistant from a centre value. Use instead: (100−3)² = 10000−600+9 = 9409

7. A student claims (a + b)² = a² + b² "because you just square each term."

(a) Disprove this with a = 3, b = 4.

Left side: (3 + 4)² =    Right side: 3² + 4² =    Equal?

(b) Show the correct expansion of (a + b)².

(c) What is the name for the missing term? Why does it appear?

Answer

Disproving (a+b)² = a²+b²

(a) a=3, b=4:

Left: (3+4)² = 49    Right: 3²+4² = 9+16 = 25    Equal? No — 49 ≠ 25

(b) Correct expansion:

(a+b)² = (a+b)(a+b) = a²+ab+ab+b² = a²+2ab+b²

(c) The missing term:

The missing term is 2ab — called the "cross product" or "middle term." It appears because multiplying two binomials produces FOUR partial products, not two. The inner and outer terms (a×b and b×a) both equal ab, and together they sum to 2ab.

8. Open-ended — reverse engineering.

A polynomial product expands to 2x³ + 5x² − x − 6.

(a) This is the product of a monomial and a binomial. Find one possible pair. (Hint: factor out what you can.)

(b) This could also be the product of two binomials (one with degree 2 and one with degree 1). Can you find them by working backward from the structure?

Answer

(a) Monomial × something:

The GCF of all coefficients is 1, so no monomial factor other than 1 divides all terms cleanly. The simplest form is 1 × (2x³+5x²−x−6), which is trivial. (If students factor x from first two terms: x²(2x+5)−(x+6) — doesn't factor cleanly. Part (b) is the primary task.)

(b) Binomial × trinomial:

Try (x+2) as a factor: 2x³+5x²−x−6 ÷ (x+2) = (2x²+x−3)

Verify: (x+2)(2x²+x−3) = 2x³+x²−3x+4x²+2x−6 = 2x³+5x²−x−6 ✓

Final answer: (x+2)(2x²+x−3)
