A company charges $25 per hour plus a $40 call-out fee. A competing company charges $35 per hour with no call-out fee.

(a) Write equations for both companies. Identify type of variation.

(b) For what number of hours is Company 1 cheaper? Write and solve an inequality.

(c) If a job takes exactly 4 hours, which company is more expensive and by how much?

Answer

S1 — Two companies

(a) Company 1: C=25h+40 (partial). Company 2: C=35h (direct).
(b) Company 1 cheaper: 25h+40 < 35h → 40 < 10h → h > 4 — for more than 4 hours, Company 1 is cheaper.
(c) At h=4: both charge $140 exactly — neither is more expensive at 4 hours.
