Angles in a Circle

One skill: using the centre-angle, edge-angle and semicircle rules · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: Two rules do most of the work. The angle at the edge is half the angle at the centre on the same arc. An angle standing on a diameter is 90°.

Worked example

Worked example

O is the centre. Angle AOB = 140°. C is on the circle, on the other side from arc AB. Find angle ACB and angle OAB.

  1. Angle ACB stands on the same arc as AOB, so it's half: 140° ÷ 2 = 70°
  2. OA and OB are both radii, so triangle AOB is isosceles.
  3. Its other two angles are equal: (180° − 140°) ÷ 2 = 20°, so angle OAB = 20°
