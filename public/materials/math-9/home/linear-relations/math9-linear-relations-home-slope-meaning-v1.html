What Slope and Intercept Mean

One skill: explaining the slope and y-intercept in real situations · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: Ask your child to say each slope in words with units, like "$8 per hour". If they can say it, they understand it.

Worked example

Worked example

A bike rental costs C = 8h + 12 dollars for h hours.

  1. The starting value, 12, is a $12 fee you pay before you ride.
  2. The slope, 8, means each hour costs $8 more.
  3. For 3 hours: C = 8(3) + 12 = $36
  4. There is a starting fee, so this is partial variation.
