Dependent Events

One skill: finding probabilities when items are not replaced · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: After something is taken out and not put back, there's one fewer item in total, and maybe one fewer of the kind you want. Ask "What's left now?"

Worked example

Worked example

A bag has 4 red and 6 blue marbles. Two are drawn without replacement. What is P(both blue)?

  1. First draw: 6 blue out of 10, so 6/10
  2. One blue is gone: 5 blue left out of 9, so 5/9
  3. Multiply: 6/10 × 5/9 = 30/90 = 1/3
