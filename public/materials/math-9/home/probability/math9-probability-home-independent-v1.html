Independent Events

One skill: finding the probability of two independent events · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Independent Events

NameDate

For the adult: Independent means the first result doesn't change the second. For "this AND that", multiply the two probabilities.

Worked example

Worked example

A spinner has 4 equal colours (red, blue, green, yellow). Spin it and flip a coin. What is P(red and heads)?

  1. The spinner doesn't affect the coin, so the events are independent.
  2. P(red) = 1/4 and P(heads) = 1/2
  3. Multiply: 1/4 × 1/2 = 1/8
