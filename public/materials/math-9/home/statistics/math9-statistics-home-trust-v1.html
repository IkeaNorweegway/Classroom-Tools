Can You Trust the Data?

One skill: spotting bias, poor samples and correlation versus cause · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: There are no calculations here. This sheet is about asking good questions. Talk the answers through together.

Worked example

Worked example

An ad says: "9 out of 10 dentists recommend Brand X."

  1. How many dentists were asked? Maybe only 10.
  2. Who chose them? The company might have picked friendly ones.
  3. What was the question? "Do you recommend Brand X?" or "Is Brand X better than nothing?"
  4. Without answers, the claim is weak evidence.
