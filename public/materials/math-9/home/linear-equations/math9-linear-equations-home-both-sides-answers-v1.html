Variables on Both Sides — Answers

One skill: solving equations with x on both sides · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 2: Multi-Step Equations

For the adult: Any correct order of steps is fine. What matters is that every step is done to both sides and the answer is checked.

1. 4x + 1 = x + 10

  • Subtract x: 3x + 1 = 10
  • Subtract 1: 3x = 9
  • Divide by 3: x = 3
  • Check: 4(3) + 1 = 13 and 3 + 10 = 13 ✓

2. 6x − 5 = 2x + 11

  • Subtract 2x: 4x − 5 = 11
  • Add 5: 4x = 16
  • Divide by 4: x = 4
  • Check: 6(4) − 5 = 19 and 2(4) + 11 = 19 ✓

3. 7x + 2 = 3x − 10

  • Subtract 3x: 4x + 2 = −10
  • Subtract 2: 4x = −12
  • Divide by 4: x = −3
  • Check: 7(−3) + 2 = −19 and 3(−3) − 10 = −19 ✓

4. 9 − 2x = x + 3

  • Add 2x: 9 = 3x + 3
  • Subtract 3: 6 = 3x
  • Divide by 3: x = 2
  • Check: 9 − 2(2) = 5 and 2 + 3 = 5 ✓

5. 3(x + 2) = x + 14

  • Expand: 3x + 6 = x + 14
  • Subtract x: 2x + 6 = 14
  • Subtract 6: 2x = 8
  • Divide by 2: x = 4
  • Check: 3(4 + 2) = 18 and 4 + 14 = 18 ✓

6. Let m be the number of months.

  • Equation: 40 + 15m = 10 + 25m
  • Subtract 15m: 40 = 10 + 10m
  • Subtract 10: 30 = 10m
  • Divide by 10: m = 3
  • After 3 months both gyms cost $85.
