Plant Parts and Their Jobs

One skill: roots, stems, leaves and flowers, and how plants reproduce · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: Use the fridge or the grocery store: most vegetables are a root, stem, leaf, flower or fruit. Guessing which is a fun way to practise.

Worked example

Worked example

Follow water and sugar through a plant.

  1. Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil.
  2. Xylem tubes carry the water up the stem to the leaves.
  3. Leaves use the water to make sugar by photosynthesis.
  4. Phloem tubes carry the sugar to the roots, fruits and growing parts.

Your turn

1. Match each part to its job: roots stem leaves flowers
(make food · reproduce · take in water and anchor · support and transport)

2. Xylem carries up the plant. Phloem carries around the plant.

3. Which part of the plant do we eat? carrot, celery, spinach, broccoli, potato, apple

4. Name two ways a plant can reproduce without seeds, with an example of each.

5. Give one advantage of growing plants from seeds, and one advantage of growing them from cuttings.

6. Canola is grown across Alberta. Name two things farmers do to help it grow well, and explain why.
