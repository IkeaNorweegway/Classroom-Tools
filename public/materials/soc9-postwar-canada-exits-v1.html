Post-War Canada: Change, Cooperation, and Conflict — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Social programs

LO 1: I can describe how Canada built social programs like health care and pensions.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one social program Canada built after the Second World War. (1 mark)

2. What problem were programs like health care and pensions meant to solve? (2 marks)

3. Why is a government program that helps everyone sometimes called a "welfare state"? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Canada in the world

LO 2: I can explain Canada's role in NATO, the Korean War and peacekeeping.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What does NATO stand for, and what kind of organization is it? (2 marks)

2. What was Canada's peacekeeping role after the Suez Crisis? (2 marks)

3. Why did Canada take part in the Korean War, and what does this show about its alliances? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Immigration and multiculturalism

LO 3: I can describe how immigration and multiculturalism changed Canada.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one group of immigrants who came to Canada after the war. (1 mark)

2. What did Canada's 1971 multiculturalism policy recognize? (2 marks)

3. How did post-war immigration change the cities and communities of Canada? (3 marks)

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________
