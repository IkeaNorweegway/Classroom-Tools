For the adult: For question 4, the honest answer is mixed: many women had to give up those jobs after the war, but attitudes began to change.

1. limiting how much of some goods each person could buy

2. factories (and other jobs usually done by men)

3. To raise money to pay for the war.

4. Women did new kinds of work, like building planes and ships.

  • Not all lasted: many were expected to leave those jobs when soldiers came home, but attitudes began to change.

5. Rationing, worry about family and friends, news of the war, and changes at work.
