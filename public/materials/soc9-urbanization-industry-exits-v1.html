Urbanization and the Growth of Industry — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Push and pull factors

LO 1: I can explain push and pull factors for people moving to Canada.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one push factor and one pull factor for a family leaving a war zone. (2 marks)

2. Why were displaced persons who came to Canada after 1945 often described as a "push" case? (2 marks)

3. Why do push and pull factors usually work together rather than alone? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Industry after the war

LO 2: I can describe how industries like oil, hydroelectric power and car-making grew after the war.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one industry that grew in Alberta after the Second World War. (1 mark)

2. How did the growth of cities create demand for hydroelectric power? (2 marks)

3. Explain how car-making and the growth of suburbs depended on each other. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Benefits and costs of city growth

LO 3: I can weigh the benefits and costs of cities growing.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one benefit and one cost of a growing city. (2 marks)

2. Why might a benefit for one group of people be a cost for another? (2 marks)

3. Which matters more to a growing city: jobs or clean air? Explain using one example. (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Backing up an opinion

LO 4: I can back up my opinion with evidence.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Write one opinion about urbanization. (1 mark)

2. Give one piece of evidence from this unit that supports your opinion. (2 marks)

3. Name one piece of evidence that could weaken your opinion, and say how you would respond. (3 marks)
