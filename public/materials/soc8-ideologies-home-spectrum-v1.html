The Political Spectrum

One skill: sorting ideas by individualism and collectivism · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: Left tends to mean more decisions made for the group (collectivism). Right tends to mean more decisions left to each person (individualism). Ask "Who decides?"

Worked example

Worked example

Where does this idea go: "The government should run the big businesses so everyone shares the profits"?

  1. Who decides? The government, on behalf of everyone.
  2. That puts the group first: collectivism.
  3. Collectivist ideas sit toward the left of the spectrum, near socialism and communism.
