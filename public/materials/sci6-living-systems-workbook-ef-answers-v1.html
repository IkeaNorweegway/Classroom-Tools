Stretch Zone — Challenge Questions
S1
A lake has these organisms: algae, water fleas, small fish, large fish, heron. Draw a food web and explain what would happen if a toxin entered the lake and killed all small fish.
Draw your lake food web here.
Answer Food web: algae → water fleas → small fish → large fish → heron. If small fish are killed: water flea population booms (no predator to control them); large fish crash (no prey); heron decline (no fish to eat). Award marks for drawing a web with ≥4 organisms and correctly predicting at least two cascade effects.
S2
An ecosystem with 50 species loses one species. Another ecosystem with 5 species loses one species. Which ecosystem is more likely to collapse? Explain using the concept of biodiversity.
Answer The 5-species ecosystem is more likely to collapse. When one of 5 species is lost, 20% of the biodiversity is gone — energy pathways are limited. In the 50-species ecosystem, other species can fill the role of the lost one (redundancy). Higher biodiversity = more backup pathways = greater resilience.
S3
Explain why it is impossible for energy to flow in a circle in a food web. Use the idea that energy is lost as heat at each step.
Answer Energy is lost as heat at every step of a food chain. There is less energy available at each higher level (≈10% passes to the next level). Because energy is always leaking out as heat, the system cannot sustain itself without a constant new input of energy from the sun. Energy flows in one direction — it cannot be recycled.
S4
Some Indigenous land management practices (like controlled burns) actually increase biodiversity. Research or think through why burning some areas might help an ecosystem in the long run.
Answer Controlled burns remove accumulated dead underbrush → reduces risk of catastrophic wildfire → allows sunlight to reach the ground → promotes new plant growth → creates a mosaic of habitats (open areas + forest edges) → supports more species (more niches available) → higher biodiversity. Accept any response capturing: burn → renewal → more habitat variety → more species.
S5
Design a balanced aquarium ecosystem that could survive without any inputs from outside. Name every organism you would include and explain the role of each.
Answer Must include: producers (aquatic plants — photosynthesize, produce O₂), primary consumers (snails or small fish — eat algae/plants), decomposers (bacteria — break down waste, recycle nutrients). Needs a light source (external). Award marks for: naming each organism, stating its role, and explaining how the system stays balanced (producers → consumers → decomposers → back to producers).
