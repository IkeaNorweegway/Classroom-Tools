Plant Parts and Their Jobs — Answers

One skill: roots, stems, leaves and flowers, and how plants reproduce · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 2: Plant Systems and Vascular Tissue · Section 4: Plant Reproduction

For the adult: The potato is the tricky one: it grows underground but is a swollen stem (a tuber), not a root.

1. roots: take in water and anchor · stem: support and transport · leaves: make food · flowers: reproduce

2. Xylem: water and minerals. Phloem: sugar (food).

3. carrot root · celery stem · spinach leaf · broccoli flower · potato stem (tuber) · apple fruit

4. Any two: runners (strawberries, spider plants), tubers (potatoes), bulbs (onions, tulips), cuttings (many houseplants).

5. Seeds: new variety, and they can spread far.

  • Cuttings: faster, and an exact copy of a good plant.

6. Any two: add fertilizer (minerals), water in dry years, control weeds (less competition for light and water), keep bees nearby for pollination.
