Estimating Square Roots — Answers

One skill: estimating the square root of a number that is not a perfect square · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Estimating square roots

For the adult: Estimates within one tenth are great. What matters is the reason: the two perfect squares and which one is closer.

1. Between 4 and 5.

2. Between 7 and 8 (49 < 50 < 64). Closer to 7: 50 is only 1 more than 49.

3. Between 3 and 4 (9 < 10 < 16).

4. 64 < 70 < 81, so between 8 and 9, closer to 8. About 8.4 (calculator: 8.37).

5. 81 < 95 < 100, so between 9 and 10, much closer to 10. About 9.7 (calculator: 9.75).

6. Any whole number from 37 to 48 (6² = 36, 7² = 49).

7. 36 < 40 < 49, so the side is between 6 m and 7 m, closer to 6. About 6.3 m (calculator: 6.32).
