Adding and Subtracting Polynomials

One skill: combining like terms to add and subtract polynomials · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Like terms and order · Section 4: Adding and subtracting

NameDate

For the adult: Like terms have the same letter and the same exponent: 3x and −5x are like terms; 3x and 3x² are not. Add their numbers and keep the power. To subtract a bracket, flip the sign of every term inside it.

Worked example

Simplify (3x + 2) − (x − 5).
Step 1: Flip every sign in the second bracket: 3x + 2 − x + 5.

Step 2: x terms: 3x − x = 2x. Numbers: 2 + 5 = 7. The answer is 2x + 7.

Your turn

1. Simplify 5x + 3 − 2x + 4. (x terms together, numbers together.)

2. Simplify 2x² + 3x − x² − 5x + 4. (Sort the like terms. Keep each sign with its term.)

3. Jay says 3x² + 2x = 5x³. Is he right? (Try x = 2 on both sides.)

4. Write 5 − 3x + x² in descending order. (Highest exponent first. Each sign moves with its term.)

5. Add (2x² + 3x − 1) + (x² − 5x + 4).

6. Find the perimeter of the rectangle.

7. Subtract (5x − 2) − (3x − 6).
