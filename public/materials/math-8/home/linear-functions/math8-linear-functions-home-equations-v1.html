y = mx + b

One skill: reading, writing and graphing y = mx + b, and finding intercepts · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: y = mx + b · Section 4: Intercepts, rate of change and special lines

NameDate

For the adult: In y = mx + b, m is the slope (the number times x). b is where the line crosses the y-axis, at the point (0, b). To graph, plot (0, b) first. Then step with the slope: a slope of 2 means right 1, up 2. In a story, m is the rate and b is the start.

Worked example

Write the equation of the line.

The line crosses the y-axis at (0, 1), so b = 1. From (0, 1) to (2, 5): rise 4, run 2. m = 4/2 = 2. So y = 2x + 1.

Your turn

1. Find m and b. a) y = 3x − 2 b) y = −x + 5 (m is the number times x. In b), −x means −1x.)

2. Write the equation of the line. (Where does it cross the y-axis? Then count the rise and run.)

3. Graph y = 2x − 3. Name two points on it. (Plot (0, −3) first. Then right 1, up 2.)

4. Find both intercepts of y = 2x − 6. Write each as an ordered pair. (For the x-intercept, put y = 0.)

5. A taxi charges $3 to start and $2 for each km. Write an equation for the cost. What do m and b mean? Find the cost of an 8 km ride.

6. A 40 L tank drains 4 L each minute. Write an equation for the water left. When is the tank empty?
