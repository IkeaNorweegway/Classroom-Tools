Operations with Rational Numbers

One skill: adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing positive and negative fractions and decimals · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Operations with rational numbers · Section 4: Order of operations and estimating

NameDate

For the adult: To add fractions, find a common bottom number first. Never add the bottoms. For × and ÷: same signs give a positive answer, different signs give a negative answer. Do × and ÷ before + and −.

Worked example

Find −1/2 + 1/3.
Step 1: Common bottom 6: −1/2 = −3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6.
Step 2: Each chip is 1/6. Two zero pairs cancel.

Step 3: One negative sixth is left. −1/2 + 1/3 = −1/6.

Your turn

1. Find −3/4 + 1/4. (Same bottom: add the tops.)

2. Find 2.5 − 6.8. (Subtracting is adding the opposite: 2.5 + (−6.8). Show the jump.)

3. Find (−1.5) × (−4). (Same signs: positive.)

4. Find −2/3 × 3/4. (Find the size with the picture. Then the sign.)

5. Find −3 + 6 × (−0.5).

6. Three days in Lethbridge had highs of −4.5 °C, 1.5 °C and −3 °C. Find the mean.

7. Estimate −4.9 × 3.1 with friendly numbers. Then find it. Is your answer reasonable?
