Circle Graphs

One skill: drawing and reading circle graphs · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Bar graphs and histograms · Section 4: Circle graphs

NameDate

For the adult: A circle graph shows parts of one whole. The whole circle is 100% and also 360°. To find a sector's angle, multiply its percent (as a decimal) by 360°. To go back, divide the angle by 360°. Check: the angles add to 360°.

Worked example

40 students pick a favourite season: winter 10, spring 6, summer 16, fall 8. Find each angle.

Step 1: Percent = count ÷ 40. Winter: 10 ÷ 40 = 25%. Spring 15%, summer 40%, fall 20%.
Step 2: Angle = percent × 360°. Winter: 0.25 × 360° = 90°. Spring 54°, summer 144°, fall 72°.
Step 3: Check: 90 + 54 + 144 + 72 = 360°.

Your turn

1. Find the angle for 50%. (0.50 × 360°.)

2. A sector is 36°. What percent is it? (36 ÷ 360.)

3. Find each angle. Write it in the circle graph. (Percent × 360°.)

4. 60 people pick a Stampede event. The Rodeo sector is 120°. How many chose the rodeo?
