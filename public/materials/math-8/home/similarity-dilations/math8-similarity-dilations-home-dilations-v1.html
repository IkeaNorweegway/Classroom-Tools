Dilations on a Grid

One skill: dilating a shape about (0, 0) and checking that it is similar · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Similar shapes · Section 4: Dilations on a grid

NameDate

For the adult: A dilation makes a shape bigger or smaller from a fixed point. Here the point is (0, 0). Multiply BOTH coordinates of every corner by the scale factor k. The image is similar: same angles, every side × k.

Worked example

Dilate △ABC about (0, 0) with k = 2.

A(1, 1) → A′(2, 2). B(3, 1) → B′(6, 2). C(1, 2) → C′(2, 4). Each side doubled: AB = 2, A′B′ = 4.

Your turn

1. Dilate △PQR about (0, 0) with k = 3. Write P′, Q′ and R′. Draw the image. (Multiply both numbers by 3.)

2. Dilate S(4, 6) and T(8, 2) about (0, 0) with k = 0.5. (× 0.5 is the same as half.)

3. Kai dilated (2, 3) with k = 2. He got (4, 5). Fix his mistake. (Did he multiply?)

4. A(2, 1) moves to A′(8, 4). Find k. (What times 2 makes 8? Check with 1 and 4.)

5. Is △A′B′C′ similar to △ABC? Explain how you know.

6. One triangle has sides 3, 4 and 5 cm. Another has sides 6, 8 and 11 cm. Are they similar?
