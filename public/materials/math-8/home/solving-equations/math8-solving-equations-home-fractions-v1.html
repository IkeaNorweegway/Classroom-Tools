Equations with Fractions and Decimals

One skill: clearing fractions and decimals, then solving · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Clearing fractions and decimals

NameDate

For the adult: Multiply EVERY term on both sides by the bottom number of the fraction (or by 10 for one decimal place, 100 for two). The fractions or decimals disappear. Then solve and check in the first equation.

Worked example

Solve (1/3)x + 2 = 5.

Step 1: Multiply every term by 3 (the 2 and the 5 too): x + 6 = 15.
Step 2: Subtract 6 from both sides: x = 9. Check: (1/3)(9) + 2 = 3 + 2 = 5 ✓
