Surface Area of Prisms and Pyramids

One skill: surface area of prisms and pyramids · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Polyhedrons and nets · Section 2: Surface area of prisms and pyramids

NameDate

For the adult: Surface area (SA) covers the outside of a solid, like wrapping paper. Unfold it into a flat net, find the area of each face and add. Answers are in cm². A pyramid's triangles use the slant height, which runs up the face.

Worked example

A box is 8 cm long, 2 cm wide and 3 cm tall. Find its surface area.

Step 1: Front and back: 8 × 3 = 24 cm² each. Sides: 2 × 3 = 6 cm² each. Top and bottom: 8 × 2 = 16 cm² each.
Step 2: SA = 24 + 24 + 6 + 6 + 16 + 16 = 92 cm².

Your turn

1. A cube is 4 cm on each edge. Find its SA. (One face is 4 × 4. A cube has 6 faces.)

2. Find the SA of this box. (Front: 5 × 3. Find each pair of faces.)

3. A pyramid has a square base 4 cm on each side. Its slant height is 6 cm. Find its SA. (Base: 4 × 4. Each triangle: 1/2 × 4 × 6.)

4. A tent's triangle ends have a base of 6 m and a height of 4 m. The slanted sides are 5 m. The tent is 10 m long. Find the SA with the floor. (2 triangles + 3 rectangles.)

5. A pyramid has a square base 10 cm on each side. Its height is 12 cm. Its slant height is 13 cm. Find its SA.

6. Will this pattern fold into a cube?
