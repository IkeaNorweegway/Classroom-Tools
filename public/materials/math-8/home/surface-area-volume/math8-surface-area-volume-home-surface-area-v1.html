Surface Area of a Box

One skill: finding the surface area of a rectangular prism · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 2: Surface area of prisms

NameDate

For the adult: Surface area is how much paper it takes to wrap a box: the area of all six faces added up. The faces come in three matching pairs (front and back, top and bottom, two sides). Grab a real box from the cupboard and measure it together.

Worked example

Worked example

Find the surface area of a box 10 cm long, 4 cm wide and 3 cm tall.

  1. Front and back: 10 × 3 = 30, two of them = 60 cm²
  2. Top and bottom: 10 × 4 = 40, two of them = 80 cm²
  3. Two sides: 4 × 3 = 12, two of them = 24 cm²
  4. Add: 60 + 80 + 24 = 164 cm²
