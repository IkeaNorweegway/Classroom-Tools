Equal Ratios

One skill: making equal ratios by multiplying both terms · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: A ratio like 2:3 is "2 for every 3." To keep the same mix, multiply both numbers by the same thing. Adding the same number changes the mix. Recipes are a great way to practise this.

Worked example

Worked example

A pancake recipe uses 2 cups of flour for every 3 eggs. How many eggs for 8 cups of flour?

  1. How many times bigger is 8 than 2? 8 ÷ 2 = 4.
  2. Multiply the eggs by the same number: 3 × 4 = 12.
  3. So 2 : 3 = 8 : 12. You need 12 eggs.
