Order of Operations with Integers

One skill: using the order of operations with negative numbers · about 15 minutes

NameDate

For the adult: The order is Brackets, Exponents, then × and ÷ from left to right, then + and − from left to right (BEDMAS). Ask your child to underline the step they will do first before they write anything.

Worked example

Worked example

Find 7 + (−2) × 5.

  1. Multiply first: (−2) × 5 = −10.
  2. Then add: 7 + (−10) = −3.
  3. Not 7 + (−2) = 5, then 5 × 5 = 25. That does the steps in the wrong order.
