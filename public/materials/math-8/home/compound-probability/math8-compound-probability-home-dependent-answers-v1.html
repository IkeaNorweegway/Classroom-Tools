Dependent Events — Answers

One skill: the probability of two dependent events (without replacement) · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Dependent events · Section 4: Probability problems

For the adult: The most common slip is using the same fraction twice (Q6). Once an item is kept out, there is one fewer in all, so the bottom number goes down by 1. If the same kind was taken, the top number goes down by 1 too.

Answers

1. a) independent (the spinner doesn't change) b) dependent (one name is gone)

2. 3 red of 4 are left. P(red) = 3/4.

3. After R: red 1/3, blue 2/3. After B: red 2/3, blue 1/3. P(red, red) = 2/4 × 1/3 = 2/12 = 1/6.

4. Dependent. 2/5 × 1/4 = 2/20 = 1/10 = 10%.

5. 3/5 × 2/4 = 6/20 = 3/10 = 30%.

6. One black sock is out. 2 black of 4 are left. 3/5 × 2/4 = 6/20 = 3/10 = 30%.
