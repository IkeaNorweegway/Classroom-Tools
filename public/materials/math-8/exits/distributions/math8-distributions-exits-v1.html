Exit ticket — Section 3 · Choosing mean, median or mode

Name:Date:
Target: I can choose the mean, median or mode for a set of data. I can explain my choice using the shape. (DI3)

1. Five people wait for a bus (minutes): 2, 3, 3, 4, 18. Find the mean and the median.

2. Which average best shows the favourite Oilers player in a class?

3. Which is the better "typical wait" in the first question: the mean or the median? Explain how you know.
