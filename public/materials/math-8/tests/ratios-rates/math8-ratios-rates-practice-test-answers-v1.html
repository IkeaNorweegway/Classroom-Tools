Part A — Choose the Answer

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. A class has 10 boys and 14 girls. The ratio of boys to girls in lowest terms is

A) 10 : 24B) 5 : 7C) 14 : 10D) 24 : 10
Answer B · Why: 10 : 14 = 5 : 7 · Common mistakes: A: boys to everyone. C: wrong order

2. In the same class, the ratio of girls to the whole class in lowest terms is

A) 14 : 10B) 12 : 7C) 7 : 5D) 7 : 12
Answer D · Why: 14 : 24 = 7 : 12 · Common mistakes: A: girls to boys

3. Which ratio is equal to 3 : 5?

A) 6 : 10B) 4 : 6C) 5 : 3D) 8 : 10
Answer A · Why: 3 : 5 × 2 = 6 : 10 · Common mistakes: B, D: added the same number

4. 2 : 9 = 8 : ?

A) 15B) 18C) 36D) 32
Answer C · Why: 2 × 4 = 8, so 9 × 4 = 36 · Common mistakes: A: added 6

5. A bus goes 150 km in 2 h. What is its speed?

A) 300 km/hB) 152 km/hC) 75 km/hD) 148 km/h
Answer C · Why: 150 ÷ 2 = 75 · Common mistakes: A: multiplied

6. A 5 kg bag of potatoes costs $8.50. What is the price for 1 kg?

A) $1.70B) $0.59C) $3.50D) $42.50
Answer A · Why: 8.50 ÷ 5 = 1.70 · Common mistakes: B: divided the wrong way

7. Which one is a rate?

A) 3 cats to 4 dogsB) 40%C) 2 : 5D) 60 km per hour
Answer D · Why: Different units (km and hours) · Common mistakes: A, C: ratios. B: a percent

8. A map scale is 1 cm : 50 km. Two towns are 3 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they really?

A) 53 kmB) 150 kmC) 16.7 kmD) 15 km
Answer B · Why: 3 × 50 = 150 km · Common mistakes: A: added

9. Here is a bag of counters: R is red, B is blue and Y is yellow.

[1](a) Write the ratio red : blue : yellow in lowest terms.  

[1](b) Write the ratio of blue to all the counters in lowest terms.  

[1](c) What percent of the counters are red?  

Answer (a) 6 : 4 : 2 = 3 : 2 : 1 (b) 4 : 12 = 1 : 3 (c) 6 out of 12 = 50%
  • 1 mark each

[3]10. A lemonade uses 2 cups of mix for every 5 cups of water. Complete the table.

Cups of mix24610
Cups of water5
Answer Water: 10, 15, 25 (multiply both by 2, 3, 5)
  • 1 mark each

[3]11. Is 4 : 6 equal to 10 : 15? Show two different ways to check.

Answer Yes. Way 1: 4 : 6 = 2 : 3 and 10 : 15 = 2 : 3. Way 2: 4/6 ≈ 0.67 and 10/15 ≈ 0.67. (Or a table: 2 : 3, 4 : 6, 6 : 9, 8 : 12, 10 : 15.)
  • 1 mark: first way
  • 1 mark: second way
  • 1 mark: yes

[3]12. Which box of granola bars is the better buy? Find the price of one bar in each box.

Answer Box A: $4.20 ÷ 6 = $0.70 a bar. Box B: $6.50 ÷ 10 = $0.65 a bar. Box B is the better buy.
  • 1 mark each

13. A car uses 7 L of gas for every 100 km.

[2](a) How much gas does it use to drive 250 km?

[1](b) Is “7 L per 100 km” a ratio or a rate? How do you know?

Answer (a) 250 is 2.5 × 100, so 2.5 × 7 = 17.5 L
  • 1 mark: method
  • 1 mark: 17.5 L

(b) A rate: it compares two different units (litres and kilometres).

  • 1 mark

[3]14. 5 movie tickets cost $62.50. How much do 8 tickets cost? Show your strategy.

Answer One ticket: $62.50 ÷ 5 = $12.50. Eight tickets: 8 × $12.50 = $100.00
  • 1 mark: strategy (unit rate or table)
  • 1 mark: $12.50
  • 1 mark: $100.00

[3]15. A fruit punch uses 3 L of juice for every 2 L of soda. Riya has 7.5 L of juice. How much soda does she need? Use a double number line or a table.

Answer 3 L juice : 2 L soda. 7.5 = 3 × 2.5, so soda = 2 × 2.5 = 5 L
  • 1 mark: double number line or table set up
  • 1 mark: scale factor 2.5 (or 1.5 L juice per 1 L soda)
  • 1 mark: 5 L

16. The Lee family drives 360 km from Edmonton into the mountains.

[2](a) They drive the first 180 km in 2 hours. What is their speed in km/h?

[2](b) At that speed, how long does the whole 360 km trip take?

[2](c) Their car uses 8 L of gas for every 100 km. How much gas do they need for 360 km?

[2](d) Gas Station A charges $1.50 per litre. Gas Station B charges $43.50 for 30 L. Which station is cheaper? Show both unit prices.

Answer (a) 180 ÷ 2 = 90 km/h
  • 1 mark each: method, answer with units

(b) 360 ÷ 90 = 4 hours

  • 1 mark each

(c) 360 km = 3.6 × 100 km. 3.6 × 8 = 28.8 L

  • 1 mark each

(d) A: $1.50 per L. B: $43.50 ÷ 30 = $1.45 per L. Station B is cheaper.

  • 1 mark: unit price for B
  • 1 mark: picks B

Part D — Explain Your Thinking

[3 marks]   /3

17. Sam says:

“1 : 3 and 2 : 4 are equal ratios. I just added 1 to both numbers.”

Is Sam right? Explain, and write a ratio that really is equal to 1 : 3.

Answer Model answer: Sam is not right. Equal ratios come from multiplying (or dividing) both numbers by the same number, not adding. 1 : 3 means the second amount is 3 times the first, but in 2 : 4 it is only 2 times. A ratio equal to 1 : 3 is 2 : 6 (or 3 : 9, 4 : 12…).
