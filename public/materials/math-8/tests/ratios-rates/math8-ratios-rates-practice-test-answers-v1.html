Part D — Explain Your Thinking

17. Model answer: Sam is not right. Equal ratios come from multiplying (or dividing) both numbers by the same number, not adding. 1 : 3 means the second amount is 3 times the first, but in 2 : 4 it is only 2 times. A ratio equal to 1 : 3 is 2 : 6 (or 3 : 9, 4 : 12…).
