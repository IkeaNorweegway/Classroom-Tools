Part A — Choose the Answer

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. The solution of 7x = 56 is

A) x = 63B) x = 49C) x = 8D) x = 392
Answer C · Why: 56 ÷ 7 = 8 · Common mistakes: B: subtracted 7

2. The solution of x3 = −4 is

A) x = −7B) x = −4/3C) x = 12D) x = −12
Answer D · Why: Undo ÷ 3 with × 3: −12 · Common mistakes: B: divided again

3. The solution of 2x + 5 = 13 is

A) x = 4B) x = 9C) x = 6.5D) x = 8
Answer A · Why: 2x = 8, x = 4 · Common mistakes: B: added 5

4. What is a good first step to solve 4x − 3 = 21?

A) divide both sides by 4B) add 3 to both sidesC) subtract 3 from both sidesD) multiply both sides by 4
Answer B · Why: Undo the − 3 first · Common mistakes: A: undoes in reading order

5. 3(x + 4) is the same as

A) 3x + 4B) 3x + 7C) x + 12D) 3x + 12
Answer D · Why: Multiply both terms · Common mistakes: A: multiplied only x

6. The solution of 2(x − 1) = 10 is

A) x = 5.5B) x = 4C) x = 6D) x = 11
Answer C · Why: x − 1 = 5, x = 6 · Common mistakes: B: subtracted 1

7. Which value is the solution of 5x − 2 = 18?

A) x = 3B) x = 4C) x = 20D) x = 3.2
Answer B · Why: 5(4) − 2 = 18

8. “A number times 4, plus 6, is 30.” Which equation matches?

A) 4n + 6 = 30B) 4(n + 6) = 30C) 6n + 4 = 30D) n + 4 + 6 = 30
Answer A · Why: Times 4 first, then plus 6 · Common mistakes: B: adds before multiplying

Part B — Show Your Work

[3 marks each]   /21

[3]9. Each bag holds the same number of counters, x. The scale is balanced. 1. Write an equation. 2. Solve it. 3. Check it.

Answer 3x = 15, so x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Check: 3 × 5 = 15 ✓
  • 1 mark each

[3]10. Solve −6x = 42. Then verify your answer.

Answer x = 42 ÷ (−6) = −7. Verify: −6 × (−7) = 42 ✓
  • 1 mark: divides by −6
  • 1 mark: −7
  • 1 mark: verifies

[3]11. Solve x4 + 3 = 1. Show each step.

Answer x/4 = 1 − 3 = −2, so x = −2 × 4 = −8. Check: −8 ÷ 4 + 3 = 1 ✓
  • 1 mark: subtracts 3
  • 1 mark: multiplies by 4
  • 1 mark: −8

[3]12. Sam solved 5x + 10 = 35 like this:

Divide by 5 first: x + 10 = 7, so x = −3.

1. What did Sam do wrong? 2. Solve it the right way.

Answer Sam divided only some of the terms (he didn't divide the 10). Undo the + 10 first: 5x = 25, so x = 5. Check: 5(5) + 10 = 35 ✓
  • 1 mark: names the mistake
  • 1 mark: 5x = 25
  • 1 mark: x = 5

[3]13. Solve 4(x − 3) = 20. Then verify your answer.

Answer x − 3 = 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so x = 8. (Or 4x − 12 = 20, 4x = 32.) Verify: 4(8 − 3) = 4 × 5 = 20 ✓
  • 1 mark: divides first or expands correctly
  • 1 mark: 8
  • 1 mark: verifies

[3]14. Solve 2(x + 5) = 7. Your answer will not be a whole number.

Answer x + 5 = 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, so x = −1.5 (or −3/2). (Or 2x + 10 = 7, 2x = −3.)
  • 1 mark: first step
  • 1 mark: second step
  • 1 mark: −1.5

[3]15. At the Calgary Stampede, Ana pays $5 to get in, plus $3 for each ride. She spends $26. Write an equation, solve it, and answer in a sentence.

Answer Let r be the number of rides. 3r + 5 = 26. 3r = 21, so r = 7. Ana went on 7 rides.
  • 1 mark: equation
  • 1 mark: r = 7
  • 1 mark: sentence

Part C — Put It Together

Show your work. [8 marks]   /8

16. A phone plan costs $25 a month, plus $2 for each GB of data used.

[2](a) Write an equation for the monthly cost, C, when g GB are used.

[2](b) Leah's bill was $41. How many GB did she use? Solve your equation.

[2](c) Her brother paid for 3 months. Each month cost (g + 20) dollars. He paid $78 in all. Solve 3(g + 20) = 78.

[2](d) Verify your answer to (c). Then write what it means in a sentence.

Answer (a) C = 2g + 25
  • 1 mark: 2g
  • 1 mark: + 25

(b) 2g + 25 = 41, so 2g = 16, so g = 8 GB

  • 1 mark each: steps, answer

(c) g + 20 = 78 ÷ 3 = 26, so g = 6

  • 1 mark each: divides by 3, answer

(d) 3(6 + 20) = 3 × 26 = 78 ✓. Her brother used 6 GB each month.

  • 1 mark: verifies
  • 1 mark: sentence

Part D — Explain Your Thinking

[3 marks]   /3

17. Kim solved 2(x + 3) = 14 like this:

2x + 3 = 14, so 2x = 11, so x = 5.5

Is Kim right? Check her answer, find her mistake, and solve it correctly.

Answer Model answer: Kim is not right. Check: 2(5.5 + 3) = 2 × 8.5 = 17, not 14. Her mistake: 2(x + 3) is 2x + 6, not 2x + 3. She only multiplied the x. Correct: 2x + 6 = 14, so 2x = 8, so x = 4. Check: 2(4 + 3) = 14 ✓
