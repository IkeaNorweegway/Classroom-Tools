Part A — Choose the Answer

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. (−6) × 4 =

A) 24B) 2C) −10D) −24
Answer D · Why: One negative: negative · Common mistakes: A: lost the sign. C: added

2. (−5) × (−8) =

A) −40B) −13C) 40D) 13
Answer C · Why: Two negatives: positive · Common mistakes: A: thinks the answer is always negative

3. (−63) ÷ 9 =

A) 7B) −7C) −54D) −72
Answer B · Why: 63 ÷ 9 = 7, one negative · Common mistakes: C, D: added or subtracted

4. (−32) ÷ (−4) =

A) 8B) −8C) −28D) 128
Answer A · Why: Two negatives: positive · Common mistakes: B: lost the sign rule

5. Each red counter is −1. Which multiplication do the counters show?

A) 3 × 4B) 3 × (−4)C) (−3) × (−4)D) 3 + (−4)
Answer B · Why: 3 groups of −4 · Common mistakes: C: the groups are not taken away

6. What is the sign of (−1) × (−2) × (−3) × (−4)?

A) zeroB) negativeC) positiveD) can't tell
Answer C · Why: Four negatives is an even number · Common mistakes: B: thinks any negative makes it negative

7. 10 − 2 × (−3) =

A) 16B) −24C) 4D) 24
Answer A · Why: 2 × (−3) = −6; 10 − (−6) = 16 · Common mistakes: B: subtracted first. C: 10 − 6

8. −3 + (−4) =

A) 7B) −12C) 12D) −7
Answer D · Why: Adding two negatives: −7 · Common mistakes: A: used the × rule for adding

Answer Three jumps of 4 to the left: 0 → −4 → −8 → −12. 3 × (−4) = −12
  • 2 marks: three jumps of −4 drawn (1 mark if the jumps are the wrong size or direction)
  • 1 mark: −12

10. A diver goes down 4 m every minute for 6 minutes.

[1](a) Write a multiplication for the change in depth.  

[1](b) What is the change in depth?  

[1](c) She started at −5 m. Where is she now?

Answer (a) 6 × (−4) (b) −24 m (c) −5 + (−24) = −29 m
  • 1 mark each

[3]11. Complete the pattern. Then explain what happens to the answers.

3 × (−2)2 × (−2)1 × (−2)0 × (−2)(−1) × (−2)(−2) × (−2)
−6−4
Answer Answers: −2, 0, 2, 4. Each answer goes up by 2, so the pattern crosses zero and the last two are positive. That shows a negative times a negative is positive.
  • 2 marks: all four values (1 mark for two or three)
  • 1 mark: explains the "up by 2" pattern

12.

[1](a) Find (−2) × 3 × (−5).  

[1](b) Find (−1) × (−2) × (−3) × 5.  

[1](c) How can you tell the sign of each answer before you multiply?

Answer (a) 30 (b) −30 (c) Count the negatives: two negatives (an even number) makes the answer positive; three negatives (odd) makes it negative.
  • 1 mark each

[3]13. 6 × (−7) = −42. Use this fact to write two division facts. Then show how one of them checks.

Answer −42 ÷ 6 = −7 and −42 ÷ (−7) = 6. Check: 6 × (−7) = −42 ✓ (or (−7) × 6 = −42).
  • 1 mark each

[3]14. In Medicine Hat, the temperature fell 21 °C in 7 hours. It fell the same amount each hour. Write a division and find the change per hour.

Answer (−21) ÷ 7 = −3 °C per hour
  • 1 mark: the division with −21
  • 1 mark: −3
  • 1 mark: units

[3]15. Find (−4) × 3 + 18 ÷ (−6). Use the order of operations.

Answer (−4) × 3 = −12. 18 ÷ (−6) = −3. −12 + (−3) = −15
  • 1 mark each step

16. Here are the morning temperatures in Grande Prairie for one week in January.

DayMonTueWedThuFri
Temperature (°C)−12−8−15−3−7

[2](a) Find the mean (average) temperature.

[2](b) On Saturday it starts at −9 °C. A cold front makes it drop 2 °C every hour for 5 hours. What is the temperature after 5 hours?

[2](c) On Sunday the temperature rises 18 °C in 6 hours. What is the change per hour?

[2](d) Next week the temperature drops 4 °C a day for 3 days. Write a multiplication for the total change. Explain why the answer is negative.

Answer (a) Sum = −45. Mean = (−45) ÷ 5 = −9 °C
  • 1 mark: −45
  • 1 mark: −9 °C

(b) 5 × (−2) = −10. −9 + (−10) = −19 °C

  • 1 mark: −10
  • 1 mark: −19 °C

(c) 18 ÷ 6 = +3 °C per hour

  • 1 mark each: division, answer with sign and units

(d) 3 × (−4) = −12 °C. It is negative because the temperature goes down: one factor is negative (a drop), so the product is negative.

  • 1 mark: −12
  • 1 mark: the reason

17. Kai says:

“(−2) × 6 is positive, because the answer takes the sign of the bigger number.”

Is Kai right? Find the answer and explain the real rule.

Answer Model answer: Kai is not right. (−2) × 6 = −12. For multiplying and dividing, the size of the numbers doesn't matter. Count the negative signs: one negative gives a negative answer, two negatives give a positive answer.
