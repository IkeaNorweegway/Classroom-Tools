Part B — Show Your Work

9. Three jumps of 4 to the left: 0 → −4 → −8 → −12. 3 × (−4) = −12

  • 2 marks: three jumps of −4 drawn (1 mark if the jumps are the wrong size or direction)
  • 1 mark: −12

10. (a) 6 × (−4) (b) −24 m (c) −5 + (−24) = −29 m

  • 1 mark each

11. Answers: −2, 0, 2, 4. Each answer goes up by 2, so the pattern crosses zero and the last two are positive. That shows a negative times a negative is positive.

  • 2 marks: all four values (1 mark for two or three)
  • 1 mark: explains the "up by 2" pattern

12. (a) 30 (b) −30 (c) Count the negatives: two negatives (an even number) makes the answer positive; three negatives (odd) makes it negative.

  • 1 mark each

13. −42 ÷ 6 = −7 and −42 ÷ (−7) = 6. Check: 6 × (−7) = −42 ✓ (or (−7) × 6 = −42).

  • 1 mark each

14. (−21) ÷ 7 = −3 °C per hour

  • 1 mark: the division with −21
  • 1 mark: −3
  • 1 mark: units

15. (−4) × 3 = −12. 18 ÷ (−6) = −3. −12 + (−3) = −15

  • 1 mark each step

Part C — Put It Together

16. (a) Sum = −45. Mean = (−45) ÷ 5 = −9 °C

  • 1 mark: −45
  • 1 mark: −9 °C

(b) 5 × (−2) = −10. −9 + (−10) = −19 °C

  • 1 mark: −10
  • 1 mark: −19 °C

(c) 18 ÷ 6 = +3 °C per hour

  • 1 mark each: division, answer with sign and units

(d) 3 × (−4) = −12 °C. It is negative because the temperature goes down: one factor is negative (a drop), so the product is negative.

  • 1 mark: −12
  • 1 mark: the reason
