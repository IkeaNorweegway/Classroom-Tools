This practice test shows you the kinds of questions on the unit test. 1. Read each question twice. 2. Write the formula, then show your work. 3. Use π ≈ 3.14. Round to one decimal place and write units.

RemindersFormula
Area of a circleA = π × r × r
Circumference of a circleC = π × d  (or 2 × π × r)
Volume of a prism or cylinderV = area of base × height
