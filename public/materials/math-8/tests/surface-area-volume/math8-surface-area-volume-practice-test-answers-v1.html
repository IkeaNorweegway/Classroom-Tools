Part D — Explain Your Thinking

17. Model answer: Sam is not right. Surface area is the covering on the outside (cm²); volume is the space inside (cm³). A 1 cm cube has SA = 6 cm² and V = 1 cm³. A 6 cm cube has SA = 6 × 36 = 216 cm² and V = 216 cm³. The numbers are different for the 1 cm cube, and even when the numbers match, the units are different: one is square units, one is cubic units.
