Part A — Choose the Answer

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. Which graph is best for showing the temperature every hour through one day?

A) pictographB) circle graphC) line graphD) bar graph
Answer C · Why: Change over time · Common mistakes: B: circle graphs show parts of a whole

2. A circle graph shows

A) change over timeB) parts of one wholeC) exact counts onlyD) a trend going up
Answer B · Why: Parts of one whole · Common mistakes: A: that is a line graph

3. In a pictograph, one picture stands for 4 students. How many students do 2½ pictures stand for?

A) 10B) 2.5C) 6D) 8
Answer A · Why: 2.5 × 4 = 10 · Common mistakes: B: counted pictures, not students

4. Which graph is best for comparing the medals won by five countries?

A) no graphB) line graphC) circle graphD) bar graph
Answer D · Why: Comparing separate groups · Common mistakes: B: no change over time here

5. A bar graph's vertical axis starts at 45 instead of 0. This can make

A) small differences look bigB) the bars widerC) the data more accurateD) no difference at all
Answer A · Why: A cut-off axis stretches small gaps

6. A picture is drawn twice as tall and twice as wide. It looks about

A) 2 times as bigB) half as bigC) the same sizeD) 4 times as big
Answer D · Why: 2 × 2 = 4 times the area · Common mistakes: A: thinks only the height counts

7. The data are 5, 9, 6, 11. Is “it goes up every time” supported?

A) yesB) yes, because 11 is more than 5C) no, it goes down from 9 to 6D) you can't tell
Answer C · Why: It drops from 9 to 6 · Common mistakes: B: looked only at the ends

8. The parts of a circle graph always add up to

A) 50%B) 100%C) 360%D) any amount
Answer B · Why: The whole is 100% · Common mistakes: C: mixed up with 360°

Part B — Show Your Work

[3 marks each]   /21

9. The bar graph shows the favourite lunch of a Grade 8 class.

[1](a) How many students are in the class?  

[1](b) How many more chose pizza than soup?  

[1](c) What fraction of the class chose tacos?  

Answer (a) 12 + 8 + 4 + 6 = 30 (b) 12 − 4 = 8 (c) 8/30 = 4/15
  • 1 mark each

10. The circle graph shows how a family spends its $2000 monthly budget.

[1](a) How much do they spend on food?  

[1](b) How much do fun and savings add up to?  

[1](c) Why is a circle graph a good choice for this data?

Answer (a) 25% of $2000 = $500 (b) 10% + 10% = 20% of $2000 = $400 (c) The data are parts of one whole budget (they add to 100%).
  • 1 mark each

11. Choose the best kind of graph for each data set. Give a reason. [1 each]

(a) the number of cans a school collects each week of a food drive

(b) how a student spends the 24 hours of one day

(c) a puppy's mass every month for its first year

Answer (a) Bar graph or pictograph (compare weeks), or a line graph (change over time). (b) Circle graph: parts of one whole day. (c) Line graph: change over time.
  • 1 mark each (a sensible graph with a reason)

12. Both graphs show the same data: Team A won 52 games and Team B won 48 games.

[1](a) Which graph makes Team A look much better?  

[1](b) In that graph, about how many times as tall is Team A's bar as Team B's?

[1](c) Which graph shows the real difference fairly? Explain.

Answer (a) Graph 1 (b) In Graph 1, Team A's bar is 7 units above the axis start and Team B's is 3, so it looks about 2 times as tall (accept 2 to 2.5). (c) Graph 2: its axis starts at 0, so the bars show that 52 and 48 are close.
  • 1 mark each

[3]13. A store's sales doubled from 2022 to 2024. Its ad shows a shopping bag for each year. The 2024 bag is drawn twice as tall and twice as wide as the 2022 bag. Explain why the ad is misleading, and how to fix it.

Answer The bag is twice as tall and twice as wide, so it looks 4 times as big, not 2 times. Fix it by drawing two bags of the same size for 2024 (or one bag the same size plus a bar graph), so the picture shows double.
  • 1 mark: says it looks 4 times as big
  • 1 mark: explains why (both height and width doubled)
  • 1 mark: a fix

[3]14. The graph shows the minutes Jo practised guitar each day. Jo says: “I practise more every day.” Is that supported by the data? Explain, then write a conclusion that is supported.

Answer Not supported: Jo practised less on Wednesday (15 min) than on Tuesday (25 min). A supported conclusion: "Jo practised the most on Friday" or "Jo's practice went up overall from Monday to Friday, with a dip on Wednesday."
  • 1 mark: not supported
  • 1 mark: points to Wednesday
  • 1 mark: a supported conclusion

[3]15. A Banff hotel shows this graph and says: “We get more visitors every month of the year.” What is wrong with this conclusion?

Answer The graph only shows June to August, the summer months. You can't tell what happens in the other 9 months; visitors probably drop in the fall. The conclusion goes beyond the data.
  • 1 mark: notes only 3 months are shown
  • 1 mark: says the rest of the year is unknown
  • 1 mark: says the conclusion is not supported

Part C — Put It Together

Show your work. [8 marks]   /8

16. Here is the canola harvest on a farm near Camrose (sample data).

Year20192020202120222023
Tonnes150160120165170

[2](a) Which kind of graph is best for this data? Why?

[2](b) Draw your graph on the grid. Label the axes.

[2](c) A seed company graphs the same data with the vertical axis starting at 110 and writes “Huge growth!” Why is that misleading?

[2](d) Is “The harvest went up every year” supported? Write a conclusion that is supported.

Answer (a) A line graph (or bar graph): the data shows change over time (years).
  • 1 mark: graph type
  • 1 mark: reason

(b) Years on the horizontal axis, tonnes (0 to 200) on the vertical axis, five points or bars at 150, 160, 120, 165, 170, with labels.

  • 1 mark: correct values
  • 1 mark: axes labelled

(c) Starting at 110 makes the small rise from 150 to 170 look huge. It also makes the 2021 drop look huge. The real change from 2019 to 2023 is only 20 tonnes, about 13%.

  • 1 mark: names the cut-off axis
  • 1 mark: explains the effect

(d) Not supported: the harvest fell in 2021 (120 tonnes). A supported conclusion: "The harvest was highest in 2023" or "Apart from 2021, the harvest went up."

  • 1 mark: not supported, with the 2021 drop
  • 1 mark: a supported conclusion

Part D — Explain Your Thinking

[3 marks]   /3

17. Raj says:

“A circle graph is a good way to show how Calgary's temperature changes over a year.”

Is Raj right? Explain what a circle graph shows, and name a better graph.

Answer Model answer: Raj is not right. A circle graph shows how one whole is split into parts that add to 100%, at one time. Temperatures over a year are not parts of a whole, and they change over time. A line graph is better: it shows how the temperature goes up and down from month to month.
