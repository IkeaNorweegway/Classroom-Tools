Part A — Choose the Answer/8

Circle the best answer. 1 mark each.

1.
Two shapes are similar. Which must be true?
AThey are the same size.
BTheir matching sides are equal.
CTheir matching angles are equal.
DTheir areas are equal.
Answer C. Similar shapes have the same shape: matching angles are equal and every side is multiplied by the same k.
Common mistakes: A, B and D describe shapes that are the same size. Similar shapes can be different sizes.
SD1[1]
2.
Which pair is not always similar?
Atwo squares
Btwo circles
Ctwo equilateral triangles
Dtwo rhombuses
Answer D. Two rhombuses can have different angles, so they can have different shapes.
Common mistakes: A, B and C always have the same shape. Only the size changes.
SD1[1]
3.
A photo is 4 cm by 6 cm. A copy is 10 cm by 15 cm. What is the scale factor from the photo to the copy?
A2.5
B6
C0.4
D9
Answer A. 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 and 15 ÷ 6 = 2.5.
Common mistakes: B subtracts (10 − 4). C divides the wrong way (4 ÷ 10). D subtracts (15 − 6).
SD2[1]
4.
Which scale factor makes a reduction?
A0.6
B1
C1.5
D6
Answer A. A scale factor between 0 and 1 makes the copy smaller.
Common mistakes: B keeps the same size. C and D make the copy bigger.
SD2[1]
5.
A square has sides of 3 cm. It is enlarged with k = 2. What is the area of the new square?
A18 cm²
B36 cm²
C12 cm²
D6 cm²
Answer B. The new sides are 6 cm, so the area is 6 × 6 = 36 cm². That is 2² = 4 times the old area of 9 cm².
Common mistakes: A doubles the area. C is the old area plus 3. D is the new side length, not an area.
SD3[1]
6.
A map uses the scale 1 cm : 4 km. Two towns are 18 km apart. How far apart are they on the map?
A72 cm
B22 cm
C4.5 cm
D14 cm
Answer C. Every 4 km is 1 cm. 18 ÷ 4 = 4.5 cm.
Common mistakes: A multiplies 18 × 4. B adds. D subtracts.
SD3[1]
7.
Dilate the point (4, −2) about (0, 0) with k = 3. Where is the image?
A(7, 1)
B(12, −6)
C(12, −2)
D(4/3, −2/3)
Answer B. Multiply both coordinates by 3: 4 × 3 = 12 and −2 × 3 = −6.
Common mistakes: A adds 3. C multiplies only the x. D divides by 3.
SD4[1]
8.
A dilation about (0, 0) takes A(2, 1) to A′(10, 5). What is k?
A8
B1/5
C4
D5
Answer D. 10 ÷ 2 = 5 and 5 ÷ 1 = 5. Image ÷ original.
Common mistakes: A subtracts the x's. B divides the wrong way. C subtracts the y's.
SD4[1]

Part B — Show Your Work/21

Show your steps.

9.
A rectangle is 3 cm by 5 cm.
a)Give the sides of a similar rectangle that is bigger.
b)Give the sides of a similar rectangle that is smaller.
c)Mia adds 2 cm to each side and gets 5 cm by 7 cm. She says it is similar. Explain why it is not.
Answer a) Answers vary. Multiply both sides by the same k > 1. For example, k = 2 gives 6 cm by 10 cm (1 mark).
b) Answers vary. Multiply both sides by the same k between 0 and 1. For example, k = 0.5 gives 1.5 cm by 2.5 cm (1 mark).
c) 5 ÷ 3 ≈ 1.67 but 7 ÷ 5 = 1.4. The sides are not multiplied by the same number, so the shape changes. Adding does not keep the shape (1 mark).
SD1[3]
10.
Every side of the rhombus doubles to make the square. Kai says the two shapes are similar. Is he right? Explain.
Answer Every side is multiplied by 2: 8 ÷ 4 = 2 (1 mark).
The angles do not match: 60° and 120° in the rhombus, 90° in the square (1 mark).
Kai is wrong. Similar shapes need matching angles equal AND sides multiplied by the same k. Here only the sides work (1 mark).
SD1[3]
11.
△ABC ~ △DEF.
a)Find the scale factor from △ABC to △DEF.
b)Find EF.
c)Find FD.
Answer a) DE matches AB: k = 4 ÷ 6 = 2/3 (1 mark).
b) EF = 9 × 2/3 = 6 cm (1 mark).
c) FD = 12 × 2/3 = 8 cm (1 mark).
SD2[3]
12.
A Calgary Stampede poster is 60 cm wide and 90 cm tall. A similar postcard is 12 cm wide.
a)Find the scale factor from the poster to the postcard.
b)Find the postcard's height.
c)Is the postcard an enlargement or a reduction? How does k tell you?
Answer a) k = 12 ÷ 60 = 0.2 (1 mark).
b) 90 × 0.2 = 18 cm (1 mark).
c) A reduction, because k is between 0 and 1 (1 mark).
SD2[3]
13.
A triangle has an area of 8 cm². A similar triangle has every side 3 times as long. Sam says its area is 24 cm². Find the right area. Explain Sam's mistake.
Answer Area grows by k² = 3² = 9 (1 mark).
8 × 9 = 72 cm² (1 mark).
Sam multiplied by k, not k². Area has two directions (length and width), and both grow 3 times: 3 × 3 = 9 (1 mark).
SD3[3]
14.
The map shows Red Deer and Lacombe.
a)Find the real distance.
b)Ponoka is 40 km from Red Deer. How far apart are they on this map?
c)A second map uses 1 cm : 10 km. How far apart are Red Deer and Lacombe on the second map?
Answer a) 5 × 5 = 25 km (1 mark).
b) 40 ÷ 5 = 8 cm (1 mark).
c) 25 ÷ 10 = 2.5 cm. The gap is shorter on the second map (1 mark).
SD3[3]
15.
Dilate △DEF about (0, 0) with k = 1.5. Write D′, E′ and F′. Then draw the image.
Answer D′(3, 3) (1 mark).
E′(6, 3) (1 mark).
F′(3, 9) (1 mark).

Part C — Put It Together/8

16.

A game designer draws a logo on a grid. It is rectangle ABCD with A(1, 1), B(4, 1), C(4, 3) and D(1, 3). She dilates it about (0, 0) with k = 2 to make a banner.

(a)
Write the vertices of the banner A′B′C′D′.
Answer A′(2, 2) and B′(8, 2) (1 mark).
C′(8, 6) and D′(2, 6) (1 mark).
SD4[2]
(b)
Find the length and width of ABCD and of A′B′C′D′. Show that both sides use the same scale factor.
Answer ABCD is 3 by 2. A′B′C′D′ is 6 by 4 (1 mark).
6 ÷ 3 = 2 and 4 ÷ 2 = 2. Both sides use k = 2 (1 mark).
SD2[2]
(c)
Find the area of each rectangle. How many times bigger is the banner's area?
Answer ABCD: 3 × 2 = 6 square units. A′B′C′D′: 6 × 4 = 24 square units (1 mark).
24 ÷ 6 = 4 times bigger. That is k² = 2² = 4 (1 mark).
SD3[2]
(d)
For a small icon, she dilates ABCD about (0, 0) with k = 1/2 instead. Write the icon's vertices. Is the icon an enlargement or a reduction?
Answer (0.5, 0.5), (2, 0.5), (2, 1.5) and (0.5, 1.5) (1 mark).
A reduction, because k = 1/2 is less than 1 (1 mark).
SD4[2]

Part D — Explain Your Thinking/3

17.
Ella dilates a triangle about (0, 0). The point (4, 6) moves to (2, 3). She says k = 2 because she divided each coordinate by 2. Is she right? Find k and say if the image is an enlargement or a reduction. Explain.
Answer Model answer: No. k is image ÷ original: 2 ÷ 4 = 1/2 and 3 ÷ 6 = 1/2, so k = 1/2. Dividing by 2 is the same as multiplying by 1/2. With k = 2 the image would be (8, 12). Since k is less than 1, the image is a reduction: it is closer to (0, 0).
