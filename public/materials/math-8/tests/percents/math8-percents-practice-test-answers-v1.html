Part A — Choose the Answer

Circle the best answer. [1 mark each]   /8

1. 45% written as a decimal is

A) 4.5B) 45C) 0.045D) 0.45
Answer D · Why: 45 ÷ 100 = 0.45 · Common mistakes: A: divided by 10

2. 0.4% written as a decimal is

A) 0.4B) 0.004C) 0.04D) 40
Answer B · Why: 0.4 ÷ 100 = 0.004 · Common mistakes: A: forgot to divide by 100

3. 3/5 written as a percent is

A) 35%B) 3.5%C) 60%D) 0.6%
Answer C · Why: 3/5 = 60/100 · Common mistakes: A: wrote the digits side by side

4. 175% written as a decimal is

A) 1.75B) 17.5C) 0.175D) 175
Answer A · Why: 175 ÷ 100 = 1.75 · Common mistakes: C: thinks a percent can't be more than 1

5. 10% of 340 is

A) 3.4B) 3400C) 340D) 34
Answer D · Why: 340 ÷ 10 = 34

6. 9 out of 12 written as a percent is

A) 75%B) 12%C) 9%D) 90%
Answer A · Why: 9 ÷ 12 = 0.75 = 75% · Common mistakes: C: used the count as the percent

7. In Alberta, the GST is 5%. What is the GST on an $80 item?

A) $40.00B) $0.40C) $4.00D) $8.00
Answer C · Why: 0.05 × 80 = 4 · Common mistakes: D: used 10%

8. A $100 price goes down 10%. Then the new price goes up 10%. The final price is

A) $100B) $99C) $110D) $90
Answer B · Why: $100 → $90 → $90 + $9 = $99 · Common mistakes: A: thinks the changes cancel

[3]11. Find 15% of $60 without a calculator.

1. Find 10% of $60. 2. Find 5% of $60. 3. Add them.

Answer 10% of $60 = $6. 5% of $60 = $3. 15% = $6 + $3 = $9.00
  • 1 mark each step

[3]12. A Calgary Flames player scored on 14 of his 40 shots. What percent of his shots went in?

Answer 14 ÷ 40 = 0.35 = 35%
  • 1 mark: 14 ÷ 40 (or 14/40)
  • 1 mark: 0.35
  • 1 mark: 35%

[3]13. A haircut in Edmonton costs $35. Ana pays 5% GST and leaves a 15% tip. Both are on the $35. What is her total?

Answer 5% + 15% = 20%. 20% of $35 = $7. Total = $35 + $7 = $42.00
  • 1 mark: adds the two percents (or finds $1.75 and $5.25)
  • 1 mark: $7
  • 1 mark: $42.00

[3]14. A bike costs $240 before tax. In Alberta the tax is 5% GST. In Ontario the tax is 13% HST. How much more does the bike cost in Ontario?

Answer Alberta: $240 × 1.05 = $252.00. Ontario: $240 × 1.13 = $271.20. Difference: $19.20 (or 8% of $240 = $19.20)
  • 1 mark: Alberta total
  • 1 mark: Ontario total
  • 1 mark: $19.20

15. An $80 video game is 25% off. Then the store takes another 10% off the sale price.

[2](a) What is the final price?

[1](b) Is that the same as 35% off $80? Explain.

Answer (a) $80 × 0.75 = $60. Then $60 × 0.90 = $54.00
  • 1 mark: $60 sale price
  • 1 mark: $54.00

(b) No. 35% off $80 is $52. The 10% is taken off the smaller sale price, so less money comes off.

  • 1 mark

Part C — Put It Together

Show your work. [8 marks]   /8

16. A school fair in Red Deer had 400 visitors in Year 1. In Year 2, it had 20% more visitors than Year 1. In Year 3, it had 25% more visitors than Year 2.

[2](a) How many visitors came in Year 2?

[2](b) How many visitors came in Year 3?

[2](c) Year 3 is what percent of Year 1? Write it as a percent and as a decimal.

[2](d) Liam says: “The fair grew 20% + 25% = 45% from Year 1 to Year 3.” Is Liam right? Explain.

Answer (a) 20% of 400 = 80. 400 + 80 = 480 visitors
  • 1 mark: 80
  • 1 mark: 480

(b) 25% of 480 = 120. 480 + 120 = 600 visitors

  • 1 mark: uses 480, not 400
  • 1 mark: 600

(c) 600 ÷ 400 = 1.5, so 150% or 1.5

  • 1 mark each

(d) No. The fair grew from 400 to 600. That is 200 more, and 200 is 50% of 400. The 25% was taken of 480, a bigger number, so the total growth is 50%, not 45%.

  • 1 mark: says Liam is wrong
  • 1 mark: explains with 50% (or with the 25% being of a bigger number)

Part D — Explain Your Thinking

[3 marks]   /3

17. Kai says:

“0.5% of $200 is $100.”

Is Kai right? Find the right answer and explain Kai's mistake.

Answer Model answer: Kai is not right. 0.5% = 0.005. 0.005 × $200 = $1.00. Kai found 50% of $200. 0.5% is half of one percent, which is much smaller than 50%.
