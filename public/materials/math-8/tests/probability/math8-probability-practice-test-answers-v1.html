Part D — Explain Your Thinking

17. Model answer: Theo added, but for "and" with independent events you multiply. The right answer is 1/2 × 1/6 = 1/12. An outcome table for a coin and a die has 2 × 6 = 12 cells, and only one cell is heads and 6. So the answer is 1 out of 12. (Adding gives 4/6, which is bigger than P(heads), and that can't be right.)
