Inequalities

Core Practice ★ — Answer Key

Answer key · Teacher copy
1★ Core

Write each one in symbols. a) x is greater than −2. b) y is at most 7. c) n is at least 4.5. d) k is less than 1/2.

Answer a) x > −2 b) y ≤ 7 c) n ≥ 4.5 d) k < 1/2 (IQ1)
At most means ≤. At least means ≥.
2★ Core

True or false? a) 6 ≥ 6 b) 6 > 6 c) −3 < −7 d) 0.75 ≤ 3/4

Answer a) True: 6 equals 6, and ≥ lets equal in. b) False: equal is not greater. (IQ1)
c) False: −3 is right of −7, so −3 > −7. d) True: 0.75 = 3/4.
3★ Core

Ben says x < 5 includes 5. Is he right? Test 5 to show it.

Answer No. 5 < 5 is false, because 5 equals 5. So 5 is not a solution: open dot. (IQ1)
Targets the "x < 3 includes 3" mix-up.
4★ Core

Rewrite with the variable first. a) 9 > x b) −1 ≤ y c) 0 < m

Answer a) x < 9 b) y ≥ −1 c) m > 0 (IQ1)
The symbol still points at the smaller amount. Targets reading 3 < x backwards.
5★ Core

Graph x > −1. Then test 2.

Answer Open dot at −1. Ray to the right. (IQ2)
Test 2: 2 > −1 is true, and 2 is on the ray. ✓
6★ Core

Graph y ≤ 2.5.

Answer Closed dot at 2.5 (halfway between 2 and 3). Ray to the left. (IQ2)
7★ Core

Write the inequality for each graph.

Answer a) x ≥ −3: closed dot, ray right. (IQ2)
b) x < 4: open dot, ray left.
8★ Core

Ria graphed x > 3 like this. Fix her graph.

Answer The ray must go right. Greater numbers are to the right. Test 5: 5 > 3 is true, but 5 is not on her ray. (IQ2)
Keep the open dot. Targets the "x > 2 points left" mix-up.
9★ Core

Is x = 4 a solution of 3x − 2 > 9? Is x = 3?

Answer x = 4: 3(4) − 2 = 10. 10 > 9 is true. Yes. (IQ3)
x = 3: 3(3) − 2 = 7. 7 > 9 is false. No.
10★ Core

Which are solutions of 2x + 5 ≥ 1? Test −3, −2 and 0.5.

Answer −3: 2(−3) + 5 = −1. −1 ≥ 1 is false. No. (IQ3)
−2: 2(−2) + 5 = 1. 1 ≥ 1 is true. Yes (the boundary).
0.5: 2(0.5) + 5 = 6. 6 ≥ 1 is true. Yes. The graph is x ≥ −2.
11★ Core

Write an inequality for each. a) Riders must be at least 122 cm tall. b) Drive no faster than 30 km/h in a playground zone. c) The temperature is below freezing.

Answer a) h ≥ 122. At least means ≥, not ≤. Targets the "at least means less than" mix-up. (IQ4)
b) s ≤ 30. c) t < 0, with t in °C.
12★ Core

A canoe holds at most 3 people. Write the inequality. Graph the solutions.

Answer p ≤ 3. People are whole numbers, so the solutions are dots at 0, 1, 2 and 3. Not a ray. (IQ4)
