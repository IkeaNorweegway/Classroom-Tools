Number

Strand Workbook — Answer Key

Answer key · Teacher copy
Every card says which topic and notes section must be taught before the question opens. The student copy has no topic labels: tell students which questions are open each day. Misconception tags mark the questions built to catch a common mistake.
1★ Core
Opens after: IN1 — Integers, Section 1

The temperature in Edmonton drops 3 °C every hour for 4 hours. Write a multiplication. Use the number line to show it.

Answer 4 × (−3) = −12. The temperature drops 12 °C. Four jumps of −3 from 0.
2★ Core
Opens after: SQ1–SQ2 — Squares and Square Roots, Sections 1–2

Look at the squares of tiles.

  1. a) How many tiles are in each square? Write each one as a square number.
  2. b) Write a square root fact for each one.
Answer a) 4² = 16, 7² = 49, 9² = 81. b) √16 = 4, √49 = 7, √81 = 9.
Misconception target

5² = 10. If a student writes 4² = 8, have them count the tiles.

3★ Core
Opens after: RR1 — Ratios and Rates, Section 1

Frozen juice is 1 can of concentrate to 4 cans of water.

  1. a) What is the ratio of concentrate to all the juice?
  2. b) What percent of the juice is concentrate?
Answer a) 1:5. b) 1 out of 5 = 20%.
Misconception target

"1:4 means 1 out of 4." Ask: how many cans are in the jug?

4★ Core
Opens after: PC1 — Percents, Section 1

Write each one as a decimal.

  1. a) 35%
  2. b) 135%
  3. c) 0.35%
Answer a) 0.35 b) 1.35 c) 0.0035
Misconception target

0.35% = 0.35. Zoom into one small square on a hundred grid.

5★ Core
Opens after: FR2 — Fractions, Section 2

Shade the area model to find 2/3 × 1/2. Write the answer.

Answer 2 of 6 parts overlap: 2/6 = 1/3.
6★ Core
Opens after: IN2 — Integers, Section 2

Find each answer. Then explain why they have different signs.

  1. a) (−5) + (−6)
  2. b) (−5) × (−6)
Answer a) −11. b) 30. Adding two negatives goes further below zero. Multiplying two negatives gives a positive. The "two negatives make a positive" rule is only for × and ÷.
Misconception target

"Two negatives make a positive" used for adding.

7★ Core
Opens after: SQ3 — Squares and Square Roots, Section 3

Mark √30 and √60 on the number line. Between which whole numbers is each one?

Answer √30 is between 5 and 6 (25 < 30 < 36). √60 is between 7 and 8 (49 < 60 < 64), closer to 8.
8★ Core
Opens after: RR3 — Ratios and Rates, Section 3

Hockey tape: 4 rolls for $9.00 or 6 rolls for $12.60. Which is the better buy?

Answer 4 rolls: $2.25 each. 6 rolls: $2.10 each. The 6-roll pack is the better buy.
Misconception target

"Bigger pack is always the better deal" (here it happens to be true, but students must show the unit prices).

9★ Core
Opens after: PC2–PC3 — Percents, Sections 2–3

Your family's dinner costs $50. Add 5% GST and a 15% tip. Fill in the receipt.

10★ Core
Opens after: FR3 — Fractions, Section 3

How many 1/3 cup scoops are in 4 cups of flour? Use the picture.

Answer 4 ÷ 1/3 = 12 scoops.
Misconception target

"Dividing always makes smaller." The picture shows 12 scoops.

11★★ Extended
Opens after: RR2 — Ratios and Rates, Section 2

Jen says 2:3 and 4:5 are equal ratios, "because I added 2 to both numbers." Is she right? Explain with a table or a picture.

Answer No. Equal ratios multiply both terms by the same number: 2:3 = 4:6. In 2:3 the second part is 1.5 times the first. In 4:5 it is only 1.25 times.
Misconception target

Adding the same number to make equal ratios.

12★★ Extended
Opens after: SQ2 — Squares and Square Roots, Section 2

Leo says √64 = 32. Explain his mistake. How could he check his answer?

Answer He took half of 64. Check by multiplying: 32 × 32 = 1024, not 64. √64 = 8 because 8 × 8 = 64.
Misconception target

√n = n ÷ 2.

13★★ Extended
Opens after: IN3–IN4 — Integers, Sections 3–4

The low temperatures in Red Deer for 5 days were −12 °C, −8 °C, −3 °C, 1 °C and −8 °C. Find the mean low. Show your steps.

Answer Sum = −30. Mean = (−30) ÷ 5 = −6 °C.
14★★ Extended
Opens after: FR2 — Fractions, Section 2

Max says 21/2 × 2 = 41/2. He multiplied 2 × 2 and kept the 1/2. Find the right answer and explain his mistake.

Answer 21/2 × 2 = 5/2 × 2 = 5. He forgot to multiply the 1/2 by 2 (that is 1 more).
Misconception target

Multiplying mixed numbers part by part.

15★★ Extended
Opens after: PC4 — Percents, Section 4

A $120 jacket is 25% off. A week later the store adds 25% to the sale price. Is the jacket back to $120? Show your work.

Answer 120 × 0.75 = $90. 90 × 1.25 = $112.50. No: the 25% increase is of $90, which is only $22.50.
Misconception target

"Down 25% then up 25% returns to the start."

16★★ Extended
Opens after: RR4 — Ratios and Rates, Section 4

On a map of Alberta, 1 cm = 40 km. Banff and Calgary are about 3.2 cm apart on the map. About how far apart are they? Draw a double number line.

Answer 3.2 × 40 = 128 km (about 130 km).
17★★ Extended
Opens after: FR3 — Fractions, Section 3

Ana wrote 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 4/3 × 1/2 = 4/6. Find her mistake. What is the right answer? How can an estimate help?

Answer She flipped the first fraction. 3/4 × 2 = 3/2 = 11/2. Estimate: a half fits into three quarters more than once, so the answer must be more than 1.
Misconception target

Flipping the first fraction.

18★★ Extended
Opens after: SQ4 — Squares and Square Roots, Section 4

A square garden has an area of 90 m². About how much fence goes around it? Show how you estimated.

Answer 81 < 90 < 100, so the side is about 9.5 m (√90 ≈ 9.49). Fence ≈ 4 × 9.5 = 38 m.

Challenge zone

Try these after the others. Each one uses ideas from more than one topic.

19★★ Extended
Opens after: PC1 + RR1 — Percents and Ratios

A town had 2000 people. Ten years later it has 2600. Write the new population as a percent of the old one. Then write the ratio old : new in simplest form.

Answer 2600 ÷ 2000 = 1.3 = 130%. 2000 : 2600 = 10 : 13.
20★★ Extended
Opens after: IN2 + FR2 — Integers and Fractions

Decide if each answer is positive or negative, and bigger or smaller than 1 in size. Do not calculate. Explain.

  1. a) (−3) × (−1/2)
  2. b) (−3/4) × 8
Answer a) Positive (two negatives); size 11/2, bigger than 1. b) Negative (one negative); size 6, bigger than 1.
21★★ Extended
Opens after: SQ3 + PC2 — Square roots and Percents

A square rink has an area of 400 m². The town makes it 21% bigger in area. About how long is a side of the new rink?

Answer New area = 400 × 1.21 = 484 m². Side = √484 = 22 m (the old side was 20 m).
22★★ Extended
Opens after: RR3 + FR3 — Rates and Fractions

A car uses 6 L of gas every 100 km. The tank holds 45 L. How far can it go on 3/4 of a tank?

Answer 3/4 × 45 = 33.75 L. 33.75 ÷ 6 = 5.625 hundreds of km = 562.5 km.
