6★ Core

Estimate first: is the answer less than 1 or more than 1? Then solve.

  1. a) 7/8 × 1/3 Estimate: less than 1 Answer: 7/24
  2. b) 3 ÷ 3/4 Estimate: more than 1 Answer: 4
7★ Core

A recipe needs 3/4 cup of sugar. You make half the recipe. How much sugar do you need?

Answer 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8 cup
8★ Core

You have 6 m of rope. You cut it into pieces 3/4 m long. How many pieces do you get?

Answer 6 ÷ 3/4 = 6 × 4/3 = 8 pieces
