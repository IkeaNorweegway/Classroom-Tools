Question because

1★ Core

Ben measures 24 sunflower seedlings. He draws the graph with gaps between the bars.

  1. 1. Why should the bars of this histogram touch?
  2. 2. How many seedlings are 10 cm or taller?
  3. 3. Which graph fits "favourite fruit"? Why?
2★ Core

Ava spins this spinner. Then she flips a coin.

  1. 1. Finish the tree.
  2. 2. Write the sample space in set notation.
  3. 3. How many outcomes? Find P(blue, heads).
3★ Core

30 people eat lunch at a seniors' centre. Sam says, "The bump is on the right. It is skewed right."

  1. 1. Which way does the tail point?
  2. 2. Name the shape. Is Sam right?
  3. 3. Will the mean be more or less than the median?
4★ Core

Leo spins a four-colour spinner. Then he rolls a die.

He says P(red and 5) = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12.

  1. 1. How many outcomes are in the table?
  2. 2. Find P(red and 5).
  3. 3. Is Leo right? Explain.
5★ Core

40 Grade 8 students name their favourite after-school activity.

Sports 18, Gaming 10, Music 8, Art 4.

  1. 1. Find each percent.
  2. 2. Find each sector angle. Check that they add to 360°.
  3. 3. Mia wants to show sports sign-ups each year from 2022 to 2025. Should she use a circle graph?
6★ Core

A bag has 4 red and 2 blue counters. Jo takes two, without putting the first back.

Jo says P(blue, then blue) = 2/6 × 2/6.

  1. 1. Fill in the bag after a blue is drawn.
  2. 2. Find P(blue, then blue).
  3. 3. Is Jo right? Are the draws independent?

7★★ Extended

Six friends get a weekly allowance: $10, $12, $12, $15, $16, $85.

Ava says the typical allowance is $25.

  1. 1. Find the mean, median and mode.
  2. 2. Which one did Ava use?
  3. 3. Which best shows a typical allowance? Why?
8★★ Extended

Kai rolls a red die and a blue die. He adds the numbers.

  1. 1. Shade the sums of 8. Find P(sum 8) as a fraction and a percent.
  2. 2. Find P(sum 10 or more).
  3. 3. Kai says a sum of 7 and a sum of 12 are just as likely. Is he right?
9★★ Extended

16 runners' 5 km times, in minutes:

21, 23, 24, 26, 27, 27, 28, 29, 31, 32, 33, 34, 36, 38, 41, 44.

  1. 1. Draw the histogram with intervals of 5.
  2. 2. What does it show that the intervals of 10 hide?
10★★ Extended

A box has 3 chocolate (C) and 7 oat (O) granola bars. Raj takes two without looking. He eats the first.

  1. 1. Fill in the tree.
  2. 2. Find P(both chocolate).
  3. 3. Find P(one of each).
  4. 4. With replacement, P(C, C) = 3/10 × 3/10. Is that more or less than your answer to 2?

11★★ Extended

This spinner is a circle graph. Red is 180°. Blue and yellow are 90° each.

  1. 1. Write each colour as a percent and a probability.
  2. 2. Spin two times. Fill in the P column. Find P(red, then blue).
  3. 3. Find P(same colour both times).
12★★ Extended

24 Grade 7 and 24 Grade 8 students say how many siblings they have.

  1. 1. Find the mode and median for each grade.
  2. 2. Which grade tends to have more siblings? Use the medians.
  3. 3. Pick one student from each grade at random. Find P(both have 0 siblings).
