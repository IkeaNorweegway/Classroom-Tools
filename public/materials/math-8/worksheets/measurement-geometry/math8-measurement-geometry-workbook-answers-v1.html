1★ Core
Opens after: PY2 — Pythagorean Theorem, Section 2

A TV screen is 36 cm tall and 48 cm wide. How long is the diagonal?

Answer √(1296 + 2304) = √3600 = 60 cm
2★ Core
Opens after: SV1 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 1

Which object does this net make? How many faces does it have?

Answer A triangular prism. 5 faces (2 triangles, 3 rectangles).
3★ Core
Opens after: VT1 — Views and Transformations, Section 1

Draw the top, front and right side views.

4★ Core
Opens after: SV4 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 4

Find the volume of the cereal box: 25 cm × 8 cm × 30 cm.

Answer 25 × 8 × 30 = 6000 cm³
5★ Core
Opens after: VT3 — Views and Transformations, Section 3

Triangle A(1, 1), B(4, 1), C(1, 3). Give the image after a translation 3 left and 4 down.

Answer A′(−2, −3), B′(1, −3), C′(−2, −1)
6★ Core
Opens after: PY3 — Pythagorean Theorem, Section 3

A 6.5 m ladder leans against a wall. Its foot is 2.5 m from the wall. How high does it reach?

Answer √(42.25 − 6.25) = √36 = 6 m
7★ Core
Opens after: SV2 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 2

Find the surface area of a box 10 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm.

Answer 2(60) + 2(40) + 2(24) = 120 + 80 + 48 = 248 cm²
8★ Core
Opens after: SV3 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 3

A soup can has r = 4 cm and h = 11 cm. How big is the label (the side only)? Use π ≈ 3.14.

Answer 2 × 3.14 × 4 × 11 = 276.32 cm²
9★★ Extended
Opens after: PY1 + PY4 — Pythagorean Theorem, Sections 1 and 4

Kara says a triangle with sides 5, 7 and 9 is a right triangle, "because a² + b² = c² works for all triangles." Explain her mistake. Test the triangle.

Answer The theorem only works for right triangles. Test: 25 + 49 = 74 ≠ 81, so it is not a right triangle.
Misconception target

The theorem used on any triangle.

10★★ Extended
Opens after: SV1 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 1

Ben drew the net of a can with r = 3 cm. He made the rectangle 6 cm long. What is wrong? How long should it be?

Answer 6 cm is the diameter. The rectangle wraps around, so its length is the circumference: 2 × 3.14 × 3 ≈ 18.8 cm.
Misconception target

Cylinder rectangle = diameter.

11★★ Extended
Opens after: VT1 — Views and Transformations, Section 1

Jen drew the front view of this object as one column 4 squares tall. Is she right? Explain.

Answer No. The 1-cube stack is behind the 3-cube stack, so from the front you see only 3 squares (one column, 3 tall). She added the hidden cube.
Misconception target

Counting hidden cubes in a view.

12★★ Extended
Opens after: SV2 + SV4 — Surface Area and Volume

Leah says "surface area and volume are the same thing for a box." Use the cereal box (25 × 8 × 30) to show they are different. Include units.

Answer SA = 2(200) + 2(750) + 2(240) = 2380 cm² (wrapping). V = 6000 cm³ (filling). Different numbers and different units.
Misconception target

SA vs volume. Also check for "6 × one face."

13★★ Extended
Opens after: PY3 — Pythagorean Theorem, Section 3

Omar found the missing leg of a right triangle with hypotenuse 13 and leg 5 like this: 13² + 5² = 194, √194 ≈ 13.9. What did he do wrong? How could he have caught it?

Answer He added instead of subtracting. Correct: √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12. A leg must be shorter than the hypotenuse; 13.9 > 13.
Misconception target

Adding squares to find a leg.

14★★ Extended
Opens after: VT4 — Views and Transformations, Section 4

After a reflection in the x-axis and then a translation 2 left, the image is P′(−1, −3). Find P. Is the image congruent to the original? Explain.

Answer Undo the translation: (1, −3). Undo the reflection: P(1, 3). Yes: reflections and translations keep every length and angle.
Misconception target

"Flipped shapes are not congruent."

15★★ Extended
Opens after: SV4 — Surface Area and Volume, Section 4

A grain bin is 9 m across and 6 m tall. A farmer says turning a box-shaped bin on its side would hold more. First find the cylinder's volume. Then explain whether turning a box changes its volume.

Answer r = 4.5. V = 3.14 × 20.25 × 6 ≈ 381.5 m³. Turning a box does not change its volume: l × w × h is the same in any order.
Misconception target

"Turning changes volume."

Challenge zone

Try these after the others. Each one uses ideas from more than one topic.

16★★ Extended
Opens after: PY2 + SV2 — Pythagorean and Surface Area

A tent is a triangular prism. Its triangle has base 3 m and height 2 m. The tent is 4 m long. Find the slant side, then the surface area (with a floor).

Answer Slant = √(1.5² + 2²) = √6.25 = 2.5 m. SA = 2(1/2 × 3 × 2) + 3 × 4 + 2(2.5 × 4) = 6 + 12 + 20 = 38 m².
17★★ Extended
Opens after: SV3 + SV4 — Surface Area and Volume

Two cans hold about the same amount. Can A: r = 4 cm, h = 10 cm. Can B: r = 5 cm, h = 6.4 cm. Which uses less metal?

Answer Volumes: A = 3.14 × 16 × 10 = 502.4; B = 3.14 × 25 × 6.4 = 502.4. SA: A = 100.48 + 251.2 = 351.68; B = 157 + 200.96 = 357.96. Can A uses a little less metal.
18★★ Extended
Opens after: VT3 + PY2 — Transformations and Pythagorean

Point A(1, 2) is rotated 180° about the origin to A′. How far apart are A and A′? (Hint: draw the right triangle between them.)

Answer A′(−1, −2). Across 2, down 4. Distance = √(4 + 16) = √20 ≈ 4.5 units.
