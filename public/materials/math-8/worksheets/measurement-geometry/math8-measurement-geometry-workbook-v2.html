Question because

1★ Core

A photo is 4 cm by 3 cm. Ben says, "Similar shapes must be the same size."

  1. 1. Divide matching sides. Is copy B similar to the photo?
  2. 2. Is copy C similar to the photo?
  3. 3. Is Ben right? Explain.
2★ Core

A triangle has angles of 48° and 67°.

  1. 1. Find the third angle x.
  2. 2. The second triangle is isosceles. The top angle is 36°. Find y.
3★ Core

Leo says, "Any 6 squares joined together make a cube net."

  1. 1. Fold each pattern in your head. Which one makes a cube?
  2. 2. In pattern B, which two squares land on the same face?
  3. 3. Is Leo right?
4★ Core

A logo is 5 cm by 3 cm. It is enlarged with a scale factor of k = 2.5.

  1. 1. Find the new sides.
  2. 2. A 15 cm by 9 cm poster is shrunk. The new length is 6 cm. Find k and the new width.
  3. 3. Is the poster copy an enlargement or a reduction?
5★ Core

Mia has straws of 4 cm, 5 cm and 10 cm. She says, "Any three straws make a triangle."

  1. 1. Add the two shorter straws. Do they meet?
  2. 2. Which of these make a triangle: 6, 6, 11 or 3, 5, 8?
  3. 3. Two sides are 6 cm and 9 cm. What lengths can the third side be?
6★ Core

A gift box is 4 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm. Ana finds 4 × 3 × 2 = 24. She says, "The surface area is 24 cm²."

  1. 1. Write the area of each face in the net.
  2. 2. Find the surface area.
  3. 3. What did Ana find? Use the right units.
7★ Core

Kai labels this right triangle. He writes 10² + 6² = 8². He says, "136 ≠ 64. So it is not a right triangle."

  1. 1. Which side is the hypotenuse? How do you know?
  2. 2. Check with a² + b² = c².
  3. 3. Use the converse. Is 9, 12, 15 a right triangle? Is 7, 9, 12?

8★★ Extended

A garden is 3 m by 2 m. Ben makes each side 3 times as long. He says, "The area triples too."

  1. 1. Find the new sides.
  2. 2. Find both areas.
  3. 3. How many times as big is the new area? Is Ben right?
9★★ Extended

A can has a diameter of 8 cm and a height of 12 cm. Jo says, "The label is 8 cm long."

  1. 1. Find the length of the label. Use π ≈ 3.14.
  2. 2. Find the area of the label.
  3. 3. Find the surface area of the whole can.
10★★ Extended

A 5.0 m ladder leans on a wall. Its foot is 1.4 m from the wall. Leo adds: 5² + 1.4² = 26.96, so h ≈ 5.2 m.

  1. 1. Which side is the hypotenuse?
  2. 2. Find h to one decimal.
  3. 3. Why can Leo's answer not be right?
11★★ Extended

Triangle ABC has A(1, 1), B(3, 1) and C(1, 3). Kai dilates it by k = 2. He adds 2 and gets A′(3, 3).

  1. 1. Find A′, B′ and C′. Draw the image.
  2. 2. Kai's B′ is (5, 3). How long is his A′B′? How long is AB? Did his triangle grow?
  3. 3. Dilate P(6, 4) by k = 1/2.
12★★ Extended

Here is a building made of cubes.

  1. 1. How many cubes are in it?
  2. 2. Draw the top, front and right side views.
  3. 3. Sam says, "The front view has 5 squares, so there are 5 cubes." Is he right?

Challenge zone

Try these after the others. Each one uses ideas from more than one topic.

13★★ Extended

A hiking map has a scale of 1 cm : 2 km. The trail goes 3.6 cm east, then 4.8 cm north.

  1. 1. Find the real length of each part.
  2. 2. Find the straight-line distance back to the start.
  3. 3. How long is that line on the map? Check it with the scale.
14★★ Extended

A square-based pyramid has a base edge of 16 cm and a height of 6 cm.

  1. 1. Find the slant height s. (Hint: half the base edge is one leg.)
  2. 2. Find the surface area.
