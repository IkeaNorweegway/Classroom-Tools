Section 1 · Slope

Learning target: I can find the slope of a line as rise over run.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture
slopehow steep a line is, and which way it goesa slope of 2 is steeper than a slope of 1/2
risehow far up (or down) you go between two pointsfrom y = 2 to y = 5 is a rise of 3
runhow far across you go between two pointsfrom x = 1 to x = 3 is a run of 2
steepnesshow fast a line climbs as you move rightan expert ski run is steeper than a bunny hill

Steep or gentle?

Picture two ski runs at Lake Louise. Which one is steeper?

The bunny hill drops 100 m over 500 m across. The expert run drops 300 m over 400 m across.

Steepness compares the rise to the run. That comparison is the slope.

Slope

slope = rise/run. Rise is up or down. Run is across.

Bunny hill: 100/500 = 1/5. Expert run: 300/400 = 3/4.

3/4 is bigger than 1/5. A bigger slope means a steeper line.

Slope on a grid

Pick two points on a line. Count across to the second point. That is the run. Then count up. That is the rise.

From A to B, the run is 2 and the rise is 3. The slope is 3/2.

The slope formula

Counting works on a grid. Subtracting works for any two points.

The points are (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂). The little 1 and 2 just name the first and second point.

Slope formula

m = y₂ − y₁/x₂ − x₁. The letter m stands for slope.

Top: change in y (the rise). Bottom: change in x (the run).

Worked Example 1 — Slope from two points

Find the slope through (1, 1) and (4, 7).

Step 1: Rise = 7 − 1 = 6.

Step 2: Run = 4 − 1 = 3.

Step 3: m = 6/3 = 2.

Check: Count on the grid. From P, go across 3 and up 6. You land on Q.

Worked Example 2 — A wheelchair ramp

A ramp at a school in Red Deer rises 1 m. It runs 12 m across. Find its slope.

Step 1: Slope = rise/run.

Step 2: Slope = 1/12.

Step 3: The ramp climbs 1 m for every 12 m across. That is gentle enough for a wheelchair.

Worked Example 3 — Points on both sides of 0

Find the slope through (−2, 1) and (2, 3).

Step 1: Rise = 3 − 1 = 2.

Step 2: Run = 2 − (−2) = 4.

Step 3: m = 2/4 = 1/2.

Explain it Leo used (2, 3) as the first point. He got 1 − 3/−2 − 2. Is his slope the same? Why?
Answer Yes. −2/−4 = 1/2. Both the top and the bottom change sign, so the slope stays the same.
Watch out — a common mistake

"Slope is run over rise. From A to B the slope is 2/3."

Why it is wrong: The run is across (2). The rise is up (3). The rise goes on top.

What is true: Slope = rise/run = 3/2. Think "up over across." A steep line has a big rise, so a big slope.

Your turn

  2. 2. Find the slope through (−1, 2) and (3, 4). m = 4 − 2/3 − (−1) = 2/4 = 1/2
  3. 3. Count the rise and run. Find the slope.

m = 2/3

  1. 4. Hill A has slope 1/4. Hill B has slope 2/3. Which is steeper? Hill B. 2/3 is bigger than 1/4.
  2. 5. Mia says the slope through (1, 1) and (5, 3) is 2. Fix her mistake. She did run over rise: 4/2. Rise 2, run 4, so m = 2/4 = 1/2.

Section 2 · Kinds of slope

Learning target: I can tell if a slope is positive, negative, zero or undefined. I can compare parallel and perpendicular slopes.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture
positive slopethe line goes up to the righta ski lift going up the hill
negative slopethe line goes down to the rightskiing down the hill
zero slopea flat, horizontal line. The rise is 0.a flat parking lot
undefined slopea vertical line. The run is 0.an elevator shaft
parallel lineslines that never meet. They have the same slope.train tracks
perpendicular lineslines that meet at a right anglethe corner of a page

linearthe graph is a straight liney goes up by 2 each time
non-linearthe graph is not a straight linea curve

Four kinds of slope

Read every line from left to right, like reading a page.

Worked Example 1 — A negative slope

Find the slope through (0, 4) and (2, 0).

Step 1: Rise = 0 − 4 = −4. The line goes down, so the rise is negative.

Step 2: Run = 2 − 0 = 2.

Step 3: m = −4/2 = −2. It is negative. The line goes down to the right.

Worked Example 2 — Flat and straight up

Find the slope of each line.

Line A: (1, 3) and (4, 3). m = 3 − 3/4 − 1 = 0/3 = 0.

Line B: (−2, 1) and (−2, 4). m = 4 − 1/−2 − (−2) = 3/0.

You can't divide by 0. What times 0 makes 3? No number works. So the slope of B is undefined.

Explain it Why can't a vertical line have a slope? Use the run in your answer.
Answer The run is 0. Slope = rise ÷ run, and you can't divide by 0. So the slope is undefined.
Watch out — a common mistake

"A flat line has no slope. Its slope is undefined."

Why it is wrong: A flat line has a rise of 0. 0/3 = 0. Zero divided by a number is 0.

What is true: Flat (horizontal): slope 0. Straight up (vertical): slope undefined. Only a run of 0 breaks the division.

Parallel and perpendicular lines

Parallel lines have the same slope. They rise the same amount for every step across.

Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle. Their slopes are negative reciprocals. Flip the fraction and change the sign.

Worked Example 3 — Compare the slopes

Line P has slope 2/3. Line Q has slope 2/3. Line R has slope −3/2.

Step 1: P and Q have the same slope. They are parallel.

Step 2: Flip 2/3 to 3/2. Change the sign: −3/2. That is R's slope.

Step 3: So R is perpendicular to P.

Linear or non-linear?

A linear graph is a straight line. In its table, y changes by the same amount each step.

x0123
y (A)1357
y (B)0149

A goes up 2 each time. It is linear. B goes up 1, then 3, then 5. It is non-linear.

  2. 2. Positive, negative, zero or undefined? A line through (4, −1) and (4, 6). The run is 0. Undefined.
  3. 3. Find the slope of the flat line.

Rise 0, run 5. m = 0/5 = 0.

  1. 4. A line has slope 4. Find the slope of a parallel line and a perpendicular line. Parallel: 4. Perpendicular: −1/4.
  2. 5. x: 0, 1, 2, 3. y: 2, 4, 8, 16. Linear or non-linear? Non-linear. y goes up 2, then 4, then 8. Not the same each time.

Section 3 · y = mx + b

Learning target: I can find m and b in y = mx + b. I can write and graph the equation of a line.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture
y-interceptwhere the line crosses the y-axis. Its x is 0.y = 2x + 1 crosses at (0, 1)
bthe y-value of the y-interceptin y = 2x + 1, b = 1
mthe slopein y = 2x + 1, m = 2
slope-intercept forman equation written y = mx + by = −3x + 4

Where do m and b come from?

Make a table for y = 2x + 1.

x0123
y1357

Each step right, y goes up 2. That is the slope, m = 2.

At x = 0, y = 1. The line crosses the y-axis at (0, 1). So b = 1.

y = mx + b

m is the slope. b is the y-intercept: the line crosses the y-axis at (0, b).

Change b: the line slides

Change m: the line turns

Worked Example 1 — Equation from a graph

Write the equation of the line.

Step 1: The line crosses the y-axis at (0, −1). So b = −1.

Step 2: From (0, −1) to (2, 3): rise 4, run 2. m = 4/2 = 2.

Step 3: y = 2x − 1.

Worked Example 2 — Graph from an equation

Graph y = −1/2x + 3.

Step 1: Plot b first: (0, 3).

Step 2: m = −1/2. Go right 2, down 1. Plot (2, 2). Repeat: (4, 1).

Step 3: Draw a line through the points. Extend it across the grid.

Worked Example 3 — Graph from a slope and one point

A line has slope 3. It goes through (1, 2). Graph it and write its equation.

Step 1: Plot (1, 2).

Step 2: Right 1, up 3: (2, 5). Left 1, down 3: (0, −1).

Step 3: The line crosses the y-axis at (0, −1). So y = 3x − 1.

Check: Put x = 1 into your equation. You should get y = 2.

Explain it Two lines have the same b but different m. Where do they meet? How do you know?
Answer At (0, b) on the y-axis. Both lines cross the y-axis at the same point.
Watch out — a common mistake

"In y = 4x + 2, the slope is 2."

Why it is wrong: The 2 is where the line starts on the y-axis. The 4 is next to x. It tells how much y grows each step.

What is true: m is the number times x: m = 4. b is the number on its own: b = 2.

Your turn
  1. 1. Find m and b. y = −3x + 5. m = −3, b = 5
  2. 2. Find m and b. y = x − 4. m = 1, b = −4
  3. 3. Write the equation of the line.

b = 1. Rise 1, run 2, so m = 1/2. y = 1/2x + 1.

  1. 4. Graph y = −2x + 4. Name two points.

(0, 4), (1, 2), (2, 0). Start at 4, then right 1, down 2.

  1. 5. A line has slope −1 and goes through (2, 3). Write its equation. Left 2, up 2 lands on (0, 5). y = −x + 5.
Retrieval check — close your notes
  1. A line goes through (0, 0) and (3, −6). Find its slope.
    Answer m = −6/3 = −2
  2. What is the slope of a vertical line?
    Answer Undefined.
  3. Line A has slope 5. Line B is perpendicular. Find B's slope.
    Answer −1/5
  4. Find 3(x − 2) when x = −1.
    Answer 3(−3) = −9
  5. ★ Stretch A line is parallel to y = 3x − 2. It crosses the y-axis at (0, 4). Write its equation.
    Answer y = 3x + 4. Same slope, new b.

Section 4 · Intercepts, rate of change and special lines

Learning target: I can find intercepts and graph flat and vertical lines. I can explain slope and b in a real situation.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture
x-interceptwhere the line crosses the x-axis. Its y is 0.(2, 0)
y-interceptwhere the line crosses the y-axis. Its x is 0.(0, −4)
horizontal linea flat line. Every point has the same y.y = 3
vertical linea straight-up line. Every point has the same x.x = −2
rate of changehow much the output changes for each 1 of the input$15 per hour
initial valuethe output when the input is 0a $4 starting fee

Two intercepts

Write each intercept as an ordered pair.

y-intercept: put x = 0. y = 2(0) − 4 = −4. The point is (0, −4).

x-intercept: put y = 0. 0 = 2x − 4. So 2x = 4 and x = 2. The point is (2, 0).

A line that is not flat crosses the x-axis exactly once.

Watch out — a common mistake

"The x-intercept is where x = 0."

Why it is wrong: At x = 0 you are ON the y-axis. That point is the y-intercept.

What is true: The x-intercept sits on the x-axis, so y = 0. The y-intercept sits on the y-axis, so x = 0.

Flat and straight-up lines

A horizontal line has equation y = b. Its slope is 0, so y = 0x + b = b.

A vertical line has equation x = a. Its slope is undefined, so it has no y = mx + b form.

y = 3 never meets the x-axis. It has no x-intercept.

Worked Example 1 — Equations of special lines

Write the equation of each line.

Line A is flat. Every point has y = −1. Equation: y = −1.

Line B is straight up. Every point has x = 4. Equation: x = 4.

Rate of change

In a real situation, the slope is a rate of change. b is the initial value: the output at the start.

Worked Example 2 — Edmonton to Red Deer

A car leaves Edmonton for Red Deer, about 150 km away. It drives 100 km each hour.

Step 1: The slope is 100/1 = 100. The rate of change is 100 km per hour.

Step 2: b = 0. At the start, the car has gone 0 km.

Step 3: Equation: d = 100t. Domain: 0 to 1.5 hours. Range: 0 to 150 km.

Worked Example 3 — A taxi fare

A Calgary taxi charges $4 to start and $2 for each kilometre.

Step 1: The slope is 10/5 = 2. Each km adds $2.

Step 2: b = 4. That is the $4 starting fee, the cost before you move.

Step 3: Equation: C = 2d + 4. A 7 km ride costs 2(7) + 4 = $18.

Worked Example 4 — Draining a tank

A 60 L water tank drains 5 L each minute.

Step 1: The water goes down, so the slope is −5 L per minute.

Step 2: The y-intercept (0, 60) means the tank starts with 60 L.

Step 3: The x-intercept (12, 0) means the tank is empty after 12 minutes.

Explain it A babysitter earns $15 an hour. Why does the graph of pay start at (0, 0), not at (0, 15)?
Answer With 0 hours worked, the pay is $0. So b = 0. The 15 is the slope: $15 more each hour.
Your turn

  2. 2. Write the equation of a horizontal line through (5, −2). y = −2
  3. 3. Write the equation of a vertical line through (5, −2). x = 5
  4. 4. A phone plan costs $30 a month plus $10 for each GB of data. Write the equation. What do m and b mean? C = 10g + 30. m = 10: $10 more for each GB. b = 30: $30 with no data used.
  5. 5. The Calgary Tower elevator rises about 3 m each second. Find the slope. Find the height after 20 s. m = 3 m per second. h = 3t. After 20 s: 3 × 20 = 60 m.
  6. 6. Make up a situation with slope −5 and b = 40. Give a domain and a range. Answers vary. A $40 gift card. You spend $5 a day. Domain: 0 to 8 days. Range: $0 to $40.

Put it all together

From memory — notes closed
  1. Ava earns $12 an hour babysitting. She also gets a $6 bus pass for each job.
    1. a) Write an equation for her pay for one job. b) Find m and b. Say what each means. c) Graph it for 0 to 5 hours.
    Answer a) P = 12h + 6.
    b) m = 12: $12 more for each hour. b = 6: the $6 bus pass, paid even for 0 hours.
    c) Start at (0, 6). Up $12 for each hour. End at (5, 66).
  2. A ski run at Lake Louise drops 240 m over 600 m across. Find its slope. Is it steeper than a run with slope −1/2?
    Answer Going down, m = −240/600 = −2/5. Compare steepness by size: 2/5 = 0.4 and 1/2 = 0.5. The −1/2 run is steeper.
  3. A line goes through (0, 2). It is parallel to y = −3x + 7. Write its equation. Then find its x-intercept.
    Answer y = −3x + 2. 0 = −3x + 2, so x = 2/3. The x-intercept is (2/3, 0).
  4. Noah says y = 5 has an undefined slope. Explain his mistake.
    Answer y = 5 is a flat line. Its rise is 0, so its slope is 0. Vertical lines like x = 5 have undefined slope.
