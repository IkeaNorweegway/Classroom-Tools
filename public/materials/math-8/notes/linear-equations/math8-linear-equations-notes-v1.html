Watch out — a common mistake

"2x + 3 = 11. Divide by 2 first: x + 3 = 5.5."

Why it is wrong: Dividing only the 2x by 2 is not allowed. You would have to divide the 3 too. It is easier to undo the + 3 first.

What is true: Undo the last step first. Subtract 3, then divide by 2. x = 4.

Section 3 · Equations with brackets

Learning target: I can solve equations like 3(x + 2) = 18 in two ways.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture

3(x + 2) means 3 groups of (x + 2).

Watch out — a common mistake

"3(x + 2) = 3x + 2."

Why it is wrong: The picture shows 3 groups of (x + 2). That is 3 bags AND 3 × 2 = 6 blocks.

What is true: Multiply both terms: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6.
