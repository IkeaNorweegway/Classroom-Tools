Watch out — a common mistake

"A box has 6 faces, so SA = 6 × the area of the front."

Why it is wrong: Only a cube has 6 equal faces. The cereal box's faces are three different sizes.

What is true: Find the three pairs and add them: 2(lw) + 2(lh) + 2(wh).

Rectangular prism

SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh (three pairs of rectangles)

Worked Example 3 — A tent (triangular prism)

Triangle: base 6 m, height 4 m, slant sides 5 m. Tent length 8 m.

2 triangles: 2 × (1/2 × 6 × 4) = m²

Bottom rectangle: 6 × 8 = m²

2 slanted rectangles: 2 × (5 × 8) = m²

SA = 24 + 48 + 80 = m²

Explain it Why don't we use the 4 m height for the slanted rectangles?
Connect it

Section 4 · Volume of prisms and cylinders

Learning target: I can find the volume of a prism or a cylinder with V = area of base × height.

Words to know

WordWhat it means (in your own words)Example or picture
volume
cubic units
area of the base (A)
Watch out — a common mistake

"Surface area and volume measure the same thing."

Why it is wrong: Surface area is the wrapping (flat, square units). Volume is the filling (space, cubic units). A box can have a big SA and a small volume.

What is true: SA → cm². Volume → cm³.

Stack layers of the base. Each layer has the same area. The number of layers is the height.

Volume of any prism or cylinder

V = area of the base × height

Rectangular prism: V = l × w × h

Triangular prism: V = (1/2 × b × h of triangle) × length

Cylinder: V = π × r² × h

Worked Example 6 — Cereal box: 20 cm × 6 cm × 30 cm

V = 20 × 6 × 30 = cm³

Worked Example 7 — The tent: triangle b = 6 m, h = 4 m, length 8 m

Area of base: 1/2 × 6 × 4 = m²

V = 12 × 8 = m³

Worked Example 8 — A grain bin: r = 5 m, h = 6 m

Area of base: 3.14 × 5 × 5 = m²

V = 78.5 × 6 = m³

Explain it Why does V = area of base × height work for all three shapes?
Watch out — a common mistake

"If you lay the box on its side, its volume changes."

Why it is wrong: The same space is inside. Only the way you look at it changes.

What is true: 20 × 6 × 30 = 6 × 30 × 20 = 3600 cm³ in any position.
