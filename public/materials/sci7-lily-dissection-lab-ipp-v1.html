Your jobTake a lily apart

Your group gets one lily flower. You will take it apart, one part at a time. You will count the parts. Then you will press the parts flat to keep.

RulesLab rules

  • 🟠Orange pollen stains. Do not rub it.
  • 🚫Do not eat any part of the lily.
  • 🐈Lilies make cats sick. Keep lilies away from cats.
  • ✂️Only your teacher uses the sharp knife.
  • 🧼Wash your hands at the end.

LookThe 4 parts of a lily

A lily has 4 kinds of parts. Each part has a job. The job is to help make seeds.

💡 Remember
Flowers make seeds. Seeds grow new plants.
Word bank
sepal petal stamen pistil
🔑 Stamen
Makes pollen. It has an orange tip.
🔑 Pistil
Makes seeds. It stands in the middle.
⚠️ Watch out
On a lily, sepals look like petals. Sepals are the outside ring.

PressPress the parts flat

Pressing dries the parts flat. Then they last a long time. We use special paper called archival paper.

🔑 Word to know
Archival paper: paper that stays white for many years.
1 Lay down the special paper.
2 Put the parts on it. Parts do not touch.
3 Cover with more paper.
4 Put heavy books on top. Wait 2 weeks.
