The Rock Cycle — Answers

One skill: how igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks form and change · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 2: The Rock Cycle

For the adult: Any rock can become any other type. There is no single fixed order around the cycle.

1. magma or lava · sediment (small pieces) · heat and pressure

2. breaks down · carries away

3. (a) igneous (b) sedimentary (c) metamorphic (d) igneous

4. In sedimentary rock. Living things are buried in the layers. The heat that makes igneous or metamorphic rock usually destroys fossils.

5. It is buried very deep, melts into magma, then cools into igneous rock.

6. Rain and wind wear away the softer rock (weathering and erosion).

  • A harder cap rock on top protects the rock underneath, leaving a pillar.
