Renewable or Non-renewable? — Answers

One skill: sorting energy resources and explaining processing · about 15 minutes

For the adult: Being processed or unprocessed is a different question from being renewable or not.

1. won’t run out (or are replaced quickly) · took millions of years to form and can run out

2. wind R · oil N · sunlight R · coal N · moving water R · natural gas N

3. Examples: dams flood land and change rivers; wind turbines can harm birds; making solar panels uses mining.

4. Any two: gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, plastics, asphalt.

5. Yes. Examples: trees processed into wood pellets for heating; corn processed into ethanol fuel.

6. Answers vary. Many Alberta homes heat with natural gas (non-renewable); electricity comes from a mix including gas, wind and solar.
