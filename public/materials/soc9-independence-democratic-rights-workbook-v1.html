Guiding Question: How did Canada become a more independent nation and expand democratic rights?

How to use this workbook

Part A — Core Questions are short answer with a word bank. Part B — Extended Questions are paragraph-length, no word bank, and ask you to apply unit concepts to a scenario.

Part A — Core Questions (Short Answer)

Word BankBalfour Declaration  ·  Statute of Westminster  ·  sovereignty  ·  foreign policy  ·  Famous Five  ·  Persons Case  ·  legal person  ·  suffrage
Q1Core2 marks

Q2Core2 marks

Part B — Extended Questions (Paragraph Response)

No word bank. Analyze and evaluate — do not just describe.

Q6Extended5 marks
A large company has branch offices across the country. For decades, head office had to approve every branch decision. Then head office says: "From now on, each branch can decide for itself. We'd rather work with you as partners than have you break away."

Was gradual, cooperative independence, like Canada's under the Statute of Westminster, a better path than a sudden break? Use the company scenario to explain your view.

Q7Extended5 marks
A student council changes its rules so any student, not just Grade 12s, can run for a certain position. But students at a partner school, who are also affected by council decisions, still cannot run at all.

Is this rule change a meaningful win, an incomplete one, or both? Use the Persons Case to support your answer.
