Canada in the Second World War — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Canada's own declaration of war

LO 1: I can explain why Canada's own declaration of war showed its independence.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In what year did Canada declare war on Germany? (1 mark)

2. How was Canada's declaration of war different from Britain's? (2 marks)

3. Why does a country deciding for itself to go to war show independence? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Key battles

LO 2: I can describe key battles where Canadians fought.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one battle where Canadians fought during the Second World War. (1 mark)

2. Where was the Battle of Dieppe fought, and what was its result? (2 marks)

3. Why do historians often study Dieppe when they explain Canada's role in the war? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Stories left out

LO 3: I can explain contributions that are often left out of the story, like those of Chinese Canadians and Cree code talkers.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one group whose wartime contribution is often left out of the story. (1 mark)

2. What were Cree code talkers' contribution to Canada's war effort? (2 marks)

3. Why does it matter that the story of the war includes groups like Chinese Canadians? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Canada's place in the world

LO 4: I can explain how the war changed Canada's place in the world.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one organization Canada helped found after the war. (1 mark)

2. How did the war change Canada's role in international affairs? (2 marks)

3. Explain one way Canada's wartime experience shaped its foreign policy afterward. (3 marks)
