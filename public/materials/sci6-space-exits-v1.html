Grade 6 Science — Space: Exit Tickets

Grade 6 Science · Alberta Curriculum

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — The solar system

LO 1: I can describe the objects in our solar system and how far apart they are.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the star at the centre of our solar system. (1 mark)

2. Name one planet and one object that is not a planet in our solar system. (2 marks)

3. Why does it take light from the Sun about eight minutes to reach Earth? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Exploring space

LO 2: I can explain how people explore space with telescopes, probes and rovers.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one tool people use to study space from Earth. (1 mark)

2. How is a rover different from a telescope? (2 marks)

3. Why do we send probes to other planets instead of only observing them from Earth? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Staying in orbit

LO 3: I can explain how satellites stay in orbit.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What is an orbit? (1 mark)

2. What two things work together to keep a satellite moving in orbit instead of flying away or falling down? (2 marks)

3. Explain why a satellite does not need an engine running all the time to stay in orbit. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Life on the International Space Station

LO 4: I can describe what life is like on the International Space Station.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one thing astronauts on the International Space Station need to bring with them from Earth. (1 mark)

2. Why do astronauts on the International Space Station appear to float? (2 marks)

3. Describe one challenge of living on the International Space Station and one way astronauts manage it. (3 marks)
