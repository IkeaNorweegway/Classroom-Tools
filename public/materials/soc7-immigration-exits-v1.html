Immigration After Confederation — Exit Tickets

Grade 7 Social Studies · Alberta Draft Curriculum

Unit Learning Outcome (LO): Students examine immigration in post-Confederation Canada.

One exit ticket per notes section. Every question maps to this LO through one local skill code (IM1–IM6, in `_courses/grade-7-social/_context.md`). 3–4 minutes each, collected, not discussed as a class.

Exit Ticket 1 — Wanted: Settlers

LO: IM1, opportunities created by immigration.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Give one push factor and one pull factor. (1 mark)

2. Why did the government want settlers in the West? (2 marks)

3. What is discrimination? Give an example of what it could look like in an immigration law. (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Free Land

LO: IM2, how access to land influenced settlement.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. How many acres was a homestead? (1 mark)

2. Name two conditions a settler had to meet to keep a homestead. (2 marks)

3. Explain why the homestead offer pulled so many families to the Prairies. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Building the Prairies

LO: IM1, how immigrants built communities and the economy.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /5

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one group that settled in Alberta and where. (1 mark)

2. What is a bloc settlement? (2 marks)

3. How did settlers help Canada's economy grow? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Keeping Some Groups Out

LO: IM4, why the government restricted immigration from certain groups.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Which group had to pay the head tax? (1 mark)

2. How did the Continuous Journey Regulation keep immigrants from India out? (2 marks)

3. Explain why these laws are evidence that discrimination was part of government policy, not just individual prejudice. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 5 — Conflict on the West Coast

LO: IM3, causes and consequences of the Anti-Asian Riots and the Komagata Maru.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In what year did the Anti-Asian Riots happen in Vancouver? (1 mark)

2. Give one cause of the riots. (2 marks)

3. What happened to the passengers of the Komagata Maru, and why? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 6 — Rights, Fairness and Evidence

LO: IM5 and IM6, judging fairness and telling fact from opinion.

Time: 3–4 minutes &nbsp; Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Fact or opinion: "The head tax rose to $500 in 1903." (1 mark)

2. What did Manitoba change about its schools in 1890? (2 marks)

3. Choose one policy from this unit. Was it fair? Give one piece of evidence. (3 marks)
