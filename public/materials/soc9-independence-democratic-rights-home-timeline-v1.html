Steps to Independence

One skill: putting the steps to independence in order and explaining each one · about 15 minutes

For the adult: Canada became independent in steps, not all at once. A timeline helps your child see the order.

Worked example

Worked example

Put these in order: Statute of Westminster, Canada declares war in 1939, Balfour Declaration, First World War.

  1. 1914–1918, First World War: Canada is at war because Britain is.
  2. 1926, Balfour Declaration: Britain and the Dominions agree they are equals.
  3. 1931, Statute of Westminster: that equality becomes law.
  4. 1939: Canada declares war on Germany on its own, a week after Britain.
