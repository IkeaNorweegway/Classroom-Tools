Q22★★ ExtendedOpens after Lesson 1 · CS — technology trade-offs in clean energy
"Smart grids, electric vehicles, and renewable energy infrastructure are designed to reduce emissions and improve energy efficiency. But they have unintended consequences: mining rare earth metals for batteries and solar panels disturbs ecosystems; rapid expansion of electric vehicles creates e-waste; connecting more renewable sources to the electrical grid can cause instability when supply and demand are mismatched."

Part A: Give one intended benefit of electric vehicles (EVs).

Part B: Give one unintended consequence of electric vehicles.

Part C: Does an unintended consequence mean the technology is a bad idea? Explain your thinking. Use the word "trade-off" in your answer.

Answer Part A: Intended benefit: EVs reduce greenhouse gas emissions by eliminating combustion / reduce dependence on fossil fuels for transportation.
Part B: Unintended consequence: Mining rare earth metals for EV batteries disturbs ecosystems / EVs create e-waste at end of battery life / grid instability from variable renewable supply.
