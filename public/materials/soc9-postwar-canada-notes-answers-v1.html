Section 3: Immigration, Multiculturalism, and Indigenous Policy

Blank / PromptExpected Answer
Non-European immigration regions (any two)India, China, Vietnam (etc.)

Retrieval Check answers:

  1. Any two: India, China, Vietnam.
  2. Both groups of newcomers (postwar displaced persons and later non-European immigrants) reshaped Canada's population and pushed it toward greater diversity and, eventually, an official policy of multiculturalism.
  3. Residential schools and the Sixties Scoop separated Indigenous children from their families and cultures, causing lasting intergenerational harm — even as Canada was simultaneously expanding rights and diversity for other groups.
