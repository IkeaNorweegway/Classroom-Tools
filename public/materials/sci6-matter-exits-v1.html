Grade 6 Science — Matter: Exit Tickets

Grade 6 Science · Alberta Curriculum

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — The particle model

LO 1: I can describe solids, liquids and gases using the particle model.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the three states of matter. (1 mark)

2. Draw or describe how the particles are arranged in a solid and in a gas. (2 marks)

3. Why can you smell perfume across a room, even though the particles are too small to see? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Changing state

LO 2: I can explain melting, freezing, evaporating and condensing.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Match each change to its name: melting, freezing, evaporating, condensing. (2 marks)

(a) liquid water becomes water vapour _______________

(b) ice becomes liquid water _______________

2. A puddle disappears on a hot, sunny day. Name the change of state and explain what happens to the particles. (2 marks)

3. Why does a cold can of pop collect water droplets on its outside on a warm day? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Thermal expansion

LO 3: I can explain why things expand when they’re heated.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What happens to the size of most materials when they are heated? (1 mark)

2. Use the particle model to explain why a thermometer's liquid rises when the thermometer warms up. (2 marks)

3. Why are gaps left between sections of a bridge or railway track? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — Why ice floats

LO 4: I can explain why ice floats.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Is ice more dense or less dense than liquid water? (1 mark)

2. When water freezes, what happens to the space between its particles? (2 marks)

3. Using the idea of density, explain why an ice cube floats in a glass of water. (3 marks)
