Evolving Citizenship and Rights — Exit Tickets

Social Studies 9 · Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Categories of people in Canada

LO 1: I can tell citizens, permanent residents, temporary residents and refugees apart.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Who is a citizen of Canada? (1 mark)

2. How does a permanent resident differ from a temporary resident? (2 marks)

3. Why might a refugee not be able to return to their home country? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Laws from 1947 to 1982

LO 2: I can trace how laws from 1947 to 1982 changed rights in Canada.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. What law created Canadian citizenship in 1947? (1 mark)

2. What did the Canadian Bill of Rights (1960) add to Canadian law? (2 marks)

3. Why did Canada's rights change again with the Charter in 1982? (3 marks)
