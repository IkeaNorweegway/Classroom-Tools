Step 1: Define the Story

The brief: Build an interactive Scratch story with original characters and at least [confirm number] scenes, using a clear beginning, problem, solution, and end. Your theme and characters are entirely your choice. Two requirements apply to every story:

  • At least one branch point — a moment where the story splits based on a choice, not just one fixed path
  • It will be shared with a real audience beyond your own class

What does "done" look like for your story — what would need to be true for someone outside your class, who's never seen it, to understand it start to finish?

Who is your real audience going to be, and does that change any choices you're making?

Reflection

What's the single biggest difference between your storyboard and the finished story?

Did you get your events/broadcasts working because you understood how they connect, or because you kept guessing until something worked? How do you know?

Now that your audience is people outside your class, is there anything you'd explain differently than you would to a classmate who already knows the story? What, and why?

If you had one more iteration, what would you change and why?
