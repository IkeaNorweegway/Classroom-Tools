Grade 6 Science — Energy Resources: Exit Tickets

Grade 6 Science · Alberta Curriculum

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Renewable or non-renewable

LO 1: I can tell renewable and non-renewable resources apart.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Is wind energy renewable or non-renewable? (1 mark)

2. Name one non-renewable resource and explain why it is non-renewable. (2 marks)

3. Why can a forest be a renewable resource, but coal cannot? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Before and after processing

LO 2: I can explain what happens to a resource before and after processing.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the resource that comes out of the ground before it is processed. (1 mark)

2. What changes to a resource during processing, and why is this needed? (2 marks)

3. Give an example of a resource in its raw form and the product made from it after processing. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Comparing energy sources

LO 3: I can compare energy sources using factors like cost and the environment.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name two factors you could use to compare energy sources. (1 mark)

2. Compare solar and natural gas using one cost factor and one environmental factor. (2 marks)

3. Which energy source would you choose for a rural school, and why? Use two factors. (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — No perfect energy source

LO 4: I can explain why no energy source is perfect.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of hydroelectric power. (2 marks)

2. Why might an energy source be cheap to run but still harmful to the environment? (2 marks)

3. Explain why a community might use more than one energy source. (2 marks)
