Grade 7 Social Studies — Building Canada's Economy

Practice Workbook

Questions are mixed on purpose. Figuring out what each question is asking is part of the learning.
Learning Outcome: Students examine the Canadian government's role in developing the economy post-Confederation.

How this workbook works

Questions from across the unit are mixed together on purpose. Your job is to figure out what each question is asking before you answer it. That thinking is where the learning happens.

★ Core For everyone. Try these first.
★★ Extended More challenging. Try after Core, or as a challenge.

Show your thinking. A correct answer with no explanation earns part marks on Extended questions.

11★★ Extended

14★★ Extended

Challenge Zone

Try these after the core and extended questions. Each one asks you to pull ideas from more than one part of the unit.

S1★★

The National Policy helped Central Canada more than the West. Equalization tries to reduce differences between regions. Compare the two: what was each trying to do, and who benefited from each?

S2★★

Trace the path of one bushel of Prairie wheat in 1900, from the field to a buyer in Britain, and name every part of the economy it touched: farming, the railway, banks and trade.

S3★★

Answer the guiding question in a strong paragraph: How did the Canadian government help shape the economy after Confederation? Use at least three government decisions and one trade-off.
