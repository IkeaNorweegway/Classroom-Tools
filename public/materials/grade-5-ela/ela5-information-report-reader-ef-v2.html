Regions of Alberta Reader

Six short texts about the land of Alberta
Edition C

About these texts

These texts give facts. They have headings that tell you what each part is about. Bold words are important words.

Six Regions, One Province

Text 1

1Alberta is big. The land is not the same everywhere.

2There are six regions. In one region, the land, plants and animals are alike.

The six regions

3The Grassland is dry and flat. The Parkland has trees and grass. The Rocky Mountains are very high. The Foothills are hills by the mountains. The Boreal Forest is a big forest in the north. The Canadian Shield is old rock and lakes.

They are connected

4Snow falls in the mountains. It melts into a river. The river goes to the city. Then it waters the farms. One drop of water helps three regions!

region a part of the land where things are alike

forest a place with lots of trees

The Grassland

Text 2

1The Grassland is flat. You can see very far. There are not many trees.

A dry, windy land

2It does not rain much. The wind blows a lot. Summers are hot.

Grasses with deep roots

3Grass grows here. Its roots go deep down. They find water. If a fire comes, the roots live. The grass grows back.

Animals

4The pronghorn runs very fast. Owls live in holes in the ground.

The badlands

5Some hills are bare and striped. People find dinosaur bones there.

roots the part of a plant under the ground

pronghorn a fast animal like a deer

Mountains Make the Weather

Text 3

1Our weather comes from the west.

Up and over

2Wet air comes to the mountains. The air goes up. It gets cold. Snow falls on the mountains.

The rain shadow

3Now the air is dry. The land past the mountains gets little rain. This dry land is in a rain shadow.

The chinook

4Sometimes a warm wind blows down from the mountains. It is called a chinook. The snow melts fast!

rain shadow dry land on the far side of mountains

chinook a warm, dry wind in winter

The Parkland and the Boreal Forest

Text 4

The Parkland

1The Parkland has groups of trees and fields of grass. The soil is black and good for farms. Most people in Alberta live here.

The Boreal Forest

2The Boreal Forest is in the north. It is the biggest region. It has lots of trees and wet land called muskeg. Moose and wolves live here. Not many people live here.

Same and different

3Both have aspen trees. Both have cold winters. But the Parkland has farms and cities. The Boreal Forest is a thick forest.

soil dirt that plants grow in

muskeg wet, soft land in the north

The Rocky Mountains

Text 5

1The Rocky Mountains are in the west. They are the highest land in Alberta.

Three zones

2At the bottom, it is warm. There are trees and grass. In the middle, there are big trees and lots of snow. At the top, it is too cold for trees. Small plants grow on the rocks.

Ice

3Some snow never melts. It turns into ice called a glacier.

Be safe

4Stay far away from elk and bears. Never feed wild animals.

zone one part or level

glacier a big, thick sheet of ice

How Ice Shaped Alberta

Text 6

1Long ago, ice covered Alberta.

Step 1: Old rock

2Under the land is very old rock.

Step 2: The ice comes

3It got very cold. Snow piled up and turned to ice. The ice was very thick.

Step 3: The ice moves

4The ice moved slowly. It scraped the land. It carried big rocks.

Step 4: The ice melts

5It got warm. The ice melted. The water made rivers and lakes.

Step 5: A new land

6Plants grew. Animals and people came.

scrape rub hard and take off the top

melt turn from ice to water
