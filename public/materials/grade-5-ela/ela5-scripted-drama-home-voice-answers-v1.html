One Line, Many Meanings — Answers

One skill: using voice and body to change the meaning of a line · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 4: Three kinds of language on stage

For the adult: There are no single right answers. Look for these signs.

Signs of strong answers

1–2. Different feelings are clearly different: your child changes volume, pace, pitch, stressed word and face, not just one.

3. Example: flat or shaky voice, looking away, a long pause before "fine."

4. Stressing She ("She got a new bike") or new.

5. Breathing from the belly, standing tall, facing the listener, speaking with energy (not screaming).
