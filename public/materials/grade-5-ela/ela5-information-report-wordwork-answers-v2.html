How this booklet fits with Words Their Way

Students keep their own WTW sort. This booklet adds explicit morphology from the Grade 5 affix list (here, the affixes that build geography and science words), Tier 2 and Tier 3 words from the reader, spelling from memory, sentence combining with facts about Alberta's regions, and one conventions target per week. Earlier weeks and the Tall Tales unit come back as review. Items marked Extended are optional. Codes: VO4, VO10, VO11, VO12, CV1, CV2, CV5, CV10, CV12–14.

Sentence combining. The Grassland is in southeastern Alberta. It is dry. It is windy.

Answer The Grassland, in southeastern Alberta, is dry and windy.

Conventions: capitals in titles, headings and place names (CV1). Fix: the grassland: wide open prairie near the red deer river

Answer The Grassland: Wide Open Prairie near the Red Deer River

Conventions: commas in a list (CV2). Add the commas.

The Boreal Forest is home to moose wolves lynx and woodland caribou.

Answer The Boreal Forest is home to moose, wolves, lynx and woodland caribou.

Sentence combining with because: Few trees grow in the Grassland. There is very little rain.

Answer Few trees grow in the Grassland because there is very little rain.

Richardson's ground squirrels are often called gophers. Rewrite with parentheses.

Week 5

Morphology: -ment and -tion (turn a verb into a thing).

VerbThing
settlesettlement

developdevelopment

Conventions: transition words (WR5, CV10). Join with for example, as a result or also. Use each once.

  1. Some animals live above the treeline. Mountain goats live among the rocks.
  2. A chinook blows in. The snow melts in an afternoon.
  3. The Parkland has rich soil. The Parkland has most of Alberta's cities.

Answer 1. Some animals live above the treeline; for example, mountain goats live among the rocks. 2. A chinook blows in. As a result, the snow melts in an afternoon. 3. The Parkland has rich soil. It also has most of Alberta's cities.

Week 6

Mixed review, from memory

  1. Related to nature = natural
  2. Bring goods in = import
  3. Carry across = transport
  4. Make protect into a thing: protection
  5. What does sub- mean? under, below
  6. Fix: the river flow east → the river flows east
  7. Add commas: spruce pine aspen and fir → spruce, pine, aspen and fir

Extended: Make three new words from these parts: trans-, ex-, sub-, port, -ment, -al.
