Reader's theatre: Paul Bunyan and the Blue Snow

Parts: Narrator 1 · Narrator 2 · Paul Bunyan · Sourdough Sam (the cook) · Logger 1 · Logger 2 · Babe the Blue Ox (sound effects and one line!) · Skater

Paul Bunyan and the Blue Snow

NARRATOR 1Gather round, folks, and we'll tell you about the coldest winter that ever was.

NARRATOR 2It was the Winter of the Blue Snow, and it happened in Paul Bunyan's logging camp.

PAUL(booming) GOOD MORNING, LOGGERS!

NARRATOR 1When Paul said good morning, the trees in the next valley fell over.

LOGGER 1Morning, Paul! Say, what's that falling from the sky?

LOGGER 2It's snow. But it's… blue!

NARRATOR 2Blue flakes as big as dinner plates came floating down. And then came the cold.

LOGGER 1(shivering) It's so cold, my words are freezing in the air! Look, there goes "Good morning," right onto the ground.

LOGGER 2Pick it up and carry it inside, or nobody will ever hear it!

NARRATOR 1Out in a snowdrift, Paul heard something small and sad.

BABE(tiny voice) Mooo…

PAULWell, look at you, little one. Blue as the sky and shivering like a leaf. Come inside my coat.

NARRATOR 2Paul named her Babe. Watch her for an hour, and you could see her grow.

BABE(bigger voice) MOOOO!

NARRATOR 1But the camp had a new problem.

LOGGER 1Paul, we're starving!

LOGGER 2Hungry loggers can't chop trees!

SAMI'm flipping pancakes as fast as I can, boss, but these fellows eat a hundred stacks each!

PAULThen we need a bigger griddle, Sam. The biggest griddle in the world.

NARRATOR 2Paul built a griddle so wide you couldn't see across it on a foggy day.

SAMBut how will I grease a griddle that big?

SKATERLeave it to us! Strap some bacon to our boots!

NARRATOR 1Fifty skaters zipped back and forth across the hot iron.

SKATER(skating) Whee! Sizzle, sizzle, slide!

SAMPancakes! Pancakes by the wagonload! Babe, haul them out!

BABE(proudly) MOOOOOO!

LOGGER 1(mouth full) Best… pancakes… ever!

LOGGER 2Now let's get back to work!

NARRATOR 2That winter the camp cut more wood than any camp in history.

NARRATOR 1And when spring came, every frozen word thawed out at once.

ALL(all talking at once) GOOD MORNING! PASS THE SYRUP! WATCH OUT FOR THAT TREE! MOOOO!

NARRATOR 2For three days nobody could hear a thing.

PAUL(whispering, for once) And that, folks, is the Winter of the Blue Snow.

Rehearsal checklist: I highlighted my lines I practised them three times I know which word in each line to stress I pause at commas and periods My voice matches the character

Repeated reading passages

Read the passage to your partner. The partner follows along and marks any word you miss. Use the numbers to count the words you read in one minute. If you finish before the minute is up, start again at the top and keep counting. Then read it again, and again. Try to sound like a storyteller, not a race car.
