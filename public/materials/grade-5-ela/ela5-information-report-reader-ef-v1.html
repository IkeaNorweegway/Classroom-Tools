Body Systems Reader

Six short texts about how the body works
Edition C

About these texts

These texts give facts. They have headings that tell you what each part is about. Bold words are important words.

Your Body Is a Team

Text 1

1Your body is made of tiny parts called cells.

2Cells make organs, like the heart. Organs work in teams. A team is called a system.

The systems

3The heart moves blood. The lungs take in air. The stomach breaks down food. Bones hold you up. Muscles move you. The brain is the boss.

They work together

4When you kick a ball, your brain tells your muscles to move. You breathe faster. Your heart beats faster. All the systems help!

cell the smallest living part

organ a body part with a job

system organs that work as a team

The Heart

Text 2

1Your heart is a muscle. It is as big as your fist. It beats all day and all night.

What it does

2The heart pumps blood. Blood goes to the lungs to get oxygen. Then the heart pumps the blood to the whole body.

Blood vessels

3Blood moves in tubes called blood vessels. Some are very thin, thinner than a hair.

Try it: Put two fingers on your wrist. Can you feel your heart beat? That is your pulse.

pump push out

oxygen a gas in the air we need to live

pulse the beat you can feel

Breathing

Text 3

1You breathe in air. The air has oxygen. Your body needs oxygen.

The path of air

2Air goes in your nose. It goes down a tube to your lungs. In the lungs, oxygen goes into your blood.

Breathing out

3When you breathe out, you get rid of a gas your body does not need.

When you run

4When you run, you breathe faster. Your muscles need more oxygen.

lungs two soft bags in your chest that hold air

breathe take air in and out

Bones and Muscles

Text 4

Bones

1You have 206 bones. Bones hold you up. Your skull keeps your brain safe. Your ribs keep your heart and lungs safe.

Muscles

2Muscles move your bones. Muscles can only pull. So they work in pairs. One pulls your arm up. One pulls it down.

Alike and different

3Bones and muscles are both alive. Bones are hard. Muscles are soft and can get short and long.

skull the bone around your brain

ribs the bones around your chest

The Brain

Text 5

1Your brain is the boss of your body. It helps you think and remember. It keeps your heart beating.

Messages

2Your brain sends messages on nerves. Nerves go to every part of your body.

Ouch!

3If you touch something hot, your hand moves away fast. That is called a reflex.

Take care of your brain

4Sleep helps your brain. Wear a helmet when you ride a bike.

nerve a thin line that carries messages

reflex a very fast move you do not think about

From Sandwich to Energy

Text 6

1Food has to be broken into tiny parts. This is called digestion.

Step 1: Mouth

2You chew the food. Spit makes it soft.

Step 2: Down the tube

3You swallow. The food goes down a tube to your stomach.

Step 3: Stomach

4The stomach mixes the food with juices.

Step 4: Small intestine

5The tiny parts go into your blood. Your blood takes them to your cells.

Step 5: Large intestine

6What is left goes out when you use the bathroom.

digestion breaking food down

stomach a stretchy bag that mixes food
