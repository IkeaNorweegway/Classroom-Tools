Lesson 1 · Where do I stand?

Four corners. Your teacher reads each statement. Move to a corner (strongly agree, agree, disagree, strongly disagree), then record where you stood.

StatementMy cornerOne reason
Recess should be longer.
Recess is just for fun.
Kids should help make school rules.

Brain dump. What do you already know about how adults make decisions at school? Who decides how long recess is?

My first opinion: Should recess be longer? Write one sentence. We'll come back to it in Lesson 30.

Lesson 9 · Fair and unfair persuasion

Use Anchor note 6. Label each sentence fair or unfair and explain.

SentenceFair or unfairWhy

Your turn. Write one persuasive sentence for your side using a fair technique. Name the technique.
