From Story to Script

One skill: turning a few lines of a story into a script · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 5: From story to script

NameDate

For the adult: In class, your child is turning stories into short plays. In a script, the character's name comes first with a colon, there are no quotation marks, and stage directions in brackets tell the actor what to do or how to say the line. Try it with a favourite book, a fairy tale, or something funny that happened at home.

Worked example

Worked example

Story: "Is anyone home?" called Goldilocks, pushing the door open. Nobody answered, so she tiptoed inside.

Script:

GOLDILOCKS: (pushing the door open, calling) Is anyone home?

(Silence. She tiptoes inside.)

Your turn

1. Turn this into a script line: "I'm starving!" shouted Max, dropping his backpack.

2. Turn this into a script line: Mom smiled and whispered, "Look who's asleep."

3. Choose two sentences of dialogue from a book at home. Copy them as a story, then write them as a script.

4. Write a three-line script about something funny that happened in your family. Include at least one stage direction.

5. Perform your script with your adult. What stage direction did you add after you acted it?
