Signal from the Future: a radio drama

Parts: Narrator · Ava (age 11) · Ben (age 10, her brother) · Radio Voice (Ava, 40 years older) · Mom · Sound-effects team

Signal from the Future

SOUND(wind howling; a door creaks)

NARRATORThe attic of the old farmhouse was dusty and dark, and in the corner sat a radio older than anyone could remember.

BEN(whispering) Ava, Mom said not to touch anything up here.

AVAI'm just looking. Help me with this switch.

SOUND(a click; static: shhhhhhh)

NARRATORThe radio crackled to life. For a moment there was only static. Then, a voice.

RADIO VOICE(crackly) Hello? Is this… is this the attic on Pine Ridge Road?

AVA(shocked) Who is this?

RADIO VOICEAva. It's me. It's you. I'm calling from the year 2066.

BEN(gasps) That's… forty years from now!

RADIO VOICEListen carefully, I don't have much time. Tomorrow, the town will vote on whether to cut down Miller's Woods to build a parking lot.

AVAI know. Everybody says it doesn't matter.

RADIO VOICEIt matters. In my time, the woods are gone, and the creek is dry, and the birds… (static) …you won't hear them anymore.

SOUND(static rises, then fades)

BENAva, what are we supposed to do? We're kids.

RADIO VOICEKids can speak. Go to the meeting. Tell them what the woods mean to you. One voice can—

SOUND(static; a long beep)

NARRATORAnd then the radio went silent.

MOM(calling from downstairs) Ava! Ben! Dinner!

AVA(slowly) Ben… what if it was real?

BENWhat if it wasn't?

AVAThen we go to a meeting for nothing. (pause) But what if it was?

NARRATORThe next evening, the town hall was packed. And when the mayor asked if anyone else wished to speak, a hand went up near the back. A small hand.

AVA(nervous, then stronger) My name is Ava. I'm eleven. Miller's Woods is where I learned the names of birds…

NARRATORForty years later, in a house on Pine Ridge Road, a woman sat by a window, listening to the birds in a forest that was still standing. She reached over and switched off an old radio. And she smiled.

SOUND(birdsong)

Rehearsal checklist: My voice shows my character's feelings I pause where the script says Sound effects come at the right moment I slow down at the most important line
