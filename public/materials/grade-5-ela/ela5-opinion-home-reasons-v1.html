Reasons and Evidence

One skill: backing up an opinion with a reason and evidence · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: The parts of an opinion piece · Section 5: Judging evidence

NameDate

For the adult: In class, your child is writing an opinion letter using TREE: a Topic sentence (the opinion), Reasons, Explanations with evidence, and an Ending. A reason says why; evidence proves the reason (a fact, a number, an example). Pick a friendly family debate: a pet, a later bedtime, what to have for dinner on Friday.

Worked example

Worked example

Opinion: Our family should have pizza night every Friday.

  1. Reason: It would save time on a busy day.
  2. Evidence: On Fridays we get home from swimming at 6:30, and pizza takes 15 minutes to arrive.
  3. The other side: Someone might say pizza every week isn't healthy. Answer: We could add a salad and make it at home.
