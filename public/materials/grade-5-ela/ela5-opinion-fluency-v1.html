Recess Radio: a call-in show

Parts: Host · Caller 1 (Jonah, student) · Caller 2 (Principal Delorme) · Caller 3 (Mr. Nguyen, parent) · Caller 4 (Ms. Kaur, supervisor) · Caller 5 (Ava, Grade 3) · Producer

Recess Radio

HOSTGood afternoon, Prairie View! You're listening to Recess Radio, where today's question is: should lunch recess be longer? The lines are open. First caller, you're on the air.

JONAHHi! I'm Jonah, Grade 5. Here's my point: twenty minutes is not enough. By the time we zip our coats and pick teams, the bell rings. We need ten more minutes.

HOSTStrong words, Jonah. Do you have evidence?

JONAHCanada's guidelines say kids need sixty minutes of hard activity a day, and most kids don't get it. Recess is where we get it.

HOSTThank you. Our next caller is a name you'll know.

PRINCIPALHello, it's Principal Delorme. I love that students care about this. But here's my concern: if recess gets longer, something else gets shorter. Where do those ten minutes come from?

HOSTA fair question. Line three, you're on.

MR. NGUYENHi, I'm a parent. I think we're asking the wrong question. Kids spend half of recess looking for a ball! Let's make recess better, not longer.

HOSTInteresting! Line four?

MS. KAURHi, I'm Ms. Kaur, one of the recess supervisors. There are three of us watching more than three hundred students. Before anyone adds time, please think about who's out there in January when it's minus twenty.

HOSTThat's a perspective we haven't heard yet. Thank you. One more caller.

AVAHi, I'm Ava, I'm in Grade 3. I don't care if it's longer. I just want a turn on the swings!

HOST(laughing) Ava, that may be the most important point of the day. Producer, any last messages?

PRODUCEROur survey says one hundred twenty-six out of one hundred eighty students want longer recess, but almost as many want better equipment and games.

HOSTSo maybe the answer is: a little longer, and a lot better? The council votes March third. Until then, keep talking, and keep listening. This has been Recess Radio!

Rehearsal checklist: I stress my opinion word I slow down for numbers I sound like my character I pause before my most important point

Repeated reading passages

Read to your partner; count words in one minute (start again if you finish early). Read it again. Sound convinced: stress the opinion and the evidence.
