Grade 6 Science — Living Systems: Exit Tickets

Grade 6 Science · Alberta Curriculum

One ticket per cluster · 3–4 minutes each · Independent, no notes · Not for marks

Exit Ticket 1 — Living and non-living parts

LO 1: I can sort the living (biotic) and non-living (abiotic) parts of an ecosystem.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one living (biotic) and one non-living (abiotic) part of a pond. (2 marks)

2. Is a fallen log in a forest biotic or abiotic? Explain. (2 marks)

3. Why does a change in water temperature affect the fish in a lake? (2 marks)

Exit Ticket 2 — Photosynthesis

LO 2: I can explain how plants make food by photosynthesis.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name the three things a plant needs for photosynthesis. (1 mark)

2. What does a plant make during photosynthesis, and what does it release? (2 marks)

3. Why do plants need sunlight, even though they make their own food? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 3 — Food webs and energy

LO 3: I can build a food web and follow the energy.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. In a food chain, a grasshopper eats grass and a frog eats the grasshopper. Which is the producer? (1 mark)

2. Draw an arrow from the sun to the grass, then from the grass to the grasshopper, then from the grasshopper to the frog. What does each arrow show? (2 marks)

3. Why is there usually less energy available at the top of a food chain than at the bottom? (3 marks)

Exit Ticket 4 — When an ecosystem changes

LO 4: I can explain what happens when one part of an ecosystem changes.

Marks: /6

Name: _________________________ Date: ____________

1. Name one thing that could cause a change in a forest ecosystem. (1 mark)

2. In a food web, what could happen to the frogs if all the grasshoppers disappeared? (2 marks)

3. Explain why one change in an ecosystem can affect many other living things. (3 marks)
