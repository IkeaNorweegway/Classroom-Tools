Step 6: Decide the Sensor Logic a Solver Would Need

What sensors (at least two) will a solving robot need, and what will each one need to detect in your challenge?

Step 7: Write the Specification

Write a specification precise enough that a group who has never seen your maze could build it and understand what a solving robot needs to do. Include a labeled diagram — treat this with the same care as a real design deliverable.

Example — too vague: "Put a wall near the start so the robot has to avoid it."

Example — precise enough to build from: "Place a solid wall, 15 cm tall, spanning the full width of the corridor, with its near edge exactly 30 cm from the start line. The gap between the wall's far edge and the opposite corridor wall is 12 cm."
