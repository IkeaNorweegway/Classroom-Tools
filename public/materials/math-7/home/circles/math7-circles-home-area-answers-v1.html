Area of a Circle — Answers

One skill: area of a circle, and choosing area or circumference · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Area of a circle · Section 4: Circle problems

For the adult: The most common slip is using the diameter in the area rule. That makes the answer 4 times too big. The second is writing cm² for a circumference. The distance around is a length, so it is in cm.

Answers

1. 2 × 2 = 4. A ≈ 3.14 × 4 = 12.56 cm².

2. 10 × 10 = 100. A ≈ 3.14 × 100 = 314 m².

3. r = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 cm. 8 × 8 = 64. A ≈ 3.14 × 64 = 200.96, about 201.0 cm².

4. The arm is the radius. 100 × 100 = 10 000. A ≈ 3.14 × 10 000 = 31 400 m².

5. Crust: C ≈ 3.14 × 30 = 94.2 cm. Cheese: r = 15 cm, A ≈ 3.14 × 15 × 15 = 706.5 cm².

6. A: 3.14 × 1 × 1 = 3.14 m². B: 3.14 × 2 × 2 = 12.56 m². 12.56 ÷ 3.14 = 4. B's area is 4 times as big.

7. No. Area uses the radius, 3 cm. A ≈ 3.14 × 3 × 3 = 28.26, about 28.3 cm².
