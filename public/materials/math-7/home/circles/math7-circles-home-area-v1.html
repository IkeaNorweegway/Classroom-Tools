Area of a Circle

One skill: area of a circle, and choosing area or circumference · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Area of a circle · Section 4: Circle problems

NameDate

For the adult: Area is the space inside a circle. It is in square units, like cm² or m². Area = 3.14 × radius × radius. Use the radius, not the diameter. The distance around (circumference) is a length, in cm or m.

Worked example

A face-off circle has a radius of 4.5 m. Find its area.

Step 1: r × r = 4.5 × 4.5 = 20.25.
Step 2: A ≈ 3.14 × 20.25 = 63.585. Round: about 63.6 m².

Your turn

1. A circle has a radius of 2 cm. Find its area. (First find 2 × 2.)

2. Find the area. (A ≈ 3.14 × r × r)

3. A plate is 16 cm across. Find its area. (Halve the diameter first.)

4. A pivot arm waters a circle of crops. The arm is 100 m long. Find the area it waters.

5. A pizza is 30 cm across. Find the length of its crust (C) and the area of its cheese (A). Give units.

6. Circle B has double the radius of circle A. How many times bigger is B's area?

7. A circle is 6 cm across. Mia says A ≈ 3.14 × 6 × 6. Is she right?
