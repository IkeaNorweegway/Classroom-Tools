Solving Equations

One skill: solving equations, including x on both sides · about 15 minutes

For the adult: An equation is a balance. Do the same thing to both sides. Answers can be negative. To check, put the answer into the first equation.

Worked example

Solve 3x + 2 = x + 10.

Step 1: A tall tile is x. Take one x off both sides: 2x + 2 = 10. Then take 2 off both sides: 2x = 8.
Step 2: Split both sides into 2 equal groups: x = 4. Check: 3(4) + 2 = 14 and 4 + 10 = 14. ✓
