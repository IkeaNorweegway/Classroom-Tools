Multiplying and Dividing Fractions — Answers

One skill: multiplying and dividing fractions · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 1: Multiplying fractions · Section 2: Dividing fractions

For the adult: The most common slips are finding a common denominator before multiplying, and flipping the first fraction when dividing. Keep the first fraction. Flip only the one you divide by.

Answers

1. 1/3 × 1/2 = 1/6. 1 × 1 = 1 and 3 × 2 = 6.

2. 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5.

3. 1/3 cup. 1/2 × 2/3 = 2/6 = 1/3.

4. 6. Each whole has 3 thirds. 2 × 3 = 6.

5. 6. 3/4 = 6/8. Then 6/8 ÷ 1/8 = 6 ÷ 1 = 6.

6. 4/5 × 2 = 8/5 = 1 3/5.

7. 4 pieces. 3 ÷ 3/4 = 3 × 4/3 = 12/3 = 4.

8. 1/2 × 4 = 4/2 = 2. Two quarters fit in a half.
