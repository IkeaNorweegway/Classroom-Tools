Multiplying and Dividing Decimals — Answers

One skill: multiplying and dividing decimals · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Multiplying and dividing decimals

For the adult: The most common slips are putting the decimal point in the wrong place (0.3 × 0.2 is not 0.6) and halving instead of dividing by a half (6 ÷ 0.5 is not 3). An estimate catches both.

Answers

1. 0.3 × 0.3 = 0.09. 3 × 3 = 9, with two decimal places.

2. 1.2 × 4 = 4.8.

3. 0.5 × 0.7 = 0.35. 5 × 7 = 35, with two decimal places.

4. 10 × 1.45 = $14.50.

5. 12. 60 ÷ 5 = 12. There are 12 halves in 6.

6. 8. 24 ÷ 3 = 8.

7. 9 bows. 3.6 ÷ 0.4 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9.

8. 2 × 4 = 8, with two decimal places: 0.08. 0.8 is bigger than both numbers, so it can't be right.
