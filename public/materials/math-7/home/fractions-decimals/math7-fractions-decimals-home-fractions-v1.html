Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

One skill: multiplying and dividing fractions · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Multiplying fractions · Section 2: Dividing fractions

NameDate

For the adult: To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms. To divide, ask "how many of these fit?" Or keep the first fraction, flip the second one, and multiply. Answers can get smaller when you multiply and bigger when you divide.

Worked example

Find 1/2 × 3/4. Then find 3 ÷ 1/4.
Step 1: 1/2 × 3/4 means half of 3/4. Multiply the tops: 1 × 3 = 3. Multiply the bottoms: 2 × 4 = 8. So 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8.

Step 2: 3 ÷ 1/4 asks: how many quarters fit in 3? Each whole has 4 quarters. 3 × 4 = 12, so 3 ÷ 1/4 = 12.
