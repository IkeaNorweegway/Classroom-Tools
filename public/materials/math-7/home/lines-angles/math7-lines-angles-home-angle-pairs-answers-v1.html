Angles Where Lines Cross — Answers

One skill: finding angles where two lines cross, and using corresponding angles with parallel lines · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 2: Parallel and perpendicular · Section 3: Angles where lines cross

For the adult: The most common slip is thinking opposite angles add to 180°. They are equal. It is the angles side by side on a straight line that add to 180°. Also check that the lines are marked parallel before using corresponding angles.

Answers

1. 180° − 70° = 110°.

2. 30°. Opposite angles are equal.

3. Beside 55°: 180° − 55° = 125°. Opposite 55°: 55°. Opposite 125°: 125°.

4. ∠6 corresponds to ∠2, so ∠6 = 65°. ∠1 is beside ∠2 on a line: 180° − 65° = 115°.

5. No. Corresponding angles are equal only when the lines are parallel. 70° ≠ 65°.

6. No. Opposite angles are equal. Angles side by side on a line add to 180°.
