Congruent Shapes — Answers

One skill: naming angles and matching the parts of congruent shapes · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 1: Lines, rays, segments, angles, polygons · Section 4: Congruent polygons

For the adult: The most common slip is ignoring the letter order. △ABC ≅ △TSR would say A matches T. Ask: "Which letter is in the same place?" For angle names, the vertex letter always goes in the middle.

Answers

1. L. E is 2nd in DEF, and L is 2nd in KLM.

2. KM. D and F are 1st and 3rd, so K and M.

3. ∠PQR, ∠RQP and ∠Q. Q is the vertex.

4. PQ matches AB: 7 cm. ∠R matches ∠C: 80°.

5. YZ matches BC: 5 cm. ZX matches CA: 3.6 cm.

6. △ABC ≅ △RST. The letters must be in matching order.

7. No. The angles all match, but the sides are different sizes.
