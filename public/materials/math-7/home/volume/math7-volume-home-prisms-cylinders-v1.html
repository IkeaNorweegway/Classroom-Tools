Prisms and Cylinders

One skill: V = base area × height, and finding a missing height or base area · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Volume = base area × height · Section 4: Finding a missing measure

NameDate

For the adult: Volume = base area × height. The base is the shape at each end. Triangle: 1/2 × base × the triangle's height. Circle: 3.14 × radius × radius. The height h is the distance from one end to the other. To go backwards, divide: height = volume ÷ base area.

Worked example

A tent's triangle end is 2 m wide and 1.5 m high. The tent is 3 m long. Find its volume.

Step 1: Base area: B = 1/2 × 2 × 1.5 = 1.5 m².
Step 2: V = B × h = 1.5 × 3 = 4.5 m³. (The 3 m is the length, end to end.)

Your turn

1. Find the volume. (First find B = 1/2 × 4 × 3.)

2. A can has a radius of 5 cm. It is 10 cm tall. Find its volume. (B ≈ 3.14 × 5 × 5)

3. A hockey puck has a radius of 3.8 cm. It is 2.5 cm thick. Find its volume to one decimal place.

4. A grain bin is 6 m across and 4 m tall. Find its volume.

5. A box holds 60 cm³. Its base area is 12 cm². How tall is it?

6. A pool holds 200 m³ of water. It is 2 m deep. Find the area of its bottom.

7. A trough is 2 m long, 40 cm wide and 50 cm deep. Find its volume in m³.
