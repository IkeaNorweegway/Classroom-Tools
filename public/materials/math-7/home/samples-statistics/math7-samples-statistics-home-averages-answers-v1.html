Mean, Median, Mode and Range — Answers

One skill: mean, median, mode and range of a small data set · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Mean, median, mode and range · Section 4: Comparing samples and drawing conclusions

For the adult: The most common slip is finding the middle of the list before putting it in order. The second is calling the biggest number the mode or the range. The mode is the most common number. The range is biggest − smallest.

Answers

1. 3 + 9 + 6 = 18. 18 ÷ 3 = 6.

2. In order: 1, 3, 6, 8, 10. The median is 6.

3. In order: 4, 8, 10, 12. The middle two are 8 and 10. (8 + 10) ÷ 2 = 9.

4. Mode: 6 (it comes up twice). Range: 9 − 2 = 7.

5. 14 + 18 + 11 + 17 = 60. 60 ÷ 4 = 15 °C.

6. No. 12 is the biggest, not the most common. 5 comes up twice, so the mode is 5.

7. A Grade 7 student reads about 22 to 26 minutes. It is an estimate: a sample is only part of the population.
