Comparing and Ordering

One skill: comparing and ordering positive and negative fractions and decimals · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 3: Fractions to decimals and back · Section 4: Comparing and ordering

NameDate

For the adult: Change fractions to decimals first (1/4 = 0.25, 1/2 = 0.5, 3/4 = 0.75). Then picture a number line: further left is smaller, so −8 < −3 and −0.5 < −0.25. To compare decimals, give them the same number of places: 0.500 > 0.125.

Worked example

Order from least to greatest: −1/2, 0.6, −0.75, 1/4
Step 1: Change to decimals: −0.5, 0.6, −0.75, 0.25. Put them on a number line.

Step 2: Read from left to right: −0.75, −1/2, 1/4, 0.6.
