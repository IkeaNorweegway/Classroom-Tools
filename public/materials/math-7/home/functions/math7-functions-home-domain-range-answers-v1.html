Domain and Range — Answers

One skill: describing domain and range in words, with real-world limits · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the filled-in class notes: Section 3: Domain and range · Section 4: Real-world limits and the vertical line test

For the adult: The most common slip is swapping the two: the domain is the inputs (across), the range is the outputs (up). Also watch for listing numbers when the input is time. Time is continuous, so say "any time from 0 to 4 hours."

Answers

1. Domain: 1, 2 and 3. Range: 2, 4 and 6.

2. Domain: whole numbers from 0 to 3. Range: $0, $6, $12 and $18.

3. Domain: 5, 6 and 7. Range: 1 and 2.

4. Domain: any time from 0 to 4 hours. Range: any height from 0 to 12 cm. Ava gave the height, the output: 0 to 12 cm is the range.

5. Domain: whole numbers from 0 to 40. Range: 0, 3, 6 and so on, up to $120 (40 × 3).

6. No. A vertical line through the middle hits the circle twice.
