Equal Ratios

One skill: equal ratios, missing terms and comparing ratios · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 1: Equivalent ratios · Section 2: Missing terms and comparing ratios

NameDate

For the adult: A ratio like 2:5 means "2 for every 5." To make an equal ratio, multiply (or divide) BOTH numbers by the same number. Never add the same number to both: 2:5 is not equal to 3:6.

Worked example

Juice is 2 cans of concentrate to 5 cans of water. How much water goes with 6 cans of concentrate?
Step 1: 2 cans became 6 cans. That is × 3.

Step 2: Do the same to the water: 5 × 3 = 15. So 2:5 = 6:15. Use 15 cans of water.
