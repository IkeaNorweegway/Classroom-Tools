Exit ticket — Section 2 · Theoretical probability

Name:Date:
Target: I can list the sample space. I can find a probability as a ratio, fraction, decimal and percent. (PB2)

1. A bag has 1 red and 4 blue counters. Find P(blue) as a ratio, a fraction, a decimal and a percent.

2. Find P(roll a number greater than 2) on a die.

3. A spinner has 4 equal parts. One is red. Sam says P(red) = 1/2, because "it's red or it's not." Explain how you know he is wrong.

Name:Date:
