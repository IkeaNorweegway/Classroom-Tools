Exit Tickets

For the teacher: print and cut. One ticket at the end of each section. Give 3–5 minutes. Read them before the next class and sort into three piles: got it, almost, not yet.

Exit ticket — Section 1 · Opposites and absolute value

Name:Date:
Target: I can find the opposite and the absolute value of a number. (PN1)

1. What number is at the point? Find its opposite and its absolute value.

2. Find |−12| and |12|.

3. Ella says |−9| = −9. Explain how you know she is wrong.
