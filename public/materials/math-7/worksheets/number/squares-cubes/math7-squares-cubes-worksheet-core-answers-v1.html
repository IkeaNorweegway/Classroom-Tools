1★ Core

Write each square of tiles as a product, a power and a number.

a)

b)

  1. a) 4 × 4 = 4² = 16
  2. b) 7 × 7 = 7² = 49
Answer a) 4 rows of 4. Two 4s multiplied: 4 × 4 = 4² = 16. (SC1)
b) 7 rows of 7: 7 × 7 = 7² = 49.
2★ Core

Find each square.

  1. a) 6² = 36
  2. b) 9² = 81
  3. c) 10² = 100
  4. d) 12² = 144
  5. e) 1² = 1
  6. f) 11² = 121
Answer a) 6 × 6 = 36. b) 9 × 9 = 81. c) 10 × 10 = 100. (SC1)
d) 12 × 12 = 144. e) 1 × 1 = 1. f) 11 × 11 = 121.
3★ Core

Ben says 8² = 16. Look at the two pictures. Which one is 8²? What is the right answer?

Answer B is 8². It is a square: 8 rows of 8. A is 8 × 2 = 16, a rectangle. (SC1)
8² = 8 × 8 = 64. Ben multiplied by the exponent. Targets the "5² = 10" mix-up.
4★ Core

Which of these are perfect squares? 25, 40, 49, 60, 81. For one that is not, explain how you know.

Answer 25 = 5², 49 = 7² and 81 = 9² are perfect squares. (SC1)
40 is not: 6² = 36 and 7² = 49, and 40 is between them. 60 is not: it is between 7² = 49 and 8² = 64.
5★ Core

Each square is made of tiles. Find the length of one side.

a)

b)

  1. a) √36 = 6
  2. b) √100 = 10
Answer a) What times itself makes 36? 6 × 6 = 36. So the side is √36 = 6. (SC2)
b) 10 × 10 = 100. So the side is √100 = 10.
6★ Core

Find each square root. Check by squaring.

  1. a) √9 = 3
  2. b) √64 = 8
  3. c) √121 = 11
  4. d) √144 = 12
Answer a) 3 × 3 = 9. b) 8 × 8 = 64. (SC2)
c) 11 × 11 = 121. d) 12 × 12 = 144.
7★ Core

Kai says √16 = 8. Check his answer by squaring. Then find √16.

Answer Check: 8 × 8 = 64, not 16. So Kai is wrong. He found half of 16. (SC2)
√16 = 4, because 4 × 4 = 16. Targets the "√16 = 8" mix-up.
8★ Core

How many small cubes are in each cube? Write it as a power.

a)

b)

  1. a) 2³ = 8
  2. b) 4³ = 64
Answer a) 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. One layer is 2 × 2 = 4. Two layers make 8. (SC3)
b) One layer is 4 × 4 = 16. Four layers: 16 × 4 = 64. So 4³ = 64.
9★ Core

Zoe wrote: 2³ = 6 and ∛27 = 9. Fix both mistakes.

Answer 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. Zoe did 2 × 3. Targets the "2³ = 6" mix-up. (SC3)
∛27 = 3, because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. 9 is one layer, not the edge. Check: 9 × 9 × 9 = 729. Targets the "∛27 = 9" mix-up.
10★ Core

Find each one.

  1. a) 5³ = 125
  2. b) 3³ = 27
  3. c) ∛8 = 2
  4. d) ∛64 = 4
  5. e) ∛125 = 5
Answer a) 5 × 5 × 5 = 25 × 5 = 125. b) 3 × 3 × 3 = 9 × 3 = 27. (SC3)
c) 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. d) 4 × 4 × 4 = 64. e) 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.
11★ Core

A square garden has sides of 9 m. Find the fence (perimeter) and the grass (area). Write the units.

Perimeter: 36 m Area: 81 m²

Answer Perimeter = 4 × 9 = 36 m. The fence is a length. (SC4)
Area = 9² = 9 × 9 = 81 m². The grass is squares. Targets the "area and perimeter are the same" mix-up.
12★ Core

A cube-shaped box has edges of 3 cm. Find its volume.

Answer V = 3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 cm³. (SC4)
Volume counts cubes, so the unit is cm³.
