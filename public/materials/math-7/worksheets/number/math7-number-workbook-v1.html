1★ Core

Use the number line.

  1. a) Find |−6|, |6| and |−2.5|.
  2. b) Name two numbers that are 4 steps from 0.
2★ Core

At 7 am it is −5 °C in Calgary. By noon it is 2 degrees colder.

Sam writes (−5) + (−2) = 7. "Two negatives make a positive."

Use the number line. Find the right answer.

3★ Core

Mia says 6² = 12. Look at the squares of tiles.

  1. a) Fill in the number of tiles in each square.
  2. b) Is Mia right? Explain.
4★ Core

A pan has 3/4 of the bannock left. Ava eats 2/3 of what is left.

  1. a) Use the area model. Find 2/3 × 3/4.
  2. b) Is the answer more or less than 3/4? Why?
5★ Core

Noor mixes 2 cans of concentrate with 3 cans of water.

  1. a) Write the ratio of concentrate to water.
  2. b) How many cans are in the jug?
  3. c) What fraction of the jug is concentrate?
6★ Core

Kai writes 1/4 = 1.4. The grid shows 1/4.

  1. a) How many hundredths are shaded?
  2. b) Write 1/4 as a decimal.
  3. c) Is Kai right? Explain.
7★ Core

At night it is −3 °C. At noon it is 4 °C.

Ben says subtracting always gives a smaller number.

  1. a) Use the chips. Find 4 − (−3).
  2. b) Is Ben right? Explain.
8★ Core

Raj says √16 = 8.

  1. a) Find √16, √81 and √144.
  2. b) Check one answer with multiplication.
9★ Core

Zoe has 4 cups of oats. One batch of muffins uses 2/3 cup.

Zoe thinks the answer must be less than 4.

  1. a) How many batches can she make? Use the picture.
  2. b) Why is the answer more than 4?
10★ Core

200 people ride the Ferris wheel on Monday.

  1. a) Find 1% of 200.
  2. b) Find 0.5% of 200.
  3. c) Tuesday has 150% of Monday's riders. How many is that?
11★ Core

Mark each number on the number line.

  1. a) −1.5
  2. b) −3/4, 3/−4 and −(3/4)
  3. c) Lily says −1.5 is between −1 and 0. Is she right?
12★ Core

Omar says 3³ = 9.

  1. a) How many small cubes make the big cube?
  2. b) Find ∛27 and ∛64.
  3. c) What did Omar do wrong?
13★ Core

Leo says 0.3 × 0.4 = 1.2. Use the hundred grid.

  1. a) How many small squares overlap?
  2. b) Find 0.3 × 0.4.
  3. c) How do you know Leo is wrong?

14★★ Extended

Ava says (−2) × 5 = 10. "5 is the bigger number, and it is positive."

  1. a) Use the counters. Find (−2) × 5.
  2. b) Find (−2) × (−2) × (−2). Count the negatives first.
15★★ Extended

Tess finds 2/5 × 3/4. She writes 8/20 × 15/20 = 120/20 = 6.

  1. a) Is 6 a sensible answer? Explain.
  2. b) Find the right answer.
  3. c) Do you need a common denominator to multiply?
16★★ Extended

Omar's juice is 2 cans of concentrate to 5 cans of water. Mia's is 3 cans to 8 cans.

  1. a) Omar uses 8 cans of concentrate. How much water does he need?
  2. b) Whose juice is stronger? Make the first terms the same.
17★★ Extended

Ben finds 3/4 ÷ 3/8. He writes 4/3 × 3/8 = 12/24 = 1/2.

  1. a) Use the picture. How many 3/8 pieces fit in 3/4?
  2. b) What did Ben do wrong? Fix his steps.
18★★ Extended

Morning lows: Edmonton −8 °C, Calgary −3 °C, Jasper −12.5 °C, Lethbridge 0.5 °C.

  1. a) Noor says −8 °C is warmer than −3 °C, because 8 > 3. Is she right?
  2. b) Order the four lows from coldest to warmest.
  3. c) Omar says 0.125 > 0.5 because it has more digits. Is he right?
19★★ Extended

Leo says 2 + 3 × 4 = 20.

  1. a) Find the right answer.
  2. b) Find (−6) + 8 ÷ (−2).
  3. c) Find 1/2 × (5 − 1) + 0.5.
20★★ Extended

Kai says a square with 4 cm sides has area = perimeter. "Both are 16."

  1. a) Is he right? Use the units.
  2. b) A square garden has an area of 64 m². How much fence goes around it?
21★★ Extended

Mia has 4.5 m of ribbon. She cuts it into pieces 0.5 m long.

She says 4.5 ÷ 0.5 = 2.25.

  1. a) How many pieces does she get? Use the picture.
  2. b) Multiply both numbers by 10. Check your answer.
22★★ Extended

Lily's pancakes use 2 cups of flour for 3 eggs. For a bigger batch she writes 4:5.

  1. a) Is 4:5 equal to 2:3? Explain.
  2. b) Fill in the double number line.

  1. a) Write one expression for the mean. Use brackets.
  2. b) Find the mean low. Does it make sense?
