Positive & Negative Numbers — Concept Reference

The key ideas on one page. Keep it beside you when you practise.

Opposites and absolute value

Opposites are the same distance from 0, on different sides: 4 and −4.

Absolute value is the distance from 0. It is never negative. |−5| = 5, |5| = 5.

Negative fractions and decimals

−1.5 is half way from −1 to −2. The left side of 0 is a mirror of the right side.

One negative sign, anywhere, gives the same number: −3/4 = 3/−4 = −(3/4) = −0.75.

Fractions and decimals

Fraction to decimal: make 10, 100 or 1000 on the bottom. Or divide top ÷ bottom. 3/4 = 75/100 = 0.75.

Decimal to fraction: read the place value, then simplify. 0.35 = 35/100 = 7/20.
