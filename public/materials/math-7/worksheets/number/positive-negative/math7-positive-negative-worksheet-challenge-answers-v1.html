Some places are below sea level. Mark each place on the line. Then order them from least to greatest.

Dead Sea shoreabout −0.43

Lowest point in the Netherlands−0.007

Which place is closest to sea level? Use absolute value.

Answer Order: −0.43 (Dead Sea shore), −0.086 (Death Valley), −0.007 (Netherlands), 0.645 (Edmonton), 1.75 (Lake Louise). (PN4)
Closest to sea level: the Netherlands point. |−0.007| = 0.007 is the smallest absolute value.
2★★ Challenge

Always, sometimes or never? Explain each one.

  1. a) The absolute value of a number is positive.
  2. b) A number and its opposite have the same absolute value.
  3. c) The opposite of a number is negative.
Answer a) Sometimes. |0| = 0, and 0 is not positive. Every other absolute value is positive. (PN1)
b) Always. Opposites are the same distance from 0.
c) Sometimes. The opposite of 5 is −5 (negative), but the opposite of −5 is 5 (positive).
3★★ Challenge

Zoe says 3/−4 is to the right of −(3/4), "because the 3 on top is positive." Is she right? Show both on the line.

Answer No. Each has one negative sign, so both are three quarters left of 0. They are the same point: −0.75. (PN2)
Targets the "three different numbers" mix-up.
4★★ Challenge

Find two numbers between −3/4 and −1/2. Write each one as a fraction and as a decimal.

Answer −3/4 = −0.75 and −1/2 = −0.5. Any number between −0.75 and −0.5 works. (PN4)
Examples: −5/8 = −0.625, −3/5 = −0.6, −7/10 = −0.7.
5★★ Challenge

Raj says −0.5 > −0.125, "because 0.5 > 0.125." Is he right? Explain.

Answer No. 0.5 > 0.125 is true for positives. But −0.5 is further from 0 on the left side, so −0.5 < −0.125. (PN4)
Targets both mix-ups: "bigger negative" and "longer decimal".

7★★ Challenge

A diver is at −4.5 m. A bird is at 3.5 m. How far apart are they?

Answer The diver is |−4.5| = 4.5 m below sea level. The bird is |3.5| = 3.5 m above. (PN1)
They are on different sides of 0, so add the distances: 4.5 + 3.5 = 8 m apart.
