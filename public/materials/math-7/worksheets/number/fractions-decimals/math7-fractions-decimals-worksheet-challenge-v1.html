1★★ Challenge

Ava has 21/4 cups of flour. One batch of bannock uses 3/4 cup.

  1. a) How many batches can she make?

2★★ Challenge

There is 2/3 of a pizza left. Four friends share it equally.

  1. a) How much of the whole pizza does each friend get?
  2. b) What fraction of the leftover pizza does each friend get?
3★★ Challenge

Kai has a board 3 m long. He cuts shelves that are 5/8 m long.

  1. a) How many whole shelves can he cut?
  2. b) How much board is left over?
4★★ Challenge

  1. a) Find the price per litre. Use a calculator. Estimate to check.
  2. b) At that price, what do 50 L cost?
