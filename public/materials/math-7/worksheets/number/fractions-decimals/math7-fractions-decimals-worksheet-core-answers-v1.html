Fractions & Decimals (× and ÷)

Core Practice ★ — Answer Key

Answer key · Teacher copy
1★ Core

Use the area model. Find 2/3 × 3/5. Write the answer in simplest form.

Answer Columns: 3 of 5 shaded. Rows: 2 of 3 shaded. The square has 3 × 5 = 15 parts. (FD1)
The overlap is 2 × 3 = 6 parts. 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5.
2★ Core

Find each product. Write it in simplest form.

  1. a) 1/2 × 3/5 = 3/10
  2. b) 4/5 × 1/4 = 1/5
  3. c) 3 × 3/4 = 21/4
  4. d) 2/3 × 9 = 6
Answer a) 1 × 3 = 3 and 2 × 5 = 10: 3/10. (FD1)
b) 4/20 = 1/5. c) 9/4 = 21/4. d) 18/3 = 6.
3★ Core

Lily says 3/4 × 8 is bigger than 8, because multiplying makes numbers bigger. Shade 3/4 of the 8 pieces. Then find 3/4 × 8.

Answer Shade 6 of the 8 pieces. 3/4 of 8 is 6, so 3/4 × 8 = 6. (FD1)
6 is less than 8. Times a proper fraction takes only part. Targets the "multiplying always makes it bigger" mix-up.
4★ Core

Kai wrote: 1/4 × 2/3 = 3/12 × 8/12 = 24/12 = 2. Use the area model to find the right answer.

Answer The square has 4 × 3 = 12 parts. The overlap is 1 × 2 = 2 parts. 1/4 × 2/3 = 2/12 = 1/6. (FD1)
Kai did not need a common denominator. Multiply the tops and the bottoms. Targets the "common denominator to multiply" mix-up.
5★ Core

Leo's pancake recipe uses 3/4 cup of flour. He makes 11/2 batches. How much flour does he need?

Answer 11/2 = 3/2. Then 3/2 × 3/4 = 9/8. (FD1)
9/8 = 11/8 cups. Check with the picture: 3/4 + 3/8 = 6/8 + 3/8 = 9/8.
6★ Core

How many 1/3s fit in 4?

Answer Each whole has 3 thirds. 4 wholes have 4 × 3 = 12 thirds. 4 ÷ 1/3 = 12. (FD2)
12 is bigger than 4. Targets the "dividing always makes it smaller" mix-up.
7★ Core

Find each quotient.

  1. a) 5/8 ÷ 1/8 = 5
  2. b) 9/10 ÷ 3/10 = 3
  3. c) 2 ÷ 1/5 = 10
  4. d) 1/3 ÷ 2 = 1/6
Answer a) Same bottoms: 5 ÷ 1 = 5. b) Same bottoms: 9 ÷ 3 = 3. (FD2)
c) 2 × 5 = 10. There are 10 fifths in 2. d) 1/3 × 1/2 = 1/6. Half of a third.
8★ Core

Sam wrote: 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 4/3 × 3/8 = 12/24 = 1/2. Fix his mistake. Check with the picture.

Answer Sam flipped the first fraction. Keep 3/4. Flip the divisor: 3/4 × 8/3 = 24/12 = 2. (FD2)
The picture shows 2 groups of 3/8 in 3/4. Targets the "flip the first fraction" mix-up.
9★ Core

Mia says 0.5 × 0.3 = 1.5. Use the picture to fix her answer. Then find 0.6 × 0.7 and 2.5 × 0.4.

Answer The overlap is 15 of 100 squares: 0.15. 5 × 3 = 15, with 2 decimal places. (FD3)
1.5 is bigger than both numbers, so it can't be right. Targets the "0.3 × 0.2 = 0.6" mix-up.
0.6 × 0.7: 6 × 7 = 42, two places: 0.42. 2.5 × 0.4: 25 × 4 = 100, two places: 1.00 = 1.
10★ Core

Gas costs $1.45 per litre. Find the cost of 20 L. Estimate first.

Answer Estimate: 20 × $1.50 = $30. (FD3)
20 × 145 = 2900. Two decimal places: 29.00. The cost is $29.00. Close to the estimate. ✓
11★ Core

Noor says 6 ÷ 0.5 = 3. Count the jumps. Then find the other quotients.

  1. a) 6 ÷ 0.5 = 12
  2. b) 2.4 ÷ 0.4 = 6
  3. c) 5 ÷ 0.25 = 20
  4. d) 7.5 ÷ 3 = 2.5
Answer a) 12 jumps of 0.5 fit in 6. Also 60 ÷ 5 = 12. Noor found half of 6. Targets the "6 ÷ 0.5 = 3" mix-up. (FD3)
b) × 10 both: 24 ÷ 4 = 6. c) × 100 both: 500 ÷ 25 = 20. d) Share 7.5 in 3: 2.5. Check: 3 × 2.5 = 7.5.
12★ Core

Evaluate. Show each step.

  1. a) 4 + 6 × 2 = 16
  2. b) (4 + 6) × 2 = 20
  3. c) 20 ÷ 4 × 5 = 25
  4. d) 3 + 2³ = 11
  5. e) −6 + 3 × (−2) = −12
  6. f) 1/2 + 1/4 × 2 = 1
  7. g) 0.5 × 4 + 1.5 = 3.5
Answer a) × first: 4 + 12 = 16. Not 10 × 2 = 20. Targets the "work left to right, always" mix-up. (FD4)
b) Brackets first: 10 × 2 = 20. c) Left to right: 20 ÷ 4 = 5, then 5 × 5 = 25.
d) Cube first: 2³ = 8, then 3 + 8 = 11. e) × first: 3 × (−2) = −6, then −6 + (−6) = −12.
f) 1/4 × 2 = 2/4 = 1/2. Then 1/2 + 1/2 = 1. g) 0.5 × 4 = 2. Then 2 + 1.5 = 3.5.
