Ratios & Percents — Concept Reference

The key ideas on one page. Keep it beside you when you practise.

Equivalent ratios

Multiply or divide BOTH terms by the same factor. 2:3 = 4:6 = 6:9.

Never add the same number to both terms.

Part to part 2:3 means 2 out of 5 in all.

Missing terms and comparing

Find the factor. Use it on the other term. 2:5 = 8:20 (× 4).

Compare: make one term match. Then compare the other term.

Cross products: a/b = c/d is the same as a × d = b × c.

Percents

A percent is a ratio out of 100. 45% = 45/100 = 0.45.

Benchmarks: 50% (÷ 2), 25% (÷ 4), 10% (÷ 10), 5% (half of 10%), 1% (÷ 100).

35% = 25% + 10%. 0.5% is half of 1%. 150% is 100% + 50%.

Problems

Discount: take it off. GST (5%): add it on.

Increase: add the percent. Decrease: subtract it.

Unit rate: the price for ONE. 1 m = 100 cm. 1 L = 1000 mL.
