1★★ Challenge

Simplify −3(2x − 1) + 4(x + 2).

Answer −3 × 2x = −6x and −3 × (−1) = +3. 4x + 8. (LE1)
−6x + 3 + 4x + 8 = −2x + 11.
2★★ Challenge

Solve 3(x − 2) = x + 4. Verify in the original equation and in one equivalent equation.

Answer 3x − 6 = x + 4. Subtract x: 2x − 6 = 4. Add 6: 2x = 10. x = 5. (LE3)
Original: 3(5 − 2) = 9 and 5 + 4 = 9. ✓ Equivalent: 2(5) − 6 = 4. ✓ (LE4)
3★★ Challenge

Solve 2(x − 3) = 4(x + 1).

Answer 2x − 6 = 4x + 4. Subtract 2x: −6 = 2x + 4. Subtract 4: −10 = 2x. x = −5. (LE3)
Check: 2(−8) = −16 and 4(−4) = −16. ✓
4★★ Challenge

Lily solves 6 − 2x = 3x − 4. She writes 6 = x − 4, so x = 10. Find her mistake. Then solve.

Answer She took 2x away from the right side. The left side has −2x, so she must ADD 2x to both sides. (LE3)
6 = 5x − 4. 10 = 5x. x = 2. Check: 6 − 4 = 2 and 6 − 4 = 2. ✓ Targets the "keep its sign" mix-up.
5★★ Challenge

Sunpeak costs a $90 card plus $30 a day. Pine Ridge costs $60 a day.

  1. a) After how many days do they cost the same?
  2. b) Mia skis 5 days. Which hill is cheaper? By how much?
Answer a) 90 + 30x = 60x. Subtract 30x: 90 = 30x. x = 3 days. Check: 90 + 90 = 180 and 60(3) = 180. ✓ (LE4)
b) Sunpeak: 90 + 150 = $240. Pine Ridge: 60(5) = $300. Sunpeak is $60 cheaper.
6★★ Challenge

A chinook comes to Lethbridge. It is −11 °C and warms 4 °C each hour. Red Deer is −2 °C and warms 1 °C each hour. When are they the same? What is the temperature then?

Answer Let x = hours. −11 + 4x = −2 + x. Subtract x: −11 + 3x = −2. Add 11: 3x = 9. x = 3 hours. (LE4)
−11 + 12 = 1 °C and −2 + 3 = 1 °C. Both are 1 °C.
7★★ Challenge

A rectangle is x + 3 long and x wide. A triangle has three sides of x + 4. They have the same perimeter. Find the perimeter.

Answer Rectangle: 2(x + 3) + 2x = 4x + 6. Triangle: 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12. (LE4)
4x + 6 = 3x + 12. x = 6. Rectangle: 9 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 30. Triangle: 10 + 10 + 10 = 30. The perimeter is 30.
