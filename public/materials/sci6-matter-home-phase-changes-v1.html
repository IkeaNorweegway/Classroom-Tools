Phase Changes

One skill: naming and explaining melting, freezing, evaporation and condensation · about 15 minutes

Need a reminder? See the class notes: Section 2: States of Matter and Phase Changes

NameDate

For the adult: A phase change is a change of state. Adding heat melts and evaporates; removing heat freezes and condenses.

Worked example

Worked example

Name the changes: an ice cube melts in your hand, a puddle dries up, and drops form on a cold glass.

  1. Ice cube melting: solid to liquid (heat added from your hand).
  2. Puddle drying up: liquid to gas, called evaporation.
  3. Drops on a cold glass: water vapour in the air turns to liquid, called condensation.
