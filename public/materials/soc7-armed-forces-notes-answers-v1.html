Armed Forces and Sovereignty

Grade 7 Social Studies  ·  Notes Answer Key  ·  Alberta Draft Curriculum 2026

Teacher Copy — Not for Students

Learning Outcome: Students relate the development of armed forces in Canada to sovereignty.

Guiding Question: How can defence of peace and security play a role in the development of a nation?

Section 1: Policing the West

Misconception Target

"The RCMP has always existed, just like it is today." — refuted at this point in the section, before the correct idea is taught.

Blank / PromptExpected Answer
To show that Canada's laws applied in the West, and to protect its claim from the United States, Parliament created the North-West Mounted Police (NWMP) in ___.1873
The NWMP (1873) became the Royal North-West Mounted Police (1904), then the Royal Canadian Mounted Police in ___, a national force that today oversees national security across Canada.1920

Retrieval Check answers:

  • Q1: 1873.
  • Q2: The American whisky trade; keeping order and enforcing Canadian law; protecting Canada's claim from the U.S.
  • Q3: The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP).
  • Q4: A country's power to run its own affairs.
  • Q5 ★ Stretch: Canada claimed the land on paper, but the NWMP enforced Canadian law there, stopped the whisky trade and was present at treaty signings, so people actually followed Canada's laws.
