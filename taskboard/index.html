How the project is going

Math 7 and Math 8 (on the June 2026 draft) are built and published, and are waiting for your teacher read-through, as are the Grade 5 ELA, Social 7 and Science 7 sets. The CEFL Town v2 game has its town and Payday jobs finished, and the student avatar and Pat's stall are the stone in progress. The Math 9 Realm game has its story bible and Chapter 1 designed, with Chapter 1 as the next build, and a separate pixel-art version (Realm v2) is at the design stage. The main session writes all teaching content and designs the games, while specialist agents (all on Sonnet) render, build the site, polish, check and playtest. Work is done in batches of one topic: content first, then one hand-off to render and wire up the site, then _status.md and a commit.

The crew: agents and their jobs
