## What's the big idea?

A **job** is one role. A **career** is your whole path of learning, work and experiences. Careers are shaped by your **interests**, your **values**, your **experiences** and the **training** you choose, and all of these can change. **Transferable skills**, like communication and problem solving, come with you from one job to the next.
