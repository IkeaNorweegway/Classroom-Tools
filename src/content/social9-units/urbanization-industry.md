## What's the big idea?

After the Second World War, millions of people moved to Canada and into its cities. Some were **pushed** out of their home countries by war or poverty; others were **pulled** here by jobs and safety. At the same time, **industries** like oil, hydroelectric power and car-making grew fast. Growth brought jobs and new neighbourhoods, but also pollution and lost farmland and wildlife habitat. Was it worth it? That's what you'll decide.
