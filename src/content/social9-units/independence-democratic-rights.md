## What's the big idea?

Canada didn't become independent all at once. After the First World War, Britain and countries like Canada agreed they were equals (the **Balfour Declaration**, 1926), and the **Statute of Westminster** (1931) made it law. Canada could now make its own decisions about war and the world. Around the same time, five Alberta women, the **Famous Five**, won the **Persons Case**, which said women were "persons" under the law and could serve in the Senate.
