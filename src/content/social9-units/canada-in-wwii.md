## What's the big idea?

When the Second World War began, Canada made its own decision to join, a week after Britain. Canadians fought at **Hong Kong** and **Dieppe**, in **Italy**, on **D-Day**, and helped free the **Netherlands**. At home, factories built ships, planes and trucks. Some who served, like **Chinese Canadians**, weren't treated as full citizens at home, and **Cree code talkers** used their language to keep messages secret. The war made Canada a more important country in the world.
