## What's the big idea?

A **distribution** shows how often each value comes up. A **bar graph** shows categories, with gaps between bars. A **histogram** groups measured data, like time or height, into equal **intervals**, and its bars touch. Then look at the **shape**. One peak or two? Do the halves match (**symmetric**)? Is there a long **tail**? The tail names the skew: a tail to the right is **skewed right**. The tail also pulls the mean, so for skewed data the **median** is the better typical value. For categories, use the **mode**. A **circle graph** shows parts of one whole: each sector's angle is its percent × 360°.
