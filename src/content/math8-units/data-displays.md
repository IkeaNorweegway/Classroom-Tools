## What's the big idea?

Every graph has a job. **Bar graphs** compare groups. **Line graphs** show change over time. **Circle graphs** show parts of one whole. **Pictographs** use pictures with a key. The way a graph is drawn can give the wrong idea even when the numbers are true, so always read the numbers on the axes, not just the shapes.
