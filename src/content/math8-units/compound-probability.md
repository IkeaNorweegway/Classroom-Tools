## What's the big idea?

Flip a coin **and** roll a die. That is a **compound event**. The **sample space** lists every outcome: S = {H1, H2, …, T6}. A tree or a table shows all 2 × 6 = 12 of them. Events are **independent** when the first result does not change the chances of the second, like a coin and a die. Then **multiply**: P(heads and 6) = 1/2 × 1/6 = 1/12. Events are **dependent** when the first result changes the second, like drawing two counters and keeping the first one out. There is one fewer counter, so the second fraction changes. A tree shows it: multiply along the branches.
