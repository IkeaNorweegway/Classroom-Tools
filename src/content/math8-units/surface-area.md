## What's the big idea?

**Surface area** is the area that covers the outside of a 3-D shape. Cut a box along its edges and lay it flat. That flat shape is a **net**. Find the area of each face and add them up. A pyramid's faces are triangles, so you need the **slant height** on each face. A can's side unrolls into a rectangle. It is as long as the distance around the circle. Volume fills a shape with cubes. Surface area covers it with squares. You can also draw a shape as flat views from the front, top and side, or as one picture on dot paper.
