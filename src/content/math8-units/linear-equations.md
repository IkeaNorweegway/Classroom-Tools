## What's the big idea?

An equation is a **balance**. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. To get x alone, **undo** what was done to it, in **reverse order**: last step first, like taking off your shoes before your socks. Then **check** by putting your answer back into the equation. If both sides match, you're right.
