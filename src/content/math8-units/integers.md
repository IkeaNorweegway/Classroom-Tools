## What's the big idea?

3 × (−2) means **three groups of −2**, which is −6. A negative first number means **take away** groups, which flips the sign. The pattern 3 × (−2), 2 × (−2), 1 × (−2), 0 × (−2) goes up by 2 each time, so (−1) × (−2) must be +2. The rule: **count the negatives**. An even number of negatives gives a positive answer; an odd number gives a negative one. This rule is only for × and ÷.
