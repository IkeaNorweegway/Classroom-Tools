## What's the big idea?

A **theorem** is a fact we can prove. Tear the three corners off a triangle. They fill a straight line, so the angles always add to 180°. Two sides of a triangle must add to more than the third side. If not, they can't meet. In a **right triangle**, draw a square on each side. The two small squares together match the big one: a² + b² = c². The longest side, c, is across from the right angle. With this rule you can find a missing side. You can also check if a corner is really square.
