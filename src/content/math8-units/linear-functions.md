## What's the big idea?

**Slope** tells how steep a line is and which way it goes. Count how far up (the **rise**) and how far across (the **run**). Slope is rise ÷ run. A line going up to the right has a positive slope. Down to the right is negative. A flat line has slope 0. A straight-up line has an undefined slope, because you can't divide by 0. The equation **y = mx + b** tells you everything about a line: m is the slope, and b is where it crosses the y-axis. In real life, the slope is a rate, like $15 an hour or 100 km each hour. And b is where you start.
