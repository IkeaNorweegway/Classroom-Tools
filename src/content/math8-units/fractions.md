## What's the big idea?

Multiplying by a fraction takes **part** of something: 1/2 × 8 is half of 8. Dividing by a fraction asks **how many fit**: 3 ÷ 1/4 counts the quarters in 3. That is why multiplying by a fraction less than 1 gives a smaller answer, and dividing by one gives a bigger answer. Change mixed numbers to improper fractions before you multiply or divide.
