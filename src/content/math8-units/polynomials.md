## What's the big idea?

A **power** is repeated multiplication. x² means x · x, and x³ means x · x · x. A **term** like 3x² is a number times a power of x. A **polynomial** is one term or a sum of terms, like x² − 3x + 5. We name it by how many terms it has. We also name it by its **degree**, the biggest exponent. **Like terms** have the same power of x. Only like terms can be put together: 2x² + 3x² = 5x². To add polynomials, put the like terms together. To subtract, flip the sign of every term you take away, then add. Polynomials can tell the area of a garden or the length of a fence.
