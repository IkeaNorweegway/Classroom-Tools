## What's the big idea?

**Probability** = favourable outcomes ÷ all outcomes, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). When two events are **independent** (one doesn't change the other, like a coin and a die), list every pair with a tree or a table. The chance both happen is the two probabilities **multiplied**. Experiments wobble around the theory; with more trials they get closer.
