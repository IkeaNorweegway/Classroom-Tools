## What's the big idea?

A **ratio** compares amounts of the same kind (cans to cans). A **rate** compares different kinds (kilometres to hours, dollars to grams). Equal ratios grow together: multiply **both** parts by the same number. A **unit rate** is the amount for one, which makes comparing prices and speeds fair.
