## What's the big idea?

An **equation** has one answer. An **inequality** has lots of answers. The sign on Highway 2 says 110. You can drive 110, or 100, or 95. So your speed s is **at most** 110: s ≤ 110. There are four symbols. < means less than and > means greater than. They leave the boundary out. ≤ and ≥ have a line under them. The line means "or equal to", so they let the boundary in. On a number line, an **open dot** ○ leaves the number out. A **closed dot** ● lets it in. The ray goes **right** for greater and **left** for less. To check a number, put it in and see if the statement is true.
