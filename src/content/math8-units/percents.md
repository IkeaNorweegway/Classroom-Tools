## What's the big idea?

**Percent** means "out of 100." 100% is one whole, so 150% is one and a half wholes, and 0.5% is half of one hundredth. To find a percent of a number, build it from easy pieces: 10% is ÷ 10, 5% is half of that. When two percents are of the same amount (tax and tip), you can add them. When the second percent is of a new amount (a sale on a sale), do one step at a time.
