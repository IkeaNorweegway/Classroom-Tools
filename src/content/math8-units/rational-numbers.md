## What's the big idea?

A **rational number** is any number you can write as a fraction, like 2/3, −0.75 or 5. Every integer counts too, because 5 = 5/1. Some fractions make decimals that **end**, like 3/8 = 0.375. Others **repeat** forever, like 1/3 = 0.333…. We put a bar over the part that repeats. Every rational number has a spot on the number line, and there is always another number between any two. You can add, subtract, multiply and divide them with the same sign rules you used for integers. Estimate first, so you know if your answer makes sense.
