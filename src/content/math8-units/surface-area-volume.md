## What's the big idea?

**Surface area** is the wrapping: add up the area of every face. A **net** (the object cut open and laid flat) helps you see every face. **Volume** is the filling. For any prism or cylinder, volume = **area of the base × height**, because the object is a stack of identical layers. Surface area is in square units (cm²); volume is in cubic units (cm³).
