## What's the big idea?

Draw a square on each side of a right triangle. The two smaller squares together have exactly the same area as the biggest one: **a² + b² = c²**. The side c is the **hypotenuse**, across from the right angle, and it is always the longest side. To find the hypotenuse, add the squares and take the square root. To find a leg, subtract.
