## What's the big idea?

You can compare two amounts by **dividing**. If Ava has 12 cards and Ben has 4, Ava has 3 times as many. Ben has 1/3 as many. In a ratio a : b, the number you multiply a by to get b is called **k**. For 2 : 5, k = 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5. Every equal ratio has the same k. Once you know k, you can find any missing amount. Multiply to go from a to b. Divide to go back. When both amounts grow together, it is **direct** proportion, and the graph is a straight line through (0, 0). When one goes up and the other goes down, like more workers and fewer hours, it is **inverse** proportion. Then a × b stays the same.
