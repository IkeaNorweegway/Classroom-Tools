## What's the big idea?

A **linear relation** grows by the same amount every step. Two numbers describe it: where it **starts** (when x = 0) and how much it **goes up by** each step. In the equation, "goes up by" is multiplied by x and "starts at" is added: seats = 2 × tables + 2. The same relation can be a picture, a table, an equation or a graph. The points on its graph line up in a straight line.
