## What's the big idea?

Two shapes are **similar** when they have the same shape. They can be different sizes. A zoomed-in photo is similar to the small one. Every length gets multiplied by the same number. That number is the **scale factor**, k. If k is more than 1, the copy is bigger. If k is less than 1, it is smaller. Area grows faster than length. Double the sides and the area is 4 times as big. Maps, blueprints and models are all scale drawings. On a grid, a **dilation** multiplies both coordinates by k. The new shape is similar to the old one.
