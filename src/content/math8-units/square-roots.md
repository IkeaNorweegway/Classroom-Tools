## What's the big idea?

A **square number** is the area of a square made of tiles: 5 × 5 = 25. Its **square root** is the length of one side: √25 = 5. Squaring goes from side to area. The square root goes back from area to side. When a number is not a perfect square, find the perfect squares on each side of it to estimate.
