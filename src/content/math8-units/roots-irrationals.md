## What's the big idea?

A **perfect square** is a number you get by multiplying a number by itself, like 49 = 7 × 7. Fractions and decimals can be perfect squares too: 9/16 = 3/4 × 3/4. Every positive number has **two square roots**, because 5 × 5 = 25 and (−5) × (−5) = 25. The √ sign means the positive one. A negative number has no real square root. When a number is not a perfect square, use the perfect squares near it to estimate: √50 is just over 7. Numbers like √50, √2 and π are **irrational**. Their decimals never end and never repeat. Rational and irrational numbers together are the **real numbers**, and each one has a spot on the number line.
