## What's the big idea?

An **equation** is like a balance. If you do the same thing to both sides, it stays level. In Grade 7 the numbers were integers. Now they can be **fractions** and **decimals**, like ½x + 3 = 5. Here is a trick. Multiply **every** term on both sides by the same number, and the fractions go away. ½x + 3 = 5 becomes x + 6 = 10, so x = 4. For decimals, multiply by 10 or 100. Brackets and x on both sides work the same way as in Grade 7. The answer can be a fraction or a decimal. Always check by putting the answer back in. Equations help you find a taxi ride, compare phone plans, or change °F to °C.
