## What's the big idea?

**Surface area** is how much wrapping paper covers the outside of a 3-D shape. **Volume** is how much fits inside. You'll learn the formulas for cones, pyramids and spheres. Then you'll take on combined shapes, like a grain bin, by splitting them into pieces you already know. The trick is noticing which faces get hidden where two shapes join.
