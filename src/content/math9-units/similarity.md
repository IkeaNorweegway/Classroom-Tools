## What's the big idea?

Two shapes are **similar** when one is a zoomed-in or zoomed-out copy of the other. The angles stay the same, and every side grows or shrinks by the same amount, called the **scale factor**. Once you spot two similar triangles, you can use the small one to measure the big one, even when the big one is a building.
