## What's the big idea?

2⁵ doesn't mean 2 × 5. It means 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2. Once you see powers as repeated multiplication, some shortcuts appear. They're called the **exponent laws**. You'll figure them out yourself by writing out the factors and counting them, so if you ever forget a rule you can rebuild it.
