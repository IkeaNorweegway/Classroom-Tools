## What's the big idea?

When something changes at a steady rate, its graph is a straight line. The **slope** tells you how fast it's changing. The **y-intercept** tells you where it started. You'll learn to switch between a table, a graph and an equation for the same situation, and pick whichever one answers your question fastest.
