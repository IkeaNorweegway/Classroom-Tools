## What's the big idea?

A **polynomial** is an expression with variables, like 3x² + 2x − 5. You'll use **algebra tiles**, which are little squares and rectangles, so you can actually see what each term means. Adding, subtracting and multiplying polynomials then becomes putting shapes together. The tiles show you why the rules work.
