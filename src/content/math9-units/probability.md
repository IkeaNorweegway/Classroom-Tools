## What's the big idea?

**Probability** measures how likely something is, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). Sometimes one event has no effect on the next, like flipping a coin twice. Those events are **independent**. Sometimes it does, like drawing two cards without putting the first one back. Those are **dependent**. You'll learn to tell them apart and work out the chance of both happening.
