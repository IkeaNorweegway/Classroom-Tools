### Example 1: Adding polynomials

**Simplify (3x² + 2x − 5) + (x² − 4x + 1).**

1. **Group the like terms.** x² terms: 3x² + x². x terms: 2x − 4x. Numbers: −5 + 1.
   *Say:* "Which terms are the same kind?"
2. **Combine each group.** 4x², −2x and −4.
3. **Answer:** 4x² − 2x − 4.

### Example 2: Subtracting polynomials

**Simplify (2x² − 3x + 4) − (x² + 5x − 2).**

1. **Subtracting a bracket means adding the opposite of every term in it.** − (x² + 5x − 2) becomes −x² − 5x + 2.
2. **Group and combine.** 2x² − x² = x². −3x − 5x = −8x. 4 + 2 = 6.
3. **Answer:** x² − 8x + 6.

**Common slip:** changing only the first sign, which gives x² + 2x + 2.

### Example 3: Multiplying two brackets

**Expand (x + 2)(x + 3).**

1. **Draw a 2 × 2 box.** Put x and 2 down the side, and x and 3 across the top.
2. **Fill in each cell.** x · x = x², x · 3 = 3x, 2 · x = 2x, 2 · 3 = 6.
   *Say:* "Every term in the first bracket multiplies every term in the second."
3. **Add the cells and combine.** x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6.
4. **Check with x = 1.** (1 + 2)(1 + 3) = 12, and 1 + 5 + 6 = 12. ✓

**Common slip:** writing x² + 6 and missing the two middle cells.
