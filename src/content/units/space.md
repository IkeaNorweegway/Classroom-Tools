## What's the big idea?

Our **solar system** is the Sun plus everything that orbits it: eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets like Pluto, asteroids and comets. It's mostly empty space, and the distances are huge. People explore it with **telescopes**, robot **probes** and **rovers**, and astronauts living on the **International Space Station**. **Satellites** stay up because they're falling around Earth so fast that they keep missing it. That's what an **orbit** is.
