## What's the big idea?

**Weather** is what's happening outside today. **Climate** is the usual pattern of weather in a place over many years. Gases in the air, like carbon dioxide, trap some of the Sun's heat and keep Earth warm enough to live on. That's the **greenhouse effect**. Burning fuels like gasoline adds more of these gases, so Earth is getting warmer. In Alberta that means shrinking glaciers, changing seasons and more wildfires.
