## What's the big idea?

**Debt** is a tool: it can speed up or undermine financial security depending on how it's used. **Compound interest** makes unpaid debt grow, so minimum payments cost a lot. A strong **credit score**, a smart **repayment strategy**, and avoiding **predatory loans** protect your financial future.
