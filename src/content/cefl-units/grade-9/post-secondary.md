## What's the big idea?

A pathway is **worth it** when its full cost (tuition, living costs, lost earnings and loan interest) is balanced by realistic job outcomes and your own goals. **Work-integrated learning** can lower the cost, and a strong plan always has a **contingency** and a clear **rationale**.
