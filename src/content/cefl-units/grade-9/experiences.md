## What's the big idea?

You don't have to wait for experience; you can **create** it through volunteering, **work-integrated learning** or a small **venture**. Experiences build skills, both **job-specific** and **employability**, and getting hired means showing those skills in a **résumé**, a **cover letter**, and a clean **digital identity**.
