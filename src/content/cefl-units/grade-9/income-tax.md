## What's the big idea?

A **tax return** compares the tax you really owe with what was already taken off your pay. **Deductions** lower the income that's taxed and **credits** lower the tax itself. **RRSPs** and **TFSAs** are registered accounts with different tax benefits, and filing a return also unlocks **government benefits**.
