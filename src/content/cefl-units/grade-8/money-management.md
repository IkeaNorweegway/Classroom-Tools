## What's the big idea?

**Financial security** comes from controlling **fixed and variable costs** so money is left to save. **Interest** works for you when you save: **compound interest** grows money faster over time. **Investing** can grow money more than saving, but carries **risk**, so the sooner you need money, the safer it should be.
