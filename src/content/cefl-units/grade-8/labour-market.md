## What's the big idea?

The **labour market** keeps changing because of **economic cycles**, **technology** and **social change**. Instead of counting on one job forever, people build **career resilience**: they keep learning, grow their **human capital** (education, knowledge, skills, experience), and network, so they can find new work when things change.
