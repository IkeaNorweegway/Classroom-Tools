## What's the big idea?

Programs after high school have **admission requirements**: specific courses and grades, and competitive ones take the highest applicants. Planning **high school courses early** keeps more doors open. **Scholarships, bursaries and grants** help pay without being repaid, and **networking** helps you learn what a program or career is really like.
