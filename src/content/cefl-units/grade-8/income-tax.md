## What's the big idea?

Canada taxes income in **brackets**: each slice is taxed at its own rate, so earning more always leaves you with more. A job's real value is its **total compensation** (salary plus benefits, bonuses and extras) after tax, and taxes fund the shared services everyone uses: the **social contract**.
