## What's the big idea?

Every dollar can only be used once, so every money choice is a **trade-off**. A **budget** is a written plan for your money that includes saving. A clear **goal** (what, how much, by when) tells you how much to save. Saving for surprises in an **emergency fund** protects your goals and gives you **financial security**.
