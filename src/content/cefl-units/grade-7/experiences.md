## What's the big idea?

**Experiences** at school, outside school, paid and unpaid, show you what kind of work you might like: indoors or outdoors, alone or on a team, working for someone or for yourself. Good work fits into a **balanced** life, and a good workplace makes people feel they **belong** and keeps them **safe**.
