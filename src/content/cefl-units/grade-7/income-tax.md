## What's the big idea?

Your **gross pay** is everything you earn. Before you get paid, **deductions** come off: **income tax**, which pays for services like hospitals and schools, **CPP**, which saves for your pension, and **EI**, which helps if you lose your job. What's left is your **net pay**, or "take-home pay."
