## What's the big idea?

**Post-secondary** means school or training after high school. All pathways can lead to good careers, but they differ in **time**, **cost**, **how you learn**, and **how hard they are to get into**. The **true cost** of a pathway includes tuition, living costs and the money you could have earned instead. Checking the **labour market** shows whether there will be jobs when you finish.
