## What's the big idea?

Everything is made of tiny particles that are always moving. **Temperature** tells you how fast they're moving on average. **Heat** is energy moving from something warmer to something cooler. Heat travels three ways: by touching (**conduction**), by moving liquids and gases (**convection**), and as invisible rays (**radiation**). Cold doesn't travel; heat does.
