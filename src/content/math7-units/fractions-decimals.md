## What's the big idea?

**Multiplying** by a fraction takes part of something. Half of 3/4 is 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8. Multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms. The answer can be smaller than what you started with. **Dividing** by a fraction asks: how many of these fit? There are 12 quarters in 3, so 3 ÷ 1/4 = 12. You can also keep the first number, flip the second one (its **reciprocal**) and multiply. Decimals work the same way. 0.3 × 0.2 is 3 tenths of 2 tenths: 0.06. To divide by a decimal, multiply both numbers by 10 first: 6 ÷ 0.5 = 60 ÷ 5 = 12. The **order of operations** says what to do first: brackets, then squares and cubes, then × and ÷, then + and −.
