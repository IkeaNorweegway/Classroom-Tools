## What's the big idea?

A **line** goes on forever both ways. A **ray** starts at one point and goes on forever one way. A **segment** stops at both ends. We name them with letters and symbols. Lines that never meet are **parallel**, like railway rails. Lines that meet at a square corner are **perpendicular**. When two lines cross, angles across from each other are equal. Angles side by side on a line add to 180°. A road across parallel rails makes equal angles at each rail. Shapes that are the same size and shape are **congruent**. Matching marks and letters show which parts match.
