## What's the big idea?

A **circle** is all the points that are the same distance from the centre. That distance is the **radius**. The **diameter** goes across, through the centre. It is two radii. The distance around a circle is its **circumference**. Measure any circle. The circumference is always about 3.14 diameters. We call this number **π** (pi). So C = π × d. Cut a circle into thin slices. Lay them tip up, tip down. They make a shape like a parallelogram. Its height is the radius. Its base is half the circumference. So the area is A = π × r × r.
