## What's the big idea?

A **prism** has two matching ends called **bases**. Its other faces are rectangles. We name a prism by its base: triangle bases make a triangular prism. A **cylinder** has two circle bases. The bases do not have to be on the bottom. **Volume** is the space inside. We count it in cubes, like cm³. One layer of cubes covers the base. Stack the layers up to the height. So volume = base area × height, or V = B × h. Know the volume and one of the others? Divide to find the missing one.
