## What's the big idea?

A yellow chip is **+1** and a red chip is **−1**. One of each makes a **zero pair**, worth 0. To **add**, put the chips together and take out the zero pairs. To **subtract**, take chips away. If there are none to take, add zero pairs first. That is why subtracting is the same as adding the opposite: 2 − (−3) = 2 + 3 = 5. To **multiply**, make groups: 3 × (−2) is three groups of −2, which is −6. For × and ÷, **count the negatives**. An even number gives a positive answer. An odd number gives a negative answer. To **divide**, ask the related multiplication: −12 ÷ 3 = −4, because 3 × (−4) = −12.
