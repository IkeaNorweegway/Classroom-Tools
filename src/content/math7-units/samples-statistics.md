## What's the big idea?

A **statistical question** has answers that vary, like "How long do Grade 7 students sleep?" The **population** is everyone the question is about. Asking all of them is a **census**. Often that costs too much or takes too long. So we ask a **sample**: part of the population. A fair sample is picked at random, so everyone has the same chance. Then we sum up the data with four numbers. The **mean** is the fair share. The **median** is the middle value in order. The **mode** comes up most often. The **range** is biggest − smallest. A second sample gives slightly different numbers, so we say "about", not "exactly".
