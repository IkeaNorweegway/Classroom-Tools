## What's the big idea?

A **ratio** compares two amounts. Juice made with 1 can of concentrate and 3 cans of water is 1:3. To make **equal ratios**, multiply or divide both numbers by the same **factor**: 1:3 = 2:6 = 4:12. Never add the same number to both. Equal ratios help you find a missing number and tell which mix is stronger. A **percent** is a ratio out of 100. Build percents from easy ones: 10% means divide by 10, and 35% is 25% + 10%. 0.5% is half of 1%. 150% is one and a half wholes. You use all of this for sales, GST, recipes and best buys.
