## What's the big idea?

Every number has a spot on the number line. **Left is smaller. Right is bigger.** So −8 is less than −3, just like −8 °C is colder than −3 °C. The **absolute value** of a number is how far it is from 0. A distance is never negative, so |−5| = 5. A fraction and a decimal can name the same spot: −3/4 = −0.75. To put numbers in order, change them to decimals, put them on a number line, and read from left to right.
