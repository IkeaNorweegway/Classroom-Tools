## What's the big idea?

An **equation** is like a balance. Both sides are equal. To keep it level, do the same thing to both sides. First, **simplify** each side. Put **like terms** together: 2x + 3x = 5x. Multiply every term in brackets: 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6. Then take the same thing off both sides until x is alone. If x is on both sides, take an x-term off both sides first. Last, **verify**: put your answer back in the first equation. Both sides should match. Equations help you find when two phone plans or two ski passes cost the same.
