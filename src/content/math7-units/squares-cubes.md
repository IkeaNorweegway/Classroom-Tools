## What's the big idea?

Some numbers of tiles make a square. 25 tiles make a 5 by 5 square, so 25 is a **perfect square**. We write 5 × 5 as **5²** and say "five squared." The 2 means two 5s multiplied, not 5 × 2. The **square root** goes backwards: √25 = 5 is the length of one side. Some numbers of small cubes make a big cube. 27 cubes make a 3 by 3 by 3 cube, so 3³ = 27 is a **perfect cube**. The **cube root** is the edge: ∛27 = 3. For a square, the fence around it is the **perimeter** (4 × side, in m). The space inside is the **area** (side², in m²). The space inside a cube is its **volume** (edge³, in m³).
