## What's the big idea?

This year's projects connect electricity, design and flight. You'll build **circuits** with littleBits, design a **bag tag** on the computer and 3-D print it, test **bridges** to see which shapes are strongest, and build a **glider**. You'll keep a design journal of what you tried, what happened and what you changed.
