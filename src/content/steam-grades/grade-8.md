## What's the big idea?

This year's projects are about **machines** and **systems**. Your class will build a **Rube Goldberg** chain where every group's step triggers the next. You'll code a **platform game** in Scratch, **solder** a motor circuit and gear it for more power, and program a **Spike Prime** robot through a maze. **Debugging**, finding and fixing problems, is a skill you'll use in every project.
