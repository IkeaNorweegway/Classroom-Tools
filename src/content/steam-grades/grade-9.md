## What's the big idea?

Grade 9 STEAM puts it all together. You'll learn a real programming language, **Java** or **Python**, and use it to control **REV robots**. You'll wire lights into **electrified miniature** scenes, and finish by designing, testing and improving a **trebuchet**. These are the skills behind careers in software, engineering and design.
