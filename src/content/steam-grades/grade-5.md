## What's the big idea?

**STEAM** stands for science, technology, engineering, arts and math, all mixed together in hands-on projects. This year you'll program **Dash** robots, design a **slow coaster**, build a **water filter**, and finish with an animated **Scratch** story. Every project uses the **design cycle**: plan it, build it, test it, then make it better. Mistakes are part of the plan.
