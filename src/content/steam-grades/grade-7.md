## What's the big idea?

This year is about **decisions** and **motion**. You'll program **Spike Prime** robots that react to their sensors, use a **micro:bit** to measure how well a box keeps heat in, write a **choose-your-own-adventure** story with branches, and design a **CO2 dragster** using Newton's laws. Planning with flowcharts and testing your ideas are the big skills.
