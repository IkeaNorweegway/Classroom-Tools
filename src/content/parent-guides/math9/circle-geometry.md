### Example 1: A tangent

**A tangent touches a circle at P. The radius is 5 cm. Point T is on the tangent, 12 cm from P. How far is T from the centre O?**

1. **Find the right angle.** A tangent meets the radius at 90°, so triangle OPT has a right angle at P.
   *Say:* "Where's the right angle hiding?"
2. **The hypotenuse is OT,** across from the right angle.
3. **Use Pythagoras.** OT² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169, so OT = 13 cm.

### Example 2: Angles at the centre and at the edge

**The angle at the centre standing on arc AB is 110°. What is the angle at the edge (on the circle) standing on the same arc?**

1. **Rule:** the angle at the edge is half the angle at the centre on the same arc.
2. **Calculate:** 110° ÷ 2 = 55°.

**Common slip:** doubling to 220°. An angle that big can't fit inside the triangle.

### Example 3: A chord

**A circle has radius 10 cm. A chord is 16 cm long. How far is the chord from the centre?**

1. **The line from the centre at 90° to the chord cuts it in half,** so each half is 8 cm.
2. **Make a right triangle:** half-chord (8), distance d, and radius (10) as the hypotenuse.
   *Say:* "Which side is the radius? That's the longest."
3. **Use Pythagoras.** d² = 10² − 8² = 100 − 64 = 36, so d = 6 cm.

**Common slip:** using the whole chord, 16, instead of half.
