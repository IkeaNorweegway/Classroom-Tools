### Example 1: Why settlers came

**Give a push factor and a pull factor for a Ukrainian farmer moving to Alberta in 1900.**

1. **Push (away from home):** too little land to farm and hard times at home.
2. **Pull (toward Canada):** 160 acres of land for a $10 fee under the Dominion Lands Act.
   *Say:* "Which one do you think mattered most to that family?"

### Example 2: Fact or opinion?

**Sort these sentences about the Komagata Maru (1914).**

1. "The ship carried 376 passengers from India." **Fact** (it can be checked in records).
2. "The passengers were not allowed to land, and the ship was escorted out by the navy." **Fact.**
3. "These newcomers would never fit into Canadian life." **Opinion** (a belief, often stated in newspapers of the time).
   *Say:* "Why might a newspaper in 1914 print an opinion as if it were a fact?"

### Example 3: Was it fair?

**Was the Chinese Head Tax fair? Use evidence.**

1. **What it was:** a fee charged only to Chinese immigrants, raised to $500 in 1903 (about two years' wages).
2. **Judge it:** no other group paid it, so it treated one group differently because of where they came from. That fits the definition of discrimination.
3. **Later:** Canada apologized in 2006.
