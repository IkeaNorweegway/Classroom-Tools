### Example 1: How a tariff works

**A sewing machine made in the U.S. costs $100. Canada adds a 30% tariff. A Canadian-made one costs $120.**

1. **Add the tariff:** $100 + $30 = $130 for the American machine.
2. **Compare:** the Canadian machine ($120) is now cheaper, so more people buy Canadian.
   *Say:* "Who is happy about this tariff? Who isn't?" (Canadian factories are; a Prairie farmer buying the machine pays more.)

**Common slip:** saying the United States pays the tariff.

### Example 2: The National Policy's three parts

**Name the three parts and what each did.**

1. **Tariffs** protected Canadian factories in Ontario and Quebec.
2. **The railway** (the CPR, finished 1885) carried goods and people across the country.
3. **Settling the West** brought farmers who grew wheat and bought eastern goods.

### Example 3: What is GDP?

**A tiny town grows $5,000 of wheat, builds $3,000 of furniture and gives $2,000 of haircuts in a year. What is its GDP?**

1. Add the value of all goods and services: $5,000 + $3,000 + $2,000 = **$10,000**.
   *Say:* "If the town grows more wheat next year, what happens to its GDP?"
