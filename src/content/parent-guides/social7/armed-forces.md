### Example 1: Steps to sovereignty

**Put these steps in order and say how each gave Canada more control.**

1. **1871:** the British Army leaves most of Canada, so Canada must defend itself.
2. **1873:** the NWMP is created to keep order in the West.
3. **1910:** the Royal Canadian Navy is created.
4. **1917:** the Canadian Corps, fighting together, takes Vimy Ridge.
5. **1931:** the Statute of Westminster gives Canada control of its foreign policy.
   *Say:* "Which step do you think mattered most? Why?"

### Example 2: Why Vimy mattered

**Explain why Vimy Ridge (April 1917) is important to Canadians.**

1. **What happened:** all four Canadian divisions fought together for the first time and captured a ridge other armies had failed to take.
2. **The cost:** about 3,600 Canadians were killed.
3. **The meaning:** many saw it as a moment when Canada acted as a nation, not just a colony.

**Common slip:** saying Canada declared war on its own in 1914.

### Example 3: Reading a memorial

**At a cenotaph, ask four questions.**

1. **Who** is named? 2. **Which wars** are listed? 3. **Who built it**, and when? 4. **What words or symbols** are used, and what do they say about how the community wanted to remember?
