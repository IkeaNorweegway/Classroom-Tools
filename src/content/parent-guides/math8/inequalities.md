### Example 1: Write "you must be at least 122 cm tall" with symbols

1. **Pick a letter.** Let h be the height in cm.
2. **Choose the symbol.** At least means 122 or more, so use ≥.
3. **Write it.** h ≥ 122.
   *Say:* "Can a rider who is exactly 122 cm go on? Then which symbol lets 122 in?"

**Common slip:** writing h ≤ 122 because "least" sounds like "less".

### Example 2: Graph x > 2 on a number line

1. **Find the boundary.** It is 2.
2. **Open or closed dot?** > leaves 2 out (2 > 2 is false), so draw an open dot ○.
3. **Which way?** Greater numbers are to the right. Draw the arrow right.
   *Say:* "Pick a number on your arrow. Is it bigger than 2?"

**Common slip:** filling in the dot, or pointing the arrow left because the symbol > looks like it points left.

### Example 3: Is x = −2 a solution of 3 − x < 4?

1. **Put it in.** 3 − (−2) = 3 + 2 = 5.
2. **Compare.** Is 5 < 4? No.
3. **Answer.** −2 is not a solution.
   *Say:* "What is 3 minus negative 2?"

**Common slip:** working out 3 − (−2) as 1 and saying yes.
