### Example 1: Find the hypotenuse

**A TV screen is 60 cm wide and 45 cm tall. How long is the diagonal?**

1. **Square both sides.** 60² = 3600 and 45² = 2025.
2. **Add.** 3600 + 2025 = 5625.
3. **Square root.** √5625 = 75 cm.
   *Say:* "Is 75 longer than both other sides? It should be."

**Common slip:** adding the sides (60 + 45 = 105). Add the squares, then take the square root.

### Example 2: Find a missing leg

**A 13 m ladder leans on a wall with its foot 5 m out. How high does it reach?**

1. **Which side is the hypotenuse?** The ladder (across from the square corner of the wall and ground).
2. **Subtract the squares.** 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144.
3. **Square root.** √144 = 12 m.
   *Say:* "Should the height be shorter or longer than the ladder?"

**Common slip:** 169 + 25 = 194, √194 ≈ 13.9 m. That's longer than the ladder, which is impossible.
