### Example 1: Multiply 2 1/2 × 1 1/3

1. **Change to improper fractions.** 2 1/2 = 5/2 and 1 1/3 = 4/3.
2. **Multiply tops, multiply bottoms.** 5 × 4 = 20 and 2 × 3 = 6. That's 20/6.
3. **Simplify.** 20/6 = 10/3 = 3 1/3.
   *Say:* "About how big should it be? 2.5 × 1.3 is about 3."

**Common slip:** 2 × 1 = 2 and 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6, giving 2 1/6. The estimate (about 3) shows that's too small.

### Example 2: How many 3/4 m pieces in a 6 m board?

1. **It's a "how many fit" question,** so divide: 6 ÷ 3/4.
2. **Keep, change, flip.** 6 × 4/3 = 24/3 = 8.
3. **Check.** 8 pieces × 3/4 m = 6 m. ✓
   *Say:* "Does 8 pieces of three-quarters of a metre make 6 metres?"

**Common slip:** flipping the 6 instead: 1/6 × 3/4. The answer would be tiny, which doesn't make sense.
