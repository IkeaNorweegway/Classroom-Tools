### Example 1: Find the slope through (1, 2) and (4, 8)

1. **Rise.** Subtract the y-values: 8 − 2 = 6.
2. **Run.** Subtract the x-values in the same order: 4 − 1 = 3.
3. **Divide.** Slope = 6 ÷ 3 = 2. The line goes up 2 for every 1 across.
   *Say:* "Which number goes on top: up or across?"

**Common slip:** dividing run by rise and getting 3/6 = 1/2.

### Example 2: Read y = −2x + 5

1. **Find m.** The number next to x is −2. The slope is −2.
2. **Find b.** The number on its own is 5. The line crosses the y-axis at (0, 5).
3. **Picture it.** Start at (0, 5). Go right 1 and down 2, again and again.
   *Say:* "Is the line going uphill or downhill? How do you know?"

**Common slip:** saying the slope is 5.

### Example 3: A taxi charges $4 to start and $2 per km

1. **Write it.** Cost = 2 × km + 4, or C = 2d + 4.
2. **Explain the numbers.** The slope 2 is the cost for each km. The 4 is the starting fee.
3. **Use it.** A 7 km ride costs 2 × 7 + 4 = $18.
   *Say:* "What would a 0 km ride cost? Why?"

**Common slip:** thinking the $4 is the cost per km and getting 4 × 7 + 2 = $30.
