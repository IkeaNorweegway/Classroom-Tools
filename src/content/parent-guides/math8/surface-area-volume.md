### Example 1: Surface area of a box

**A box is 10 cm long, 4 cm wide and 3 cm tall.**

1. **Front and back.** 10 × 3 = 30, two of them = 60 cm².
2. **Top and bottom.** 10 × 4 = 40, two of them = 80 cm².
3. **Two sides.** 4 × 3 = 12, two of them = 24 cm².
4. **Add.** 60 + 80 + 24 = 164 cm².
   *Say:* "Which faces match? How many pairs are there?"

### Example 2: Volume of a can

**A can has radius 4 cm and height 10 cm. Use 3.14 for π.**

1. **Area of the base (a circle).** 3.14 × 4 × 4 = 50.24 cm².
2. **Times the height.** 50.24 × 10 = 502.4 cm³.
   *Say:* "How many layers like the bottom are stacked up?"

**Common slip:** using the diameter (8) instead of the radius (4), which gives 4 times too much.
