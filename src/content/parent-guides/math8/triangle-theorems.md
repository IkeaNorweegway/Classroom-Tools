### Example 1: Find a missing angle

A triangle has angles of 48° and 75°. Find the third angle.

1. **Add the angles you know.** 48° + 75° = 123°.
2. **Subtract from 180°.** 180° − 123° = 57°.
   *Say:* "What do all three angles of a triangle add to?"

**Common slip:** subtracting from 360° (that is for four-sided shapes).

### Example 2: Do the sticks make a triangle?

Can sticks of 4 cm, 5 cm and 10 cm make a triangle?

1. **Add the two shorter sides.** 4 + 5 = 9.
2. **Compare with the longest.** 9 is less than 10, so the short sticks can't reach each other. No triangle.
   *Say:* "Are the two short ones together longer than the long one?"

**Common slip:** checking only that the numbers are positive, or adding the longest side to another one.

### Example 3: How high does the ladder reach?

A 6.0 m ladder leans on a wall. Its foot is 1.5 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

1. **Find the hypotenuse.** The ladder is across from the square corner, so c = 6.0.
2. **The height is a leg, so subtract.** 6.0² − 1.5² = 36 − 2.25 = 33.75.
3. **Take the square root.** √33.75 ≈ 5.8 m.
   *Say:* "Is the side you want the longest side, or a shorter one? Should your answer be shorter than the ladder?"

**Common slip:** adding the squares (36 + 2.25) and getting 6.2 m. That is taller than the ladder itself.
