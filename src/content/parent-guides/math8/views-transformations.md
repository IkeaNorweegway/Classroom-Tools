### Example 1: Draw the views

**A row of 3 blocks, with one extra block on top of the left one.**

1. **Top view (looking down).** 3 squares in a row.
2. **Front view.** 3 columns: the left one is 2 squares tall; the others are 1.
3. **Right side view.** 1 column, 2 squares tall (the tall stack shows above the near block).
   *Say:* "What can you actually see from here? Not what you know is there."

### Example 2: Slides, flips and turns

**Point A is at (3, 2). Find A′ after each move.**

1. **Slide 5 left, 1 up.** (3 − 5, 2 + 1) = (−2, 3).
2. **Flip in the x-axis.** (3, −2): x stays, y changes sign.
3. **Half turn about (0, 0).** (−3, −2): both signs change.
   *Say:* "Which number changes, and which stays the same?"

**Common slip:** flipping in the x-axis gives (−3, 2). That's a flip in the y-axis.
