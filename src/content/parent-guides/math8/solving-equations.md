### Example 1: Solve ½x + 3 = 5

1. **Clear the fraction.** Multiply every term by 2: x + 6 = 10.
2. **Solve.** Take 6 from both sides: x = 4.
3. **Check.** Half of 4 is 2, and 2 + 3 = 5. ✓
   *Say:* "Did every term get multiplied by 2, even the 3 and the 5?"

**Common slip:** writing x + 3 = 5 and getting x = 2. Half of 2 plus 3 is 4, not 5.

### Example 2: Solve −2(x − 3) = 10

1. **Multiply out the brackets.** −2 times x is −2x. −2 times −3 is +6. So −2x + 6 = 10.
2. **Solve.** Take 6 from both sides: −2x = 4. Divide by −2: x = −2.
3. **Check.** −2 − 3 = −5, and −2 × (−5) = 10. ✓
   *Say:* "What is a negative times a negative?"

**Common slip:** writing −2x − 6, which gives the wrong answer x = −8.

### Example 3: A taxi costs $3.50 plus $1.75 per km. The fare is $17.50. How far?

1. **Write the equation.** Let x be the km: 3.50 + 1.75x = 17.50.
2. **Clear the decimals.** Multiply every term by 100: 350 + 175x = 1750. Take 350 away: 175x = 1400. So x = 8.
3. **Answer in words and check.** The ride was 8 km. 8 × $1.75 = $14, plus $3.50 is $17.50. ✓
   *Say:* "What does the 8 stand for?"

**Common slip:** multiplying only 1.75 by 100, which leaves 3.50 + 175x = 17.50.
