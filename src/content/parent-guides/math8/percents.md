### Example 1: Find 15% of $60 in your head

1. **10% of $60.** Divide by 10: $6.
2. **5% of $60.** Half of 10%: $3.
3. **Add them.** 15% = $6 + $3 = $9.
   *Say:* "What's 10% first? Can you build 15% from that?"

**Common slip:** dividing by 15. Percent means out of 100, so 15% of 60 is 0.15 × 60, not 60 ÷ 15.

### Example 2: A sale on a sale

**A $100 hoodie is 20% off. Then the store takes 20% off the sale price. What do you pay?**

1. **First sale.** 20% of $100 is $20. The price is $80.
2. **Second sale.** 20% of $80 is $16. The price is $64.
   *Say:* "The second 20% is of which price?"
3. **Total discount.** $100 − $64 = $36. That's 36% off, not 40%.

**Common slip:** saying "40% off, so $60." The second discount is smaller because it comes off a smaller price.
