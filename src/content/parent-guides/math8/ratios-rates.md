### Example 1: Scale a recipe

**Pancakes use 2 cups of flour for every 3 eggs. How many eggs for 8 cups of flour?**

1. **How many times bigger?** 8 ÷ 2 = 4. The recipe is 4 times bigger.
2. **Multiply both parts.** 3 eggs × 4 = 12 eggs.
   *Say:* "If the flour is 4 times bigger, what happens to the eggs?"
3. **Write it.** 2 : 3 = 8 : 12.

**Common slip:** adding 6 to both (2 + 6 = 8, so 3 + 6 = 9 eggs). That changes the recipe.

### Example 2: Better buy

**Hockey tape: 3 rolls for $6.00 or 5 rolls for $9.00.**

1. **Price for one roll in each pack.** $6.00 ÷ 3 = $2.00. $9.00 ÷ 5 = $1.80.
2. **Compare.** $1.80 is less, so the 5-pack is the better buy.
   *Say:* "What does ONE roll cost in each pack?"

**Common slip:** choosing the 3-pack because $6 is less than $9. It costs less in total but more per roll.
