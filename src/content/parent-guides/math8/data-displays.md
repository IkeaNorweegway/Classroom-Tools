### Example 1: Choose the graph

**Your child wants to show the temperature each month of the year.**

1. **What changes?** Temperature, over time.
2. **Which graph shows change over time?** A line graph.
   *Say:* "Is this parts of a whole, groups to compare, or change over time?"

**Common slip:** a circle graph. Months aren't parts of one whole, and temperatures can be negative.

### Example 2: Spot the trick

**A bar graph shows Team A with 52 wins and Team B with 48. The side axis starts at 45.**

1. **Read the numbers.** 52 and 48: a difference of 4.
2. **Look at the bars.** Team A's bar looks more than twice as tall.
3. **Why?** The axis starts at 45, not 0, so a small gap looks huge.
   *Say:* "Where does the number line start?"
