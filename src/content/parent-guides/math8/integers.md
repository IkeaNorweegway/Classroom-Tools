### Example 1: (−6) × (−4) and (−30) ÷ 5

1. **(−6) × (−4).** Two negatives (even), so the answer is positive. 6 × 4 = 24. Answer: 24.
2. **(−30) ÷ 5.** One negative (odd), so the answer is negative. 30 ÷ 5 = 6. Answer: −6.
   *Say:* "How many negative signs are there?"

**Common slip:** writing (−6) × (−4) = −24 because "the answer should be negative when you see negatives."

### Example 2: Order of operations with negatives

**Find 7 + (−2) × 5.**

1. **Multiply first.** (−2) × 5 = −10.
2. **Then add.** 7 + (−10) = −3.
   *Say:* "Which step comes first, adding or multiplying?"

**Common slip:** adding first: 7 + (−2) = 5, then 5 × 5 = 25.
