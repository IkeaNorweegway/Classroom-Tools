### Example 1: List the outcomes

**Flip a coin and roll a die. What is P(heads and an even number)?**

1. **List everything.** H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, H6, T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6: 12 outcomes.
2. **Circle the ones that work.** H2, H4, H6: 3 outcomes.
3. **Probability.** 3/12 = 1/4.
   *Say:* "How many outcomes are there in all? How many work?"

### Example 2: The multiply rule

**There's a 30% chance of rain Saturday and a 40% chance Sunday. What's the chance of rain on both days?**

1. **Write each as a decimal.** 0.3 and 0.4.
2. **Multiply.** 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12, or 12%.
   *Say:* "Is it more or less likely to rain both days than just on Saturday?"

**Common slip:** adding to get 70%. That's bigger than either chance alone, which can't be right for "both."
