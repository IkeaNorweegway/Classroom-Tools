### Example 1: A book on a table

**A book sits still on a table. What forces act on it?**

1. **Gravity** pulls the book down.
2. **The table** pushes the book up.
3. **The arrows are the same length,** so the forces are **balanced** and the book doesn't move.
   *Say:* "If the forces are balanced, what happens?" (Nothing changes.)

**Common slip:** saying there are no forces because nothing is moving.

### Example 2: A small car and a big truck bump

**Which one pushes harder on the other?**

1. **They push on each other equally,** in opposite directions. That's an action-reaction pair.
2. **The car is affected more** because it has less mass, so the same force changes its motion more.
   *Say:* "If the forces are equal, why does the car get bumped further?"
