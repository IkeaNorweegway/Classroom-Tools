### Example 1: How big and how far?

**If the Sun were a basketball, how big would Earth be, and how far away?**

1. **Earth** would be about the size of a peppercorn.
2. **It would be about 26 metres away,** about the length of a school gym.
3. **Neptune** would be almost a kilometre away.
   *Say:* "So is the solar system crowded, or mostly empty?"

### Example 2: Why doesn't a satellite fall down?

1. **Gravity pulls the satellite toward Earth.**
2. **But it's also moving sideways very fast.**
3. **As it falls, Earth's surface curves away,** so it keeps falling around Earth instead of into it. That's an orbit.
   *Say:* "What would happen if it stopped moving sideways?" (It would fall to Earth.)
