### Example 1: Reading a food chain

**grass → grasshopper → bird → hawk. What does each arrow mean?**

1. **The arrow points from the food to the eater.** It shows energy moving.
2. **Grass is the producer.** It makes its own food from sunlight.
3. **The grasshopper, bird and hawk are consumers.**
   *Say:* "Where did the hawk's energy first come from?" (The Sun, through the grass.)

### Example 2: One change ripples through

**Wolves return to a park. What happens?**

1. **Wolves hunt elk,** so elk spend less time grazing along rivers.
2. **Plants along the rivers grow back.**
3. **The roots hold the riverbanks** in place, and birds and beavers return.
   *Say:* "What else could this one change affect?"

**Common slip:** stopping after the first effect. Ecosystems are connected in many ways.
