### Example 1: Renewable or not?

**Sort: wind, oil, sunlight, coal.**

1. **Renewable:** wind and sunlight. They keep coming and won't run out.
2. **Non-renewable:** oil and coal. They took millions of years to form and are used up much faster.
   *Say:* "Could we run out of it in our lifetimes?"

### Example 2: Choosing an energy source

**A town is choosing between a wind farm and a natural gas plant. What should it think about?**

1. **Cost:** how much it costs to build and run.
2. **Reliability:** gas works any time; wind only works when it's windy.
3. **Environment:** gas releases carbon dioxide; wind turbines take up land and can affect birds.
4. **Decision:** many towns use a mix, so one source can back up the other.
   *Say:* "What would you choose, and what's the trade-off?"
