### Example 1: Weather or climate?

**Sort: (a) It's snowing in Calgary today. (b) Calgary usually has cold winters with chinooks.**

1. **(a) is weather:** what's happening right now.
2. **(b) is climate:** the usual pattern over many years.
   *Say:* "Is it about today, or about many years?"

### Example 2: The greenhouse effect

**Why is a car warmer inside on a sunny day, and how is Earth similar?**

1. **Sunlight passes through** the car windows and warms the seats.
2. **The heat can't escape easily,** so the car gets warmer.
3. **Earth is similar:** greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide trap some heat. Adding more gases traps more heat.
   *Say:* "What adds extra greenhouse gases?" (Burning fuels like gasoline, coal and natural gas.)

**Common slip:** saying the greenhouse effect is the hole in the ozone layer.
