### Example 1: Push and pull

**In 1948 a family leaves a war-damaged city in Europe and settles in Edmonton. Why?**

1. **Push factors** (reasons to leave): war damage, few jobs, difficult living conditions.
2. **Pull factors** (reasons to come): jobs in Alberta's growing oil industry, safety, family already in Canada.
   *Say:* "Which reasons pushed them out, and which pulled them here?"

### Example 2: Was the boom worth it?

**Weigh the growth of Alberta's cities after oil was struck at Leduc in 1947.**

1. **Benefits:** jobs, new businesses, schools and roads, money for public services.
2. **Costs:** pollution, farmland turned into suburbs, wildlife habitat broken up.
3. **A judgement with evidence:** "Mostly worth it, because…" or "Not worth it, because…", using at least two facts.
   *Say:* "Which fact best supports your view?"

**Common slip:** listing only one side before deciding.
