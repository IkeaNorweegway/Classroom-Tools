### Example 1: Which Charter right?

**A school bans students from wearing a religious head covering. Which rights are involved?**

1. **Fundamental freedoms:** freedom of religion.
2. **Equality rights:** not being treated unfairly because of religion.
3. **Could there be a limit?** Under section 1, a limit must be reasonable and justified, for example for a real safety reason. Courts decide.
   *Say:* "What would the school need to show to justify the rule?"

### Example 2: Citizen or not?

**Match each person to their status: (a) born in Calgary, (b) moved here five years ago to live permanently, (c) studying here for two years, (d) fled persecution and was given protection.**

1. **(a) Citizen.**
2. **(b) Permanent resident:** can live and work here for good, but can't vote yet.
3. **(c) Temporary resident:** here for a set time.
4. **(d) Refugee:** given protection in Canada.
   *Say:* "Which of these people can vote?" (Only the citizen.)
