### Example 1: Who handles it?

**Which level of government handles a pothole, a new school and a passport?**

1. **Pothole:** municipal (the city).
2. **New school:** provincial (Alberta runs education).
3. **Passport:** federal (citizenship and travel).
   *Say:* "Is it a local, provincial or national job?"

### Example 2: How a bill becomes law

**Follow a bill through Parliament.**

1. **First reading:** the bill is introduced.
2. **Second reading:** MPs debate the main idea.
3. **Committee stage:** a small group studies it closely and may suggest changes.
4. **Third reading:** a final debate and vote in the House of Commons.
5. **Senate:** senators go through the same steps.
6. **Royal assent:** the Governor General signs, and it becomes law.
   *Say:* "Where could the bill be changed or stopped?"
