### Example 1: Causes of the Great Depression

**Why did the Great Depression happen?**

1. **Overproduction:** factories and farms made more than people could buy.
2. **The 1929 stock market crash:** people and businesses lost money and stopped spending.
3. **Trade collapsed:** countries put taxes on imports, so exports like Canadian wheat had fewer buyers.
4. **Drought on the Prairies:** crops failed for years in a row.
   *Say:* "Which of these made the others worse?"

### Example 2: Free trade, winners and losers

**Who might gain and who might lose when trade with the U.S. gets easier?**

1. **Gain:** shoppers pay lower prices, and businesses that export find more customers.
2. **Lose:** some workers lose jobs if their products can be made more cheaply elsewhere.
3. **A balanced view:** "Free trade helped ___, but hurt ___."
   *Say:* "Can you think of someone in each group?"
