### Example 1: An independent decision

**How did joining the Second World War show Canada's independence?**

1. **In 1914,** Canada was at war the moment Britain declared war.
2. **In 1939,** Britain declared war on September 3; Canada's Parliament debated and declared war on September 10.
3. **That week** showed Canada was making its own decision.
   *Say:* "Why does a one-week gap matter?"

### Example 2: Two perspectives on serving

**Why might a Chinese Canadian soldier have felt differently about serving than a soldier with full rights?**

1. **The fact:** many Chinese Canadians volunteered even though they couldn't vote in federal elections.
2. **A perspective:** some hoped serving would prove their loyalty and win them equal rights.
3. **The result:** Chinese Canadians gained the federal vote in 1947.
   *Say:* "What would you be fighting for, if you didn't have full rights at home?"
