### Example 1: Steps to independence

**Put these in order and explain what each changed: Statute of Westminster, Canada declares war in 1939, Balfour Declaration, First World War.**

1. **First World War (1914–1918):** Canada is at war automatically, because Britain is.
2. **Balfour Declaration (1926):** Britain and the Dominions agree they are equals.
3. **Statute of Westminster (1931):** that equality becomes law; Canada controls its own foreign policy.
4. **1939:** Canada declares war on Germany on its own, a week after Britain.
   *Say:* "What's different between 1914 and 1939?"

### Example 2: The Persons Case

**What did the Famous Five ask, and what happened?**

1. **The question:** does the word "persons" in Canada's constitution include women? If so, women could be appointed to the Senate.
2. **1928:** the Supreme Court of Canada said no.
3. **1929:** the Famous Five appealed to the Privy Council in Britain, which said yes, women are persons.
   *Say:* "Why did they have to go all the way to Britain?"

**Common slip:** saying the case gave women the right to vote.
