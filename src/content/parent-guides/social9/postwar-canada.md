### Example 1: Before and after

**How did health care change after the war?**

1. **Before:** families paid doctors and hospitals themselves, and a serious illness could mean huge bills.
2. **The change:** Saskatchewan started public hospital and medical insurance, and by the early 1970s every province had joined a national plan.
3. **After:** most medical care is paid for through taxes.
   *Say:* "Who pays now, and who paid before?"

### Example 2: Progress and harm at the same time

**How can one era include both progress and harm?**

1. **Progress:** new social programs, peacekeeping and growing multiculturalism.
2. **Harm:** residential schools and the Sixties Scoop took Indigenous children from their families, languages and cultures.
3. **Both are true:** history has to include both, and many of the effects are still felt today.
   *Say:* "Whose experience of this time would be very different from the official story?"
