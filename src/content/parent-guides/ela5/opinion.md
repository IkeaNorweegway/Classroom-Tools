### Example 1: Fact or opinion?

**"This cereal has 9 grams of sugar, and it's the tastiest breakfast ever!"**

1. "9 grams of sugar": you can check the label. **Fact.**
2. "The tastiest breakfast ever": people disagree. **Opinion.**
   *Say:* "Could that be checked? How?"

### Example 2: Reason and evidence

**Opinion: recess should be longer.**

1. **Reason:** Kids need more activity.
2. **Evidence:** Canada's guidelines say kids need 60 minutes of hard activity a day, and most kids don't get it.
   *Say:* "Which part is the reason? Which part proves it?"

### Example 3: Answering the other side

1. **The other side:** "Longer recess means less learning time."
2. **Answer:** "That's a fair point. However, students who move focus better when they come back."
   *Say:* "How can you disagree with someone and still be respectful?"
