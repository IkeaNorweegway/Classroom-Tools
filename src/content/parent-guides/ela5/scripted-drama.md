plainSummary: "Your child is reading, writing and performing short plays. They are learning how scripts work (character names, dialogue without quotation marks, and stage directions in brackets), how actors use words, body language and voice to show a character's feelings, and how to project their voice. In groups, they turn a story they read this year into a short play, each student writes a scene, and the class performs for an audience. The unit ends with a look back at everything they learned this year."

### Example 1: Story to script

**Story:** *"I'm starving!" shouted Max, dropping his backpack.*

1. **Script:** MAX: (dropping his backpack, shouting) I'm starving!
2. Name, colon, stage direction in brackets, no quotation marks.
   *Say:* "What's the difference between what Max says and what Max does?"

### Example 2: One line, many meanings

**"I didn't take your lunch."**

1. Stress **I**: someone else did. Stress **your**: I took someone else's.
2. Same words, different meaning.
   *Say:* "Which word would a guilty person stress?"

### Example 3: Show, don't tell

1. *Tells:* JADA: I am so hungry.
2. *Shows:* JADA: (staring at Priya's lunch) Are you… going to finish those?
   *Say:* "How can a character show a feeling without naming it?"
