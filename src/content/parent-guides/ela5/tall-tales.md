### Example 1: What does it really mean?

**"It was so cold that the words froze in the air."**

1. **Is it real?** No, words can't freeze. It is an **exaggeration**.
2. **What does it really mean?** It was extremely cold.
3. **Why did the author use it?** It helps us picture the cold, and it's funny.
   *Say:* "Can you make up your own exaggeration about how hot it was in summer?"

### Example 2: Telling a story short

**Summarize *The Three Little Pigs* in one sentence.**

1. **Somebody:** three little pigs. **Wanted:** safe houses. **But:** a wolf blew down two. **So:** they hid in the brick house. **Then:** they were safe.
2. **Summary:** Three pigs built houses, but a wolf blew two down, so they hid in the brick house and were safe.
   *Say:* "What small details did we leave out?"

### Example 3: Show, don't tell

**Instead of "The giant was hungry," show it.**

1. *Tell:* The giant was hungry.
2. *Show:* The giant's stomach growled so loudly that windows rattled three towns away.
   *Say:* "What could the giant do, say or look like that would show it?"
