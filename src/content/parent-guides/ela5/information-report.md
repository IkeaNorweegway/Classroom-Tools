### Example 1: Finding facts fast

**Question: What is a chinook?**

1. Read the **headings** first. One says "The chinook."
2. Read just that part: "a warm, dry wind that can raise the temperature by 20 degrees or more in a few hours."
   *Say:* "How did the heading save you time?"

### Example 2: Notes in your own words

**Book:** "Ice sheets are so heavy that they slowly flow. As the ice moved, it scraped the land like a giant bulldozer."

1. **Notes:** ice sheets heavy · flow slowly · scraped land
2. **Own words:** The ice was so heavy it crept along and scraped the ground.
   *Say:* "Close the book. How would you explain it to your little cousin?"

### Example 3: A TIDE paragraph

1. **T**opic sentence: *The Foothills are a land of rolling hills and ridges.*
2. **I**mportant evidence: *They lie just east of the Rocky Mountains and get more rain and snow than the prairie.*
3. **D**etail: *For example, the Athabasca River winds through the Foothills near Hinton.*
4. **E**nding: *The Foothills are where the mountains slowly turn into plains.*
   *Say:* "Which sentence is the detail? What makes it a detail?"
