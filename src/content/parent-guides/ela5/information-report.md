### Example 1: Finding facts fast

**Question: How long is the small intestine?**

1. Read the **headings** first. One says "Step 4: the small intestine."
2. Read just that part: "about 6 metres long."
   *Say:* "How did the heading save you time?"

### Example 2: Notes in your own words

**Book:** "Bones are alive. They have blood vessels and nerves, and they can repair themselves when they break."

1. **Notes:** bones alive · blood vessels + nerves · fix themselves
2. **Own words:** Bones are living, and a broken bone can heal itself.
   *Say:* "Close the book. How would you explain it to your little cousin?"

### Example 3: A TIDE paragraph

1. **T**opic sentence: *The heart is the engine of the circulatory system.*
2. **I**mportant evidence: *It is a muscle the size of your fist that beats about 100,000 times a day.*
3. **D**etail: *For example, the right side sends blood to the lungs.*
4. **E**nding: *Without this pump, blood would not move.*
   *Say:* "Which sentence is the detail? What makes it a detail?"
