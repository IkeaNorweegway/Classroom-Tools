### Example 1: What does a comparison mean?

**"Hope is the thing with feathers / That perches in the soul." (Emily Dickinson)**

1. **Compared:** hope and a small bird.
2. **In common:** both are small, easy to miss, and keep singing even in a storm.
   *Say:* "Why a bird, and not a lion?"

### Example 2: Show the feeling

1. *Tell:* I was happy on the rink.
2. *Show:* Out here, gliding, I am a stone skipped across a lake.
   *Say:* "What feeling does that line give you?"

### Example 3: Reading with expression

**"Dash, dash, / With a mighty crash" (E. Pauline Johnson)**

1. These words are loud and fast because the river is crashing through rapids.
2. Read them loud and quick; read the calm parts soft and slow.
   *Say:* "How should this line sound? Why?"
