### Example 1: Topic or theme?

**Story: *The Tortoise and the Hare***

1. **Topic:** effort (one word).
2. **Theme:** Steady effort can beat talent that doesn't try (a sentence).
   *Say:* "What does the story say *about* effort?"

### Example 2: A flash-forward

**"Sixty years later, the old man watched children fill their buckets with clean water from a filter he had designed."**

1. The words "Sixty years later" tell the reader the story jumped ahead.
2. It shows what a young character's choice led to.
   *Say:* "Why might an author show us the future before the story ends?"

### Example 3: Show the future

1. *Tell:* It was the future.
2. *Show:* The door slid open when it recognized her face.
   *Say:* "What detail would show us your story is in the future?"
