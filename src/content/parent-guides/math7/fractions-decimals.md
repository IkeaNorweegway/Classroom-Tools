### Example 1: Find 1/2 × 3/4

1. **Say it in words.** 1/2 × 3/4 means half of 3/4.
2. **Multiply the tops.** 1 × 3 = 3.
3. **Multiply the bottoms.** 2 × 4 = 8. So 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8.
   *Say:* "Is your answer smaller than 3/4? It should be, because you took half of it."

**Common slip:** changing to 2/4 × 3/4 first and writing 6/4.

### Example 2: Find 2/3 ÷ 1/6

1. **Ask how many fit.** How many sixths fit in 2/3? 2/3 = 4/6, so 4 sixths fit.
2. **Or use the reciprocal.** Keep 2/3. Change ÷ to ×. Flip 1/6 to 6/1.
3. **Multiply.** 2/3 × 6 = 12/3 = 4.
   *Say:* "Which fraction do you flip: the first one or the one you divide by?"

**Common slip:** flipping the first fraction: 3/2 × 1/6 = 3/12.

### Example 3: Find 4.8 ÷ 0.6

1. **Make the divisor a whole number.** Multiply both numbers by 10: 48 ÷ 6.
2. **Divide.** 48 ÷ 6 = 8.
3. **Check.** 8 × 0.6 = 4.8. ✓
   *Say:* "How many pieces of 0.6 m fit in 4.8 m of ribbon?"

**Common slip:** answering 0.8 by keeping the decimal point.
