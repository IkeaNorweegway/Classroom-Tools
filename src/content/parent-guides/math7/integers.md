### Example 1: Find −5 + 3

1. **Make chips.** 5 red chips (−5) and 3 yellow chips (+3).
2. **Make zero pairs.** Each red and yellow pair is worth 0. There are 3 pairs.
3. **Count what is left.** 2 red chips: −5 + 3 = −2.
   *Say:* "Which colour is left over? How many?"

**Common slip:** writing 2 instead of −2.

### Example 2: Find 2 − (−3)

1. **Add the opposite.** Keep 2, change − to +, change −3 to 3: 2 + 3.
2. **Add.** 2 + 3 = 5.
3. **Picture it.** On a thermometer, going from −3 to 2 is 5 degrees.
   *Say:* "What is the opposite of −3?"

**Common slip:** writing 2 − (−3) = −1 by subtracting 3.

### Example 3: Find (−2)(3)(−5) and −24 ÷ (−6)

1. **Count the negatives.** (−2)(3)(−5) has 2 negatives. Even, so positive. 2 × 3 × 5 = 30. Answer: 30.
2. **Divide with the same rule.** −24 ÷ (−6): two negatives, so positive. 24 ÷ 6 = 4. Answer: 4.
3. **Check with ×.** 4 × (−6) = −24. ✓
   *Say:* "How many negative signs? Even or odd?"

**Common slip:** using the "two negatives" rule for adding, like −3 + (−4) = 7.
