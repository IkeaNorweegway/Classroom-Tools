### Example 1: A complete circuit

**Why does the light only turn on when the switch is closed?**

1. **A circuit is a loop:** battery → switch → light → back to the battery.
2. **A closed switch** completes the loop, so electricity flows and the light turns on.
3. **An open switch** breaks the loop, so nothing flows.
   *Say:* "Where's the gap when the light is off?"

### Example 2: Why triangles?

**Build a square and a triangle out of straws and pins, then push on a corner.**

1. **The square** folds into a diamond shape. It isn't rigid.
2. **The triangle** keeps its shape. Its sides can't move without one of them stretching or breaking.
3. **So bridges and towers use triangles** to stay stiff and strong.
   *Say:* "Where else have you seen triangles holding something up?"
