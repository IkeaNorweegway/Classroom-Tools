### Example 1: Writing an algorithm

**Write an algorithm to move a robot three squares forward, turn right, then move one square.**

1. **Sequence:** list the exact steps in order: forward, forward, forward, turn right, forward.
2. **Loop:** the first three steps repeat, so write "repeat 3 times: forward".
3. **Test it:** walk through the steps, or run them on the robot, and fix anything that goes wrong.
   *Say:* "Could a robot follow your steps exactly, with no guessing?"

### Example 2: Using the design cycle

**Your child's paper airplane only flies 2 metres. What next?**

1. **Plan:** pick one thing to change, like the wing size.
2. **Build:** make the change.
3. **Test:** throw it the same way three times and measure.
4. **Improve:** keep the change if it helped, then try the next idea.
   *Say:* "What did you change, and how do you know it helped?"

**Common slip:** changing the wings and the nose at once, then not knowing which one made the difference.
