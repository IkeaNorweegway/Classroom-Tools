### Example 1: Mechanical advantage

**A lever lets your child lift a 60 N weight by pushing with 20 N. What is the mechanical advantage?**

1. **Formula:** mechanical advantage = output force ÷ input force.
2. **Calculate:** 60 ÷ 20 = 3.
3. **The trade-off:** they have to push their end about 3 times as far as the weight moves.
   *Say:* "What do you give up to get the easier push?"

### Example 2: Debugging a game

**In your child's game, the character falls straight through a platform. How should they fix it?**

1. **Make the bug happen again** so they can watch it.
2. **Find the code** that checks for touching the platform.
3. **Make one change,** like stopping the fall when the character touches the platform.
4. **Test again.** If it's not fixed, undo and try the next idea.
   *Say:* "What exactly did you change, and what happened?"
