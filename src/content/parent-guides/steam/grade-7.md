### Example 1: An if-then rule for a robot

**A robot should stop before it hits a wall. Write the rule.**

1. **The sensor:** a distance sensor measures how far away the wall is.
2. **The rule:** if the distance is less than 10 cm, then stop and turn; else keep moving forward.
3. **Test it:** try it in the hallway and adjust the 10 cm if needed.
   *Say:* "What should the robot do when the if part isn't true?"

### Example 2: Newton's laws and the dragster

**Two dragsters get the same push from a CO2 cartridge. One weighs 50 g and the other 70 g. Which goes faster?**

1. **Third law:** the cartridge pushes gas backward, and the gas pushes the car forward.
2. **Second law:** the same push speeds up a lighter car more, so the 50 g car accelerates faster.
3. **First law:** the car keeps moving until friction and air drag slow it down.
   *Say:* "What else could you change to make it faster?" (Reduce drag and friction.)
