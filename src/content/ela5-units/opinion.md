## What's the big idea?

An **opinion piece** tries to convince a reader to believe or do something. You'll read a **news article** (which informs and shows both sides), two **editorials** that disagree, a **photo essay**, and real survey numbers, all about one question: **should recess be longer?** You'll learn to separate **facts** from **opinions**, judge which **evidence** is strong, and see the issue from other people's **perspectives**. Then you'll pick your side and write a letter with **POW + TREE**: a clear opinion, reasons with evidence, an answer to the other side, and an ending that asks for action.
