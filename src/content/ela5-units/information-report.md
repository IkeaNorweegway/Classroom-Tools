## What's the big idea?

An **information report** teaches a reader about a topic, using facts. You'll read about Alberta's six **natural regions**: the dry Grassland, the Parkland, the Foothills, the Rocky Mountains, the huge Boreal Forest and the rocky Canadian Shield. Along the way you'll notice how information texts are built: **headings** for each subtopic, **diagrams** with captions, **bold words** and a **glossary**. Then you'll become a researcher. You'll pick one region, find **reliable sources**, take notes **in your own words**, and write a report with **POW + TIDE**: a topic sentence, important evidence, a detail and an ending for every paragraph.
