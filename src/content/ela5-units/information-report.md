## What's the big idea?

An **information report** teaches a reader about a topic, using facts. You'll read about how your heart, lungs, bones, brain and stomach work, and notice how information texts are built: **headings** for each subtopic, **diagrams** with captions, **bold words** and a **glossary**. Then you'll become a researcher. You'll pick one body system or organ, find **reliable sources**, take notes **in your own words**, and write a report with **POW + TIDE**: a topic sentence, important evidence, a detail and an ending for every paragraph.
