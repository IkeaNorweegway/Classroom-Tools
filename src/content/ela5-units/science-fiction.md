## What's the big idea?

**Science fiction** starts with "**What if?**" and imagines how science and technology could change people's lives and the **choices** they make. You'll read four stories set in the future, figure out each story's **theme** (its message, as a full sentence), notice how the **setting** shapes what characters do, and learn a trick authors use: the **flash-forward**, which jumps ahead in time to show what a choice leads to. Then you'll plan your own story with **POW + WWW**, written in the **third person**, with a flash-forward of your own.
