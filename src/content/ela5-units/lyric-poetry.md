## What's the big idea?

A **lyric poem** shares a feeling in a few words, using **sound**, **rhythm** and **pictures in words**. You'll listen to poems out loud (that's how they're meant to be heard), notice how **stanzas** organize them, and explain what **comparisons** like "hope is the thing with feathers" really mean. You'll learn a poem **by heart** and recite it, and you'll write your own poems about a **place** that matters to you, using your senses and one strong comparison.
