## What's the big idea?

A **tall tale** is a story about a hero who is bigger, stronger or faster than any real person could be, told with a straight face as if it were true. You'll read five of them, figure out how stories are **built** (beginning, problem, events, resolution, ending), and learn how authors **show** what characters are like. Then you'll use a writer's strategy called **POW** to plan, write and revise your own tall tale, and tell it out loud like the storytellers who first made these tales.
