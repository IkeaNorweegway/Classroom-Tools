## What's the big idea?

A square can be pushed out of shape, but a **triangle** can't. That's why bridges use **triangulation**. Piling on more sticks doesn't always help: it adds weight without adding strength. You'll test your bridge with more and more **load** until it breaks. When it fails, that's **data**: it shows you exactly where the weak spot is.
