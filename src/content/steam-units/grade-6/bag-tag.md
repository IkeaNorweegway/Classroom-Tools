## What's the big idea?

**CAD** means designing in 3D on a computer. But a screen design and a real print are not the same. Thin parts snap and small text disappears. That's the **CAD-to-print gap**. While your tag prints, you'll build a **portfolio** page to show your work, using a **grid**, a clear **hierarchy** and strong **contrast** so people can read it easily.
