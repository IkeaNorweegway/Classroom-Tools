## What's the big idea?

Four forces act on anything that flies: **lift** pushes up, **weight** pulls down, **thrust** pushes forward and **drag** pulls back. A glider has no engine, so balance is everything. This is your capstone: you'll research, design, build and test, then use your flight **data**, not guesses, to decide what to change.
