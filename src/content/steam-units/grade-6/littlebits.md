## What's the big idea?

Electricity needs a complete path, a **circuit**, to flow. In littleBits, every circuit goes **power → input → output**. A sensor bit works like an "if" in code, and the pulse bit works like a **loop**. You'll plan each build with a **circuit diagram** first, then pick your project: a game or an instrument.
