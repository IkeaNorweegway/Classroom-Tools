## What's the big idea?

A trebuchet is a giant **lever**. The **lever-arm ratio** decides how much speed you get. The falling **counterweight** transfers its **energy** to the projectile. At full size, the forces get much bigger, so the frame needs **bracing**. You'll prototype small first, but scaling up is not just "bigger": new problems show up. This is your capstone.
