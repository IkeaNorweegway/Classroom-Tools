## What's the big idea?

An **LED** needs the right circuit, with a resistor, to light without burning out. A **sensor** can trigger a behaviour, like lights turning on when someone comes close. You might add a small **moving part** as a stretch. The big idea: the look and the electronics are **one design**. Plan where the wires and parts go before you build the scene around them.
