## What's the big idea?

Games fake physics. **Gravity** is just a number that pulls the player down every frame. **Collision detection** checks whether the player is touching a platform, a wall or an enemy. **Game state** keeps track of score, lives, and whether you've won or lost. A better game isn't about more stuff. It's about controls and rules that feel fair.
