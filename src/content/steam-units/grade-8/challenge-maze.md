## What's the big idea?

Real robots use **several sensors** at once. You'll program a robot to solve a maze, then flip roles and **author** a challenge for another group. Your **spec** (the written rules) has to be clear enough for a stranger. The hardest part is spotting what you assumed without noticing. This is your capstone, and you'll document it in a video.
