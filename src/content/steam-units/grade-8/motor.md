## What's the big idea?

First you'll wire and **solder** a motor circuit safely. Then you'll find out what the motor can do **alone**. That's your baseline. **Mechanical advantage** lets the same motor do a bigger job with **gears**, **levers** or **pulleys**. There's always a trade-off: more force usually means less speed.
