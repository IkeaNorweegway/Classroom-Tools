## What's the big idea?

**Simple machines**, like levers, ramps, pulleys and wheels, move energy from one place to another. In a chain reaction, every stage has to **transfer energy** to the next. Working once isn't enough. It has to be **reliable**, every run. And the hand-off is your job too: if your stage can't trigger your neighbour's, the whole class chain stops.
