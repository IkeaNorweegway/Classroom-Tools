## What's the big idea?

**Scratch** lets you tell stories with code. Your story needs a **shape**: a beginning, a problem, a solution and an end. In Scratch, **events** start the action ("when green flag clicked"), and **broadcasts** let one sprite tell another when it's their turn. This is your capstone: you'll use the design cycle from the whole year to plan, build, test and improve your story.
