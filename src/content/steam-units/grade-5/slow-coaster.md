## What's the big idea?

A marble at the top of a ramp has **stored energy**. As it rolls down, that energy turns into **motion**. **Friction** steals some of it along the way. Your challenge is to control that energy so the marble keeps moving as long as possible, inside a set space. Every engineer works with **constraints**: rules about size, time and materials that the design has to fit.
