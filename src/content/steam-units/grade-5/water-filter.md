## What's the big idea?

A **filter** works in **layers**. Big gravel catches big bits, sand catches smaller ones, and cloth catches even smaller ones. You'll design a filter, test it the same way every time, and use your **data** to make it better. The most important idea: water that looks clear can still have germs you can't see. Clear is not the same as safe.
