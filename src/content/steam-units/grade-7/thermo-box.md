## What's the big idea?

Heat moves three ways: **conduction**, **convection** and **radiation**. A good **insulator** slows that movement down, often by trapping air. Light can be blocked, reflected or let through. Your micro:bit **logs data** over time, so you can see a **trend** and judge your box on the whole graph, not just one lucky reading.
