## What's the big idea?

When the CO2 shoots out the back, it pushes the car forward: **action and reaction**. **Friction** and **drag** work against you. Should you cut weight or improve the shape? Let the **data** decide. You'll shape your car by **cutting away** wood, so plan carefully, because you can't put it back. This is your capstone.
