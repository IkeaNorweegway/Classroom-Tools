## What's the big idea?

A **branch** is a point where the story splits. A **variable** works like memory: it remembers what the player chose so a later scene can change. Good choices actually matter to the ending. Before you share it, check the structure: every path has to reach a real ending, with no dead ends and no loops that never stop.
