## What's the big idea?

The big ideas from earlier grades carry over: **sequence**, **loops** and **conditionals** work the same way on a new tool. Spike Prime adds **variables**, which are labelled boxes that remember a number, like speed or a count. Change the variable and you change what the robot does, so you can predict its behaviour before you run it.
