## What's the big idea?

Before Canada could exist, its leaders had to agree on **how it would be run**. At the **Quebec Conference** (1864) and the **London Conference** (1866–67) they worked out the plan, and the **British North America Act** made it law on July 1, 1867. The Act set up three **branches** of government: one to carry out laws (the **executive**), one to make them (Parliament, the **legislative** branch) and one to interpret them (the courts, the **judicial** branch). It also split power between a **federal** government in Ottawa and the **provinces**. That system, called **federalism**, is still how Canada works.
