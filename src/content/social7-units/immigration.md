## What's the big idea?

To fill the West, the government offered **free land** under the **Dominion Lands Act** (1872). Hundreds of thousands of immigrants came, built towns and turned the Prairies into wheat farms. But Canada did not welcome everyone. Laws like the **Chinese Head Tax** and the **Continuous Journey Regulation** were written to keep some groups out, and events like the **Komagata Maru** and the **Anti-Asian Riots** show the conflict and **discrimination** newcomers faced. You'll weigh the evidence and decide: were these policies fair?
