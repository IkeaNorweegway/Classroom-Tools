## What's the big idea?

Canada was **built on purpose**. In 1867 four colonies joined to form the **Dominion of Canada**, partly because leaders worried the United States might take over British North America. Then the government **expanded west**: it took over Rupert's Land from the Hudson's Bay Company, signed **treaties** with First Nations, built a railway and brought in settlers. These choices built a country, but they also changed who controlled the land. The **Métis** at Red River and **First Nations** across the Prairies experienced this growth very differently than the government planned.
