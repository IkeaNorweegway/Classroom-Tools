## What's the big idea?

A country that can't keep order or defend itself has to rely on someone else. After 1867 Canada built its own **police** (the **North-West Mounted Police**, later the RCMP) and slowly its own **military**: Britain's army left in 1871, Canada started a navy in 1910, and Canadians fought in the **Boer War** and the **First World War**, where the **Canadian Corps** won victories like **Vimy Ridge**. Each step gave Canada more say over its own affairs, called **sovereignty**. In 1931 the **Statute of Westminster** gave Canada control of its own foreign policy.
