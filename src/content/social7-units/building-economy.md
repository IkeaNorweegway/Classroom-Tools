## What's the big idea?

After 1867 the government made deliberate choices to **grow the economy**. Sir John A. Macdonald's **National Policy** put **tariffs** (taxes) on imported goods so people would buy Canadian-made products, built the **Canadian Pacific Railway** to connect the regions, and settled the West to grow wheat. Different **regions** built different economies from their resources, like wheat on the Prairies and later oil in Alberta. Today we measure an economy with **GDP**, share wealth between provinces with **equalization**, and rely on **banks** and the **Bank of Canada** to keep money working.
