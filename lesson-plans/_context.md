# Lesson Plans Context

This folder holds lesson-by-lesson teaching plans — including sub plans (substitute teacher packages) and full lesson plans.

## What belongs here

- Sub plans (`sci6-[unit]-subplan-lesson[N]-v[N].md`) — complete packages a substitute can pick up and deliver
- Full lesson plans if built (`sci6-[unit]-lesson[N]-v[N].md`)

## What does NOT belong here

- Teacher notes packages (reference material, not lesson-by-lesson) → `notes-packages/`
- Worksheets or activity sheets used within a lesson → `worksheets/`

## Naming

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sub plan | `[subject]-[unit]-subplan-lesson[N]-v[N].md` | `sci6-forces-subplan-lesson1-v1.md` |
| Full lesson plan | `[subject]-[unit]-lesson[N]-v[N].md` | `sci6-forces-lesson1-v1.md` |

Files go in: `lesson-plans/[subject]/[unit]/filename.md`
